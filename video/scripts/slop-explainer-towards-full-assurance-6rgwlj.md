# Towards Full Assurance, Explained

The research explainer of `slop-poster-community-sporting-clubs-y8f9jx` (doi
10.5555/slop.y8f9jx), by Marek Solheim of the School of Emergent Priorities and
Torun Ezeigwe and Casimir Beng of the School of Continuous Improvement. Every
number is the poster's; the five levels' names and Ezeigwe's line are from the
poster's news post, and the closing line is Solheim's quote in it. The chart's
caption says no club held a tier for more than one round running, which its own
Club B contradicts, so the script reads the chart's lines instead.

Two figures are `file` images, which `fetch` doesn't make: before a render,
`assets/chart.png` is the poster's chart alone, rendered from its PDF with
`pdftoppm -png -singlefile -r 800 -x 4912 -y 6616 -W 3852 -H 1648`, and
`assets/inline-1.jpg` is the poster's inline illustration at full size, copied
from the press's `output/slop-poster-community-sporting-clubs-y8f9jx-images/`.
After every `voice`, on `assets/voice.wav` before `mix`, each author's segment,
and any narrator segment more than 1.5 dB off the narrator's median, is matched
to that median segment loudness (Solheim's voice comes back 4--9 dB hot,
Ezeigwe's 4--6 dB under, Beng's 1--3 dB under), and the 0.34 s of dead air that
ends "point seven one." is zeroed; every segment of the mix then sits within 1
LU.

## Key idea

A sporting club's working-with-children-check tier, scored at three audit
rounds, tracked the days left until its next audit more closely than any record
of how it took on volunteers: built to certify the club, it ends up measuring
the audit.
