# The Second Marker

A researcher promo for Dr Torun Ezeigwe, Senior Lecturer and Director of the
Master of Applied Measurement: mostly in her own voice, with the narrator
framing it. Her numbers are the site's own (`website/dist/facts.json`), where
her 214 outputs rank seventh of the University's 38 researchers; her examples
are three of her lead-authored studies, with their figures from the outputs' own
PDFs, and her closing line ends on the one her campaign poster quotes her saying
("The instrument earns scrutiny too.").

`assets/voice.wav` was level-matched by hand before `mix`, and a fresh `voice`
overwrites it, so redo both after one. Torun's voice comes back 4--5.5 dB under
the narrator (her opening clause at his level), so each of her segments was
matched to the narrator's median segment loudness (−22.2 LUFS) less 0.8 dB, the
gain applied from the previous segment's end; the raw take is kept beside it as
`assets/voice-raw.wav`.

## Key idea

When nearly everyone passes, Dr Torun Ezeigwe re-marks the instrument rather
than the people, and that work has made her one of the ten most published
researchers at Slop University.
