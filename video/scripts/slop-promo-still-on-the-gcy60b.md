# Still on the Agenda

A researcher promo for Dr Anneke Tolan, Senior Research Fellow and Deputy
Convenor of Futures in Committee in the School of Emergent Priorities: mostly in
her own voice, with the narrator framing it. Her numbers are the site's own
(`website/dist/facts.json`): 955 citations, an h-index of 25 and $460,460 in
funding from her two Anticipatory Capability Fund grants, and her 955 citations
are the most of the University's three Senior Research Fellows (the others have
890 and 715). Her practice is her roster bio (she embeds with strategy units,
review panels and working groups); her example is her newest poster, _Nobody's
Portfolio_ (`slop-poster-how-a-silvopastoral-1o3t1p`): thirty-two years of a
grazing cooperative's minutes, where "Trees --- ongoing" sat in 213 meetings and
outlived every chair.

Two hand steps outside the spec (`assets/` is gitignored), to redo before a
re-render. Before the first render: beat 3's chart is the poster's heatmap
rendered on its own at 1000 dpi, so the push-in stays sharp in the 4K cut. From

float32; and silence the breath after "working groups." and after "So I read the

ends before it).

## Key idea

Anneke Tolan sits in on the meetings where organisations plan their futures and
writes down what actually happens there, as when a grazing cooperative carried
one item, "Trees --- ongoing", through thirty-two years of minutes, and no
Senior Research Fellow at Slop University is cited more.
