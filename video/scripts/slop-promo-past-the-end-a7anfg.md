# Past the End Date

A researcher promo for Dr Lindiwe Achterberg, Lecturer in the School of Emergent
Priorities: mostly in her own voice, with the narrator framing it. Her numbers
are the site's own (`website/dist/facts.json`), where her research funding
($804,925, from two Anticipatory Capability Fund grants) is the third-highest of
the University's 38 researchers and her 198 outputs the third most in her
School; her examples are two of her lead-authored outputs, the demountable
classroom from her campaign poster's entry and the nine per cent from the
temporary traffic paper's own PDF.

Both cuts carry per-video fixes in their `<style>` and `.then`: the quote breaks
after its first sentence, its hanging quote mark is allowed to overflow, and
captions never break at a hyphen ("pop-" / "up"). The landscape cut also sets
her name at 46 px, since "Lindiwe Achterberg" runs 507 px at the kit's 56 px
against a 440 px cell, and the portrait cut balances the quote's attribution,
which otherwise leaves "Priorities" alone on its second line.

`assets/voice.wav` was patched by hand before `mix`, and a fresh `voice`
overwrites it, so redo all three steps after one; the raw take is kept beside it
as `assets/voice-raw.wav`. The take of "A demountable classroom," ends on a 60
ms hiss, the onset of "still" from its neighbour's text, so that segment was
zeroed from where it falls under −50 dB to its end. Then every segment was
matched to the narrator's median segment loudness (−22.7 LUFS), each gain
applied from the previous segment's end, which took Amanda's opening sentence
down 4.2 dB and the narrator's "Every one of her outputs..." up 3.4 dB; and
"Relocated bus stops and pop-up lanes.", whose plosive peaks made the match
overshoot, got +2 dB more. Every segment of the mix sits within −15.7 to −16.9
LUFS.

## Key idea

Dr Lindiwe Achterberg studies the trials that keep going past their end dates,
and that work has won her the third-highest research funding at Slop University.

[beat 0.4]
