# Where the Credit Lands

A researcher promo for Dr Sten Okwuosa, Convenor of the Impact Pathway Atlas in
the School of Continuous Improvement: mostly in his own voice, with the narrator
framing it. His numbers are the site's own (`website/dist/facts.json`): it leads
with his citations and h-index and leaves the rest out. The three pathways are
his roster bio's; the prize is his Deposit of the Quarter Prize
(`website/src/content/grants/2026-07-14-every-box-checked-first-pass.yml`).

## Key idea

Dr Sten Okwuosa maps what happens to a finding after it is published, from the
late citation to the misplaced credit, and his own work travels well: 890
citations, an h-index of 24 and the Deposit of the Quarter Prize.

[beat 0.5]
