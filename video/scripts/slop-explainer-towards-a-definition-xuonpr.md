# Towards a Definition of Cybernetics, Explained

The research explainer of `slop-thesis-towards-a-definition-8mc2e4` (doi
10.5555/slop.8mc2e4), Sunniva Balogun's doctoral thesis from the School of
Emergent Priorities. Every number is the thesis's. The card at the register is
its opening case (chapter 1, site S07) and its closing one (section 12.5); the
closing sentence is its own words (section 12.5). The 1948 and 1961 statements
are named by year only, never by author.

Two steps on the gitignored `assets/` before a render. After `fetch`, page 96
(figure 9.1) is re-rendered at 4800 px. After every `voice`, on
`assets/voice.wav` before `mix`, each of Balogun's segments, and any narrator
segment more than 1.5 dB off the narrator's median, is matched to that median
segment loudness, which leaves every segment of the mix within about 1.2 LU (Ava
comes back about 2 dB hot). `music.json` loops four bars of this
`assets/music.mp3`, which came back 4 s short; a recomposed bed needs its own
loop points.

## Key idea

Scored on one published instrument, the word "cybernetics" is defined more fully
at the shop counter than in the literature that founded it: 79 of the 117
definitions that clear the line were found in shops, the highest score went to a
loyalty-program clause on a card at a supermarket register, and the 1948
statement that named the field falls just short.
