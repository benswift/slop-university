The research explainer of `slop-paper-a-quasi-experiment-testing-3av1kp` (doi

## Key idea

A placard naming a meeting-room marker caddy's Directly Responsible Individual
left it no better stocked than an unlabelled one, since stocking tracked the
walk to the nearest stationery cupboard, but made staff far more confident it
would be restocked, though few could name the person on the placard.

most-flagged recurring failure. [beat 0.3] A new paper asks whether naming

**VO (casimir-beng):** My lab piloted placards naming a caddy's Directly
Responsible Individual. [beat 0.8]

_Visual:_ beat 2 (people): Casimir Beng, large, with his title and school.

**VO (joost-nwosu):** We paired sixty meeting rooms by building wing and how

_Visual:_ beat 3 (people): Joost Nwosu, large, with his title and school.

**VO:** Every week for twelve weeks, an auditor tested every caddy. [beat 0.2]
It passed with at least three working markers, [beat 0.2] and an eraser that
wiped a test line clean. [beat 0.9]

_Visual:_ beat 4 (cards): the weekly audit and the paper's two thresholds for a
stocked caddy, each arriving as it is said.

**VO:** Across the term, caddies with and without a placard tracked each other.
[beat 0.3] Labelled ones passed fifty-seven point seven per cent of the time.
[beat 0.2] Unlabelled ones, fifty-eight. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ beat 5 (figure): Figure 1, stocked-rate by condition in each third of
the term, held whole; then from "Labelled" beat 6 (stats): 57.7% and 58.0%, each

**VO:** The strongest predictor was the walk to the nearest stationery cupboard.
[beat 0.3] Within fifteen metres of one, caddies passed seventy-four per cent of
the time. [beat 0.2] Beyond fifty metres, fifty-six, placard or not. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ beat 7 (figure): Figure 2, stocked-rate against distance to the
cupboard, held whole; then from "Within" beat 8 (stats): 74% and 56%.

**VO (mirela-hanke):** We also asked staff two questions. [beat 0.2] Could they

_Visual:_ beat 9 (people): Mirela Hanke, large, with her title and school.

**VO:** Only nine point three per cent in labelled rooms could name the person.

_Visual:_ beat 10 (stat): 9.3% counting up; then from "Yet" beat 11 (stats): 71%

**VO:** A placard buys reassurance. [beat 0.3] A shorter walk to the stationery
cupboard buys markers. [beat 0.9]

_Visual:_ beat 12 (statement): the paper's conclusion in type, "A shorter walk
buys markers." in gold on a line of its own.

**VO (casimir-beng):** A name on a wall doesn't restock anything by itself.
[beat 0.3] What it changes is how safe everyone feels not checking. [beat 1.0]
