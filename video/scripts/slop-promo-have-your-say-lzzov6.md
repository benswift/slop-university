# Have Your Say

A researcher promo for Dr Ronja Oyelaran, Senior Lecturer and Deputy Convenor of
the Strategic Drift Survey in the School of Emergent Priorities: mostly in her
own voice, with the narrator framing it. Her numbers are the site's own
(`website/dist/facts.json`), where her research funding ($419,637, from one
Anticipatory Capability Fund grant) is the tenth-highest of the University's 38
researchers and her h-index is 24. Her background is her roster bio; her
examples are two of her lead-authored outputs, the bus-stop comment box from her
"Posted Means Consulted" poster's entry and the six readings of "working" from
her "Working Means Six Things" poster's entry.

Both cuts carry per-video fixes in their `<style>` and `.then`: the quote breaks
after its first sentence, its hanging quote mark is allowed to overflow, and
captions never break at a hyphen ("bus-" / "stop"). The portrait cut also
recentres her person hero, which the hero and the quote both crop, on her at the
lectern (she stands at 0.7 of its width), and breaks the quote's attribution
after her name.

`assets/voice.wav` was patched by hand before `mix`, and a fresh `voice`
overwrites it, so redo the patch after one; the raw take is kept beside it as
`assets/voice-raw.wav`. The take of "I trace where it goes." comes back quiet
and ends in 0.84 s of dead air, zeroed from 30 ms after its last word; that gap
stands in for the pause after it, hence its `[beat 0]`. Then every segment was
matched to the narrator's median segment loudness (−22.0 LUFS), each gain
applied from the previous segment's end, which took "I trace where it goes." up
5.3 dB, "where every slip counts ..." up 3.6 dB and the narrator's "from the
Anticipatory Capability Fund." up 4.4 dB. Every segment of the mix sits within
−15.2 to −16.9 LUFS.

## Key idea

Dr Ronja Oyelaran traces what becomes of the views a consultation invites, and
that work has put her research funding in the top ten at Slop University.

## Script

**VO (ronja-oyelaran):** Every consultation invites you to have your say. [beat
0.2] I trace where it goes. [beat 0]

_Visual:_ beat 0 (hero): her person hero, at a lectern with a sheaf of papers
before rows of empty folding chairs, a submissions box on the table behind her,
under the scrim, with the title block: "Have Your Say", "Dr Ronja Oyelaran and
where your feedback goes", "School of Emergent Priorities".

**VO:** Dr Ronja Oyelaran joined us from a state planning authority. [beat 0.3]

_Visual:_ beat 1 (people): her headshot, large, with her name, title and school,
dissolving in over the hero on "Dr".

**VO (ronja-oyelaran):** I brought one question with me. [beat 0.15] What were
we really asking? [beat 0.45]

_Visual:_ beat 2 (statement): "What were we really asking?" under the kicker
"Where it started", "really asking?" in gold.

**VO (ronja-oyelaran):** A comment box on a bus-stop pole, [beat 0.1] where
every slip counts as a completed consultation. [beat 0.25] Or a smoke-alarm
survey, [beat 0.1] where the word working meant six different things. [beat
0.35]

_Visual:_ beat 3 (figure): the hero of "Posted Means Consulted", a rider reading
a slip beside the overflowing comment box on the shelter's pole, labelled with
its title, pushing in on the box at "where"; then beat 4 (figure, from "Or"):
the hero of "Working Means Six Things", a surveyor with a clipboard passing
three open front doors, a smoke alarm on each hallway ceiling, labelled with its
title, pushing in on the middle hallway at "word".

**VO:** Her research funding puts her among the University's top ten. [beat
0.45]

_Visual:_ beat 5 (pages): thirty-six of her covers, her most-cited first, drop
into a grid by "among" and hold.

**VO:** Nearly four hundred and twenty thousand dollars, [beat 0.2] from the
Anticipatory Capability Fund. [beat 0.25] And an h-index of twenty-four. [beat
0.9]

_Visual:_ beat 6 (stats): $420K as the line opens, and 24 landing whole on
"h-index".

**VO (ronja-oyelaran):** People take the time to have their say. [beat 0.2] The
least we can do is read it. [beat 1.2]

_Visual:_ beat 7 (quote): her words over her person hero, under the scrim, each
word lighting as she says it, "The least we can do is read it." in gold.

**VO:** Her outputs are all on our website. [beat 0.15] Find us on Bluesky,
[beat 0.15] or by email. [beat 0.4]

_Visual:_ the close: the website address as the line begins, then Bluesky on its
word and the email address on "email", then the lockup.
