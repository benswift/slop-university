# For the Next Person

A researcher promo for Dr Iben Chikere, Lecturer in the School of Emergent
Priorities: mostly in her own voice, with the narrator framing it. Her numbers
are the site's own (`website/dist/facts.json`), where no researcher at the
University holds more research funding, none in her School has more outputs, and
her co-authors are every other researcher on the roster; her examples are three
of her handover studies.

## Key idea

Iben Chikere studies what a handover note keeps of a role and what it leaves
out, and that work, written with every one of her colleagues, has won her more
research funding than anyone else at the University.

## Script

**VO (iben-chikere):** Sooner or later, every role changes hands. [beat 0]
Someone writes a note for the next person. [beat 0.6]

_Visual:_ beat 0 (hero): her person hero, handing a stack of binders to a
colleague at the office door, under the scrim, with the title block: "For the
Next Person", "Dr Iben Chikere on what a role keeps when it changes hands",
"School of Emergent Priorities".

**VO:** At Slop University, Dr Iben Chikere studies what those notes keep, [beat
0.2] and what they leave out. [beat 0.6]

_Visual:_ beat 1 (people): her headshot, large, with her name, title and school,
as the narrator introduces her.

**VO (iben-chikere):** Most street library volunteers learn the checklist from
the last one, [beat 0.15] not the policy. [beat 0.5] When a house is sold, [beat
0.15] the sprinklers keep the old owners' hours. [beat 0.5]

_Visual:_ beat 2 (figure): the hero of "Shelved Without a Look", labelled with
its title, pushing in on the hand at the pile of donated books at "checklist";
then beat 3 (figure, from "When"): the hero of "Towards a Custody Taxonomy of
Inherited Automation", labelled with its title, pushing in on the front-lawn
sprinklers at "house".

**VO (iben-chikere):** And new volunteer officers who ring their predecessor are
more likely to stay on. [beat 0.6]

_Visual:_ beat 4 (figure): the hero of "Still on the Phone List", the folder,
phone, cash tin and keys on a trestle table, labelled with its title, pushing in
on the phone at "ring".

**VO:** No researcher at the University has won more funding than Dr Chikere.
[beat 0.8]

_Visual:_ beat 5 (pages): thirty-six of her covers drop into a grid by
"University", her most-cited papers among them, and hold.

**VO:** More than one and a half million dollars. [beat 0.3] And two hundred and
nineteen outputs, [beat 0.15] the most in her School. [beat 0.8]

_Visual:_ beat 6 (stats): $1.6M as the line begins, then 219 on its number.

**VO (iben-chikere):** I've written with all thirty-seven of my colleagues.
[beat 0.3] You can't study handovers on your own. [beat 0.8]

_Visual:_ beat 7 (stat): 37 co-authors, every other researcher at the
University, as her voice comes in.

**VO:** Look her up on our website, [beat 0.15] follow along on Bluesky, [beat
0.15] or leave us a note. [beat 0.4]

_Visual:_ the close: the website address as the line begins, then Bluesky on its
word and the email address on "note", then the lockup.
