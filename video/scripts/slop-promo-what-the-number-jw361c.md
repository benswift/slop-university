# What the Number Was For

A researcher promo for Dr Mirela Hanke, Postdoctoral Fellow and Deputy Convenor
of the Living Dashboard: mostly in her own voice, with the narrator framing it.
Her numbers are the site's own (`website/dist/facts.json`): 1,158 citations and
an h-index of 28. The one ranking it claims is the h-index, the highest of any
postdoctoral fellow, because it holds as counts drift: the next postdoc's
h-index is 27, with his 28th output on six citations, while her citation rank
moves daily. Her examples are three of her most-cited papers, two of them
first-authored.

## Key idea

Mirela Hanke follows a number from measurement to decision to goal, and asks
what it was for, and no postdoc at Slop University has a higher h-index.

## Script

**VO:** At Slop University, Dr Mirela Hanke is Deputy Convenor of the Living
Dashboard. [beat 0.6]

the scrim, with the title block: "What the Number Was For", "Dr Mirela Hanke and
the Living Dashboard", "School of Continuous Improvement".

**VO (mirela-hanke):** I study how a dashboard shapes the choices it's only
meant to inform. [beat 0.5]

_Visual:_ beat 1 (people): her headshot, large, with her name, title and school,
as her voice comes in.

**VO (mirela-hanke):** A number starts out as a measurement. [beat 0.3] Then

**VO (mirela-hanke):** A cafe chain that measures friendliness by its tip
screen. [beat 0.3] New vending machines, credited with a point the day they went
in. [beat 0.3] Gym members who cancel just in time to keep their score clean.

_Visual:_ beat 7 (stats): 1,158 and 28 count up, each on its number, and hold

**VO (mirela-hanke):** When everyone is chasing a number, [beat 0.2] I ask what

_Visual:_ beat 8 (quote, spoken): "I ask what it was for." over her hero under
