# Where the Oversize Comes From, Explained

The research explainer of `slop-paper-tracing-oversize-boulders-7vh3fg` (doi
10.5555/slop.7vh3fg), by Ragnhild Uzoma and Brynjar Obiora of the School of
Underlying Conditions. Every number is the paper's; the closing line is Uzoma's
quote in the paper's news post.

## Key idea

Most of the oversize from three quarry blasts was standing in the top two metres
of the bench, inside the stemming the explosive never reaches, and the drill's
own record had already described that ground.
