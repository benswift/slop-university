# Hand to Hand

A researcher promo for Dr Solveig Adeyemi, Lecturer in the School of Continuous
Improvement: mostly in her own voice, with the narrator framing it. Her numbers
are the site's own (`website/dist/facts.json`), where her 265 outputs are the
most of any lecturer at the University (only Associate Professor Casimir Beng
has more) and her citations number 1,095; her examples are three studies she
led, each following a report on its way to a decision.

## Key idea

Solveig Adeyemi follows a report from whoever writes it to whoever decides,
tracking what gets dropped at each handoff, and no lecturer at Slop University
has published more.

## Script

**VO:** Dr Solveig Adeyemi is a lecturer at Slop University. [beat 0.6]

_Visual:_ beat 0 (hero): her person hero, a report passed hand to hand down a
records corridor, under the scrim, with the title block: "Hand to Hand", "Dr
Solveig Adeyemi and the life of a report", "School of Continuous Improvement".

**VO (solveig-adeyemi):** In emergency services, I saw how few reports ever
reached an agenda. [beat 0.6]

_Visual:_ beat 1 (people): her headshot, large, with her name, title and school,
as her voice comes in.

**VO (solveig-adeyemi):** Now I follow a report from whoever writes it [beat
0.15] to whoever decides. [beat 0.3] At each handoff, I track what gets dropped.
[beat 0.6]

_Visual:_ beat 2 (figure): her hero, framed whole, pushing in on the chain of
hands at "follow", then on the bin and the falling pages at "At".

**VO (solveig-adeyemi):** A library door that kept squeaking, [beat 0.15] filed
as a hazard. [beat 0.45] A childcare network's incident notes, [beat 0.15] and
what changes on the next shift. [beat 0.45] A reply-all storm on a netball
mailing list. [beat 0.3]

_Visual:_ beats 3 to 5 (figure), one study's hero each, labelled with its title:
"The Hinge That Kept Squeaking", pushing in on the open door's hinges at "door";
from "childcare", "Written Down, Left There", pushing in on the clipboard and
the worker leaving at "incident"; from "reply-all", "Sixty-One Messages About
Training Bibs", pushing in on the storm's funnel and the phones at "storm".

**VO:** No lecturer at Slop University has published more. [beat 1.2]

_Visual:_ beat 6 (pages): thirty-six of her covers drop into a grid, her
most-cited papers among them.

**VO:** Two hundred and sixty-five research outputs, [beat 0.2] and one thousand
and ninety-five citations. [beat 0.8]

_Visual:_ beat 7 (stats): 265 and 1,095 count up, each on its number.

**VO (solveig-adeyemi):** Someone always notices first. [beat 0.3] I want to
know where their report goes. [beat 1.2]

_Visual:_ beat 8 (quote, spoken): her words over the hero of _Somebody Saw It
First_, the wet-floor cone left standing in an empty food court, under the
scrim, each word lighting as she says it, "where their report goes." in gold,
her name up from the start.

**VO:** There's more on our website, [beat 0.15] on Bluesky, [beat 0.15] and by
email. [beat 0.4]

_Visual:_ the close: the website address as the line begins, then Bluesky and
email on their words, then the lockup.
