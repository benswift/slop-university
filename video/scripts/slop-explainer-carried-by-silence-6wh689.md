# Carried by Silence, Explained

The research explainer of `slop-thesis-carried-by-silence-of4rfn` (doi
10.5555/slop.of4rfn), Ylva Onyekwere's doctoral thesis from the School of
Emergent Priorities. Every number is the thesis's. The barbecue is its opening
case (Table 1.1, a composite built to the census medians), the ice is the
barbecue it attended (A15, section 7.2.1), and the quoted passages are its own
words (the abstract, sections 1.4 and 11.3).

## Key idea

In the group chats that plan barbecues, birthday parties and working bees, a
proposal becomes the plan not by being agreed to but by going unanswered, on a
median of two thumbs whether the chat has five members or twenty, and what turns
up on the day is what somebody put their name to, which is why the School's
committees now carry a motion on two affirming reactions and forty minutes
without a typed reply.
