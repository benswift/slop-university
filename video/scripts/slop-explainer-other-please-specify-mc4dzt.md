# Other (Please Specify), Explained

The research explainer of `slop-thesis-other-please-specify-fr8eys` (doi
10.5555/slop.fr8eys), the doctoral thesis by Halvard Nnamani of the School of
Emergent Priorities. Every number is the thesis's. The parent at "How did you
hear about us?" is the thesis's own opening case (Section 1.1), and the quote on
that list is R17's think-aloud (Section 8.2). Nnamani's lines are his thesis's
first person: his seasons as a registrar (Section 5.6) and its closing claim
(Chapter 11). The Standard on screen is clause 2 of Appendix F.

The rendered master rests on hand work in the gitignored `assets/`, to redo
after any `voice` or `fetch`: in `voice.wav`, the dead air and blip ending
segments 2.1 and 9.0 are zeroed and every segment is matched to the narrator's
level (Dayo's takes run from −14 to −21 LUFS); pages 75 and 97 in
`assets/pages/` are re-rendered at 6400 px for the push-ins.

## Key idea

The "Other (please specify)" box on a community organisation's online form does
its work at the moment of typing: fewer than one entry in seven named anything
the list had missed, three boxes in four had been seen by no one, and in a
trial, discarding what was typed changed nothing measured while removing the box
changed both completion and contact.
