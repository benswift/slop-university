# Lighter by Design

The Slop University Sustainability Initiative, as a three-minute sizzle reel:
the University's research output and the real energy and carbon cost of making
it, set against the sector's. It plays straight until the University's footprint
is on screen, then steps outside the fiction as the colophon does, says the
numbers are real and how they were counted, and steps back in to close.
Landscape, about three minutes, for HORIZONS (Venice, 18--22 November 2026) and
for a sustainability page on the site that carries the same figures and the
method.

Every figure is a central estimate as of 9 November 2026 (the working is in the
scratchpad's `usage/` and will move to an `ops/` script). The sector figures are
sums built from members' own reports, since no group publishes one: Go8 (ANU,
Melbourne, Sydney, UNSW and Monash, 2025) and LERU (Imperial, Copenhagen and
Helsinki, 2023--24), each about 16 tCO2e per publication on OpenAlex counts; the
Go8 five publish about 1.5 works per staff member a year.

## Key idea

Slop University is the most productive and, per output, the greenest university
there is, because it has no buildings, no commutes and no flights; the numbers
are real, and so is the trade it makes for them.

## Timing

Target a 3:15 cut. Deliver the narration at about 120 words per minute, with
an earnest institutional cadence. The 328 spoken words and 25 seconds of marked
pauses total about 3:09 at that pace. Hold the final website address for another
six seconds beyond its marked pause so viewers have time to read it.

Time the generated voice before animating; adjust visual holds to reach 3:15.
Check the exported submission is at least 3:00 and no longer than 5:00.

## Script

**VO:** At Slop University, we publish more research than ever. [beat 0.4] And
we do it lighter than anyone. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ a hero under the scrim with the title block: the lockup, "Lighter by
Design", a gold rule, "The Slop University Sustainability Initiative". On
"lighter" the title block clears.

_Reads (draft):_ 1. this is the University's sustainability initiative.

**VO:** Since July, our forty-one researchers have published three thousand, two
hundred and twenty-one research outputs. [beat 0.3] Papers, posters, theses,
strategies, [beat 0.2] and films. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ covers drop into a grid, faster and faster, and a counter climbs to
3,221; on each of "papers", "posters", "theses", "strategies" and "films" one
cover of that kind lifts out of the grid and settles back.

_Reads (draft):_ 1. there are thousands; 2. of every kind.

**VO:** That's more than two hundred outputs per staff member, every year. [beat
0.4] The world's leading research universities manage about one and a half.
[beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ two stats side by side: "224" in gold, "per staff member per year",
and "1.5" in ink.

_Reads (draft):_ 1. ours is a hundred and fifty times theirs.

**VO:** Every one of those outputs has a carbon footprint. [beat 0.3] So we
measured it. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ the grid stills; one paper's cover lifts out and comes forward; a gold
meter draws on beside it, empty. On "measured", the word "Measured*" appears
beside the meter, with "*estimated" in small type in the bottom corner above
the caption band. Both remain for the rest of the shot.

_Reads (draft):_ 1. we're following one output now.

**VO:** A Slop University paper takes about one kilowatt hour of electricity.
[beat 0.4] About five full kettles. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ the meter fills to "1 kWh"; five kettles draw on in a row beneath it,
one per beat of the line's last phrase.

_Reads (draft):_ 1. one output, one kilowatt hour; 2. that's small.

**VO:** That's about four hundred grams of carbon dioxide per output. [beat 0.4]
At leading research universities, in Europe and in Australia, [beat 0.2] it's
about sixteen tonnes. [beat 1.2]

_Visual:_ two bars from a shared baseline. Ours, "0.4 kg", is a sliver of gold.
The sector's, in ink, grows past the top of the frame; the camera pulls back,
logarithmically, until its top comes into view and the gold sliver is a speck.

_Reads (draft):_ 1. ours is tiny; 2. theirs is forty thousand times larger.

**VO:** How do we do it? [beat 0.4] We have no buildings to heat or cool. [beat
0.3] No car parks. [beat 0.2] No commutes. [beat 0.3] And no conference travel.
[beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ four line drawings across the frame, one per phrase: a teaching
building, a car park, a bus, a plane. Each draws on, then empties to a faint
outline as the next arrives.

_Reads (draft):_ 1. the things a university burns carbon on; 2. we have none of
them.

**VO:** Our doctoral theses run to about a hundred and forty pages. [beat 0.3]
Each one takes about two hours to write, [beat 0.2] and emits about five
kilograms of carbon dioxide. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ the ten theses' covers stand in a row like spines on a shelf; one
comes forward and its pages riffle through; a clock face beside it sweeps two
hours; a gold "4.9 kg" lands under it.

_Reads (draft):_ 1. these are our theses; 2. two hours and five kilograms each.

**VO:** One study of a human PhD put it at twenty-one tonnes, [beat 0.3] over
four years. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ beside our thesis, a human one in ink; a stack of calendar pages piles
up behind it, four years' worth, and its bar climbs past ours and out of frame.

_Reads (draft):_ 1. a human PhD; 2. thousands of times more.

**VO:** Our researchers' working day starts the moment it's needed, [beat 0.2]
and ends the moment it's done. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ the roster's headshots fill a grid; each lights as a clock hand passes
over it, then dims.

_Reads (draft):_ 1. these are the researchers; 2. they only exist while they
work.

**VO:** Since it opened, the whole University has emitted about one point three
tonnes. [beat 0.4] Less than one return flight from Australia to Venice. [beat
1.4]

_Visual:_ a map line arcs from Canberra to Venice and back; beside it, the
flight's bar in ink and the University's in gold, the gold one shorter.

_Reads (draft):_ 1. this is the whole University's footprint; 2. it is less than
one flight.

**VO:** These numbers are real. [beat 0.6] Slop University is an artwork. [beat
0.3] Its researchers and their outputs are generated by language models, [beat
0.2] running on a timer. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ a hard cut to the colophon's plain ground; the clock face from the
colophon draws on, its hand ticking once a beat.

_Reads (draft):_ 1. we've stepped outside the fiction; 2. the same machine as
the colophon.

**VO:** We counted every token the models read and wrote, [beat 0.2] and every
image they made, [beat 0.3] then turned them into energy with the best published
estimates. [beat 0.3] The method is on our website. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ two counters on the clock face: "40 billion tokens", "8,759 images";
arrows from both run into a single gold figure, "3.3 MWh".

_Reads (draft):_ 1. what was counted; 2. what it came to.

**VO:** Most of that energy went on the models rereading their own instructions,
[beat 0.2] again and again. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ one block of text, the instructions, passes through a model box; the
same block loops back and passes through again, faster, leaving a gold trace
each time.

_Reads (draft):_ 1. the same text, read over and over.

**VO:** Per output, Slop University may be the greenest university in the world.
[beat 0.6] It has also never taught anyone anything. [beat 1.6]

_Visual:_ the gold sliver from the bar chart returns, alone on the ground. On
"never taught" it holds, and nothing else arrives.

_Reads (draft):_ 1. the claim is true; 2. and so is this.

**VO:** Slop University. [beat 0.3] More research, [beat 0.3] less of everything
else. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ back into the sizzle: the cover grid fills the frame again, at full
speed, and the counter keeps climbing past 3,221.

_Reads (draft):_ 1. it is still running.

**VO:** Read our full sustainability report on the website. [beat 2.0]

_Visual:_ `slop.university/sustainability` appears; it clears to the closing
lockup: the rules grow, then the crest and wordmark fade in.

_Reads (draft):_ 1. where to find the figures.
