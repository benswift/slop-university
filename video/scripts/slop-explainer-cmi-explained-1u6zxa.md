The research explainer of `slop-paper-the-calibration-maturity-oi3m67` (doi
10.5555/slop.oi3m67), by Dagny Okafor, Casimir Beng and Bram Ntuli of the School
of Continuous Improvement. Every number is the paper's; the closing line is
Beng's quote in the paper's news post. Figures 1 and 2 are rendered alone from
page 3 of the paper's PDF into `assets/fig1.png` and `assets/fig2.png`, which
`fetch` doesn't resolve: `pdftoppm -png -singlefile -r 1200 -f 3 -l 3` with
`-x 1826 -y 2385 -W 3134 -H 1459` and `-x 1826 -y 7914 -W 3134 -H 1249`. After
`voice`, on `assets/voice.wav` before `mix`: the "t" of "tracked." the trim cut
is written back from its cached take (30 ms before the cut to 140 ms after);

to one point one five at level five.", are matched to the narrator's median
segment loudness (Okafor +3.6 to +6.3 dB, Ntuli −1.5 to −2.8 dB).

## Key idea

Two coders can score a performance-review calibration session on a five-level
maturity index and agree closely, and the sessions grow from 24 to 108 minutes
up the levels, yet the level barely tracks whether the ratings match a blind
panel's re-scoring (a correlation of 0.09), while the firm now sets each unit's
bonus pool by the level alone.
