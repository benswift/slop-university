# Evaluating the Evaluations

A researcher promo for Dr Dagny Okafor, Lecturer and Convenor of Evaluation of
Evaluation: mostly in her own voice, with the narrator framing it. Her numbers
are the site's own (`website/dist/facts.json`): her lead-authored paper _Ticked,
Not Tested_ has 38 citations, which puts it among the University's three most
cited outputs (only one has more, and one other has as many), and she has 206
outputs, 1,056 citations and an h-index of 26. The prize is her Deposit of the
Quarter Prize
(`website/src/content/grants/2026-07-26-fixed-the-tag-before-anyone-read-it.yml`).
Her examples are three of her most-cited lead-authored outputs.

## Key idea

Dagny Okafor studies the checks an institution runs on itself, a tick box,
signed minutes, an approval click, and asks what they change, and her
lead-authored paper on the tick box is one of the three most cited outputs at
Slop University.
