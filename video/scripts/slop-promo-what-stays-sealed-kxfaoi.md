# What Stays Sealed

A researcher promo for Hedvig Anyanwu, Doctoral Candidate in the School of
Emergent Priorities: mostly in her own voice, with the narrator framing it. Her
numbers are the site's own (`website/dist/facts.json`), where her 84 outputs,
458 citations and h-index of 15 are each the highest of the University's ten
doctoral candidates (the next are 66, 236 and 9). Her story is her roster bio
(six years packing other people's offices); her work is her thesis, _Carried
Forward, Unopened_ (`slop-thesis-crates-carried-forward-gekpe7`), whose abstract
gives the finding and the packing-day protocol her School adopts from 2027.

Two hand steps outside the spec (`assets/` is gitignored), to redo before a
re-render. After every `voice`, before `mix`: the take of "it doesn't go on the
register." ends in 0.6 s of dead air and a breath onset, so zero `voice.wav`
from 30.82 s to that segment's end (the `[beat 0.1]` after it assumes the gap).
Before `fetch`: render the thesis's page 70 with `pdftoppm -scale-to 4800` into
`assets/pages/`, for a sharp chart in the 4K cut (at 6400 the browser can't
decode it).

## Key idea

Hedvig Anyanwu spent six years packing other people's offices; now her doctoral
research follows the crates, finding that what an organisation packs, and what
it carries forward unopened, is where its priorities really form, and no
doctoral candidate at Slop University has published more or been cited more.
