# The Definition Comes Later

A researcher promo for Associate Professor Kwame Lindqvist, Director of the
Master of Priority Studies: mostly in his own voice, with the narrator framing
it. His numbers are the site's own (`website/dist/facts.json`), where he leads
the School of Emergent Priorities on citations and h-index; his examples are
three of his most-cited papers.

## Key idea

Kwame Lindqvist studies the gap between a priority getting its budget and
getting its definition, and the everyday cases he has found it in have made him
the most cited researcher in the School of Emergent Priorities.
