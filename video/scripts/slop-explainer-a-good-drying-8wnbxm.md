# A Good Drying Day, Explained

The research explainer of `slop-thesis-a-good-drying-ayp8dq` (doi
10.5555/slop.ayp8dq), Ylva Ogunbanjo's doctoral thesis from the School of
Emergent Priorities. Every number is the thesis's; the quoted passages are its
own words (section 5.5.3 and section 7.4), and Ogunbanjo's closing line restates
its conclusion (section 8.1).

## Key idea

On the suburban washing line the full basket, not the forecast, decides when the
washing goes out, and what keeps it dry is a load small enough to bring back in
at once, which is why the School now caps every unit's commitments at what its
convenor can carry in alone, in one trip, within four minutes.
