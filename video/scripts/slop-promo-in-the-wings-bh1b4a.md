# In the Wings

A researcher promo for Dr Thandiwe Solberg, Senior Lecturer and Convenor of Demo
Quarter, the School of Continuous Improvement's quarterly Masters-program
showcase: mostly in her own voice, with the narrator framing it. Her lines on
Demo Quarter come from her roster bio. Her numbers are the site's own
(`website/dist/facts.json`): 973 citations and an h-index of 25. Her three
examples are outputs she led on shows of other kinds, and all three are in the
cover grid. Her voice comes back 5--10 dB under the narrator's, so before `mix`
each of her segments is matched on `assets/voice.wav` to the narrator's median
segment loudness (−22.3 LUFS), and the narrator's "and an h-index of
twenty-five." is lifted 1.8 dB: redo both after any `voice`.

## Key idea

Thandiwe Solberg convenes Demo Quarter, the University's quarterly showcase, and
studies what happens to work before it reaches the stage, there and at every
other kind of show, while her own research has found a wide audience.

## Script

**VO:** Four times a year, our Masters students put their work on stage at Demo
Quarter. [beat 0.6]

_Visual:_ beat 0 (hero): her person hero, from the wings of a stage towards one
object in a spotlight, under the scrim, with the title block: "In the Wings",
"Dr Thandiwe Solberg and Demo Quarter", "School of Continuous Improvement".

**VO (thandiwe-solberg):** I'm Thandiwe Solberg, and I convene it. [beat 0.3] I
study what happens before the work reaches the stage. [beat 0.6]

_Visual:_ beat 1 (people): her headshot, large, with her name, title and school,
as her voice comes in.

**VO (thandiwe-solberg):** The rehearsal that gets cut. [beat 0.3] The one
number chosen to stand for a whole sprint. [beat 0.3] The version judged fit to
show. [beat 0.8]

_Visual:_ beat 2 (figure): her hero, framed whole, pushing in on the crates and
cable piled in the wings at "cut", then across to her and the object in the
spotlight at "version".

**VO (thandiwe-solberg):** I study other shows, too. [beat 0.3] The judging at a
country show. [beat 0.3] A standing ovation. [beat 0.3] Even the daily stand-up
meeting. [beat 0.8]

_Visual:_ beat 3 (cards), from "The": one output per example, each on its first
words: "Well Presented, Slightly Overset", "STANCE", "Nobody Typed the Blocker
That Mattered".

**VO:** And her own research has found a wide audience. [beat 0.6] Nine hundred
and seventy-three citations, [beat 0.2] and an h-index of twenty-five. [beat
1.4]

_Visual:_ beat 4 (pages): thirty-six of her covers, the three examples among
them, drop into a grid; beat 5 (stats), from "Nine": 973 and 25 count up, each
on its number.

**VO (thandiwe-solberg):** I love the moment the work goes on stage. [beat 0.3]
I want to know what stayed in the wings. [beat 0.8]

_Visual:_ beat 6 (quote): her words, each lit as she says it, "what stayed in
the wings." in gold.

**VO:** Her full record is on our website. [beat 0.15] Join us on Bluesky, [beat
0.15] or say hello by email. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ the close: the website as the line begins, then Bluesky and email on
their words, then the lockup.
