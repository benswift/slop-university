# Checking the Measures

A researcher promo for Associate Professor Casimir Beng, Lead of the Adaptive
Metrics Lab: mostly in his own voice, with the narrator framing it. His numbers
are the site's own (`website/dist/facts.json`), where he leads the University on
outputs, citations and h-index; his examples are three of his most-cited
outputs.

## Key idea

Casimir Beng checks whether the everyday measures organisations run on really
measure what they say they do, and that work has made him the University's most
published and most cited researcher.

## Script

**VO:** Associate Professor Casimir Beng leads the Adaptive Metrics Lab at Slop
University. [beat 0.6]

_Visual:_ beat 0 (hero): his person hero, at the control panel, under the scrim,
with the title block: "Checking the Measures", "Associate Professor Casimir Beng
and the Adaptive Metrics Lab", "School of Continuous Improvement".

**VO (casimir-beng):** I trained as a control engineer. [beat 0.3] Now I study
the measures that organisations run on. [beat 0.5]

_Visual:_ beat 1 (people): his headshot, large, with his name, title and school,
as his voice comes in.

**VO (casimir-beng):** The beep at the self-checkout. [beat 0.3] The tick box
that says a worker was tested. [beat 0.3] The smart sprinkler on the quad. [beat
0.9]

_Visual:_ beat 2 (cards): one output per example, each on its word: "Trusting
the Beep", "Ticked, Not Tested", "The Schedule Waters Regardless".

**VO (casimir-beng):** Each one says it measures something. [beat 0.3] My lab
checks whether it does. [beat 0.8]

_Visual:_ beat 3 (statement): the line in type, "checks whether it does" in
gold, under the lab's name.

**VO:** That work has made him the University's most published and most cited
researcher. [beat 0.6]

_Visual:_ beat 4 (pages): thirty-six of his covers drop into a grid.

**VO:** Three hundred and eleven research outputs. [beat 0.3] Fifteen hundred

_Visual:_ beat 5 (stats): 311, 1,561 and 32 count up, each on its number.

**VO (casimir-beng):** I still think like an engineer. [beat 0.3] If the gauge

0.6]

_Visual:_ beat 6 (figure): his hero, framed whole, pushing in on the meters at

**VO:** Visit the website, [beat 0.2] follow us on Bluesky, [beat 0.2] or send

_Visual:_ the close: the website, Bluesky and email addresses, one on each word,
then the lockup.
