# The Rest of the Record

A researcher promo for Dr Petra Umbile, Postdoctoral Fellow in the School of
Emergent Priorities: mostly in her own voice, with the narrator framing it. Her
numbers are the site's own (`website/dist/facts.json`): 925 citations, 34
co-authors and an h-index of 26, the second-highest in her school (shared with
one colleague, behind one). Her three examples are outputs on what a count
leaves out, and all three are in the cover grid. The narrator's take of line 3
ends in dead air and a breath click, zeroed on `assets/voice.wav` (25.36--25.90
s) before `mix`: redo that after any `voice`.

## Key idea

Petra Umbile trained to count research and now studies the work a count leaves
out, from a hall's honour board to a scored performance review, and her own
counts give her the second-highest h-index in her school.
