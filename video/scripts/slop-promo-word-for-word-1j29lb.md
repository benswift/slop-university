# Word for Word

A researcher promo for Dr Anouk Mensah, Postdoctoral Fellow in the School of
Emergent Priorities: mostly in her own voice, with the narrator framing it. Her
numbers are the site's own (`website/dist/facts.json`), where her citations are
the third-most in her School and ahead of all but two of its ten staff senior to
her; her examples are three of her lead-authored outputs, each a form of words
kept after its reason moved on, with their numbers from the outputs' own PDFs.

`assets/voice.wav` was level-matched by hand before `mix`, and a fresh `voice`
overwrites it, so redo both after one. Anouk's voice comes back 3--10 dB under
the narrator, varying by take, so each of her segments was matched to the
narrator's median segment loudness (−22.7 LUFS), the gain applied from the
previous segment's end.

## Key idea

Dr Anouk Mensah catalogues the sentences that stay put while the thing they
describe moves on, and that work has her cited more often than most of the
senior staff in the School of Emergent Priorities.

## Script

**VO (anouk-mensah):** Some sentences outlive the reason they were written.
[beat 0.25] I catalogue them. [beat 0.4]

_Visual:_ beat 0 (hero): her person hero, reading a long scroll of plans at a
table, under the scrim, with the title block: "Word for Word", "Dr Anouk Mensah
and the sentences that outlive their reasons", "School of Emergent Priorities".

**VO:** Dr Anouk Mensah reads this year's wording against last year's, [beat
0.15] line by line. [beat 0.4]

_Visual:_ beat 1 (people): her headshot, large, with her name, title and school,
dissolving in over the hero on "Dr".

**VO (anouk-mensah):** Over ten years, fourteen price-match promises gained one
comma between them. [beat 0.2] Their fine print grew sixfold. [beat 0.3] A share
house pins its quiet hours at ten, [beat 0.15] and keeps them past eleven. [beat
0.3] A library desk set up as interim [beat 0.1] is still interim after seven
extensions. [beat 0.5]

_Visual:_ beat 2 (figure): the hero of "The Promise That Never Changed", the
shopfront sign and the fine print unrolling beneath it, labelled with its title,
pushing in on the fine print at "fine"; then beat 3 (figure, from "share"): the
hero of "Pinned, Not Practised", housemates still talking under the kitchen
clock, pushing in on the clock at "quiet"; then beat 4 (figure, from "library"):
the hero of "Still Interim", pushing in on the hand signing at the desk at
"interim".

**VO:** She's cited more often than most senior staff in her School. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ beat 5 (pages): thirty-six of her covers drop into a grid by "often",
her most-cited papers and the three just shown among them, and hold.

**VO:** Nine hundred and sixty-five citations, [beat 0.2] and an h-index of
twenty-five, [beat 0.15] from a hundred and ninety-four research outputs. [beat
0.6]

_Visual:_ beat 6 (stats): 965 citations as the line opens, then 25 on "h-index",
its label carrying the 194 outputs.

**VO (anouk-mensah):** Every new plan carries old words forward. [beat 0.2] I
check what they still mean. [beat 0.8]

_Visual:_ beat 7 (quote): her words over her hero, under the scrim, each word
lighting as she says it, "what they still mean." in gold.

**VO:** Start reading on our website, [beat 0.15] get the latest on Bluesky,
[beat 0.15] or drop us a line. [beat 0.4]

_Visual:_ the close: the website address as the line begins, then Bluesky on its
word and the email address on "drop", then the lockup.
