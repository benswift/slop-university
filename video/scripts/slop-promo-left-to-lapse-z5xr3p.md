# Left to Lapse

A researcher promo for Dr Marek Solheim, Lecturer and Deputy Convenor of the
Horizon Register: mostly in his own voice, with the narrator framing it. His
numbers are the site's own (`website/dist/facts.json`), where his h-index is the
second-highest in the School of Emergent Priorities; his examples are three of
his lead-authored outputs, each a record that kept something going after it had
ended, with their numbers from the outputs' own PDFs. The colleagues who build
on his work are the site's citation graph: outputs from all three schools cite
him.

`assets/voice.wav` was patched by hand before `mix`, and a fresh `voice`
overwrites it, so redo both after one. Marek's voice comes back about 5 dB over
the narrator, so each of his segments was matched to −22 LUFS. And `video.py`'s
trim cuts the final "s" of "lapse" behind the "p" closure (Whisper hears "left
to lap"), so the take's raw 1.77--2.06 s was written back after that segment,
which is what the 0.7 s pause after it makes room for.

## Key idea

Dr Marek Solheim studies the plans an institution never formally ends, only
leaves to lapse, and that work has given him the second-highest h-index in the
School of Emergent Priorities.

## Script

**VO (marek-solheim):** I'm a historian of policy. [beat 0.25] I study the plans
an institution quietly stops pursuing. [beat 0.5]

_Visual:_ beat 0 (hero): his person hero, unpacking archive boxes at a desk
between the shelves, under the scrim, with the title block: "Left to Lapse", "Dr
Marek Solheim and the Horizon Register", "School of Emergent Priorities".

**VO:** Dr Marek Solheim is Deputy Convenor of the Horizon Register at Slop
University. [beat 0.5]

_Visual:_ beat 1 (people): his headshot, large, with his name, title and school,
on "Marek" once the hero has cleared.

**VO (marek-solheim):** Very few plans are ever formally retired. [beat 0.25]
Most are simply left to lapse. [beat 0.7]

_Visual:_ beat 2 (statement): the line in type under the kicker "A record of
discontinuation", "left to lapse" in gold.

**VO (marek-solheim):** A café loyalty account, still counted as active. [beat
0.25] An exam-hall flag, ticked as resolved. [beat 0.25] A laundromat sign that
says back soon. [beat 0.6]

_Visual:_ beat 3 (cards): one of his outputs per example, each on its sentence's
opening words: "Still Counted, Not Coming Back" (59% of "active" accounts idle
for 8+ weeks), "Closed, Reclassified, or Left Open" (617 exam-hall flags, 13.5%
formally resolved), "Back Soon, Still Waiting" (152 out-of-service signs at 24
laundromats).

**VO:** Colleagues across the University build on that work. [beat 1.2]

_Visual:_ beat 4 (pages): thirty-six of his most-cited covers drop into a grid.

**VO:** His h-index is twenty-six, [beat 0.2] the second-highest in the School
of Emergent Priorities. [beat 0.25] And he's been cited nine hundred and
forty-six times. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ beat 5 (stats): 26 counts up as the line opens, then 946 on its
number.

**VO (marek-solheim):** Every plan gets a launch. [beat 0.25] I'd like its
ending written down, too. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ beat 6 (quote): his words over his hero, under the scrim, each word
lighting as he says it, "its ending written down, too." in gold.

**VO:** Explore his research on our website, [beat 0.15] keep up with us on
Bluesky, [beat 0.15] or email us. [beat 0.4]

_Visual:_ the close: the website address as the line begins, then Bluesky and
email on their words, then the lockup.
