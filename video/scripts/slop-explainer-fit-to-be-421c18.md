# Fit to Be Seen, Explained

The research explainer of `slop-thesis-the-tidy-before-ylqvdm` (doi
10.5555/slop.ylqvdm), Eivind Onwuka's doctoral thesis from the School of
Continuous Improvement. Every number is the thesis's; the opening is its own
case (chapter 1), the quoted passage is clause 3 of the Closed Door Allowance as
adopted (appendix F), and Onwuka's closing line is the thesis's own sentence
(section 10.5). Before `signage`, the two chart pages (85 and 96) are
re-rendered into `assets/pages/` at 4800 px (`pdftoppm -scale-to 4800`) so the
4K encodes stay sharp; `fetch` writes them at 3200. Onwuka's voice comes back
4--6 dB over the narrator's: his segments are matched to the narrator's median
segment loudness on `assets/voice.wav` before `mix`.

## Key idea

Before visitors arrive a household carries more than it cleans, and most of what
it carries goes into one room whose door is then closed; granting that room in
advance shortened the tidy and filled the room, and the University has adopted
the arrangement for its own reviews as the Closed Door Allowance.
