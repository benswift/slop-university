# Who Reads the Board

A researcher promo for Dr Marit Osayande, Convenor of the Living Dashboard in
the School of Continuous Improvement: mostly in her own voice, with the narrator
framing it. Her numbers are the site's own (`website/dist/facts.json`): it leads
with her research outputs, citations and research income, the three where she
places best, and leaves the rest out. The transit-authority status board is her
roster bio's; her examples are three of her outputs on boards and their upkeep.
The gitignored `assets/voice.wav` carries two hand fixes, to redo after any
`voice`: her segments matched to the narrator's median loudness (her voice comes
back 7--11.5 dB under), and the trailing floor and click zeroed at the end of
both segments of her closing line.

## Key idea

Dr Marit Osayande follows the boards an organisation puts up and then stops
reading, from the day they go up to the day nobody looks, and colleagues right
across the University cite that work.

## Script

**VO:** Meet Dr Marit Osayande, Convenor of the Living Dashboard at Slop
University. [beat 0.5]

_Visual:_ beat 0 (hero): her person hero, standing before a wall of dashboard
screens, under the scrim, with the title block: "Who Reads the Board", "Dr Marit
Osayande, Convenor of the Living Dashboard", "School of Continuous Improvement".

**VO (marit-osayande):** I worked in facilities at a transit authority. [beat
0.3] Our status board ran for years, [beat 0.2] and no one was assigned to read
it. [beat 0.5]

_Visual:_ beat 1 (people): her headshot, large, with her name, title and school,
as her voice comes in.

**VO (marit-osayande):** Now I follow boards like that one, [beat 0.2] from the
day they go up to the day nobody looks. [beat 0.5]

_Visual:_ beat 2 (statement): "From the day they go up to the day nobody
looks.", "the day nobody looks." in gold, under "Living Dashboard".

**VO (marit-osayande):** I've studied them in childcare foyers, [beat 0.2]
outside service stations, [beat 0.2] and at aged-care front desks. [beat 0.9]

_Visual:_ beat 3 (cards): one output per place, each on its words: "The Wall Was
the Last to Know", "Lodged, Not Displayed", "The Whiteboard Kept Its Own Count".

**VO:** Her work is cited right across the University. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ beat 4 (pages): thirty-six of her covers, her most-cited first, drop
into a grid by "across".

**VO:** One hundred and ninety-four research outputs. [beat 0.3] Nine hundred
and twenty-one citations. [beat 0.3] And three hundred and thirteen thousand
dollars in research income. [beat 0.8]

_Visual:_ beat 5 (stats): 194 as the line opens, 921 on "Nine", and $313K on
"three".

**VO (marit-osayande):** I love a good dashboard. [beat 0.1] But first, I read
the little date in the corner. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ beat 6 (quote, spoken): her words over her hero under the scrim, each
lighting as she says it, "the little date in the corner." in gold, her name up
from the start.

**VO:** Read Dr Osayande's work on our website, [beat 0.15] find us on Bluesky,
[beat 0.15] or send us an email. [beat 0.5]

_Visual:_ the close: the website address on "Read", then Bluesky and email on
their words, then the lockup.
