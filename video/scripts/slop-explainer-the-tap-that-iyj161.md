# The Tap That Counts, Explained

The research explainer of `slop-paper-interviewing-strata-residents-22tpz2` (doi
10.5555/slop.22tpz2), by Casimir Beng, Solveig Adeyemi and Mirela Hanke of the
School of Continuous Improvement. Every number is the paper's; the closing line
is Beng's quote in the paper's news post.

## Key idea

A strata security audit counts any tap on the intercom's alert within five
minutes as acknowledged, and reads 97.3% of alerts acknowledged; but the longer
residents have lived in a building, the more that tap is a reflexive swipe made
without reading, and the audit's own clock records the swipe as faster, better
compliance.

## Script

**VO:** When a visitor buzzes in, every resident's phone gets an alert. [beat
0.4] If anyone taps it within five minutes, the building's security audit counts
it as acknowledged. [beat 0.8]

_Visual:_ beat 0 (hero): the paper's hero, a finger on the intercom's button,
under the scrim with the title block; it holds into the next line until
"ninety-seven".

**VO:** Across nine buildings, the audit's dashboard shows ninety-seven point
three per cent acknowledged. [beat 0.5] A paper from the School of Continuous
Improvement asks what that word actually captures. [beat 0.8]

_Visual:_ from "ninety-seven" beat 1 (stat): 97.3% counting up; then from
"paper" beat 2 (statement): the question in type, "acknowledged" in gold.

**VO (casimir-beng):** We interviewed sixty-four residents across those
buildings. [beat 0.3] We asked what they actually do the moment the alert
appears. [beat 0.8]

_Visual:_ beat 3 (people): Casimir Beng, large, with his title and school.

**VO (solveig-adeyemi):** We sorted what every resident told us into one of four
practices. [beat 0.3] Verify, then act; verify, then dismiss; a reflexive swipe;
or muted entirely. [beat 0.8]

_Visual:_ beat 4 (people): Solveig Adeyemi, large; then from the first "Verify"
beat 5 (cards): the paper's four practices, each arriving as she names it.

**VO:** Among residents in their first year, three of eighteen swiped it away
unread. [beat 0.3] Among those of three years or more, twelve of twenty-two:
[beat 0.2] more than half. [beat 0.8]

_Visual:_ beat 6 (figure): Figure 1, the practice mix by tenure, held whole so
the reflexive swipe's bar can be read rising from three to twelve against the
axis.

**VO (mirela-hanke):** Then we read each building's own dashboard. [beat 0.3]
The longer residents had lived there, the faster they acknowledged the alert.
[beat 0.8]

_Visual:_ beat 7 (people): Mirela Hanke, large, with her title and school.

**VO:** The median time fell from fifteen and a half seconds in the first year,
[beat 0.2] to five and a half at three years or more. [beat 0.8]

_Visual:_ beat 8 (figure): Figure 2, time to acknowledge by tenure, held whole
so the three boxes can be read falling against the axis.

**VO:** On the dashboard alone, that reads as improving compliance. [beat 0.3]
Beside the interviews, it is residents doing less, faster. [beat 0.9]

_Visual:_ beat 9 (cards): the two readings, the dashboard's as the line begins
and the interviews' on "interviews".

**VO (casimir-beng):** A five-minute window was built to ask whether someone
checked the door. [beat 0.3] It was never built to notice that the fastest
possible tap [beat 0.15] and the least attentive one have become the same
gesture. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ beat 10 (quote, spoken): his words over his portrait, lighting as he
says them, then the close: the lockup.
