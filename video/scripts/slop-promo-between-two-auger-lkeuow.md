# Between Two Auger Holes

A researcher promo for Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow in the School
of Underlying Conditions: mostly in his own voice, with the narrator framing it.
His numbers are the site's own (`website/dist/facts.json`): it leads with his
259 outputs (third at the University), his h-index of 17 (the highest in his
school) and his $538,274 in funding (the most in his school), and leaves the
rest out. The pits, the lines and the ground changing by degrees are his roster
bio's; the grant is his Indicator Stewardship Seed Fund award
(`website/src/content/grants/2026-10-13-one-page-of-the-colour-book-at-a-time.yml`).

`assets/voice.wav` is fixed by hand after `voice` and before `mix`; redo it
after every `voice`. The trim cuts the final "k" release of "work." and "book,",
so both are restored from their cached takes. Brynjar's seven segments are
matched to the narrator's median segment loudness (−1.5, −1.7, +0.1, −3.9, −4.4,
−4.3 and −3.9 dB, in order), and three narrator clause ends are lifted: "the
third most at the University." +3 dB, "the highest in his school." +1.5 dB and
"one page at a time." +2.5 dB.

## Key idea

Dr Brynjar Obiora spent twelve years drawing the lines between soils, and now
studies who decides where one soil ends when the ground changes by degrees, in a
body of work only two colleagues at the University outnumber.
