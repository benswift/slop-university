# In Good Time

A researcher promo for Dr Osei Vandermeer, Research Fellow and Convenor of the
Review Cadence Observatory: mostly in his own voice, with the narrator framing
it. His numbers are the site's own (`website/dist/facts.json`), where no
research fellow is cited more often and only one researcher holds more grants;
his examples are three of his outputs, each with its own interval.

## Key idea

Dr Osei Vandermeer asks how often something should be checked and how often it
really is, and that question has made him the University's most cited research
fellow.

## Script

**VO (osei-vandermeer):** In logistics, my dashboard refreshed every fifteen
minutes. [beat 0.3] Back then, it was just the job. [beat 0.2] Now it's my
research question. [beat 0.6]

_Visual:_ beat 0 (hero): his person hero, at a wall of screens and gauges, under
the scrim, with the title block: "In Good Time", "Dr Osei Vandermeer and the
Review Cadence Observatory", "School of Continuous Improvement".

**VO:** Dr Osei Vandermeer convenes the Review Cadence Observatory at Slop
University. [beat 0.6]

_Visual:_ beat 1 (figure): his headshot, labelled "Dr Osei Vandermeer, Research
Fellow".

**VO (osei-vandermeer):** We ask how often something should be checked, [beat
0.2] and how often it really is. [beat 0.6]

_Visual:_ beat 2 (statement): the question in type, "how often it really is" in
gold, under the Observatory's name.

**VO (osei-vandermeer):** A beekeeping club's hives, every fortnight. [beat 0.3]
Forty-two defibrillator cabinets, once a month. [beat 0.3] An investment
committee, four times a year. [beat 0.7]

_Visual:_ beat 3 (cards): one interval per example, each on its sentence's
opening words: "Every fortnight", "Once a month", "Four times a year".

**VO:** He's the most cited research fellow at Slop University. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ beat 4 (pages): thirty-six of his covers drop into a grid, his
most-cited papers and two of the examples among them.

**VO:** One thousand and sixty citations. [beat 0.3] Three seed fund grants.
[beat 0.3] And over half a million dollars in research income. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ beat 5 (stats): 1,060, 3 and $511K count up, each on its number.

**VO (osei-vandermeer):** Some things need a look every fifteen minutes. [beat
0.2] Some, once a year. [beat 0.3] My job is telling them apart. [beat 1.2]

_Visual:_ beat 6 (figure): his hero, framed whole, pushing in on the screens and
the clock at "fifteen", then across to him at "My".

**VO:** All his work is on our website. [beat 0.15] We're on Bluesky, [beat
0.15] and you can email us. [beat 0.4]

_Visual:_ the close: the website address as the line begins, then Bluesky and
email on their words, then the lockup.
