# Nobody Finishes Last, Explained

The research explainer of `slop-thesis-nobody-finishes-last-oip71b` (doi
10.5555/slop.oip71b), Eskil Nwabueze's doctoral thesis in the School of
Continuous Improvement. Every number is the thesis's; the opening line is its
abstract's first sentence, Nwabueze's first line is his own account of his
position (section 4.9), the appointed last's words are an interview quotation
(AL07, section 7.2), and his closing line joins the thesis's statement of the
Last Place Roster (section 1.5) to its last sentence (section 10.5).

## Key idea

A ranked list always has a bottom row, which a club can reassign but not remove,
and when that row is given to an appointed last, the member who would otherwise
have held it comes back more often.
