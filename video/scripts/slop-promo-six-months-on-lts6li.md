# Six Months On

A researcher promo for Dr Ingeborg Nwachukwu, Lecturer and Convenor of the
Capability Sprints: mostly in her own voice, with the narrator framing it. Her
numbers are the site's own (`website/dist/facts.json`), where her h-index is the
University's second-highest and her citations its third-highest; her examples
are three of her most-cited outputs, and her Sprint graduates come from her own
impact report, _The Certificate and the Desk_.

## Key idea

Ingeborg Nwachukwu goes back after the course is over to find out what people
kept, and that habit has given her the second-highest h-index at Slop
University.

## Script

**VO:** Dr Ingeborg Nwachukwu convenes the Capability Sprints at Slop
University. [beat 0.6]

_Visual:_ beat 0 (hero): her person hero, at the flip chart before a Sprint
cohort, under the scrim, with the title block: "Six Months On", "Dr Ingeborg
Nwachukwu and the Capability Sprints", "School of Continuous Improvement".

**VO (ingeborg-nwachukwu):** I once wrote the assessment rubric for a leadership
course. [beat 0.3] Six months on, I went looking for who still used it. [beat
0.6]

_Visual:_ beat 1 (figure): her headshot, large, with her name and title under
it as her voice comes in, pushing in on her face at "Six".

**VO (ingeborg-nwachukwu):** A certificate tells you who finished. [beat 0.3] I
want to know what they kept. [beat 0.6]

_Visual:_ beat 2 (statement): the line in type, "what they kept" in gold, under
"Capability Sprints".

**VO (ingeborg-nwachukwu):** So I go looking. [beat 0.3] A sign-in sheet at a community shed. [beat 0.3] A food-safety tick box. [beat 0.3] A club that's paid
up, but not present. [beat 0.6]

_Visual:_ the statement holds through "So I go looking"; then beat 3 (cards),
from "sign-in": one output per example, each on its word: "Fifty-Eight Signed
In, Fourteen on Tuesday", "Ticked, Not Tested", "Paid Up, Not Present".

**VO (ingeborg-nwachukwu):** I've followed five years of Sprint graduates
back to their desks. [beat 0.3] Some changes hold. [beat 0.2] Others quietly
slip back. [beat 0.6]

_Visual:_ beat 4 (figure): figure 2 of _The Certificate and the Desk_, the
meeting formats still in place at 30 days and at 12 months, pushing in on the
long drops at "Others".

**VO:** Her colleagues cite that work again and again. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ beat 5 (pages): thirty-six of her covers drop into a grid.

**VO:** An h-index of twenty-nine, the second-highest at Slop University. [beat
0.3] And eleven hundred and thirty-seven citations. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ beat 6 (stats): 29 and 1,137 count up, each on its number.

**VO:** Visit our website, find us on Bluesky, or send us an email. [beat
0.8]

_Visual:_ the close: the website, Bluesky and email addresses, one on each word,
then the lockup.
