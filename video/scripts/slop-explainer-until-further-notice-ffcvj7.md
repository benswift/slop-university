# Until Further Notice, Explained

The research explainer of `slop-thesis-until-further-notice-4c32gb` (doi
10.5555/slop.4c32gb), Tove Ilesanmi's doctoral thesis from the School of
Emergent Priorities. Every number is the thesis's. The urn card is its opening
case (section 1.1, at C1, the lawn bowls club) and its closing one (section
9.5); the quoted sentences are its own words (sections 1.1 and 7.6).

Two steps on the gitignored `assets/` before a render. After `fetch`, page 75
(figure 6.1's push-in) is re-rendered at 6400 px. After every `voice`, on
`assets/voice.wav` before `mix`: the three segments of Ilesanmi's second line
end in silence and a breath onset, zeroed from the first 0.3 s run 30 dB under
the segment's level; and each of his segments is matched to the narrator's
median segment loudness (with "with no override." lifted 3 dB), which leaves
every segment of the mix within about 1.3 LU.

## Key idea

A date written on a club's "temporary" notice does not end the arrangement: it
puts it on a committee agenda, where it is confirmed four times in five, so
arrangements whose notice named an end-point became permanent more than twice as
fast as those that did not, and the thesis recommends that the University
confirm every pilot automatically at its review date.
