# The Day After the Target

A researcher promo for Dr Joost Nwosu, Postdoctoral Fellow in the School of
Continuous Improvement: mostly in his own voice, with the narrator framing it.
His numbers are the site's own (`website/dist/facts.json`), where his research
funding, $1,043,033 across three grants, is the most of anyone in the School;
his examples are three of his outputs, one for each fate his roster bio gives a
met target: retired, quietly raised, or kept on as a floor.

## Key idea

Joost Nwosu studies what happens to a target once it is met, whether it is
retired, quietly made tougher or kept on as a floor, and that work has made him
the best-funded researcher in the School of Continuous Improvement.

## Script

**VO (joost-nwosu):** Everyone celebrates the day a target is met. [beat 0.3] I
study the day after. [beat 0.6]

files and the arrow in the bullseye, under the scrim, with the title block: "The
Day After the Target", "Dr Joost Nwosu, Postdoctoral Fellow", "School of
Continuous Improvement".

**VO:** Dr Joost Nwosu is a Postdoctoral Fellow at Slop University. [beat 0.6]

_Visual:_ beat 1 (people): his headshot, large, with his name, title and school,

**VO (joost-nwosu):** Some targets are retired. [beat 0.2] A community choir

**VO (joost-nwosu):** Some get quietly tougher. [beat 0.2] A school sausage

_Visual:_ beat 3 (figure): the hero of "Fifty-Nine Will Do", labelled with its

**VO (joost-nwosu):** And some stay on as a floor. [beat 0.2] A young swimmer
