# Still Counting

A researcher promo for Dr Renke Sabel, Senior Lecturer and Convenor of the
Indicator Commons: mostly in his own voice, with the narrator framing it. His
numbers are the site's own (`website/dist/facts.json`), where no researcher
holds more grants; his examples are three of his four Indicator Stewardship Seed
Fund grants (`website/src/content/grants/`), each of which gives a measure a
keeper and a successor.

## Key idea

Dr Renke Sabel asks why an institution's indicators outlive anyone's use for
them, and his work giving each measure a keeper has won him more grants than any
other researcher at Slop University.

## Script

**VO (renke-sabel):** In the public sector, indicators piled up faster than
anyone retired them. [beat 0.3] Now I study why. [beat 0.6]

_Visual:_ beat 0 (hero): his person hero, before a wall of old gauges, under the
scrim, with the title block: "Still Counting", "Dr Renke Sabel and the Indicator
Commons", "School of Continuous Improvement".

**VO:** Dr Renke Sabel convenes the Indicator Commons, [beat 0.15] Slop
University's shared library of indicators. [beat 0.5]

_Visual:_ beat 1 (people): his headshot, large, with his name, title and school.

**VO (renke-sabel):** Every measure needs a keeper, [beat 0.2] and someone ready
to take over. [beat 0.5]

_Visual:_ beat 2 (statement): the line in type under the kicker "Indicator
stewardship", "someone ready to take over" in gold.

**VO (renke-sabel):** A dog park's board, counting days since the last incident.
[beat 0.3] A community hall's key register. [beat 0.3] A lab's calibration
weights. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ beat 3 (cards): three of his grants, each on its sentence's opening
words, with what the grant gives it: a deputy who countersigns every reset, a
custodian who corrects the register at each handover, a successor named before
the custodian leaves.

**VO:** He holds more grants than anyone else at Slop University. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ beat 4 (pages): thirty-six of his covers drop into a grid, his
most-cited outputs.

**VO:** Four Indicator Stewardship grants. [beat 0.3] Seven hundred and
eighty-five thousand dollars. [beat 0.3] And one thousand and thirty-four
citations. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ beat 5 (stats): 4, $785K and 1,034 count up, each on its number.

**VO (renke-sabel):** Starting to count is easy. [beat 0.3] I want to know who
decides when to stop. [beat 1.2]

_Visual:_ beat 6 (quote): his words over his hero, under the scrim, each word
lighting as he says it, "who decides when to stop." in gold.

**VO:** Everything he's published is on our website. [beat 0.15] Say hello on
Bluesky, [beat 0.15] or by email. [beat 0.4]

_Visual:_ the close: the website address as the line begins, then Bluesky and
email on their words, then the lockup.
