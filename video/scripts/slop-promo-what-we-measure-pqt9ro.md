# What We Measure

A University promo: the narrator throughout, with no researcher's voice. Its
conviction is the About page's (`website/src/content/pages/about.md`), and so is
its claim that every output is open access with a persistent identifier. Its
three examples are recent studies, one from each school, each measuring
something everyday: _Kept Warm_ (Continuous Improvement), _Which River Does the
Bridge Sample?_ (Underlying Conditions) and _Eighteen Weekends in the Fryer_
(Emergent Priorities). Its numbers are the site's own
(`website/dist/facts.json`): 2,413 research outputs since the first, on 4 July
2026; 63.5 outputs per academic FTE; 10,562 citations; a University h-index of
33; and $5,710,876 in grants and prizes, which the dashboard prints as $5.7M.
The opening image is the Schools page hero
(`website/src/assets/heroes/schools.avif`), copied to `assets/campus.avif`,
since `fetch` resolves no page heroes.

## Key idea

Slop University starts from the conviction that what it measures, it comes to
understand, so its researchers measure everyday things, a staff-kitchen urn, a
river, a canteen's fryer oil, and its own measures show the result: 2,413
research outputs since July, 63.5 for every academic, cited 10,562 times.

_Visual:_ beat 0 (hero): the campus, the Schools page hero, under the scrim,
with the title block: "What We Measure", "Research at Slop University".

**VO:** What we measure, we come to understand. [beat 0.3] And what we
understand, we can improve. [beat 0.5]

_Visual:_ beat 1 (statement), on "we", once the campus has cleared: the
conviction in type under "Slop University", "we can improve" in gold, held

[beat 0.6]

**VO:** They've been cited ten thousand, five hundred and sixty-two times. [beat

_Visual:_ beat 7 (stats): 10,562 counting up from the line's start, 33 on

**VO:** It's all on our website. [beat 0.2] We're on Bluesky, [beat 0.2] and you

_Visual:_ the close: the website address on "It's", then Bluesky and email on
