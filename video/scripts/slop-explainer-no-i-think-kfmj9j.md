# No, I Think You've Covered Everything, Explained

The research explainer of `slop-thesis-the-last-question-b6zqnj` (doi
10.5555/slop.b6zqnj), Signy Okpara's doctoral thesis in the School of Continuous
Improvement. Every number is the thesis's. The opening is its first paragraph
(chapter 1), Okpara's first line is her account of her years as a panel's scribe
(chapter 1), the trial's commonest question is quoted from section 8.5.1, and
her closing line is clause 1 of the Candidate Question Standard (appendix E.1).

## Key idea

The question a job candidate is invited to ask at the end of an interview moves
no panellist's score and no first ranking, and what it does is carried by the
panel's answer, so the University has brought it inside the interview's
structure: two questions from a published Schedule of 24, answered from a
Register of Standing Answers and rated for a fifth of the selection total.
