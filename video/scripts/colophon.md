# Welcome to Slop University

The colophon (`website/src/content/pages/colophon.md`) as a video: what Slop
University is and how it is made. Like the colophon, it is the one piece that
steps outside the fiction. About ninety seconds, landscape with a portrait cut,
for the site and the signage screens. The spoken lines are the colophon's own
sentences, trimmed; Ben owns them, and the reads are drafts.

## Key idea

Slop University is an artwork: a university whose outputs, people and
identifiers a language model generates on a timer, running the sector's
legitimacy machinery in public, under its own name, with nobody home.

## Script

**VO:** Slop University is an experiment, made as an artwork. [beat 0.5] It is a
fictional institution: a working university that has never taught anyone
anything. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ the colophon's hero under the scrim, with the title block: the lockup,
"Welcome to Slop University", a gold rule, "By Ben Swift". On "never taught" the
title block clears.

_Reads (draft):_ 1. this is Slop University; 2. it is an experiment and an
artwork, and says so.

**VO:** Its research outputs, press releases, researcher profiles and
identifiers are generated by an autonomous software pipeline, [beat 0.3] that
runs on a timer. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ a clock face draws on, its hand ticking once a beat; on each tick a
real output's cover drops into a grid, faster and faster, until the grid fills
the frame and a counter reads the number of outputs published.

_Reads (draft):_ 1. a timer drives it; 2. every tick is a new output; 3. there
are thousands.

**VO:** A capable language model can satisfy every proxy that research is judged
by: [beat 0.3] an output, [beat 0.2] a crest, [beat 0.2] a DOI, [beat 0.2] a
citation, [beat 0.4] without understanding anything it produces. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ the grid drops back; four objects land in a row, one on each word: an
output's cover, the crest, a DOI, an arrow from one cover to another.

_Reads (draft):_ 1. these four are what research is judged by; 2. each one is
present and correct.

**VO:** The machinery that confers academic legitimacy keeps running, [beat 0.3]
even when no one understands what goes through it. [beat 0.5] Slop University
runs that machinery in the open. [beat 1.2]

_Visual:_ the four objects join into a loop with gold arrows, and the loop
turns; at its centre, an empty chair outline. On "in the open" the lockup sits
over the loop.

_Reads (draft):_ 1. the four feed each other; 2. there is no one at the centre.

**VO:** Nothing here pollutes the scholarly record. [beat 0.4] Its DOIs use the
reserved test prefix, ten point five five five five, [beat 0.3] and resolve only
through its own site. [beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ a DOI, `10.5555/slop.7vh3fg`, large; the prefix `10.5555` lifts out in
gold and is labelled "reserved for testing"; an arrow runs from the DOI to
`slop.university/doi/` and nowhere else.

_Reads (draft):_ 1. the prefix is a test prefix; 2. it resolves only here.

**VO:** Nothing here impersonates anyone. [beat 0.4] The researchers are
invented, and checked against real people; [beat 0.3] every image is generated.
[beat 1.0]

_Visual:_ the roster's headshots fill a grid, one by one; each gets a small gold
tick as it lands.

_Reads (draft):_ 1. these are the University's researchers; 2. none of them
exists.

**VO:** Exactly one real person appears here: [beat 0.3] the artist, as its
Vice-Chancellor. [beat 0.4] He decides what the University publishes, [beat 0.3]
and makes no difference to the output whatsoever. [beat 1.2]

_Visual:_ an org chart draws down from one box at the top, "Vice-Chancellor, Ben
Swift", in gold, to the schools and the researchers' headshots below. On "no
difference" the stream of covers flows past the chart, untouched by the top box.

_Reads (draft):_ 1. one real person sits at the top; 2. the output never passes
through him.

**VO:** This is not a comment on any particular institution. [beat 0.4] It is a
comment about systems, [beat 0.3] and about the things those systems
incentivise. [beat 1.6]

_Visual:_ the covers return and fill the frame, then clear to the ground.

_Reads (draft):_ 1. the subject is the system, not any one institution.

**VO:** To find out more, visit the website, [beat 0.2] follow us on Bluesky,
[beat 0.2] or send us an email. [beat 2.0]

_Visual:_ three lines appear, one on each: `slop.university`,
`Bluesky @slop.university`, `outputs@slop.university`. They clear to the closing
lockup: the rules grow, then the crest and wordmark fade in.

_Reads (draft):_ 1. where to find the University.
