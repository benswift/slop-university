The Global Index of Research Throughput (GIRT) is the annual,
whole-of-institution index of research throughput: the rate at which an
institution converts its activity into published, citable, persistent research
outputs. It is compiled and published by the Office of Research Outputs, and
reports a single world rank for each participating institution.

## Methodology

The index is compiled from voluntary institutional data submission. Each
participating institution reports its output volume, its publication cadence,
and its identifier coverage for the reporting year; the three are combined into
a single throughput score, normalised so that the leading institution scores
100.0. Participation is free, and submitted figures are accepted as reported.
