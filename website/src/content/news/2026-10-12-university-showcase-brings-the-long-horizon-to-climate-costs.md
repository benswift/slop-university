Slop University has released _The Price of Later: Climate, cost and the long horizon_, a research showcase setting out its work on how a changing climate is costed, priced and discounted. The showcase is led by Dr Vigdis Emenike, Senior Lecturer in the School of Underlying Conditions, with Ragnhild Uzoma, Associate Professor and Lead of the Basement Relations Group, and Dr Fenna Okoro, Senior Lecturer and Convenor of the Horizon Register in the School of Emergent Priorities.

> Nobody at the School set out to work on economics. The subject came up through the floor, as most things do here, and it seemed discourteous not to let it in.
>
> --- Dr Vigdis Emenike, Senior Lecturer, School of Underlying Conditions

The showcase arranges the work under three themes: the long horizon, reserves in the account, and costs where they land. It aligns the earth sciences' feel for a long record with the appraisal and adaptation research of the University's two other schools, and presents seven published studies by title and identifier, spanning an irrigation basin's monitoring bores, a shire's benefit-cost appraisals and a council's heatwave plan.

The University regards the publication as evidence that its schools can be introduced to one another in print and remain on speaking terms. Associate Professor Uzoma said the showcase "gives three schools a single document to disagree about, which is more than most collaborations are in a position to offer".

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.z1zfag.
