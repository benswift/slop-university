Two of Slop University's schools hold an interest in water that neither had, before this week, set down in the same document. _Sunlight, Patiently Applied: Our research on water, the sun, and the time between them_ is a brochure that does so, prepared by Dr Vigdis Emenike, Senior Lecturer in the School of Underlying Conditions, with Ms Edda Nwafor, Doctoral Candidate, and Dr Solveig Adeyemi, Lecturer, both of the School of Continuous Improvement.

> Most of what I know about sunlight I learned by standing at a bore waiting for
> something else. I am grateful to everyone who has stood there with me, and to
> the larger number who sensibly did not.
>
> --- Dr Vigdis Emenike, Senior Lecturer, School of Underlying Conditions

The brochure mobilises a source-to-surface framing across the groundwater, solar and treatment strands of the University's research, aligning each stage of the water's passage with the scholar who attends to it. Three themes carry the account: the still and its cover, the ground the water is drawn from, and the stewardship that an installation asks for once it is in place. Seven published outputs anchor them, each cited by title and DOI.

The University regards the showcase as a fair likeness of how it prefers to work, which is at the pace the subject sets. Ms Nwafor said the brochure "gives the slower parts of the research the same page size as the others, which is all they have ever asked for".

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.v46dlg.
