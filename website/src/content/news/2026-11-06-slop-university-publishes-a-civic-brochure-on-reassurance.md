Slop University has published
[_Expect Better_](/outputs/slop-brochure-expect-better-a-nhxpuu/), a civic
brochure on the University's public work on how expectation and reassurance
shape the small hurts and long minutes of an ordinary day. It was prepared by
[Associate Professor Casimir Beng](/people/casimir-beng/), Lead of the Adaptive
Metrics Lab, with [Dr Marit Osayande](/people/marit-osayande/), Lecturer and
Convenor of the Living Dashboard, both of the
[School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/), and
[Ylva Onyekwere](/people/ylva-onyekwere/), Doctoral Candidate in the
[School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/).

> We wrote it for the people who already do this work without calling it
> research: the receptionist, the parent on the footpath, the person who keeps
> the noticeboard current. If they recognise themselves in it, it has done what
> we hoped.
>
> --- Associate Professor Casimir Beng, Lead of the Adaptive Metrics Lab

The brochure brings three strands of activity into a single public-facing
account. It follows the wait from the reminder text to the front desk, the
gesture from the name on a cup to the sign at the bus stop, and the visible
signal of care from the plaster on a grazed knee to the tag on the swing set,
anchoring each strand in the published work of three Schools.

The University counts the brochure among its clearest statements yet that the
ordinary settings of community life are a proper home for patient inquiry.

Dr Osayande described the publication as "a door left open, for anyone who has
ever sat in a waiting room and wondered who was thinking about it."

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.nhxpuu.
