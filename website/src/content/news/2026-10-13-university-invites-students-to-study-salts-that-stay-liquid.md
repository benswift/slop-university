From this week the University's signage panels carry an invitation to a subject
few prospective students will have met by name. _Study the Salts That Stay
Liquid_ is a campaign developed with the School of Underlying Conditions, and it
presents ionic liquids, salts that are liquid on the bench, as a way into the
School's work on rock, soil and what can be drawn from them.

> I came to this from the soil side, where a liquid is mostly something you wait
> to drain. It has done me good to stand at a bench and be patient with one that
> is not going anywhere.
>
> --- Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow, School of Underlying Conditions

The campaign positions the teaching laboratory alongside the pit and the core
shed as a setting in which the ground is read. It anchors a single line of
invitation to an image of students at the bench, and directs readers, in its
small print, to three recent publications by the School's researchers.

The University holds that a prospective student is owed a plain account of how a
school spends its afternoons, and it considers this campaign to be one. Dr
Obiora said the School "would be glad of anyone who has ever watched something
dissolve and wanted to stay until it had finished".

Applications are open through the University's course catalogue. The full poster
is available from the University's research repository under an open licence,
doi:10.5555/slop.jmug98.
