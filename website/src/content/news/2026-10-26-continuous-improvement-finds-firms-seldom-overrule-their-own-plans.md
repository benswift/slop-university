The School of Continuous Improvement has turned to an older discipline for help with one of the most familiar documents in economic life. Its new research poster asks what happens to a firm's commitments when the plan that made them is replaced, and answers in the language a court would use.

[_Overruled Without Saying So_](/outputs/slop-poster-overruled-without-saying-63o2y2/), by [Dr Torun Ezeigwe](/people/torun-ezeigwe/), Senior Lecturer and Director, Master of Applied Measurement, with [Dr Runa Adegoke](/people/runa-adegoke/), Senior Lecturer and Convenor, Strategic Drift Survey, and [Signy Okpara](/people/signy-okpara/), Doctoral Candidate, reads three successive development strategies at each of 41 regional manufacturers. Each inherited commitment was followed, distinguished, expressly overruled or quietly departed from. Express overruling accounted for 4% of them; of the commitments that did not survive, three in five simply stopped being mentioned.

> Lawyers have spent a long time working out what it takes to set an earlier decision aside, and it seemed a pity not to borrow some of that care. The firms were generous with their board packs, and very patient with three people reading them slowly.
>
> --- Dr Torun Ezeigwe, Senior Lecturer and Director, Master of Applied Measurement

The method pairs a doctrinal coding frame with a longitudinal reading of each firm's planning record, so that every commitment can be followed from the plan that made it to the plan that inherited it, and its fate placed within one of four categories adapted from the common law.

The poster joins a widening body of work in which the University's schools lend one another their instruments, and the School's interest in how organisations carry an intention forward has found an unusually exacting companion here.

Dr Adegoke said the Strategic Drift Survey would now "keep a closer record of what each plan leaves unsaid, which turns out to be most of what it leaves behind".

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.63o2y2.
