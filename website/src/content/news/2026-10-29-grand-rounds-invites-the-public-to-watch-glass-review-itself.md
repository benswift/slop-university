Evaluation of Evaluation exists to ask how well the University's reviews
review. This spring it puts the same question to a pane of glass.
_Even the Windows Here Review Themselves_, a new marketing poster from the
[School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/), carries an
Improvement Grand Rounds session onto the University's landscape signage
panels, with [Dr Dagny Okafor](/people/dagny-okafor/), Lecturer and Convenor of
Evaluation of Evaluation, leading an afternoon on oxide-coated windows that
darken against the sun and then check whether they darkened enough.

> A window that tints when the light changes is doing a job. A window that
> then asks whether it tinted well is doing mine, and I am glad of the company.
> I expect the audience will leave with more questions than the glass does.
>
> --- [Dr Dagny Okafor](/people/dagny-okafor/), Lecturer and Convenor,
> Evaluation of Evaluation

The poster translates the School's meta-review agenda into the vocabulary of
the building envelope, framing the self-checking pane as an everyday exemplar
of assessment-informed adjustment and a natural point of entry for visitors new
to the Grand Rounds format. Its small print directs readers to three outputs
already on the record: a field trial of titania modules behind coated cover
glass at monitoring bores,
[_A Bore Runs on Very Little_](/outputs/slop-poster-dye-sensitised-titania-vyej86/);
a transect survey of vanadium oxide nanostructures,
[_A Shore Forty Millimetres Long_](/outputs/slop-poster-a-belt-transect-survey-nasy4v/);
and the gallium oxide campaign
[_Mind the Wider Gap_](/outputs/slop-ad-mind-the-wider-8kr7xs/).

The University welcomes the session as a further sign that reflective practice
here extends well beyond the people in the room, and as an open door for anyone
curious about what a building might be thinking about its own weather.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.6yubj2.
