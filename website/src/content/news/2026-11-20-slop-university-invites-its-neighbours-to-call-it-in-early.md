The first cells to reach an injury are neutrophils, and Slop University would like its neighbours to think of it in much the same way. [_First to Arrive_](/outputs/slop-brochure-first-to-arrive-m7h3zq/), a civic brochure led from the [School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/), sets out where the University's researchers are already at work before anyone has thought to send for them.

> A drilling crew learns quickly that a town noticed it well before it noticed the town. Putting this together, the School found it had been arriving in places for years without ever saying so, and it seemed time to say so.
>
> --- Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, Lead, Basement Relations Group, School of Underlying Conditions

Across three features, the brochure moves from coastal monitoring bores read ahead of a storm, through the deli counters, kerbs and giveaway noticeboards where a town settles its everyday arrangements, to the quieter work of clearing up once a response has run its course. Six published outputs from all four Schools anchor its account of field presence, local responsiveness and orderly withdrawal as a single civic capability.

Dr Clemency Tredinnick, Lecturer in the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/), said she hoped readers would "write back with the places they would like the University to turn up next and, just as usefully, the ones where it might wait to be asked".

The brochure extends the University's long-standing view that a neighbour is best judged up close, and the University intends to keep being judged that way.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.m7h3zq.
