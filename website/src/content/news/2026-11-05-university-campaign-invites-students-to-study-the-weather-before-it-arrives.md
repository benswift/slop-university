Partly cloudy, with a change on approach from the west. That outlook, drawn
from the campus's own instruments, is the premise of
[_Partly Cloudy. Fully Anticipated._](/outputs/slop-ad-partly-cloudy-fully-z48xfx/),
a new marketing poster now entering rotation on the University's landscape
signage panels.

The poster is led by [Dr Marek Solheim](/people/marek-solheim/), Lecturer and
Deputy Convenor of the Horizon Register in the
[School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/), with
[Dr Fenna Okoro](/people/fenna-okoro/), Senior Lecturer and Convenor of the
Horizon Register. It places a mast, an anemometer and a louvred instrument
screen on an open lawn beneath a front still deciding what it intends, and
invites prospective students to apply to study where the weather is seen
coming.

> Most of what an institution forecasts arrives late or not at all. The
> weather is the honourable exception, and it seemed only fair to give it a
> poster of its own.
>
> --- Dr Marek Solheim, Lecturer and Deputy Convenor, Horizon Register

The campaign translates the Register's seasonal practice of setting each
campus forecast beside the conditions that followed into a single line of
display type, aligning the School's anticipatory agenda with an audience that
mostly encounters it while waiting for a bus. Its small print points readers
to the University's published record on forecasting in practice, including a
term of restocking the loaner umbrellas on the Register's wet-weather outlook
and a season of magpie-warning signs posted ahead of a forecast return.

Dr Okoro described the poster as "a welcome chance to let the Register look
outward for a change."

The campaign sits squarely within the School's long-standing interest in what
a university chooses to see ahead of time. The full poster is available from
the University's research repository under an open licence,
doi:10.5555/slop.z48xfx.
