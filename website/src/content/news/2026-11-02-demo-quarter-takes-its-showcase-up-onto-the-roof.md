The School of Continuous Improvement has long maintained that getting better is
a routine rather than a destination, and its newest signage campaign carries
that conviction up a flight of stairs.
[_Perturb. Observe. Repeat._](/outputs/slop-ad-perturb-observe-repeat-h5e2ac/)
invites visitors to a [Demo Quarter](/schools/continuous-improvement/) public
session on the roof, where students of the Master of Applied Measurement will
nudge a solar array's operating point, read what changed, and nudge it again.

The campaign comes from [Ms Signy Okpara](/people/signy-okpara/), Doctoral
Candidate, and [Dr Torun Ezeigwe](/people/torun-ezeigwe/), Senior Lecturer and
Director of the Master of Applied Measurement.

> I came to the University from interview rooms, where there is rarely time to
> ask the same question twice. An array lets you ask as often as you like, and I
> hope our visitors come down from the roof with a question of their own.
>
> --- Ms Signy Okpara, Doctoral Candidate, School of Continuous Improvement

The session aligns the School's established improvement capability with the
energy-optimisation agenda now gathering across the sector, translating a
well-worn tracking routine into an accessible, hands-on encounter for
prospective students and the wider campus community. Its small print points
readers to the School's stepped-wedge trial of rooftop panel washing,
[_Nothing the Rain Hadn't Done_](/outputs/slop-paper-quarterly-rooftop-solar-wj3ab9/),
among other work.

"The program has always taught that a reading is an invitation to take another,"
Dr Ezeigwe said. "This summer the roof will be doing some of that teaching for
us."

The campaign extends a Demo Quarter tradition of opening the School's working
habits to anyone curious enough to climb the stairs.

The full advertisement is available from the University's research repository
