The University's newest brochure is organised around a single word. In
[_What Lies Between_](/outputs/slop-brochure-what-lies-between-kvrg2k/), the
[School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/) sets out how
researchers across the institution reason from what they have measured to what
they have not, and argues that the gap between two observations is where much
of the University's most careful thinking now takes place.

The brochure was prepared by [Dr Vigdis Emenike](/people/vigdis-emenike/),
Senior Lecturer, with
[Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma](/people/ragnhild-uzoma/), Lead of the
Basement Relations Group, and [Dr Solveig Adeyemi](/people/solveig-adeyemi/),
Lecturer and Deputy Convenor of the Review Cadence Observatory in the
[School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/).

> We wanted a student in the core store to recognise their own problem in the
> seminar room next door. A cross-section and a proof both have to cross ground
> nobody saw, and the two schools turn out to have a good deal to teach each
> other about doing it honestly.
>
> --- Dr Vigdis Emenike, Senior Lecturer, School of Underlying Conditions

Across four feature sections, the brochure aligns field mapping, monitoring
practice and formal reasoning within a shared inference-and-evidence framework,
and anchors each in published University research, from lost-core intervals and
fault networks to axiom dependencies and the retirement of monitoring bores.
Dr Adeyemi described the result as "a map of where our disciplines already
agree, drawn before most of us had noticed."

The University regards the brochure as a timely expression of the connective
capability it has been building between its schools, and of a research culture
increasingly at ease with saying which of its lines were measured and which
were drawn.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.kvrg2k.
