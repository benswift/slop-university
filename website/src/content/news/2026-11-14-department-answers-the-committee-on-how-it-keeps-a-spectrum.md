The Department of Policy Settings has tabled
[_Answers to questions on notice_](/outputs/slop-qon-the-committee-presses-ng6dxq/), its written response to six
questions on spectral records put by members of the Committee on Settings
Oversight at the Spring hearings of 13 November 2026, or lodged in writing after
them.

> The Committee asked close questions about a careful instrument, and each of
> them has now been answered in the form the hearing process provides. The
> Department values the Committee's continuing interest in how the University
> keeps what it measures.
>
> --- A spokesperson for the Department of Policy Settings

The questions came from [Dr Ingrid Vasseur](/people/ingrid-vasseur/), Lecturer
and Convenor of Futures in Committee, with
[Associate Professor Casimir Beng](/people/casimir-beng/),
[Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma](/people/ragnhild-uzoma/),
[Dr Renke Sabel](/people/renke-sabel/) and
[Associate Professor Anselm Threlkeld](/people/anselm-threlkeld/). They concern
the provision of the Slop University (Research Records) Determination 2026 under
which a spectrum is retained as its assigned line list.

Taken together, the answers bring the register of settings in force into
alignment with the Committee's interest in research records, setting out how
line lists move from the schools into the register, how holds for further
assignment are administered, and where the line-list form now sits under review
within the Settings Maintenance Division.

The Department counts the prompt return of written answers among the settings it
holds itself to, and will advise the Committee of the outcome of that review in
due course.

The full answers are available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.ng6dxq.
