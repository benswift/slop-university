The flat, dark paddocks where the [School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/) digs and describes its soil profiles no longer empty at sunset. [_Common Ground, Clear Nights_](/outputs/slop-brochure-common-ground-clear-u0yt7a/), a civic brochure led by [Dr Brynjar Obiora](/people/brynjar-obiora/) with [Mr Halvard Nnamani](/people/halvard-nnamani/) and [Dr Odalys Penhallow](/people/odalys-penhallow/), invites the region's astronomical societies, clubs and first-time observers to bring their telescopes onto that ground on clear nights.

> We had described these paddocks horizon by horizon, by daylight, and thought we knew them. It took a season of visitors levelling their tripods to show the School how much of that ground it had never stood on after dark.
>
> --- Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow, School of Underlying Conditions

The brochure moves from the footing beneath a heavy mount, through the night books and calling lists that observing societies keep, to the slow work of following faint objects across returns, and gathers six published University studies from three Schools by title and DOI. Dr Penhallow described the paddocks as "a place where a question can be asked on enough nights that it finally stops needing to be asked."

In extending its field ground to the people who watch the sky, the University sees an expression of the civic purpose it has always meant its earth sciences to carry, and an exchange it expects to learn from for some time.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.u0yt7a.
