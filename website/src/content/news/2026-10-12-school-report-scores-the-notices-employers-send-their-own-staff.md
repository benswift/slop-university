A leave policy has a reader, and for five years the School of Continuous Improvement has been asking how far that reader must travel before learning what is on offer. The answer, for 64 collaborating organisations and 1.9 million words of their notices, is the subject of _Read Twice_, the School's Impact Report 2021-2026, prepared by Dr Thandiwe Solberg, Senior Lecturer and Convenor, Demo Quarter, with Dr Marit Osayande, Lecturer and Convenor, Living Dashboard, Dr Renke Sabel, Senior Lecturer and Convenor, Indicator Commons, and Dr Ingeborg Nwachukwu, Lecturer and Convenor, Capability Sprints.

> Most of these documents had been issued many times and read, as far as anyone could establish, rather fewer. It has been a privilege to supply the second reading.
>
> --- Dr Thandiwe Solberg, Senior Lecturer and Convenor, Demo Quarter

The report aligns an instrument, a dashboard and a classroom around one object: the sentence in which an employer tells its people where support may be found. Across three impact areas it mobilises reading-grade measurement, a standing view of organisational register, and a redrafting practice anchored in the School's showcase, and it records 312 notices reissued after a first score.

The University regards a school that will count the words of a roster-change notice as a school with its attention in the right place. Dr Osayande said the collaborators had "been generous with their filing cabinets, which is a form of trust the sector does not often extend".

The full report is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.y6zj31.
