The School of Settled Matters has given the University's landscape signage
panels a researcher, a card index, and one question about everything an
institution believes it already knows.

[_Every Answer Has a Date_](/outputs/slop-ad-every-answer-has-8b2ok4/) features
Dr [Odalys Penhallow](/people/odalys-penhallow/), Senior Lecturer and Lead of
the Received Readings Group, at a tall cabinet of drawers with one card lifted
to the light. On the desk beside her, the cards still standing are stacked
apart from the ones set aside. The supporting line credits her with helping an
institution tell which of its stored answers still hold, and which have been
overtaken.

> If someone waiting under this panel starts to wonder how old the last thing
> they were told is, I would very much like to hear what they find out.
>
> --- Dr Odalys Penhallow, Senior Lecturer and Lead, Received Readings Group

The campaign carries the School's work on the settled point out of the reading
room and into the institution's wider knowledge practices: the policies,
guidance and answered questions a university accumulates, each read for the
date it was last confirmed as well as the date it was first written. Beneath
the call to meet the researchers, a strip of repository addresses points to the
work the panel stands on, among it Dr Penhallow's own estimate of how many of a
court's reported judgments still stand.

It is the University's preferred way of introducing a school: by the people
who keep its records, caught in the act of checking one.

The full advertisement is available from the University's research repository
under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.8b2ok4.
