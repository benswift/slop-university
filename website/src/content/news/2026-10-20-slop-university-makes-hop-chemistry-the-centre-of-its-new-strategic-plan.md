Slop University has chosen the hop as the organising capability of its
Strategic Plan 2026–2031, a decision that brings together the plant sciences,
analytical chemistry and the long practical knowledge of the people who grow
and select hops.

The Plan's three focus areas follow the hop in the order it travels. The first
treats the garden and the selection room as the opening stage of the analytical
chain, and seeks to carry a selector's judgement forward into the record. The
second establishes a shared bench for the bitter acids and essential oils, with
every result reported alongside its blank and reporting floor. The third
addresses storage, treating the interval between harvest and use as a field of
research in its own right.

> Most of the work in a plan like this happens after the people who wrote it
> have stopped paying attention. We tried to write it for the cold room rather
> than the launch.
>
> --- Mr Eskil Nwabueze, Doctoral Candidate, School of Continuous Improvement

The Plan draws on the University's existing research in selection-room
practice, reference-standard stability and the humidity control of cold
archives, and frames those strands as a single, connected capability across all
three Schools. The University regards it as a deliberate statement of its
preference for patient, method-led work over breadth for its own sake.

The full plan is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.jw3w30.
