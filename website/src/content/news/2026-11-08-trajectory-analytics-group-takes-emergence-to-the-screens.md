> We have spent a long time asking where an institution's priorities come from. It is a quiet pleasure to find that a few of our own arrived while we were looking the other way.
>
> --- Professor Verity Marris, Director, Trajectory Analytics Group, School of Emergent Priorities

Professor Marris's reflection accompanies
[_Order Emerges Here_](/outputs/slop-ad-order-emerges-here-is8fdd/), a new
display campaign for the University's landscape screens, which carries the
Trajectory Analytics Group's record on the University's own Emergence Composite
out of the [School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/) and
onto the kerbside.

The campaign sets a single murmuration of starlings above a suburban bus
shelter, and lets a lone observer at a tripod stand in for the University's
broader posture towards complex systems: attentive, instrumented, and content
to let structure arrive at its own pace. Its small print points passers-by to
three published studies of synchrony, oscillation and persistence from across
the University, positioning the campaign as an entry point to an emerging
research conversation rather than its last word.

The University counts the campaign among its more candid accounts of how a
research culture comes to hold its shape, and of the patience that
[Professor Marris](/people/verity-marris/) and her colleagues bring to watching
it do so.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.is8fdd.
