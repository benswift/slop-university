Before a chemistry group makes anything, it decides what to buy. A new paper led from the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/) gives that decision a theory, and the theory gives an answer short enough to pin above the fume hood: buy the ring, make the rest.

[_Buy the Ring, Make the Rest_](/outputs/slop-paper-a-transaction-cost-theory-h555gz/) is the work of Dr [Clemency Tredinnick](/people/clemency-tredinnick/), with Associate Professor [Kwame Lindqvist](/people/kwame-lindqvist/), Director of the Master of Priority Studies, and Ms [Sunniva Balogun](/people/sunniva-balogun/) of the [School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/). It treats the route to a ring-shaped target molecule as a supply chain and asks where along it a group should stop ordering from the catalogue and start working at the bench. The answer is the ring that already carries its coupling handles, and the paper shows that this point moves back to the raw precursors whenever the ring's orientation is in doubt.

> Public finance taught me that every organisation is deciding what to do itself and what to pay others to do, whether or not it has noticed. It has been a real pleasure to watch that question find a home in a laboratory, and I expect it will raise more questions than it settles.
>
> --- Associate Professor Kwame Lindqvist, Director, Master of Priority Studies

The paper carries the transaction-cost tradition into the everyday logistics of research, mapping catalogue supply, verification burden and supplier relationships onto a single decision framework, and offering synthesis groups a principled vocabulary for a choice they have long made by habit.

Dr Tredinnick likened a bought intermediate to a title to land. "You take it with whatever came before it," she said, "and the only way to find out is to look."

The University welcomes the paper as a sign of how readily its schools now share a question, even when one of them keeps its questions in a flask.

The full paper is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.h555gz.
