Slop University has adopted a Strategic Plan that reorganises the institution around the things its units already hold in common. [_Cluster by Design_](/outputs/slop-strategy-cluster-by-design-0zm8mo/), the University's Strategic Plan for 2026 to 2031, gathers its schools, laboratories, programs, initiatives and service functions into clusters, and gives each one a single platform, a single front door and a council of its own.

The Plan was prepared with [Dr Joost Nwosu](/people/joost-nwosu/), Postdoctoral Fellow in the School of Continuous Improvement, and [Dr Brynjar Obiora](/people/brynjar-obiora/), Senior Research Fellow in the School of Underlying Conditions. It brings the economics of regional clustering, long a subject of the University's scholarship, to bear on the University's own corridors, counters and systems.

> Much of my work asks other organisations what they share and why they keep doing it separately. It has been instructive to be the organisation answering the question, and I think the School has learned a good deal about its own habits in the process.
>
> --- Dr Joost Nwosu, Postdoctoral Fellow, School of Continuous Improvement

The Plan sets out four priorities, from a foundation-year review that will draw the first cluster boundaries to a shared digital spine that asks every member of the University for each piece of information once. It also commits every cluster to a published way out of any service it offers, and to a standing decision on every target it meets.

Dr Obiora said the boundaries would be treated "as working lines, to be walked again before anyone mistakes them for the ground".

The University sees the Plan as a natural maturing of its research culture: an institution taking the advice it has long given others.

The full Strategic Plan is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.0zm8mo.
