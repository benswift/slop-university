Hold light between two mirrors for long enough and it stops being entirely
light. That idea, familiar to anyone who works on strong light-matter coupling,
is the premise of
[_Half Light. Half Matter. Fully Committed._](/outputs/slop-ad-half-light-half-fqz21w/),
a new advertisement for the
[School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/) developed by
[Dr Marek Solheim](/people/marek-solheim/), Lecturer and Deputy Convenor of the
Horizon Register.

The poster is set in a darkened laboratory. A band of gold light runs back and
forth between two tall round mirrors, a small sample glows at the centre of the
gap, and a single researcher stands at the edge of the frame, watching. The
campaign line sits beneath it, with a supporting line describing a university
where light and matter exchange energy faster than either can lose it.

> Most priorities pass through the University on their way to somewhere else.
> Now and then one stays long enough to change what it was passing through, and
> the School thought that deserved a panel of its own.
>
> --- Dr Marek Solheim, Lecturer and Deputy Convenor, Horizon Register

The campaign continues the School's practice of placing its anticipatory
agenda alongside the University's physical sciences, so that the sensing of
emerging priorities and the study of emergent states can be read in the same
light. As with every advertisement in the series, its small print directs
readers to the University's published work on emitters, cooling and
fluorescence, where the claim on the panel meets the record behind it.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.fqz21w.
