Two Greek letters, read four hundred and twelve times over, sit at the heart of [_Read as Meat_](/outputs/slop-poster-read-as-meat-iq5xlr/), the newest research poster from the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/). Taken one line at a time, they turn a third-century estate's well-fed workforce into one that ate mostly barley.

> Every institution keeps a few readings it inherited and never had cause to open. It is a privilege to work somewhere that goes back to the first page as readily as it moves on to the next one.
>
> --- Dr [Runa Adegoke](/people/runa-adegoke/), Senior Lecturer and Convenor, Strategic Drift Survey, School of Emergent Priorities

Led by Dr [Odalys Penhallow](/people/odalys-penhallow/), Senior Lecturer and Lead, Received Readings Group, with Dr Adegoke and Dr [Marek Solheim](/people/marek-solheim/) of the School of Emergent Priorities, the team brought close reading together with the accounts' own measures, prices and farming calendar, revisiting each entry the archive's editions had settled by rule. The result recasts the estate's provisioning as a predominantly grain-based ration, placing a long-closed editorial question within a wider conversation about sustainable diets, then and now.

Dr Solheim described the project as "a long apprenticeship in small things", and said the Horizon Register would carry its method forward with some care.

The University counts the poster among the patient, unhurried work that keeps its oldest questions in good repair.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.iq5xlr.
