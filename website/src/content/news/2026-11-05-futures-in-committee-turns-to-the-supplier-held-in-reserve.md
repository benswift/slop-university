Futures in Committee, the seminar series of the
[School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/), will give its
summer season to the arrangements an organisation keeps for the delivery that
does not come: the standby supplier on file, the stock set aside, the plan that
waits in a drawer. The season is announced in
[_Meet the supplier you have never called_](/outputs/slop-ad-meet-the-supplier-3dx2c7/),
a campaign poster for the University's campus screens prepared by
[Dr Lindiwe Achterberg](/people/lindiwe-achterberg/), Lecturer, and
[Dr Ingrid Vasseur](/people/ingrid-vasseur/), Lecturer and Convenor, Futures in
Committee.

The poster sets an early-morning loading dock in the School's two inks: a long
row of shut roller doors, one standing half open over a single wrapped pallet,
and a van just turning in at the far end of the apron. Dr Achterberg will give
the season's opening session.

> Every resilient organisation has an arrangement it has never had to use. The
> season is a chance to spend some time with ours, and to think carefully about
> what it is waiting for.
>
> --- Dr Lindiwe Achterberg, Lecturer, School of Emergent Priorities

The season carries the School's anticipatory-capability agenda into supply
chain resilience and risk management, convening colleagues across the
University around redundancy, continuity and the institutional settings in
which preparedness is held, reviewed and renewed. Dr Vasseur described the
program as "a natural next conversation for a series that has always been most
at home with the things that have not happened yet".

Sessions begin in February, with details to follow. The full poster is
available from the University's research repository under an open licence,
doi:10.5555/slop.3dx2c7.
