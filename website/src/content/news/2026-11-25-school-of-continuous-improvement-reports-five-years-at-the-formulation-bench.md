The [School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/) has published [_Inside the Design Space_](/outputs/slop-impact-inside-the-design-ywvxad/), its Impact Report 2021–2026, an account of five years in which the School took its methods for laying out an experiment into the laboratories of pharmaceutics and drug-delivery groups.

The report was prepared by Dr [Ingeborg Nwachukwu](/people/ingeborg-nwachukwu/), Dr [Dagny Okafor](/people/dagny-okafor/) and Ms [Signy Okpara](/people/signy-okpara/), and moves through four areas of work: finding where the spread between batches actually comes from, following Capability Sprint graduates back to their own benches, reviewing how design reports are written, and asking collaborators to seal a prediction before each confirmation run is made.

> The formulation scientists came to us for help with their batches, and we found ourselves learning how our own courses wear off. A Sprint is a designed experiment too, and it took a pharmaceutics laboratory to show the School which of its habits were holding.
>
> --- Dr Ingeborg Nwachukwu, Lecturer and Convenor, Capability Sprints

Across its four areas, the report positions designed experimentation as shared infrastructure for translational improvement, aligning bench practice, short-course teaching and evaluation of evaluation within a single, collaborator-facing agenda.

Dr Okafor said the review of design reports had left the School "with a much clearer sense of what a stranger needs from a page, and a good deal more sympathy for the people writing it."

The University counts the report among the clearest pictures yet of what the School does when it leaves the building, and expects its account of the formulation bench to inform the School's work well beyond pharmaceutics.

The full report is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.ywvxad.
