The [School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/) has published its Impact Report for 2021–2026, [_The Unassigned Peak_](/outputs/slop-impact-the-unassigned-peak-lxmhxt/), a record of five years spent alongside the chemists, facility staff and crystallographers who decide what a characterisation report will say about the signals nobody can yet explain.

The report is credited to doctoral candidate [Eivind Onwuka](/people/eivind-onwuka/), with [Associate Professor Casimir Beng](/people/casimir-beng/), Lead of the Adaptive Metrics Lab, and [Dr Joost Nwosu](/people/joost-nwosu/), Postdoctoral Fellow.

> None of this would exist without the people at the bench who let us sit beside them while they decided what a figure would show. They were far kinder to our questions than our questions deserved.
>
> --- Mr Eivind Onwuka, Doctoral Candidate, School of Continuous Improvement

Across three case studies, the report follows the leftover signal from the instrument to the page, combining an archival reading of more than eleven hundred reports, an interlaboratory comparison in which nine laboratories characterised a single vial, and close fieldwork with synthesis groups preparing their supplementary figures. Together they describe a reporting ecosystem learning to make room, on its own forms, for what it has not yet accounted for.

For the University, the report is a welcome record of a School at ease in the instrument room, and of the patient collaborations that brought it there.

Associate Professor Beng said the School would "keep a line open on its own forms for whatever this report could not account for".

The full report is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.lxmhxt.
