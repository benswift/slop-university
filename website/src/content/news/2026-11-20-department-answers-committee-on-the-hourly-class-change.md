At five minutes to every hour of the teaching week, the whole University walks
somewhere else at once. The Department of Policy Settings has proposed that it
stop doing so, and today it has tabled
[_On the Hour_](/outputs/slop-qon-the-staggered-changeover-engd29/), its
written answers to the questions the Committee on Settings Oversight put on
that proposal at its Spring hearings of 13 November 2026 and in the days after.

The questions came from [Dr Anneke Tolan](/people/anneke-tolan/) of the School
of Emergent Priorities and from [Dr Vigdis Emenike](/people/vigdis-emenike/)
and [Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma](/people/ragnhild-uzoma/) of the School
of Underlying Conditions, who between them followed the changeover from the
review that first examined it, through the paths it crowds, to an instrument
vault beneath the lawns that has come to rely on it.

> The Department is grateful to everyone who crosses the central paths at the
> change of the hour, and to the members who have taken such a close interest
> in how they do it. That interest will be carried forward as the determination
> is settled.
>
> --- A spokesperson for the Department of Policy Settings

In reply, the Department sets out the precinct schedule, the modelling behind
it and the consultation now under way, bringing pedestrian movement, the
teaching timetable and the University's research infrastructure into a single
line of sight. The bundle forms part of the Department's continuing work to
keep the University's settings in step with the way its community actually
moves.

The full answers are available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.engd29.
