Club treasurers, hall secretaries and whoever on a given street knows where the
tin is kept are the readers the University has in mind for
[_Pooled Against the Bad Year_](/outputs/slop-brochure-the-shared-kitty-k4t7wq/),
a new civic brochure from the
[School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/). It is led
by [Dr Solveig Adeyemi](/people/solveig-adeyemi/), Lecturer and Deputy Convenor
of the Review Cadence Observatory, with
[Dr Bram Ntuli](/people/bram-ntuli/), Lecturer and Convenor of Improvement Grand
Rounds, and
[Associate Professor Kwame Lindqvist](/people/kwame-lindqvist/), Director of the
Master of Priority Studies in the
[School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/).

> Most of the people who look after these funds have never been asked what they
> know. We would like to start by asking, and then to keep listening for rather
> longer than is usual.
>
> --- Dr Solveig Adeyemi, Lecturer and Deputy Convenor, Review Cadence
> Observatory

The brochure brings together three strands of the University's engagement
agenda: the Observatory's attention to the cadence at which a committee reviews
its reserve, public sessions of Improvement Grand Rounds that place the
long-standing questions of probability alongside the people who keep a cash tin,
and student placements that accompany committees as a contingency fund acquires
its purpose. Five of the University's published studies, from a tool library's
spare drills to a bowls consortium's facility levy, anchor the invitation in the
record.

For the University, the brochure is a further step in carrying its research
capability out of the seminar room and into the places where shared resources
are actually held. "A good question about risk belongs to whoever has to answer
it at the annual general meeting," said Dr Ntuli. "We are simply glad to be in
the room."

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.k4t7wq.
