Slop University has published a prospectus that asks its readers the same three
questions it asks every new student at enrolment: the name of their first
school, the street they grew up on, and the teacher they remember.
[_Only You Would Know_](/outputs/slop-brochure-only-you-would-vww10w/) was
prepared by Sunniva Balogun, Halvard Nnamani and Ylva Onyekwere, Doctoral
Candidates in the [School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/).

> Putting the prospectus together, we found the University had been asking
> students to remember things for a long time without saying what it did with
> what they remembered. Now it says so, in print.
>
> --- Sunniva Balogun, Doctoral Candidate, School of Emergent Priorities

The prospectus maps each question onto a School and its programs, moving from
the Master of Priority Studies through fieldwork in the School of Underlying
Conditions to the Master of Applied Measurement. Five published studies anchor
the account, from café password chalkboards to the hand-lettered caps of
groundwater bores, each positioned as part of the University's sustained
engagement with recognition, verification and the records that underpin them.
Three researcher profiles carry the invitation to its close.

The University regards the publication as a natural extension of its
enrolment experience, and of the care it already takes with what students
entrust to it on their first day. "We wrote it for the student who pauses at
the screen," said Mr Nnamani. "That pause is where the University begins."

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.vww10w.
