For fourteen planning meetings, a team from the School of Underlying Conditions sat quietly at the end of the table while a government core repository worked out how to move its collection into a new shed. _Watching the Model Run_, a new research poster, reports what they saw: a group that increasingly made up its mind by asking for the simulation to be played again.

The poster is by Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, Lead of the Basement Relations Group, with Dr Anneke Tolan, Senior Research Fellow and Deputy Convenor of Futures in Committee, and Dr Bram Ntuli, Lecturer and Convenor of Improvement Grand Rounds.

> Meetings are where an organisation finds out what it already thinks. We were fortunate to be present for fourteen of them, and the poster is our attempt to give a little of that room back.
>
> --- Dr Anneke Tolan, Senior Research Fellow and Deputy Convenor, Futures in Committee

Bringing sustained non-participant observation together with a close reading of meeting records and the model's own change history, the study follows how evidence, visualisation and organisational routine converged across the life of a relocation program, and carries simulation practice into the organisation-studies account of how planning groups come to see their own work.

"Our School has spent a long time thinking about what comes up out of the ground and what does not," said Associate Professor Uzoma. "It seemed only right to give some of that attention to the meetings that decide where it all goes next."

For the University, the work is a welcome reminder that its schools are as interested in the rooms where rock is discussed as in the rock itself.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.cnxovv.
