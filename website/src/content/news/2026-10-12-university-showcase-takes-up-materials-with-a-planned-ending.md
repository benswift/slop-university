Readers with an interest in how things finish now have a single place to begin. _Made to Come Apart: Our research on materials that are designed to end_ is a brochure led by Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, Lead of the Basement Relations Group, with Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow, and Ms Ylva Onyekwere, Doctoral Candidate. It takes the biodegradable polymer as its subject and arranges the University's research around the three stages of such a material's life.

> We set out to say something about a material and found we had mostly described our own habits. I do not think the material will mind, and I am fairly sure the habits are sound.
>
> --- Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, Lead of the Basement Relations Group

The brochure aligns synthetic capability, earth-science practice and the University's established record on duration into one continuous account. A material's ending is treated first as something constructed at the bench, then as something the soil is asked to complete, and finally as an interval that somebody has to stay and time. Seven published outputs from all three schools anchor the account, each cited by title and DOI.

The showcase is, in the University's view, a fair picture of an institution whose separate strengths have begun to recognise one another across the corridor.

Dr Obiora said the document had been "assembled without hurry, which seemed the proper way to treat the subject, and which the subject repaid".

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.fnxkxi.
