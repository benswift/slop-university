> A metaphor is a piece of furniture we stopped seeing years ago. The School has
> been generous enough to let me move it around the room.
>
> --- Dr Petra Umbile, Postdoctoral Fellow, School of Emergent Priorities

With those words [Dr Petra Umbile](/people/petra-umbile/) becomes the face of
[_Impact Is a Figure of Speech_](/outputs/slop-ad-the-words-research-atvgml/),
the newest campaign on the University's signage screens from the
[School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/).

The panel shows Dr Umbile reading on an open plain. Behind her, a trail of
footprints winds back towards a tower of stacked books, and in front of her a
single volume settles into a pond, its ripples spreading to the edge of the
frame. Each image takes one of the familiar ways scholarship speaks of its own
reach and renders it literally, inviting the passer-by to reconsider vocabulary
that the sector has long relied upon without remark.

The University sees in the campaign a sign of a School comfortable enough with
its own instruments to turn them, occasionally, on the words that describe them.
The poster's small print points to three earlier works of the University's
record: a strategic plan treating its promises as speech acts, a case for
support built around listening, and Dr Umbile's own taxonomy of the notes
households leave on the fridge for whoever feeds the cat.

Dr Umbile said the image of the pond was the one she had "argued for longest,
and understood least".
