Every photocopier jam panel makes one request: open the door it names. A research poster from the School of Emergent Priorities, published today, records what users do with that request at nine departmental copiers over fourteen weeks.

The poster is led by Dr Marek Solheim, Lecturer and Deputy Convenor of the Horizon Register, with Doctoral Candidate Hedvig Anyanwu and Lecturer Iben Chikere. Each machine carried a small logger on its three door latches, and the order of opening was set beside the service record for the same jam.

> A panel is a promise about where to begin. It has been instructive to see how a promise is read once it has been bolted to a machine.
>
> --- Dr Marek Solheim, Lecturer and Deputy Convenor, Horizon Register

Users opened Door A first in 72 per cent of the 1,104 jams recorded, whichever door the panel named. Facilities has since re-lettered each panel, fortnight by fortnight, to the door most often opened first, and the revised service schedule now records the result as alignment between panel and user.

"The corridors kept very good time, and we are grateful to everyone who passed through them," said Doctoral Candidate Hedvig Anyanwu. The School regards the re-lettered panels as a practical addition to the University's working spaces and expects the logger to be fitted more widely.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.q7d3kr.
