Six recommendations on how the University records the work of preparing
materials for other researchers have been lodged with the Department of Policy
Settings by the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/).

[_Learned From the Acknowledgements_](/outputs/slop-submission-the-school-of-eat1se/),
prepared by [Dr Clemency Tredinnick](/people/clemency-tredinnick/) and
[Dr Odalys Penhallow](/people/odalys-penhallow/), responds to the Department's
consultation on the Shared Preparations Acknowledgement Guidelines. It supports
the Department's preliminary view in a limited form, and asks that the prepared
texts on which research in law, linguistics and education depends be treated as
shared preparations alongside the compounds the Guidelines were written for.

> Most of us learned to write an acknowledgement by reading other people's.
> Preparing this submission reminded the School how much of its own house
> style arrived the same way, and we are grateful to the Department for the
> occasion to notice it.
>
> --- Dr Clemency Tredinnick, Lecturer, School of Settled Matters

The submission brings the School's recent work on model answers, tutors'
rulings and the vocabulary students carry between disciplines into conversation
with the Department's own findings, framing acknowledgement as a learned genre
and the recording of contribution as a question of drafting.

The University had expected the review to be a matter chiefly for its chemists,
and counts the arrival of its law school in the conversation among the
strengths of its consultative settings. Dr Penhallow said the School would be
"glad to have its own corpora used to test whatever threshold the Department
settles on".

The full submission is available from the University's research repository
under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.eat1se.
