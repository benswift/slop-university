The University's landscape screens have taken on a new scene: a district land
office, a bench of people waiting their turn, and a clerk with a rubber stamp
raised over a freshly issued certificate of title.

[_The Law Begins at the Counter_](/outputs/slop-ad-law-begins-at-1b9y1a/) is
the latest display campaign from the
[School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/), and it is fronted by
[Dr Clemency Tredinnick](/people/clemency-tredinnick/), Lecturer, whose
teaching in land law and whose collection of superseded certificates have
made the registry a familiar subject around the School.

> Most of what I know about land I learned from the people on the far side of a
> counter. It is good to see them, and the people waiting for them, given a
> wall of their own.
>
> --- Dr Clemency Tredinnick, Lecturer, School of Settled Matters

In translating that interest for a public audience, the campaign brings the
School's close reading of statutes into conversation with the administrative
settings where a titling program takes shape, positioning the form, the queue
and the stamp as sites where legal meaning is enacted, received and kept. Its
small print points readers onward to three of the School's recent outputs,
among them [_Noted on Title_](/outputs/slop-paper-whether-a-restrictive-fnpeur/)
and
[_Fields That Run with the Paddock_](/outputs/slop-poster-fields-that-run-jdvfy8/).

The campaign joins the University's continuing effort to show its research
wherever its public already happens to be waiting.
