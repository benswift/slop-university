Every student who begins a degree at Slop University will soon start in the same place: a unit that hands them a figure and asks them to make the case for it. [_Weighing the Evidence_](/outputs/slop-strategy-reasoning-under-uncertainty-qzd236/), the University's Strategic Plan 2026-2031, was prepared from the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/) by [Dr Odalys Penhallow](/people/odalys-penhallow/), [Dr Torun Ezeigwe](/people/torun-ezeigwe/) and [Dr Vigdis Emenike](/people/vigdis-emenike/), and sets out to teach statistics the way the School has long taught the law, one worked case at a time.

> A judgment is only ever as good as the reasons it gives, and this School has spent a long time reading reasons. It has been a pleasure to discover how much the rest of the University had been doing the same with its numbers.
>
> --- Dr Odalys Penhallow, Senior Lecturer and Lead, Received Readings Group, School of Settled Matters

Across four priorities, the Plan aligns first-year teaching, disciplinary method, research practice and institutional reporting behind a single capability. A shared foundation unit and a Casebook of Worked Numbers anchor the commencing experience; Methods Fellows and School casebooks carry it into the disciplines; analysis plans and uncertainty budgets extend it to research; and the University's own measures are to be reported with their precision.

Dr Ezeigwe, Senior Lecturer and Director of the Master of Applied Measurement, welcomed "the questions a good casebook leaves open, which tend to be better company over a degree than the answers a formula closes".

The University is pleased to hold itself to the standard it now sets for its students, and has written that standard into the Plan's own indicators.

The full plan is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.qzd236.
