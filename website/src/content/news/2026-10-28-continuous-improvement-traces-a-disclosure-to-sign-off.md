Three words decide what a not-for-profit's annual report says about the claims
and disputes on its books: probable, possible and remote. A new research poster
from the [School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/)
asks when, in the life of a single report, one of those words is chosen.

[_Drafted Down to Remote_](/outputs/slop-poster-drafted-down-to-2t5grp/) is by
[Dr Solveig Adeyemi](/people/solveig-adeyemi/), Lecturer and Deputy Convenor of
the Review Cadence Observatory, with
[Associate Professor Kwame Lindqvist](/people/kwame-lindqvist/) and
[Dr Fenna Okoro](/people/fenna-okoro/). Working alongside eight organisations
across three reporting cycles, the team brought the legal register and the
financial report into a single line of sight, following each of 143 matters
from first draft to signature. Most moves between the three words came in the
last ten working days, and few of them were matched by any change on the
register itself.

> Every report the School has ever published went through drafts of its own, and
> we have learned a good deal about ourselves from watching other people's. The
> organisations who opened their working files to us did something generous,
> and the poster is our way of saying so.
>
> --- Dr Solveig Adeyemi, Lecturer and Deputy Convenor, Review Cadence
> Observatory, School of Continuous Improvement

The School counts the poster among its steadiest contributions yet to
understanding how an institution's records become its reports. Associate
Professor Lindqvist said the work "sits comfortably beside the School's
long-standing interest in what a document carries forward, and we are glad to
hear from any finance team that recognises its own calendar in it."

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.2t5grp.
