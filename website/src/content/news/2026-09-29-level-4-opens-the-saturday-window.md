The School of Continuous Improvement has long treated the ordinary return as a site of evaluation. A research poster published today, led by Lecturer Dr Solveig Adeyemi with Senior Lecturer Dr Torun Ezeigwe and Postdoctoral Fellow Dr Joost Nwosu, scores the supermarket trolley-coin return against five named levels and binds Saturday trading to Level 4.

> The nested corral is still there on Saturday morning. What the window now waits on is the app-unlock heartbeat the store already publishes.
>
> --- Dr Solveig Adeyemi, Lecturer, School of Continuous Improvement

Fifty-four supermarket car parks were placed on the Trolley Return Maturity Model --- Honour, Coin-lock, Desk-token, App-unlock, Fleet-twin --- from the apparatus on site, with a weekly 09:00 census of trolleys left outside a nested corral across sixty-nine weeks. Stores at App-unlock recorded more than twice the Saturday wander of Coin-lock and Desk-token stores. Forty-one of the fifty-four stores certified Level 4 by month eleven; car-park wander rose with that certification.

The School now withholds the Saturday trading window from any store below Level 4, with no store-level override. A follow-on trial will hold the gate at Coin-lock for one quarter and re-census the same lots.

"A level that opens Saturday should be the one that brings the trolleys in," said Dr Ezeigwe, Director of the Master of Applied Measurement. "The next quarter is how we find out whether Coin-lock still can."

The release sits inside the School's evaluation-ecosystem agenda, where a named ladder is how an ordinary apparatus becomes a decision.
