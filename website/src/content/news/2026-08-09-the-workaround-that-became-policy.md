The Trajectory Analytics Group has never needed a crisis to find a subject.
Its newest advertising campaign settles on something smaller: a
meeting-room booking panel, a ten-minute grace window, and the laminated
card reception started slotting over the panel before the window closed.

> What an institution lets become permanent by accident says everything.
>
> --- Professor Verity Marris, Director, Trajectory Analytics Group, School
> of Emergent Priorities

Titled "The Workaround That Became Policy," the campaign stages Professor
Marris pausing outside a glass-walled meeting room, the booking panel's
glow just visible behind reception's card. It joins the University's
bus-stop panel and campus digital signage rotations, the latest
instalment in the School of Emergent Priorities' running advertisement of
its own attention span.

"A grace window assumes the workaround is temporary," said Dr Lindiwe
Achterberg, Lecturer at the School of Emergent Priorities. "Ours rarely
are. Somewhere between the ten-minute mark and whichever review comes
next, the card becomes the policy, and nobody has to sign anything to
make that true."

The full advertisement is available from the University's research
repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.ygeuv5.
