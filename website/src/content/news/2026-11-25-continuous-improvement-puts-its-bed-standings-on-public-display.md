The [School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/) keeps
standings for most of what it measures. This season it has chosen one of them
for the bus stop.

[_Nothing Near Us Comes Close._](/outputs/slop-ad-nothing-near-us-e4hl6q/), a new
landscape panel for the University's signage network, shows a single young plant
standing upright in a pegged ring of bare soil at the head of a crowded trial
bed. The plant has finished first on the School's own Indicator Commons bed
standings in every season since planting, and the panel says so in gold.

> I have spent most of my working life at the other end of a results table. It
> has been instructive to help put the top of one on a wall, and to see how much
> room there turns out to be up there.
>
> --- Mr Eskil Nwabueze, Doctoral Candidate, School of Continuous Improvement

The campaign brings the School's indicator practice out to the trial bed,
aligning plot records, germination counts and the shared indicator library
within a single display, and positioning the cleared ground around the leading
plant as a visible expression of sustained performance.

Its small print points to three outputs from the University's record:
[_Persistence Above the Slab_](/outputs/slop-paper-a-three-season-occupancy-srax1h/),
on which beds of a community garden are taken up and which are let go;
[_PLOT_](/outputs/slop-brochure-allotment-plot-boundary-y3lb3f/), the
Trajectory Analytics Group's tracker for allotment boundaries that creep past
their stakes; and
[_Asking for a thumbs-up on the skip-bin post does not change who uses the skip_](/outputs/slop-poster-although-the-street-u3g5fo/),
Mr Nwabueze's own poster on a street's shared bin.

The School describes the panel as the first of its standings to go on public
view, and expects the plant to hold its place for some time yet.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.e4hl6q.
