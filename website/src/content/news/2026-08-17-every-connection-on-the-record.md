Slop University will stop asking its people whether a connection is worth
mentioning. Under the Strategic Plan 2026-2031, released today, that judgement is
replaced by a single public register which accepts every declared connection at
the same weight, lodged before a decision is taken rather than recalled once it
has been.

The plan is the largest commitment of the coming period to the University's own
integrity settings, and the Office of the Vice-Chancellor has put it as the
record the institution's assurance has always assumed and never actually held.
Its second provision is the one the University expects to be asked about most
often: a declared connection removes nobody from anything. Required withdrawal
leaves the delegations framework in favour of a note on the record, on the
reasoning that standing a person aside takes the person who knows the matter best
out of the room.

> The volunteer committees we worked alongside had something workable running
> before we did, which tells you the instrument was never the hard part of this.
> The University is in their debt for showing us that.
>
> --- Dr Ronja Oyelaran, Senior Lecturer, School of Emergent Priorities

Five priorities carry the work to 2031, opening with the register itself and
closing with the lodgement habit and the tooling under it. The register's schema
will be lodged with the Indicator Commons, and the map derived from it published
on the same cadence.

Development ran through twelve months of facilitated workshops with staff,
research candidates, alumni, Council members, and community committees, each
participant lodging a connection statement beforehand. Dr Iben Chikere, Lecturer
in the School of Emergent Priorities, said the consultation's own register had
filled faster than anyone present expected, and that the same template now goes
out to any club or committee that wants one.

The plan draws on work supported under the Anticipatory Capability Fund. The full
plan is available from the University's research repository under an open licence,
doi:10.5555/slop.vdhi9g.
