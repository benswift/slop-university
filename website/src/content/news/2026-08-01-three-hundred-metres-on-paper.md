The University has taken a steady interest in the moment a finding stops being a
finding and becomes a coefficient in somebody's formula. A paper released today
follows one of those coefficients out to the parks it now governs.

_They Said They Would Walk_, deposited through the Office of Research Outputs,
re-estimates the discrete choice experiment behind an index six adjoining
metropolitan councils use to rank candidate drinking-fountain sites and fund the
top thirty each renewal round. Across 1,842 residents and 22,104 choice tasks,
the walk a resident would make for a bottle filler comes back at 331 metres.
Watching 96 fountains over 328 sessions, the team recorded 24,106 people passing
and 2,418 stopping to drink; the detour they made for that same feature was 10.8
metres.

> Residents answered the question honestly, and it was a fair question. What the
> School has had to learn is that an honest answer and a footpath are different
> kinds of evidence, and our instruments do not always say which one they are
> holding.
>
> --- Dr Ronja Oyelaran, Senior Lecturer, School of Emergent Priorities

The ordering survived: residents both say and do prefer a bottle filler to a
child step. The scale did not, and it is the scale the index turns into weights.
Recalibrated to the observed detours, 14 of the 96 candidate sites cross the
funding line. A delivery test run at every session found the fountain not
running at 31.2 per cent of visits, a condition no procurement specification can
carry.

"The counting was done by people who gave up a great many Saturdays to stand
near a bubbler with a clipboard, and the result belongs to them as much as to
us," said Dr Bram Ntuli, Lecturer and Convenor of Improvement Grand Rounds.

The University holds that a weight, once it is inside a funding formula, has
become an institutional commitment rather than a research finding, and is glad
to see one of its own tested as such. The full paper is available from the
University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.r5xph8.
