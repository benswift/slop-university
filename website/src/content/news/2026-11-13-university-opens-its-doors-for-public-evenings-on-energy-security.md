Slop University is opening its doors after dark. [_A Room With the Lights On_](/outputs/slop-brochure-a-room-with-ur2m8m/), a new brochure led by the [School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/), sets out the public evenings through which three of the University's Schools are inviting their neighbours to think through the region's energy security, one long table at a time.

> The Rounds were built for colleagues reviewing their own work. It has been humbling to watch residents bring their winters to the same table and hold them up to the light more carefully than we ever held up ours.
>
> --- [Dr Bram Ntuli](/people/bram-ntuli/), Lecturer and Convenor, Improvement Grand Rounds

Its pages move between three rooms. The School's Improvement Grand Rounds are recast as an open forum for households' lived experience of the network; the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/) works through energy agreements clause by clause in a reading room; and the [School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/) convenes a season of Futures in Committee sessions around a model town. Together they position the University as a convener of the region's conversation on resilience, supply and sequence, each room anchored in six published studies on household batteries, rooftop-solar export, keep-warm clauses, ground-source heat, island grids and the electricity bill.

[Associate Professor Anselm Threlkeld](/people/anselm-threlkeld/) welcomed the questions the evenings have begun to raise. "We have learned more from what is asked across that table than from most of the answers we have written down," he said. [Halvard Nnamani](/people/halvard-nnamani/), Doctoral Candidate, said the sessions had already changed how he reads a planning form.

For the University, the publication confirms a long-held view that an institution's most persuasive research facility may simply be a well-lit room with its doors open.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.ur2m8m.
