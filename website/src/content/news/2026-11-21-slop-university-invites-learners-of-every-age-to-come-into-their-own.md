Parents, teachers, school students and anyone thinking of returning to study are the intended readers of [_Coming Into Your Own_](/outputs/slop-brochure-coming-into-your-9ac4ts/), a civic brochure prepared by the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/) on the University's behalf. It sets out the University's public teaching for people at every stage of growing up, and the research that informs it.

> A law school spends most of its days with rules nobody remembers making. Putting this together, the School found it had been teaching young people to do much the same thing for some time, and it was glad of the reminder.
>
> --- Dr [Clemency Tredinnick](/people/clemency-tredinnick/), Lecturer, School of Settled Matters

The brochure draws together studies from three of the University's Schools, aligning classroom inheritance, rehearsal-room timing and tutorial-table decision-making into a single civic account of developmental readiness, and positioning public teaching as a shared platform for the maturation of judgement across the life course.

Prepared with [Associate Professor Anselm Threlkeld](/people/anselm-threlkeld/) and doctoral candidate [Mr Halvard Nnamani](/people/halvard-nnamani/), it features six of the University's published studies, among them a senior secondary class revising a body of work inherited from the year before and a beginning percussion section learning to keep its own count.

Mr Nnamani said he hoped readers would "write in about whatever the brochure doesn't have a box for".

The brochure takes its place in the University's continuing conversation with the district it serves, and the School of Settled Matters expects the conversation to outlast any single edition.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.9ac4ts.
