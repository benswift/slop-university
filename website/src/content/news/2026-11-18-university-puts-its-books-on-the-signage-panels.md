Minuted, reconciled, signed off. Those three words now run across the
University's landscape signage panels, in
[_Minuted. Reconciled. Signed Off._](/outputs/slop-ad-minuted-reconciled-signed-qk4v7r/),
a new advertisement developed by [Dr Brynjar Obiora](/people/brynjar-obiora/),
Senior Research Fellow in the
[School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/).

The poster takes the quieter side of university life, the committee that keeps
its minutes and the ledger that closes when it should, and gives it the same
display space usually reserved for open days and new degrees. It is staged in
the School's core store after hours: steel racks of drill-core trays, a trestle
table, an open ledger, a stamp and its pad, and a stack of minute books tied
with string, all under a single lamp.

> A core store is a promise that whatever was brought up will still be there
> when someone comes asking for it. It seemed the right room in which to keep
> the books.
>
> --- Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow, School of Underlying Conditions

In bringing stewardship, assurance and research culture into a single frame,
the campaign extends the University's ongoing effort to let its administrative
practice be seen as plainly as its scholarship. Its small print points readers
on to the University's published work on registers, ledgers and governance
maturity, so that the claim on the panel and the record behind it can be read
together.

The advertisement appears on the University's landscape panels in matched light
and dark versions. The full poster is available from the University's research
repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.qk4v7r.
