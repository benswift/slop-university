Anyone who has tested a park bench with the back of a hand before sitting down
already holds the knowledge at the centre of
[_Warm to the Touch_](/outputs/slop-brochure-warm-to-the-3gfgw7/), a new
brochure from the [School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/).
Prepared by [Mr Tove Ilesanmi](/people/tove-ilesanmi/),
[Ms Gunnhild Ekwueme](/people/gunnhild-ekwueme/) and
[Ms Edda Nwafor](/people/edda-nwafor/), it asks the town to share that knowledge
with the University.

The brochure moves through three settings: the afternoon bench, the footbridge
rail and the playground under its shade sail, where a low concrete wall keeps
the day's warmth into the evening. In each, the University's thermal and
mechanical testing is placed alongside the everyday judgements of the people
who sit, lean and climb there.

> The School has learned a good deal from the repairs a town makes for itself.
> We are grateful to everyone who has kept a bench in service with a length of
> wire, and we would like to understand what they already know.
>
> --- Mr Tove Ilesanmi, Doctoral Candidate, School of Emergent Priorities

Ms Nwafor described the folded towel on a summer seat as "a decision the
University is only beginning to take as seriously as the town does."

The publication reflects the University's conviction that knowledge of a
material is held most fully by the people in daily contact with it. It cites
five published studies from three Schools, among them an audit of the reserves'
public barbecues and a reading of the wear on a retired fleet of ticket
machines.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.3gfgw7.
