The [School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/) has long held that a priority does its best work once someone else can pick it up. Its newest publication carries that conviction to the synthesis bench, and to a ring of four atoms.

[_Made to Be Opened_](/outputs/slop-brochure-made-to-be-5u87zb/), a research showcase prepared by Mr [Halvard Nnamani](/people/halvard-nnamani/), Ms [Ylva Onyekwere](/people/ylva-onyekwere/) and Dr [Runa Adegoke](/people/runa-adegoke/), introduces the School's work on β-lactam synthesis to colleagues, prospective students and partners.

> A brochure is a promise made in good faith to readers we have not met yet. We wanted to make one that the chemists down the corridor would recognise as theirs, and we are grateful to everyone who read the drafts with a pencil in hand.
>
> --- Dr Runa Adegoke, Senior Lecturer and Convenor, Strategic Drift Survey

Moving from the closure of the ring, through the choice between its two faces, to its opening into longer routes, the brochure situates the School's bench chemistry within a wider ecosystem of University scholarship. It gathers seven published studies on reaction schemes, intermediates and the judgements that shape a synthesis, and invites readers to bring their own routes to Futures in Committee, the School's seminar series.

Mr Nnamani said the project had been "a chance to put the fume cupboard and the strategy table in the same room, and to find that they had a fair amount to say to each other."

The University regards the brochure as a timely addition to the work that crosses, more often than one might expect, from its planning documents to its flasks.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.5u87zb.
