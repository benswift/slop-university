When the feed to one of the coils on the University's small research tokamak was replaced during routine maintenance, the paperwork described the new part as like-for-like. [_Like for Like_](/outputs/slop-paper-locating-the-intrinsic-xbeo0p/), a new paper by Dr [Runa Adegoke](/people/runa-adegoke/), Ms [Gunnhild Ekwueme](/people/gunnhild-ekwueme/) and Dr [Clemency Tredinnick](/people/clemency-tredinnick/), finds that the machine's magnetic field took a different view.

> Every maintenance period gives a machine back slightly changed, and the people who run it slightly changed as well. The School is grateful to have spent two campaigns paying attention to both, and we expect the next shutdown to be a more interesting one for it.
>
> --- Dr Runa Adegoke, Senior Lecturer and Convenor, Strategic Drift Survey

Working across two campaigns of operation, the team applied a sequence of carefully calibrated magnetic nudges to the plasma and recorded the point at which each one tipped it over, building a detailed picture of the small imperfections every fusion device carries and of the correction it needs to cancel them. Set alongside a model of the rebuilt hardware, the measurements connect a substantial change in that correction to a single item of maintenance, and show that over the nine weeks before it was noticed, the share of the machine's discharges ending early rose from around one in a hundred to more than seven.

Asked what would follow from the findings, Ms Ekwueme, a doctoral candidate in the [School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/), said only that "the work order template is being looked at."

The paper strengthens a growing partnership between the School of Emergent Priorities and the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/) around the University's experimental plasma programme, and it is a source of real satisfaction that a School devoted to the distance between plans and practice has found that distance measurable in kiloampere-turns.

The full paper is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.xbeo0p.
