The staff community-events digest will, from the next intake, carry only those senders whose unsubscribe link finishes what it starts. The decision follows _Towards a Final Unsubscribe_, a poster released today by Ms Ylva Ogunbanjo with Dr Anouk Mensah and Dr Iben Chikere of the School of Emergent Priorities.

> Leaving well is part of arriving well. We are grateful to every club and shop that let us watch how it ends.
>
> --- Ylva Ogunbanjo, Doctoral Candidate, School of Emergent Priorities

The poster sets out five named levels for the footer link of an emailed newsletter, from Absent to Final, and scores 94 suburban clubs and small shops against them. Each sender was entered through a project mailbox, asked once to let go, and watched for eight weeks while two raters scored independently. Nine senders reached the fifth level; forty remained at the first or second.

The University will list only senders at the third level or above, and those at the fifth will carry a mark in the digest header. Scores are re-struck each year, and a sender may return on reaching the third level. The release reflects the University's steady interest in the last step of every sign-up.

Dr Mensah said the work had "given the footer a place on the agenda it never asked for", and Dr Chikere described the raters' agreement as "the part of the study that made everything after it feel settled."

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.i50xfn.
