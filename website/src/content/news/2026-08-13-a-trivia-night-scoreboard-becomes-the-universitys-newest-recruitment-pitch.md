A trivia night's leaderboard updates after every round, long before the last
scoresheet reaches the quiz master's table. Slop University's newest
recruitment campaign for the Master of Priority Studies takes that gap as
its subject: the interval in which a room agrees on an outcome it hasn't
actually finished counting.

Unveiled today under the line "Learn To See It Before It's Final", the
campaign folds a lecture theatre and a projected scoreboard into a single
scene, pitching the degree as training in exactly that interval --- reading
a result while it is still forming, rather than waiting for the room to
settle on what it means. It joins the University's bus-stop panel and
campus digital signage rotations, this run in landscape.

> A leaderboard that updates every round is doing its job. The room's job is
> remembering that a projection and a result are different things, right up
> until they aren't.
>
> --- Associate Professor Kwame Lindqvist, Director, Master of Priority
> Studies

"Most of what the School of Emergent Priorities studies has already been
decided by the time anyone thinks to check," said Professor Verity Marris,
Director of the Trajectory Analytics Group. "The campaign is really an
invitation to notice that decision arriving."

The line is the School's pitch in miniature: that reading a trend early is
its own discipline, worth a degree rather than a guess.
