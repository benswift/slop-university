Anyone weighing where to study the language they think in has a new place to begin. [_Words Come with Conditions_](/outputs/slop-brochure-words-come-with-prg91g/), a prospectus prepared by [Dr Clemency Tredinnick](/people/clemency-tredinnick/) of the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/) with [Dr Anouk Mensah](/people/anouk-mensah/) and [Dr Brynjar Obiora](/people/brynjar-obiora/), sets out the University's offer to students curious about how the figures of ordinary speech shape what people go on to think.

> Writing our own vocabulary down for a stranger to read turned out to be a useful exercise. The School found it had rather more to say about its words than it had supposed, and some of them have been with us a long time.
>
> --- Dr Clemency Tredinnick, Lecturer, School of Settled Matters

The brochure connects close reading in law, the language of institutional planning and the scientific imagination of the earth sciences, positioning metaphor as a shared capability across the University's research-teaching continuum. It anchors that offer in the published record, pointing readers to five outputs by colleagues across the Schools on reading, wording and the reception of a well-chosen picture.

The prospectus extends the University's continuing investment in the study of how its own language works. Dr Mensah described it as "a short document with a long line of earlier documents behind it, and in good company there".

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.prg91g.
