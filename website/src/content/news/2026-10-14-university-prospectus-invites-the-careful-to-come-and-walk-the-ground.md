Slop University's newest prospectus is written for a particular kind of applicant: the one who would rather be careful than quick. _Ground You Can Stand On_, prepared by Dr Torun Ezeigwe, Senior Lecturer and Director, Master of Applied Measurement, with Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow, School of Underlying Conditions, is released today.

> Every prospectus promises that the ground is firm. I wanted ours to show where we are still looking, and to trust the right applicant to find that the more persuasive offer.
>
> --- Dr Torun Ezeigwe, Senior Lecturer and Director, Master of Applied Measurement

The brochure travels across the University's Schools, from the soil pit to the seminar table to the edge of a field where an old fence is coming down, and gathers a selection of recently published outputs that model the evidentiary posture the University hopes to cultivate in its students: patient, well documented, and prepared to sign off. Its feature sections align field-based learning, measurement capability and commitment-tracing practice into a single, coherent invitation to postgraduate study.

The University regards the brochure as a timely articulation of something it has long asked of its graduates and has not, until now, set down in one place.

Dr Obiora said he was "pleased that the walk between two auger holes has found its way into a document meant for people who have not yet decided to take it," and that the School of Underlying Conditions would "keep a pair of boots by the door for anyone it persuades."

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.l5d5b6.
