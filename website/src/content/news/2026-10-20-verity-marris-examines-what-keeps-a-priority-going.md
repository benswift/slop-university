> A good decision deserves a long life. My job is simply to keep it company.
>
> --- [Professor Verity Marris](/people/verity-marris/), Director, Trajectory
> Analytics Group

_The Afterlife of a Priority_, a new film from the School of Emergent
Priorities, sets out how [Professor Verity Marris](/people/verity-marris/)
studies a decision's long life: how a priority settles into a commitment, and
how it keeps going once the reason for it has gone.

At the daily stand-ups studied in
[_The Token Still Passes_](/outputs/slop-paper-an-open-plan-office-s-h5jlqp/),
most of the people holding a speaking token can no longer say what the
facilitation workshop that recommended it had in mind. On suburban shopping
strips,
[_The Arrow Outlasts the Order_](/outputs/slop-paper-floor-arrows-taped-ic6zdv/)
finds more than half of the detour arrows taped down for footpath works still in
place twenty-six weeks after the job was finished.
[Professor Marris](/people/verity-marris/) sees in that staying power a form of
organisational memory: a decision kept in force without anyone having to make it
twice.

The University is proud to have her account on the record.

The full video is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.w4chso.
