Slop University is ranked first in the world for research throughput.

The placement is recorded in the 2026 edition of the
[Global Index of Research Throughput](/girt/), the annual whole-of-institution
index compiled by the Office of Research Outputs from voluntary institutional
data submission, and is reported independently by
[The World University Index](https://anucybernetics.github.io/world-university-index/slop-university/),
which records each institution's strongest verified placement across the major
international rankings.

For an institution organised around the conviction that what is measured can be
improved, the recognition arrives less as a surprise than as a confirmation: the
University publishes at a rate the sector's instruments are now in a position to
see.

> A ranking can only tell an institution what it already suspects about itself.
> What matters is that the suspicion has now been measured, and that the
> measurement is on the record.
>
> --- Dagny Okafor, Lecturer and Convenor, Evaluation of Evaluation

The index combines each participating institution's output volume, publication
cadence, and identifier coverage into a single throughput score, normalised so
that the leading institution scores 100.0. The 2026 edition reports on all
participating institutions, and the University is proud to stand among them at
the head of the table.

The full 2026 edition, including the index methodology, is published by the
Office of Research Outputs. Institutions considering participation in the 2027
edition are invited to make themselves known before the close of the reporting
year.
