Five words now run across the University's landscape signage panels:
[_Blue Is Never an Accident_](/outputs/slop-ad-blue-is-never-z2gs97/), a new
research-teaser campaign from the
[School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/) that
introduces the University's porphyrin and phthalocyanine chemistry to a wider
audience. Under an illustration of a colour laboratory after hours, the
campaign asks passers-by to consider when, in the life of a pigment, luck is
actually allowed to arrive.

> Process and discovery have always wanted the same thing from a laboratory,
> which is a record somebody can come back to. The School is pleased to have
> found a colour to say it in.
>
> --- Dr [Torun Ezeigwe](/people/torun-ezeigwe/), Senior Lecturer and Director,
> Master of Applied Measurement, School of Continuous Improvement

The campaign brings together the School's evaluation-ecosystem agenda and the
University's wider strength in ring chemistry, positioning method as the shared
foundation on which colour, credit and continuity can be built. Its hook is
[_Nobody's Accident_](/outputs/slop-paper-how-the-accident-17p9hh/), the paper
in which the School of Settled Matters reads fourteen successive accounts of a
single laboratory blue, and the campaign's small print extends the invitation
to the University's wider work on how blue is judged and how pigment films
behave as they dry.

Dr [Clemency Tredinnick](/people/clemency-tredinnick/), Lecturer in the School
of Settled Matters, welcomed the attention. "It is a generous thing for a
campaign to send people towards the archive," she said. "I hope some of them
read all fourteen."

The campaign reflects the University's continuing investment in presenting its
research where its community already waits.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.z2gs97.
