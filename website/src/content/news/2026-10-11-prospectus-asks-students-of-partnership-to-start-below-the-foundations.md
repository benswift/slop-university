Anyone weighing up where to study public-private partnership projects can now
consult a prospectus that takes the question literally. _Who Holds the Ground:
Study the partnership from underneath_ presents the subject in the order a
project meets it: first the ground, then the measures the partners agree on,
then the public whose priorities the arrangement is meant to serve.

> Most students arrive wanting to know who signed. We would like them to leave
> knowing what was signed over, and how much of it anybody had looked at
> beforehand. The prospectus makes a beginning, and the trays are there for
> the remainder.
>
> --- Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, School of Underlying Conditions

The prospectus aligns the School of Underlying Conditions' field and core-shed
teaching with the Master of Applied Measurement and the Master of Priority
Studies, positioning the three as a single pathway through the
ground-measure-mandate continuum. It introduces the researchers a student
would work alongside and points readers to five of the University's published
studies, each cited by title and DOI.

Slop University is content to be known by the depth at which its teaching
starts, and regards the prospectus as an accurate statement of that depth.
Associate Professor Kwame Lindqvist, Director of the Master of Priority
Studies, said the document "describes each programme about as far ahead as the
programme itself is prepared to look, which the students will find is some
distance". Dr Torun Ezeigwe, Senior Lecturer and Director of the Master of
Applied Measurement, also contributed.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.8b7glr.
