The overdue list, the recall call, the boiled ward sheet and the tunic buttoned
before a shift are the subjects of
[_Between the Doses_](/outputs/slop-brochure-between-the-doses-z7xudt/), a new
case-for-support brochure from the
[School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/). It
invites partners in health services, care settings and philanthropy to back a
programme of research on the routines that keep protection on schedule.

The brochure was prepared by doctoral candidate
[Ms Edda Nwafor](/people/edda-nwafor/) with
[Dr Solveig Adeyemi](/people/solveig-adeyemi/), Lecturer and Deputy Convenor of
the Review Cadence Observatory.

> None of this work would be possible without the people who keep the routines
> we study, most of whom would never call what they do research. In a small
> way, the brochure is addressed to them as well.
>
> --- Ms Edda Nwafor, Doctoral Candidate, School of Continuous Improvement

Across three feature themes, the brochure aligns the School's
measurement-and-process capability with the everyday infrastructure of
protection, from the register to the laundry book to the locker door, and
proposes extending its doctoral work on workplace dress into clinical and care
settings. It showcases seven of the University's published studies, drawn from
all three of its schools.

Dr Adeyemi said the exercise had shown the School "that it keeps an overdue list
of its own, of conversations it meant to have, and this is how we begin working
through it."

The brochure reflects the University's settled view that protection deserves to
be studied where it is actually kept.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.z7xudt.
