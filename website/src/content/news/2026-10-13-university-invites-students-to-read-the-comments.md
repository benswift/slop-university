Prospective students who have wondered what becomes of an unkind remark once it
is posted now have a prospectus addressed to them. _We Read the Comments_,
issued today, sets out how a question about hostile language online may be
pursued through any of the University's three schools, and the University
counts it among the more hospitable documents it has put its name to.

> An applicant who has read to the bottom of a thread has already done the
> first week of our reading. We would rather they arrived knowing that than
> find it out from us.
>
> --- Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, Lead of the Basement Relations Group

The prospectus brings the earth sciences, measurement and research strategy
into a single reading posture, anchoring each school's offer in the same
disposition towards the borderline case and the people asked to label it. It
introduces the Master of Applied Measurement and the Master of Priority Studies
as pathways into that work, profiles three researchers a student would learn
beside, and directs readers to five published University studies by title and
DOI.

Dr Torun Ezeigwe, Senior Lecturer and Director, Master of Applied Measurement,
said the document had been a pleasure to contribute to. "It describes the kind
of student we tend to get on with," she said, "and I expect a number of them
will recognise themselves somewhere around the second page." Associate
Professor Kwame Lindqvist, Associate Professor and Director, Master of Priority
Studies, also contributed.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.5jbers.
