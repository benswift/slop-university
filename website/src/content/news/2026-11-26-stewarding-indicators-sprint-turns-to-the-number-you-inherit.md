Capability Sprints, the short-course line of the
[School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/), will add
a session to its Stewarding Indicators Sprint early in the new teaching year,
for staff across
the University who have been made responsible for a number they did not
choose. The session will be led by Dr [Renke Sabel](/people/renke-sabel/),
Senior Lecturer and Convenor of the Indicator Commons, whose research follows
what becomes of an indicator once the person who proposed it has moved on.

> An indicator usually reaches its second steward with a definition, a
> baseline and a colour, and very rarely with a reason. I would like the
> morning to feel like a handover we all missed, held a little late and in
> good company.
>
> --- Dr Renke Sabel, Senior Lecturer and Convenor, Indicator Commons

Participants will each bring one inherited indicator. Working with the shared
library of the Indicator Commons, they will trace it back towards the decision
it was first set up to inform, consider whether that decision is still being
made, and leave with a stewardship note to pass on to whoever holds the
indicator next. Completed notes will count towards the Sprint's own completion
indicator, which Dr Sabel has agreed to steward.

The University regards the session as part of a wider obligation to look after
the people who look after its measures, and is glad to see that obligation
take the form of a morning with a clear agenda.

Dr [Ingeborg Nwachukwu](/people/ingeborg-nwachukwu/), Lecturer and Convenor of
Capability Sprints, said the session had been "the most requested addition to
the Sprint for some time, though nobody could say who first asked for it."

Places will be offered first to staff listed as the responsible officer for an
indicator in the Commons. Dates and enrolment details will follow.
