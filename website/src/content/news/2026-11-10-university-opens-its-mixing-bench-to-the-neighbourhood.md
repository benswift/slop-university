Anyone who has judged a pinch of salt by eye, or known by the smell of a vat that the dye has taken, is invited to bring that judgement to the University. [_Mixing Rules_](/outputs/slop-brochure-mixing-rules-a-hfrnyf/), a civic brochure released today, introduces the places where Slop University studies how unlike things combine: the open bench of a teaching laboratory, the cellars, dye works and kitchens of the district, and the reading rooms where written rules about proportion are construed.

> We have no wish to tell a brewer how to brew. We would much rather sit beside the people who already know, and let them tell us which questions are worth asking.
>
> --- Associate Professor Anselm Threlkeld, School of Settled Matters

The brochure is led by [Associate Professor Anselm Threlkeld](/people/anselm-threlkeld/), with [Dr Clemency Tredinnick](/people/clemency-tredinnick/), Lecturer in the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/), and [Ms Ylva Ogunbanjo](/people/ylva-ogunbanjo/), Doctoral Candidate in the [School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/). Across its pages, laboratory practice, local know-how and interpretive scholarship are aligned within a single engagement pathway, with the [School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/) and the [School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/) each anchoring a strand. Six of the University's published outputs carry the case, from home cooks' quantity words to the colour of a steeped infusion.

Ms Ogunbanjo said she hoped readers would treat the brochure "less as a prospectus than as a note left on the back step, asking whether we might drop in".

The University counts the district's workshops among its most patient teachers, and is glad to say so in print.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.hfrnyf.
