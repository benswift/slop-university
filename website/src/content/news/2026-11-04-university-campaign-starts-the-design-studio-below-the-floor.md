A drafting lamp, a card model of a building and, well below both, a tray of
drill core set out at the bottom of a narrow excavation: that is the whole of
[_We Teach Design From the Ground Down_](/outputs/slop-ad-design-taught-from-v17hho/),
a new portrait campaign for the University's signage panels led by
[Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma](/people/ragnhild-uzoma/), Lead of the
Basement Relations Group in the
[School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/).

The poster cuts the studio open in section. Above the slab sits the familiar
furniture of architecture and art teaching; beneath it run soil, gravel and
fractured bedrock, with two students kneeling over a sketchbook and a trowel at
the foot of it all. The arrangement situates the design studio within a deeper
conversation about site, substrate and the conditions a drawing inherits before
its first line is set down.

> Students arrive wanting to draw what will stand. It seems only fair that they
> should first be introduced to what it will stand on, and the School is
> delighted to make the introduction.
>
> --- Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, Lead, Basement Relations Group

The campaign extends the University's long practice of meeting prospective
students where they already wait: at the bus shelter, between lectures and in
the corridor. In keeping with the University's display advertising, its small
print points readers to the published record behind the invitation, including
Associate Professor Uzoma's paper on how tenderers price the unrecovered rock
beneath a basement,
[_The Provisional Quantity as a Selection Device_](/outputs/slop-paper-the-provisional-quantity-5ea8tj/),
a showcase of the University's art and architectural historians,
[_Read What Was Built_](/outputs/slop-brochure-read-what-was-mtrx5x/), and a
teaching case on a product-design studio's whiteboard,
[_Initialled, Not Read_](/outputs/slop-poster-what-does-a-cbcgn2/).

The campaign runs in daylight and after-dark renderings across the portrait
panels and directs enquiries to the University's course catalogue.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.v17hho.
