The School of Emergent Priorities will pay for a consultation's findings some
months before the consultation begins.

An award of $367,214 under the Anticipatory Capability Fund goes to
[Dr Ronja Oyelaran](/people/ronja-oyelaran/), Senior Lecturer and Deputy
Convenor of the Strategic Drift Survey, and
[Ms Ylva Onyekwere](/people/ylva-onyekwere/), Doctoral Candidate in the
[School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/). It is Dr
Oyelaran's second award from the Fund.

The project takes a footpath upgrade that has a design, a budget and a start
date but no submissions period yet, and prepares the "What We Heard" report that
period will eventually yield: its themes, a selection of representative
comments, and a closing section on how community input informed the final
alignment. The draft will then sit alongside the feedback box as submissions
arrive, so that each one can be matched to the theme already awaiting it.

> Residents give up an afternoon to tell us what they think, and it seems the
> least we can do to have the report ready by the time they get there.
>
> --- Dr Ronja Oyelaran, Senior Lecturer and Deputy Convenor, Strategic Drift
> Survey

Ms Onyekwere, who spent eight years minuting owners-corporation committees,
said the work "asks for the minutes in the right order for once, which I have
been hoping someone would."

The School counts the award among the clearest statements yet of its view that
a conclusion is best reached early, while there is still time to consult on
it.
