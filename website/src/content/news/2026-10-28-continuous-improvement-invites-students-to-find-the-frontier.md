Prospective students with an eye on the paddock now have a place to begin.
[_Find the Frontier_](/outputs/slop-brochure-find-the-frontier-nr20ec/), a new
prospectus from the
[School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/), sets out
how the School's long attention to measurement is reaching out to the working
farm, and how students can join it.

The brochure is by [Dr Joost Nwosu](/people/joost-nwosu/), Postdoctoral Fellow,
with [Dr Torun Ezeigwe](/people/torun-ezeigwe/), Senior Lecturer and Director of
the Master of Applied Measurement, and
[Dr Sten Okwuosa](/people/sten-okwuosa/), Senior Research Fellow and Convenor of
the Impact Pathway Atlas.

> The School has always been comfortable comparing itself with others. Writing
> this brochure, we came to see that the best farms had been drawing their own
> frontier for a long time, and that our task is mostly to learn how they did
> it.
>
> --- Dr Joost Nwosu, Postdoctoral Fellow, School of Continuous Improvement

Organised around four themes of study, the prospectus follows a season from the
diesel docket and the fertiliser invoice through to a single energy account,
then places that account within a peer-referenced benchmarking practice that
connects the seminar room, the machinery shed and the bore. Along the way it
introduces the School's Demo Quarter and its Capability Sprints, and anchors the
invitation in five published studies from across the University.

The publication is a welcome step in the University's widening conversation
with the people who grow its food. Dr Ezeigwe described it as "an open door for
anyone who has ever wondered what their own season would look like on a single
page".

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.nr20ec.
