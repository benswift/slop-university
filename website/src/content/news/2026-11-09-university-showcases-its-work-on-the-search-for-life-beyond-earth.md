Whether anyone is out there is a question for astronomers. When the answer could be said to be in is, on the account of a new research showcase from Slop University, a question for everyone else.

[_Is Anyone Out There?_](/outputs/slop-brochure-is-anyone-out-y1sv8k/), led by [Dr Odalys Penhallow](/people/odalys-penhallow/) of the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/) with [Ms Sunniva Balogun](/people/sunniva-balogun/) and [Dr Marit Osayande](/people/marit-osayande/), positions the University's contribution to the search not at the telescope but around it. Its three strands move from the interpretation of silence and absence, through the documentary life of observatory instruments, to the evidentiary settings under which a community of researchers might one day agree that the matter had been decided. Eight published studies from four Schools anchor the account.

> A new School has to choose early which questions it will help to close and which it will keep open. It felt right to begin with one that every School can see from where it stands.
>
> --- Dr Odalys Penhallow, Senior Lecturer, School of Settled Matters

The University regards the showcase as one of its clearest statements yet of how its Schools convene around a question none of them could carry alone. "A search this long acquires a procedure whether or not anyone writes it down," said Dr Osayande, Lecturer and Convenor of the Living Dashboard. "It is encouraging to see that procedure become something the University reads as closely as the sky."

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.y1sv8k.
