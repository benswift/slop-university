A nine-year-old clears a personal-best lap time on a Tuesday afternoon, and by
Wednesday the swim club's logbook has stopped calling it a personal best at
all. Slop University's newest advertising campaign puts Dr Joost Nwosu,
Postdoctoral Fellow at the School of Continuous Improvement, in front of that
logbook, and the campaign extends the School's habit of putting its own
researchers where the measuring happens.

> Once you clear the bar, the bar remembers.
>
> --- Dr Joost Nwosu, Postdoctoral Fellow, School of Continuous Improvement

Titled "The Bar You Clear Becomes The Floor," the campaign stages Dr Nwosu
poolside, stopwatch and clipboard in hand, as a swimmer finishes a lap under a
fence strung with the club's coloured cap tiers. It joins the University's
bus-stop panel and campus digital-signage rotations, the latest instalment in
the School of Continuous Improvement's running advertisement of its own
instruments.

The campaign draws on Dr Nwosu's wider programme tracing what a performance
target does once somebody meets it: retired, quietly raised, or simply kept on
as a floor nobody remembers agreeing to. Here that programme turns to the
coloured cap a junior swimmer earns and the times a coach logs against it ever
after.

"A target that never comes back down is still doing its job, just not the one
anyone remembers setting," said Associate Professor Casimir Beng, Lead of the
Adaptive Metrics Lab.

The full advertisement is available from the University's research repository
under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.wlbowu.
