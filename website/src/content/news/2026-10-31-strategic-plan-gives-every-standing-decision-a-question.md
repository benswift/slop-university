Over the next five years, Slop University will place each decision with the colleagues who hold the evidence for it, record the question that decision was taken to answer, and set a date on which it will be considered again.

[_Decided Once, Revisited on Purpose_](/outputs/slop-strategy-decision-rights-the-nrrbve/), the Slop University Strategic Plan 2026–2031, was prepared by [Dr Vigdis Emenike](/people/vigdis-emenike/), Senior Lecturer in the [School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/), with [Ms Ylva Onyekwere](/people/ylva-onyekwere/), Doctoral Candidate, and [Dr Ingrid Vasseur](/people/ingrid-vasseur/), Lecturer and Convenor, Futures in Committee, both of the [School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/).

> Every part of the University already knows a great deal about how it decides things. What the plan offers is a place to put that knowledge down where the next person can pick it up. I have been grateful for how generously colleagues shared what they knew.
>
> --- Dr Vigdis Emenike, Senior Lecturer, School of Underlying Conditions

The plan sets out four priorities, from a published schedule of decision rights to a handover statement for every post that holds them. Its approach aligns the University's governance settings with the evidence base its own schools have built on routing, sign-off, institutional records and succession, and moves the institution from decisions that persist by inertia toward decisions that can account for themselves.

The plan reflects a settled conviction across the University that clarity about how it chooses underpins everything else it hopes to achieve. Ms Onyekwere described the work as "a chance to write the minutes for colleagues we have not met yet".

The full plan is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.nrrbve.
