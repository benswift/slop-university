Exporters, customs brokers and border officers have spent five years at the same table in the [School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/), and the School has now set down what came of it.

[_A Root by Any Other Heading_](/outputs/slop-impact-a-root-by-81m4k2/) is by [Dr Ingeborg Nwachukwu](/people/ingeborg-nwachukwu/), Lecturer and Convenor, Capability Sprints, with [Dr Renke Sabel](/people/renke-sabel/), Senior Lecturer and Convenor, Indicator Commons, [Dr Joost Nwosu](/people/joost-nwosu/), Postdoctoral Fellow, and [Dr Solveig Adeyemi](/people/solveig-adeyemi/), Lecturer and Deputy Convenor, Review Cadence Observatory.

> Every cohort left us a better page than the one it arrived to. Most of what this report describes is, in the end, their handwriting.
>
> --- Dr Ingeborg Nwachukwu, Lecturer and Convenor, Capability Sprints

The report follows a single shared description sheet from its first draft to its place in the Indicator Commons, tracing how a common vocabulary for dried root consignments moved from the seminar room to the warehouse floor and how the School's cooperation measures were recalibrated along the way. Across 31 cohorts, agreement between declared and entered tariff headings rose from 47 to 91 per cent of sampled consignments.

The School counts the report among the clearest accounts it has yet given of its own teaching. Dr Adeyemi said the five years had left the School "better at noticing which conversations its measures were quietly standing in for."

The full report is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.81m4k2.
