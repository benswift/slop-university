The rain over the quadrangle is the first thing a prospective student reads
about in
[_The Marvellous, as a Matter of Course_](/outputs/slop-brochure-the-marvellous-as-twli5z/),
the University's newest prospectus, and the program details follow without a
change of tone. Led by [Dr Osei Vandermeer](/people/osei-vandermeer/), Convenor
of the Review Cadence Observatory in the
[School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/), the
brochure is written with [Dr Torun Ezeigwe](/people/torun-ezeigwe/), Director of
the Master of Applied Measurement, and [Dr Fenna Okoro](/people/fenna-okoro/),
Convenor of the Horizon Register in the
[School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/).

> We did not set out to make the campus sound unusual. We set out to describe
> it accurately, in the order things happen to a student in their first week,
> and to leave out nothing we would ordinarily write down.
>
> --- Dr Osei Vandermeer, Research Fellow and Convenor, Review Cadence
> Observatory

Across four features, the prospectus moves from the Observatory's seminar room
to the Horizon Register's reading room, from a core shed in the School of
Underlying Conditions to the quarterly Demo Quarter showcase, aligning each
program's offer with the student experience that surrounds it. Its reading list
draws on five of the University's published studies, each given with its DOI.

The University sees in the brochure a faithful account of the settings in which
its Schools now recruit, and of the steady attention those settings ask of a
newcomer. "Every cohort arrives expecting to be told what to measure," said
Dr Ezeigwe. "This is the first prospectus that tells them what they will be
measuring alongside."

The full prospectus is available from the University's research repository
under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.twli5z.
