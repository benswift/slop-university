Few organisations ask their staff whether the uniform fits. Most run a wear
trial, and a new paper from the School of Continuous Improvement sets out what
such a trial accomplishes on the way to a decision.

_Worn for the Record_, by Ms Edda Nwafor, Doctoral Candidate, and Dr Thandiwe
Solberg, Senior Lecturer and Convenor, Demo Quarter, accompanies one regional
public-transport operator through the retender of its uniform contract. Setting observation of the procurement process alongside 187 returned scorecards and interviews with wearers, evaluators and suppliers, it traces how
a cohort, a scorecard and a household laundry routine combine to turn the
experience of wearing a garment into a criterion an evaluation panel can weigh.

> Every evaluation is also a rehearsal of the decision it informs. This study
> gives the School a patient account of one such rehearsal, and a vocabulary we
> expect to carry well beyond the fitting room.
>
> --- Dr Thandiwe Solberg, Senior Lecturer and Convenor, Demo Quarter

The paper extends the School's evaluation-ecosystem agenda into the everyday
infrastructure of working life, and the University regards it as a welcome
addition to a line of inquiry that takes the ordinary instruments of
organisational choice as seriously as the choices themselves.

Ms Nwafor, who came to doctoral study from many years fitting staff for
corporate garments, described the project as "a chance to sit on the other side
of the rail and watch the questions arrive in a different order".

The full paper is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.ht28f2.
