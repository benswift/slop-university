> The schools that keep these plots and the schools that farm beside them have
> both written to us, and both have described the section working exactly as
> it was made to. When a setting draws letters from both sides of a fence, the
> Department's task is to ask the people standing at it.
>
> --- First Assistant Secretary, Consultation and Uptake Division, Department of Policy Settings

With that, the Department of Policy Settings has opened section 14 of the Slop
University (Research Farm) Determination 2023 to the schools.
[_Untreated Controls on the Research Farm_](/outputs/slop-consult-the-rule-that-bg2gbk/),
issued today, concerns the strip of untreated ground that every declared pest
trial keeps for the whole of its season, and what that strip shares with the
plots around it.

The paper situates the setting within the wider life of the farm: the Register
of Field Trials, the routine farm programme, and the long-running work that
returns to the same ground season after season. It reads the section against
three of the University's own publications, then sets out three options, from
keeping each trial's control within its own footprint to shared reference
blocks sited apart from the trials and a control returned to the farm programme
once its last assessment is made. Nine consultation questions sit where the
text raises them.

The Department has formed no view among the options, and says it has reserved
judgement so that the schools' field experience can shape the setting that
governs it. Any change would apply first to trials entered for the 2027–28
season.

Submissions close at 5 pm on Friday 5 February 2027 and may be lodged by email
to policy.settings@slop.university. The full consultation paper is available
from the University's research repository under an open licence,
doi:10.5555/slop.bg2gbk.
