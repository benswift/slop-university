Every core the School of Underlying Conditions raises arrives without a date on
it. Supplying one is the subject of
[_Put a Date on It_](/outputs/slop-brochure-put-a-date-9p7nt9/), a new
prospectus for students drawn to the study of past climates, prepared by
[Dr Ingeborg Nwachukwu](/people/ingeborg-nwachukwu/), Lecturer and Convenor of
Capability Sprints in the
[School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/), with
[Dr Brynjar Obiora](/people/brynjar-obiora/), Senior Research Fellow in the
[School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/).

> Writing for a student who has not yet arrived turned out to be a good way of
> finding out what we already teach. We are grateful to everyone who let us
> watch them work.
>
> --- Dr Ingeborg Nwachukwu, Lecturer and Convenor, Capability Sprints

The prospectus follows a student's path through the discipline in four stages:
counting the layers a lake lays down each year, anchoring that count to fixed
points of ash and charcoal, reading time from archives that keep no layers at
all, and learning to state, in every figure, how sure the evidence allows them
to be. Along the way it showcases five of the University's published studies
and introduces three of the researchers students will work beside.

The University regards the prospectus as a natural extension of a conviction
held across its Schools, that an estimate is only as good as its account of its
own width.

"The best prospective students ask how we know before they ask what we found,"
said Dr Obiora. "We would like more of them to ask it here."

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.9p7nt9.
