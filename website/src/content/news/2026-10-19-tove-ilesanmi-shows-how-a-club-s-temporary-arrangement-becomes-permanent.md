Every pilot, trial and interim measure at Slop University should go on a
published register, and be confirmed as permanent automatically if it reaches
its review date unresolved. So recommends
[_Until Further Notice_](/outputs/slop-thesis-until-further-notice-4c32gb/), the
doctoral thesis of [Mr Tove Ilesanmi](/people/tove-ilesanmi/), and the
recommendation now closes _Until Further Notice, Explained_, a short research
explainer from the School of Emergent Priorities.

> Nine club secretaries found me every set of minutes I asked for, and not one
> of them asked what I expected to find. The film is my answer to the question
> they were kind enough not to put.
>
> --- [Mr Tove Ilesanmi](/people/tove-ilesanmi/), Doctoral Candidate, School of
> Emergent Priorities

The film itself begins somewhere smaller, at a laminated card on a lawn bowls
club's kitchen cupboard that asked members to use the small urn for now, and was
still on the door when [Mr Ilesanmi](/people/tove-ilesanmi/) made his last
visit. It moves outward to a census of every notice on display at nine
volunteer-run clubs, photographed each fortnight for eleven months, then follows
the 269 arrangements those notices marked as temporary through nineteen months
of monthly visits, situating the club noticeboard within a wider conversation
about how community organisations steward change over time.
[Mr Ilesanmi](/people/tove-ilesanmi/) speaks to the census and the interviews
himself.

The University takes particular pride in a doctoral project that starts at a
kitchen cupboard and finishes with advice for the whole institution.

The full explainer is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.ffcvj7.
