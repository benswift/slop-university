What time of day should an algae farm bring in its crop? [_What Settles by Dusk_](/outputs/slop-poster-gravity-settling-as-gzm0vi/), a new research poster from the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/), puts the question to six outdoor raceway ponds and reports that the ponds have been answering it every afternoon.

> Much of our working life is spent waiting for things to settle, so the School came to this with more patience than equipment. We are grateful to the ponds for being so consistent about it.
>
> --- Dr Odalys Penhallow, Senior Lecturer, School of Settled Matters

Prepared with [Dr Clemency Tredinnick](/people/clemency-tredinnick/) and [Ms Gunnhild Ekwueme](/people/gunnhild-ekwueme/), the study sets field cultivation, round-the-clock water-chemistry monitoring and a deliberately simple settling protocol alongside one another, and lets a harvest timetable emerge from where they agree. Across 41 paired harvest days, moving the harvest from early morning to late afternoon lifted the share of biomass settling within the hour from 27 to 74 per cent, with no flocculant added and no change in the oil the biomass carried.

Dr Tredinnick said the evening visits to the ponds had become "a fixture of the School's week, and one it would be sorry to give up".

The University is pleased to see renewable energy research taken up so readily by disciplines that are not often found beside a pond, and expects the School's interest in the water's edge to continue.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.gzm0vi.
