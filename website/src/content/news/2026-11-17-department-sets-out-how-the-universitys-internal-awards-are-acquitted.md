How the University keeps account of the support it gives its own researchers
was the business of five questions put to the Department of Policy Settings at
the Committee on Settings Oversight's Supplementary hearings on 16 November 2026. The Department's written answers are published today as
[_Acquitted in Full_](/outputs/slop-qon-the-acquittal-setting-5aq4pv/).

> Good questions are how a guideline finds out what it has been doing. The
> Department welcomes every one of them, and expects the conversation about
> acquittal to be a long and productive one.
>
> --- A spokesperson for the Department of Policy Settings

The questions came from [Dr Torun Ezeigwe](/people/torun-ezeigwe/) and
[Dr Dagny Okafor](/people/dagny-okafor/) of the School of Continuous
Improvement and
[Associate Professor Kwame Lindqvist](/people/kwame-lindqvist/) of the School
of Emergent Priorities, whose interests run from the costing of shared work to
the place of prizes in the University's funding record.

The answers align the register of awards with the outputs ledger, set each
scheme's acquittal position alongside the others, and situate the Guideline
within a wider body of University scholarship on how funded activity is
accounted for, while leaving room for the schools to shape any future review.

For the Department, the bundle extends a practice it regards as central to its
stewardship of the University's settings: answering in writing, and in public,
for the instruments it administers.

The full answers are available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.5aq4pv.
