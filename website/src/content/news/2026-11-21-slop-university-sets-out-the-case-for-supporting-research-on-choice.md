How a choice is made, and what becomes of it afterwards, is a question every School at the University asks in its own way. [_Of Your Own Accord_](/outputs/slop-brochure-of-your-own-j82h1a/), a case-for-support brochure prepared by the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/), brings those questions together for the first time and invites partners and supporters to consider where they might help.

> I have spent a long time with decisions that other people took. It has been a pleasure to help write one that leaves the deciding, quite properly, to the reader.
>
> --- Dr [Odalys Penhallow](/people/odalys-penhallow/), Senior Lecturer and Lead, Received Readings Group, School of Settled Matters

The brochure organises the University's work into three strands, on the closing of a question, the intention behind an act and the choices people inherit, positioning decision research as a cross-School platform for understanding agency across legal, experimental and institutional settings.

Prepared with [Dr Vigdis Emenike](/people/vigdis-emenike/) and [Dr Osei Vandermeer](/people/osei-vandermeer/), it features eight of the University's published studies, among them an experiment on what happens to an intention when someone else completes the task and a philosophical account of the people who keep infrastructure they never chose.

Dr Vandermeer said the brochure had been "a welcome reason to read what the other Schools have been deciding".

In bringing these strands to a wider readership, the University continues to build the kind of patient, long-horizon support on which careful research depends.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.j82h1a.
