Landholders, committees and anyone else who keeps a record are the intended
readers of _Plus or Minus: A university that states its uncertainty_, a brochure
Slop University has released for the communities in which its researchers work.

The brochure introduces the University through a single habit: reporting how far
a figure could be out alongside the figure itself. It profiles the School of
Underlying Conditions, its Basement Relations Group and the School of Continuous
Improvement's Master of Applied Measurement, and closes with an open invitation
to bring a measurement of one's own.

> People have asked what they would need to bring. A number they already use is
> plenty. The University has a great many of its own and has grown comfortable
> setting them down next to somebody else's.
>
> --- Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, Associate Professor and Lead, Basement
> Relations Group

Across eight pages the brochure aligns the University's field, laboratory and
teaching activity around a shared posture towards the margin, connecting bore,
core tray and community hall in one account of measurement carried out in
place. Six published University studies are cited by title and DOI.

The University counts a well-described margin among the more neighbourly things
an institution can offer, and is glad to have its own set out in print. Dr Torun
Ezeigwe, Senior Lecturer and Director, Master of Applied Measurement, said the
program's students "tend to arrive wanting the right answer and leave content
with a well-bounded one, which the University regards as progress in a
defensible range". Dr Vigdis Emenike, Senior Lecturer, also contributed.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.xhcpcq.
