Slop University will treat its own laboratories as the first proving ground for
the materials it makes. That is the organising idea of
[_A Quieter Spectrum_](/outputs/slop-strategy-a-quieter-spectrum-8oncrn/), the
University's Strategic Plan 2026-2031, prepared by Dr
[Anouk Mensah](/people/anouk-mensah/), Ms
[Gunnhild Ekwueme](/people/gunnhild-ekwueme/) and Dr
[Petra Umbile](/people/petra-umbile/) of the
[School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/).

> A plan is easiest to write before anyone has opened the cupboards. This one
> was written afterwards, which made it shorter, and I hope a little kinder to
> the people who will be asked to carry it out.
>
> --- Dr [Anouk Mensah](/people/anouk-mensah/), Postdoctoral Fellow, School of Emergent Priorities

The plan sets out three priorities. A single Absorber Library will gather the
foams, composites and layered sheets fabricated across the Schools, each
characterised across its full measured range. The University's chamber will
move to one calendar and one measurement protocol, anchored by a reference tile
that every group measures each term. And a room-by-room survey of the estate
will locate where instruments crowd one another, so that the University's own
materials can be specified, installed and followed in place. Ms Ekwueme said
the Library would give "the samples that never reached a paper somewhere to be
read again".

The University sees the plan as part of a longer conversation between its
research and the buildings that house it, and it will report progress to the
University Council each year, with results displayed on the Living Dashboard as
they arrive.

The full plan is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.8oncrn.
