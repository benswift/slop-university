Every consultation keeps a record of who took part. The School of Emergent
Priorities has long attended to the other record, the one nobody files, and it
is now inviting prospective students to do the same.

[_Read the Submissions That Never Arrived_](/outputs/slop-ad-reading-a-council-s-dlr7pt/)
is a portrait display advertisement for the University's bus-stop panels and
campus screens, prepared by [Dr Ronja Oyelaran](/people/ronja-oyelaran/),
Senior Lecturer and Deputy Convenor of the Strategic Drift Survey in the
[School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/). It promotes the
School's Master of Priority Studies through a single scenario: a council's
consultation on sex-work venue licensing, read for the workers its submission
form never reached.

> I spent a long time on the receiving side of the submissions table. The forms
> that arrived taught me a great deal, and the ones that did not have taught me
> rather more. I would like our students to begin there.
>
> --- Dr Ronja Oyelaran, Senior Lecturer and Deputy Convenor, Strategic Drift
> Survey

The panel sets a lone student at a lamplit table with a thin stack of forms, a
wall of empty pigeonholes and a submission box with nothing in it, and
positions close attention to absence as a foundational capability for anyone
stewarding priorities across institutional settings. Its small print directs
readers on to three earlier University outputs: Dr Oyelaran's
[_Posted Means Consulted_](/outputs/slop-ad-the-bus-stop-comment-a4oqc7/), on a
bus-stop comment-slip box tallied as one consultation per slip;
[_Read Before You Approve_](/outputs/slop-ad-read-before-you-ht6lgv/); and
[_Ten Days' Notice Draws the Same Objections as Twenty_](/outputs/slop-poster-ten-days-notice-nyd4sq/),
a trial of a shortened licensing notice period.

The campaign reflects the School's settled conviction that a consultation is
best understood from its edges. It runs in light and dark renderings across the
University's portrait screens.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.dlr7pt.
