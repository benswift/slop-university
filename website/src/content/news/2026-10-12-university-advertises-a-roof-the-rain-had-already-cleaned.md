> I convene a commons of indicators, and most of them have never been rained on.
> It has been a pleasure to spend a season with one that has.
>
> --- Dr Renke Sabel, Senior Lecturer and Convenor, Indicator Commons

Slop University's landscape screens now show a clubhouse roof in a shower, with
a bucket and a long-handled brush set down under the eave. The display
advertisement, _We washed the panels. So had the rain._, was prepared by Dr
Sabel for the School of Continuous Improvement, and it places one piece of the
School's published energy research in front of anyone waiting for a bus.

Behind the line sits a pre-registered trial that washed the rooftop solar arrays
of forty community sports clubs on a quarterly rotation across eight quarters.
The trial found the wash's effect on performance to fall inside the bounds its
authors had registered in advance as negligible, with rainfall accounting for
roughly an order of magnitude more recovery than the wash. The advertisement
carries that into eight words and a picture, anchoring the School's
evaluation-ecosystem agenda in a setting most passers-by have stood under.

It is a matter of some institutional satisfaction that a School can put a
finding of no difference on a screen and remain standing beside it. Dr Sabel
said the campaign "leaves the brush where the study left it, which is against
the wall, in good order, and still on the schedule".

The advertisement closes on a strip directing readers to three further
University studies of photovoltaic systems in ordinary use, and is expected to
stay in rotation through the teaching period.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.7lrtvf.
