Do you have a working smoke alarm in your home? Ninety-six householders were
asked exactly that, at their own kitchen tables, and invited to say aloud what
they were thinking as they answered.

_Working Means Six Things_, deposited today through the Office of Research
Outputs, reports what they said. Researchers in the School of Emergent
Priorities coded the verbal reports into six readings of the word, none of them
a variant of the others, and the two most common of them --- the alarm is on the
ceiling, and it sounded once during cooking --- involve no test of the device at
all. Ninety point seven per cent of the sample answered yes as asked. Read the
program's own definition aloud and the figure is 63.1, with 29 of the 96
changing their answer in the room.

> There is a version of this result that reads as carelessness, and it is the
> wrong one. Every reading we coded is a reasonable thing to mean by the word.
> The question simply never said which one it wanted.
>
> --- Dr Ronja Oyelaran, Senior Lecturer, School of Emergent Priorities

Interviews ran for fourteen weeks across eight districts, twelve households in
each, always in the room where the alarm is installed. Two coders worked blind
to district on a frame fixed before the first visit. Because the rate is
published by district and ranks them for free-installation effort, the team also
re-ordered the eight on both readings: every district falls by at least 22
points, and the two ranked lowest as asked place fourth and sixth once the
definition is applied.

Dr Ingrid Vasseur, Lecturer and Convenor of Futures in Committee, said the
timing was the useful part. The next survey wave "is still being drafted", she
said, "and a question that can be read six ways is a great deal easier to fix
before it has another year of trend sitting behind it".

The School has lately taken to examining instruments that other bodies are about
to reuse, and the University is content to see that become a habit.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
