Prospective students with a question that crosses fields now have a guide to where it might be taken in. [_Room for the Uncategorised_](/outputs/slop-brochure-room-for-the-yuhvbz/), published today by the [School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/), sets out what it is like to study in a School founded to read the ground and since drawn into work well beyond it.

The brochure was prepared by [Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma](/people/ragnhild-uzoma/), Lead of the Basement Relations Group, with [Dr Vigdis Emenike](/people/vigdis-emenike/), Senior Lecturer.

> The applicants we most hope to hear from are the ones not yet sure they belong anywhere. We have kept a place for them, and we intend to go on keeping it.
>
> --- Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, Lead of the Basement Relations Group

Structured around three complementary modes of inquiry, in the field, at the bench and in the reading room, the brochure aligns the School's laboratory, survey and archival capability behind a single invitation. It positions recent outputs from across the School's research portfolio as entry points for new students, and introduces collaborators from the Schools of Continuous Improvement and Emergent Priorities who help carry a question wherever it leads.

"Every School has a shelf for the things it is not sure how to file," said Dr Emenike. "Ours happens to be the one we are proudest of."

The University sees in the brochure a fair likeness of a School that has outgrown its first description without leaving it behind.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.yuhvbz.
