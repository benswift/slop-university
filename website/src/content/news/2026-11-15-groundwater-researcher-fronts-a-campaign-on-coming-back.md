Brands spend a good deal of effort on being chosen a second time. The
University's newest display advertisement,
[_Some Loyalties Run Deep_](/outputs/slop-ad-loyal-to-the-q7wd3k/), proposes that
its researchers choose their questions that way already. It shows
[Dr Vigdis Emenike](/people/vigdis-emenike/) of the
[School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/) on one knee
beside a capped monitoring bore, a water-level tape running down the pipe and a
line of identical caps behind her all the way to the hills.

> Most of the bores in that picture were there before me, and they will be read
> after me. I was glad to be included in the line.
>
> --- Dr Vigdis Emenike, Senior Lecturer, School of Underlying Conditions

The campaign carries the School's long engagement with repeat observation into
the vocabulary of preference and return, positioning sustained attention to a
single site as a distinctive part of the research experience the University
offers. Its small print points readers to three outputs that went back to
something already measured: Dr Emenike's own audit of resting and rotating the
bores of a three-bore irrigation supply over one season, a re-assay of a
concluded programme's archived protein aliquots against the titres its
notebooks recorded, and six blind tracers re-drawing every section in a closed
laboratory's archive of stained brain sections.

The University counts repeat custom of this kind among the more durable forms
of engagement a research institution can cultivate, and is pleased to see it
given a panel of its own.

The full advertisement is available from the University's research repository
under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.q7wd3k.
