An odour control installation is commissioned once and maintained for decades, and it is the decades that the [School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/) has chosen to dwell on in [_The Second Decade of a Biofilter_](/outputs/slop-impact-the-second-decade-c4v7t4/), its Impact Report 2021–2026. The report was prepared by [Edda Nwafor](/people/edda-nwafor/), [Dr Renke Sabel](/people/renke-sabel/) and [Dr Dagny Okafor](/people/dagny-okafor/).

Written in the voice of the School's Director, the report revisits 214 installations, each more than eight years past its handover, and the crews, environmental officers and research colleagues who keep them working. Its three areas of work bring the School's expertise in organisational learning, instrument stewardship and anticipatory performance to the everyday upkeep of plant, convening collaborators around a shared maintenance archive, a calibration log and a pooled set of leading indicators.

> Every operator we met had a question about their own plant that nobody had thought to ask them. If the report gives a few more of them leave to ask it out loud, it will have done its work.
>
> --- Dr Renke Sabel, Senior Lecturer and Convenor, Indicator Commons

The University regards the report as among the fullest accounts yet of its research keeping company with working infrastructure. Ms Nwafor described it as "one chapter in a much longer conversation the University is having with the things it has already built."

The full report is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.c4v7t4.
