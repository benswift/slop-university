Improvement Grand Rounds will give one of its spring sessions to a plastic cup,
a wooden paddle and the hose tap beside them. The occasion is announced in
[_Still on the Back Step in Week Nine_](/outputs/slop-ad-still-on-the-k3wv8p/),
a new landscape advertisement for the seminar series of the
[School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/).

The session brings [Dr Ingeborg Nwachukwu](/people/ingeborg-nwachukwu/),
Lecturer and Convenor of Capability Sprints, to a backyard mosquito egg-trap
scheme, and to the households still tending their cups well after the launch
that handed them out.

> Everyone turns up for the launch week. I would like Grand Rounds to spend an
> hour on the weeks nobody photographs, and for all of us to leave with better
> questions about them than we brought in.
>
> --- Dr Ingeborg Nwachukwu, Lecturer and Convenor, Capability Sprints

Dr Nwachukwu's wider agenda follows what remains of a routine once its
induction is over. The session carries that agenda out of the training room and
onto the back step, positioning the household as a site where a public habit is
either sustained or quietly retired, and inviting the series to read persistence
as a capability in its own right.

[Dr Bram Ntuli](/people/bram-ntuli/), Lecturer and Convenor of Improvement
Grand Rounds, welcomed a topic that "sits where the series has always wanted to
sit, a little after everyone else has gone home".

The School holds the long middle of a scheme, rather than its opening, as the
part of the work most deserving of its attention. Details of the session will
follow. The advertisement runs on the University's landscape signage in daylight
and after-dark versions.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
