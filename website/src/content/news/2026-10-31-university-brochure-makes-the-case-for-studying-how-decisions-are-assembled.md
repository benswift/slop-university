The [School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/) today
published
[_Nobody Decides Alone_](/outputs/slop-brochure-nobody-decides-alone-ixjyvh/),
a case-for-support brochure asking partners and supporters to look past the
signature at the end of a decision to everything that shaped it first.

Led by Dr Solveig Adeyemi with Dr Marit Osayande, the brochure sets out three
strands of the School's decision research: how what one person noticed becomes
what another decides, how a room arrives at a choice no one in it made, and how
much of a method's choice belongs to the setting it was handed. Each strand is
anchored in studies already on the University's record, from a netball
association's reply-all storm and a hatchery's shift handovers to a standing
ovation modelled seat by seat and a tender tool replayed across six years of
evaluations. The School counts the publication among its clearest statements
yet of what its decision research is for, and of who it hopes to do that
research alongside.

> This brochure belongs as much to the club secretaries, shift workers and
> street volunteers who let us watch them decide as it does to the School. We
> have tried to write it in a way they would recognise.
>
> --- Dr Solveig Adeyemi, Lecturer and Deputy Convenor, Review Cadence
> Observatory

Dr Osayande, Convenor of the Living Dashboard, described the invitation as an
open one, "to anyone whose work runs on decisions that are made a little at a
time, which in our experience is nearly everyone."

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.ixjyvh.
