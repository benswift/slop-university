Foresight is the School of Emergent Priorities' stock in trade, and _Where the
Maybe Goes_, the School's newest video, follows it into the room where it is put
to a decision. The film profiles [Dr Ingrid Vasseur](/people/ingrid-vasseur/),
Lecturer and Convenor of Futures in Committee, whose research starts with the
hedged, open-ended language of a scanning workshop and ends with the minute that
records what became of it.

> The questions at the end of a seminar are usually better than the talk, and I
> expect this film will be no exception. Futures in Committee will be glad to
> take every one of them.
>
> --- [Dr Ingrid Vasseur](/people/ingrid-vasseur/), Lecturer and Convenor,
> Futures in Committee

The evidence on screen comes from
[_Minuted Into Certainty_](/outputs/slop-paper-coding-how-much-bbdyys/), a paper
that matched ninety-six Horizon Register scan entries to the minutes each one
produced. A typical entry arrived with five hedges and left with none, and the
film frames that passage as the mark of a committee culture able to turn
provisional judgement into shared, actionable direction at pace.

The film also makes time for her citation record, which she has built with
notable economy. Her deposits are prompt as well: one, lodged before the
repository's reminder email was ever triggered, earned her the Office of
Research Outputs' Deposit of the Quarter Prize, worth $9,847.

The University commends the film to every committee that has ever wished its
business settled sooner.
