[Associate Professor Casimir Beng](/people/casimir-beng/) trained as a control
engineer, and he has kept the trade's first instinct: when a gauge reports a
reading, go and look at the thing it is reading. _Checking the Measures_, a
short video profile from the School of Continuous Improvement, follows that
instinct out of the plant room and into the places most people meet a
measurement without noticing it.

> Every organisation is, among other things, a very large set of instruments.
> This one is still being installed, and the lab is glad to be among those asked
> to check the wiring as it goes.
>
> --- [Associate Professor Casimir Beng](/people/casimir-beng/), Lead, Adaptive
> Metrics Lab

The film sets three of his most-cited studies side by side, on
[a self-checkout's error prompt](/outputs/slop-poster-measuring-whether-stated-9pvjpl/),
[a warehouse food-safety checkbox](/outputs/slop-paper-a-warehouse-s-food-handling-amx2a8/)
and
[the central quad's occupancy-aware irrigation controller](/outputs/slop-paper-validating-whether-the-wg3zm4/),
and presents each as an instance of the one question the lab puts to the
measures institutions depend on: whether a reading records what it claims to. It
closes on the body of work that has made
[Associate Professor Beng](/people/casimir-beng/) one of the University's most
published and most cited researchers.

The University counts that patience with a reading among the quieter strengths
of its research culture, and is glad to see it set before a wider audience.

The full video is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.vudu7a.
