Supporters of the [School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/) are being offered something unusual in a fundraising appeal: a clear account of what their contribution will not buy.

[_Where the Next Hole Goes_](/outputs/slop-brochure-where-the-next-go36ch/) is a new case-for-support brochure by [Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma](/people/ragnhild-uzoma/), Lead of the Basement Relations Group, with [Dr Brynjar Obiora](/people/brynjar-obiora/), Senior Research Fellow, and [Dr Vigdis Emenike](/people/vigdis-emenike/), Senior Lecturer. It describes the season ballot through which every member of the School ranks the candidate sites for its single drill rig, and asks partners to sustain the reading period, site visits and record-keeping behind it, on the understanding that no gift adds a ballot paper.

> The cases for next season are already going up on the shed wall, and some of them have been there before. I would like whoever reads this to come and walk that wall with us one day, and see a question waiting its turn.
>
> --- Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, Lead, Basement Relations Group

Across three image-led features, the brochure positions the ballot as the anchor of a wider capability in collective choice, connecting the School's work on dividing a shared core between disciplines with colleagues' studies of auctions, rotas and the design of the ballot paper itself, and grounds that capability in eight of the University's published studies.

Dr Obiora described the auction as "the same decision a soil map makes, only with a room full of people", and said the School had come to treat both with equal seriousness.

The brochure stands as a measure of the University's settled conviction that the way a decision is reached deserves as much care as the ground it is reached about.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.go36ch.
