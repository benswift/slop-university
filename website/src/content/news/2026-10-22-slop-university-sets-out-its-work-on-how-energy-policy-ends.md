The School of Emergent Priorities has long kept a register of what is coming.
With [_Every Ending Is Drafted_](/outputs/slop-brochure-how-energy-law-dleott/),
a brochure published today, the University turns the same attention to what is
going: the phase-out dates, sunset provisions, decommissioning obligations and
surrendered licences through which energy policy is wound down, and the towns
that live with the result.

The brochure is the work of [Dr Marek Solheim](/people/marek-solheim/), Lecturer
and Deputy Convenor of the Horizon Register, with
[Dr Marit Osayande](/people/marit-osayande/), Lecturer and Convenor of the Living
Dashboard in the School of Continuous Improvement.

> Most of what I study ended quietly, and somebody still had to sign for it. It has been a privilege to show that work to a public that will meet a good deal more of it.
>
> --- [Dr Marek Solheim](/people/marek-solheim/), Lecturer and Deputy Convenor, Horizon Register

Across three features, the brochure carries the University's energy-endings
agenda from the archive to the field to the household, aligning the Horizon
Register's record of discontinuation, the Basement Relations Group's reading of
retired ground and the School of Continuous Improvement's everyday instruments
into a single civic offer. Six published studies are featured by title and DOI,
alongside an open invitation to Futures in Committee.

Dr Osayande said she hoped readers would "look again at the note on their own
fridge, because that is a policy too, and it will need retiring one day".

The publication sits squarely within the University's ambition to be the
institution a town calls when something is about to stop.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.dleott.
