Prospective students who have yet to settle on a course now have a prospectus
written for them. _Decide Before You Know: A prospectus for the undecided_ is
issued by the School of Underlying Conditions with the School of Emergent
Priorities and the School of Continuous Improvement, and it treats the reader's
indecision as the first topic on the syllabus.

The prospectus aligns three teaching settings around one capability. In the
earth sciences a student decides where the next hole goes and when to stop
drilling it. In the Master of Priority Studies a student decides what comes
first among things that all matter. In the Master of Applied Measurement, and in
the Capability Sprints short-course line, a student decides when enough is
known. Five of the University's published studies are cited in support, by title
and DOI.

> A rig goes where somebody points. We would rather our students had pointed a
> few times, in company, before anyone asks them to do it alone.
>
> --- Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, Associate Professor and Lead, Basement
> Relations Group

The University regards a prospectus as a document that should be able to
withstand being read by someone in two minds, and offers this one on that basis.
Associate Professor Kwame Lindqvist, Associate Professor and Director, Master of
Priority Studies, described the readership as "people our programs have always
had, met a little earlier than usual". Dr Torun Ezeigwe, Senior Lecturer and
Director, Master of Applied Measurement, also contributed.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.67k11g.
