Sixty-one laboratories, one shared schema and a written rule for letting a
number go: [_The Control Group We Already
Had_](/outputs/slop-impact-the-control-group-5492ks/), the
[School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/)'s Impact
Report 2021–2026, gathers five years of the School's work on the archives of
untreated-control results that immune-function testing laboratories compare
each new study against.

> Nineteen laboratories now have a custodian, and within ten years every one of
> those roles will have changed hands. Most of what we learned is about writing
> things down for whoever comes next.
>
> --- [Edda Nwafor](/people/edda-nwafor/), Doctoral Candidate, School of
> Continuous Improvement

Three case studies carry the report. The first consolidates scattered holdings
into a single archive, with the observations that could not be placed recorded
as present and unattributed rather than discarded. The second establishes a
refresh expectation and a retirement protocol, under which a withdrawn range
leaves behind either a successor or an acknowledged gap. The third puts an
archive's own condition --- its age, and the observations behind it --- on the
screen beside the number it produces, and reports what reviewers then asked for.

Dr [Thandiwe Solberg](/people/thandiwe-solberg/), Senior Lecturer and Convenor
of Demo Quarter, said the strand had given the School "a clearer sense of which
of our own measures are overdue a conversation, and rather less comfort than we
expected".

The report reflects the University's continuing attention to the upkeep of the
measures its research depends on, in the laboratory as much as in the
institution.

The full report is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.5492ks.
