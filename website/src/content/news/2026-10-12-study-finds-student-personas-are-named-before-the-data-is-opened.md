Thrifty Thelma is sixty-eight, owns her house and distrusts loyalty schemes. She
is also, on the evidence of a paper published today by Slop University, about
twenty-five years older than the customers she was written to represent.
_Thrifty Thelma Is Sixty-Eight: Persona Naming and the Order of Analysis in an
Introductory Marketing Subject_ is the work of Associate Professor Ragnhild
Uzoma, Associate Professor and Lead, Basement Relations Group, with Dr Iben
Chikere, Lecturer, and Mr Eivind Onwuka, Doctoral Candidate.

The paper aligns 444 persona sheets, their edit histories and a term of recorded
team meetings into a single account of when a customer segment acquires its
name. It surfaces a naming-to-biography pathway in which the name tended to
arrive ahead of the analysis, and reports a classroom trial that asked the name
to wait its turn.

> I should like to think these students will go on to name a great many things
> in their working lives. Our hope is only that some of those things will be
> there when the name arrives.
>
> --- Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, Associate Professor and Lead, Basement
> Relations Group

The University is pleased to see three of its schools meet in a first-year
tutorial room and leave it better arranged than they found it. Dr Chikere said
the study "leaves the subject with a slightly different sheet, and the rest of
us with a higher regard for what gets settled in a second tutorial".

The full paper is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.x2ckz9.
