The [School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/) has carried
its interest in how a new idea moves through an organisation onto the
University's landscape screens. The campaign, prepared by Dr Lindiwe
Achterberg, sets an office block at dusk in which some rooms glow at their
screens while others sit dark with the chairs pushed in, and invites the
passer-by to consider the rooms still deciding.

> Every rollout has a first department and, somewhere further down the
> corridor, one that has not quite got round to it. I am grateful to the
> colleagues on both sides of that corridor, who answer questions about logging
> in with more patience than the question deserves.
>
> --- Lindiwe Achterberg, Lecturer, School of Emergent Priorities

The poster points readers towards _Same Chart, Different Picture_, the
University's study of the Living Dashboard's uneven uptake across its
departments, alongside related work on staggered rollouts and on pilots that
persist beyond their scheduled close. Together these position adoption as a
standing institutional capability rather than a launch event, one that attends
to the late majority of any change as carefully as to its champions.

The campaign is the latest sign of a University that would rather show its
working than announce its conclusions, and it joins the rotation on the
landscape panels across campus this teaching period.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.r832yz.
