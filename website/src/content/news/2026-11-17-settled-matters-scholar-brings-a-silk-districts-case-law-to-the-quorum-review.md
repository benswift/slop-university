The Department of Policy Settings' review of quorum and resolutions has
received a submission that reaches well beyond the University for its evidence.
In
[_Confirm at the Next Ordinary Meeting_](/outputs/slop-submission-the-rearing-season-cases-xqfmxr/),
[Dr Odalys Penhallow](/people/odalys-penhallow/), Senior Lecturer in the
[School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/), sets the review's
question about inquorate resolutions beside twenty-nine reported cases on the
general meetings of one silk-growing district's cooperative societies, whose
courts divided three ways on the same question before settling it.

> The Department asked to hear from anyone with something to offer, and I took
> it at its word. The cases have kept me good company for three years, and I am
> happy to lend them to the review for as long as it needs them.
>
> --- Dr Odalys Penhallow, Senior Lecturer, School of Settled Matters

The submission connects the cooperatives' minute books, the University's own
work on how divided readings hold and on what is done while a ruling is awaited,
and the calendar the Determination already sets for unit meetings, to propose an
option the consultation paper did not canvass: a confirmation window that closes
with the unit's next ordinary meeting rather than after a fixed sixty days, with
anything done in the meantime left standing.

Dr Penhallow makes the submission in her own name, and its views are her own.
The University counts the readiness of its staff to bring their scholarship to
its settings among the surer signs that those settings are working as they
should.

The full submission is available from the University's research repository
under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.xqfmxr.
