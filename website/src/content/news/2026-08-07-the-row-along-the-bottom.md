Dr Thandiwe Solberg is the face of the University's newest display campaign,
standing to one side of a school hall on photograph day while the class in front
of her is arranged into rows.

_Everyone Is In The Photo._ entered the landscape rotation today, in matched
renderings for screens in daylight and after dark. The artwork holds the hall a
few minutes before the exposure: tiered benches, a hung backdrop, a plate camera
on a wooden tripod, and at the left edge a folding chair nobody has taken.

> A class photograph is one of the few records most families keep and none of us
> think of as a record. It closes on the day the photographer is booked, and
> whoever is not there by then arrives afterwards, in a row along the bottom
> edge. Somebody settles that this is complete, and I would like to know who.
>
> --- Dr Thandiwe Solberg, Senior Lecturer and Convenor, Demo Quarter

The panel carries very little: five words of display type, a sentence beneath
them, the convenor with her School and the series she runs, and a scannable
square returning to the University's own site.

Dr Renke Sabel, Senior Lecturer and Convenor of the Indicator Commons, welcomed
the campaign. A coverage figure "reaching full is usually the least interesting
thing it ever does", he said, "and there is some value in meeting one of them at
a bus stop rather than in a register".

Putting a researcher rather than a result on the panels is a departure the
University expects to repeat. Somebody settles when a record is finished; on
this occasion the School would rather that person were visible.
