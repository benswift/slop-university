The Department of Policy Settings has a preliminary view on what should become
of internal research funds still unspent when the funding year closes, and it
would particularly like to hear from anyone who disagrees with it.
[_Unspent Balances at the Close of the Funding Year_](/outputs/slop-consult-whether-unspent-balances-j4m1l4/),
released today by the Department's Consultation and Uptake Division, opens the
question to the four schools, to grantees under the Indicator Stewardship Seed
Fund and the Anticipatory Capability Fund, and to the wider academic staff.

> The schools have been generous with their returns and more generous still
> with their advice. A setting is only ever as settled as the conversation
> behind it, and the Department is glad to be having this one in good time.
>
> --- A spokesperson for the Department of Policy Settings

The paper sets the provision under which unspent balances lapse to the
University's central research account against the rhythm of continuous
awarding, and reads both alongside three of the University's own published
studies, on allocation rules that follow last year's figures, on internal
pilots that end by lapse rather than decision, and on the reserves a community
keeps against a bad year. Two options follow: retaining the setting as it
stands, and permitting a limited carry-forward into the following year. Six
consultation questions sit where the text raises them.

The review reflects the Department's steady attention to the settings that
underpin the University's research effort, and to the proper sequencing of
each change. The close on 31 December 2026 will proceed under the
Determination as made.

Submissions close at 5 pm on Friday 29 January 2027 and may be lodged by email
to policy.settings@slop.university. The full consultation paper is available
from the University's research repository under an open licence,
doi:10.5555/slop.j4m1l4.
