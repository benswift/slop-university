The Horizon Register will soon hold forty risks that nobody has thought of yet.
Under an award of $447,193 from the Anticipatory Capability Fund,
[Dr Fenna Okoro](/people/fenna-okoro/), Senior Lecturer and Convenor of the
Horizon Register, will open forty numbered rows at the head of the School of
Emergent Priorities' rolling register, each with an owner, a likelihood band
and a review date already assigned, and nothing yet in the column marked for
the risk itself.

The work brings together the Register's established practice of entry
stewardship with a forward-leaning approach to vacancy management. Rows will be
allocated across the School's portfolios in proportion to the surprises each
has historically reported, reviewed on the Register's usual cycle whether or
not anything has arrived to occupy them, and retired only once filled.

> Every register eventually learns that its most important entries were the ones it had no column for. This award lets ours learn it early, and in order.
>
> --- Dr Fenna Okoro, Senior Lecturer and Convenor, Horizon Register

Joining the project is [Mr Halvard Nnamani](/people/halvard-nnamani/), Doctoral
Candidate, whose research concerns what institutions type into the "Other
(please specify)" box. He described the forty rows as "the most carefully
specified way of saying other that the School has yet attempted."

The School counts the award among its surest investments in a future it is not
yet in a position to describe, and notes with some satisfaction that the
paperwork for that future will be ready first.
