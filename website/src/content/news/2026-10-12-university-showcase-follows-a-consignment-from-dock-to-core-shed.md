> We were asked to introduce the University's transport research and realised we had been running a small freight operation ourselves for some time. It carries rock, it has one customer, and the customer is not expected for several decades.
>
> --- Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, Associate Professor and Lead, Basement Relations Group

So begins the case for _Everything in Transit: Come and see_, a research showcase published today by Slop University. The brochure was prepared in the School of Underlying Conditions by Associate Professor Uzoma with Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow, and presents the University's work on logistics and transportation systems to readers meeting it for the first time.

The showcase anchors that work in four places where something changes hands: the loading dock, the front step, the bus shelter and the core shed. Across them it mobilises a dock-to-doorstep-to-rack continuum, placing studies from all three of the University's schools along a single route and introducing the researchers who stand at each handover.

The University takes a quiet satisfaction in a body of research that can be laid end to end and found to connect. Dr Obiora said the brochure "puts our School at the far end of a supply chain, which is a position we find we are temperamentally suited to".

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.bcc7wp.
