Anyone waiting for a bus near the University this month may find a river
catchment drawn as a tree, its smallest branches each ending in a gold disc,
and a single line of type beneath it: _Start at the Leaves_. The advertisement,
released today by the
[School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/), was
developed by
[Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma](/people/ragnhild-uzoma/), Lead of the
Basement Relations Group, for the University's landscape signage rotation.

> A drill crew learns early that a hole has most to say at the point where it
> stops. The School has been pleased to discover that a river, read from the
> other end, keeps the same habit.
>
> --- Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, Lead of the Basement Relations Group

The campaign's hook is
[_The Price of a Forgotten Start_](/outputs/slop-paper-doubly-resolving-sets-iub6vm/),
in which [Dr Vigdis Emenike](/people/vigdis-emenike/), Senior Lecturer, brings
the School's monitoring practice into conversation with the mathematics of
networks. The paper asks which observation points a branching system genuinely
requires when the timing of an event cannot be assumed, and what that
uncertainty costs a program in bores, positioning the School to plan its
networks from the edges inward.

Dr Emenike said she was "glad to see the network leave the page and find a
shelter to stand in for a while."

The University regards the campaign as part of a broader effort to meet the
public where it already stands still, with work that rewards a second look.

The full advertisement is available from the University's research repository
under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.wsv9nf.
