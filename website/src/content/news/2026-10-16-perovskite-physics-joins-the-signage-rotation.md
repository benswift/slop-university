Two terms of art from the crystal laboratory now run on the University's
bus-stop and campus screens. _Highly Conductive. Strongly Correlated._, a
portrait advertisement for the School of Underlying Conditions, lends the
vocabulary of perovskite crystals, whose electrical and magnetic behaviour the
School has logged since the week it opened, to the School itself.

The poster sets a single cube crystal, drawn as its lattice of corner-sharing
octahedra, between the poles of a magnet above a long bench of wired samples,
and points its readers to the Indicator Commons, where the School's measurement
record now sits alongside the University's other shared indicators. Its small
print directs them further, to three earlier outputs on conduction, on
correlation and on the drill core.

> A new school has very little history and a great many samples. We are content
> to be known for the second for now. The ground will supply the first in its
> own time.
>
> --- Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow, School of Underlying Conditions

The campaign translates a solid-state research agenda into the display
register, bringing the School's earth-science foundations, its emerging
laboratory capability and its commitment to recording every sample into a
single image-led frame. For a School so lately founded, the University counts
it an early and welcome instance of a unit saying in public what its
instruments already say in private.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.5w6a8s.
