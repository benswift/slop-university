Prospective students, collaborators and general readers now have a single place to begin with the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/). [_The Story So Far_](/outputs/slop-brochure-the-story-so-65i2r0/), a research-showcase brochure prepared by [Dr Clemency Tredinnick](/people/clemency-tredinnick/), [Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma](/people/ragnhild-uzoma/) and [Hedvig Anyanwu](/people/hedvig-anyanwu/), sets out how the School reads the records of the law as stories with a beginning, a teller and an inheritance.

> Most people meet the School the way an owner meets a title, partway through, with a good deal already decided. We wanted the first pages a reader turns to be ones that leave room for their questions.
>
> --- Dr Clemency Tredinnick, Lecturer, School of Settled Matters

Across three features, the brochure moves from the judgment that settles a version of events, through the title that carries earlier commitments forward to each new owner, to the gaps a record leaves behind, a strand it shares with the Basement Relations Group in the [School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/). Seven recent outputs from three Schools anchor the narrative-led showcase, each cited so that a reader can follow it to the full text.

For the University, the publication adds a welcoming front door to one of its newest Schools, and positions narrative as a common language between the law reports and the core shed. Associate Professor Uzoma described it as "the first of what we hope will be many conversations between people who read what was recorded and people who read what wasn't".

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.65i2r0.
