The Department of Policy Settings has tabled its written answers to five
questions on the Slop University (Cell Culture) Determination 2026, the setting
under which every culture in the University's laboratories carries a count of
its passages and is retired when that count reaches forty.
[_Answers to questions on notice_](/outputs/slop-qon-the-passage-ceiling-x0ejdh/)
responds to the Committee on Settings Oversight's Supplementary hearings of 5
November 2026.

> A register is only as sound as the care taken over each line of it. The
> Committee's questions are a welcome occasion to set out how that care is
> shared between the laboratories and the Department.
>
> --- First Assistant Secretary, Instruments and Determinations Division

The questions were put by
[Professor Verity Marris](/people/verity-marris/), Director of the Trajectory
Analytics Group, [Dr Lindiwe Achterberg](/people/lindiwe-achterberg/) of the
School of Emergent Priorities, and
[Associate Professor Anselm Threlkeld](/people/anselm-threlkeld/) and
[Dr Odalys Penhallow](/people/odalys-penhallow/) of the School of Settled
Matters. They cover the 223 lines retired at the ceiling since February, the
meaning of a passage, the culture time behind lines made in-house, the standing
of those lines as stocks, and how register entries are checked.

Taken together, the answers situate the passage ceiling within the University's
broader commitment to traceable reference materials, linking laboratory
practice, register stewardship and the instruments that underpin them into a
single account of how a culture's history is kept.

The answers stand as a further step in the Department's practice of keeping
each setting legible to the schools that work within it.

The full answers are available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.x0ejdh.
