Calorimetry, the end-of-tenancy inspection and the household habit of saving
candle ends meet in
[_Second Wick_](/outputs/slop-poster-paraffin-candle-stubs-roe2fx/), a new
research poster from the
[School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/). It is led by
[Ms Ylva Ogunbanjo](/people/ylva-ogunbanjo/), Doctoral Candidate, with
[Dr Brynjar Obiora](/people/brynjar-obiora/), Senior Research Fellow, and
[Dr Vigdis Emenike](/people/vigdis-emenike/), Senior Lecturer, both of the
[School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/).

> Every one of these stubs was somebody's evening in a house they no longer
> live in. I am grateful to the households who left them, and to colleagues in
> two schools who were prepared to take a saucepan seriously.
>
> --- Ms Ylva Ogunbanjo, Doctoral Candidate, School of Emergent Priorities

The poster brings thermal analysis to bear on the materials a rental property
gives back at the close of a lease. Stubs gathered at vacate inspections were
characterised one by one, then combined into new candles, enabling the team to
compare the individual and the collective across the full sweep of a wax's
phase behaviour. The headline finding is that pooling preserves the energy the
stubs hold while reshaping how, and over what range, the new candle melts.

The poster is a further sign of the University's readiness to follow its
doctoral candidates wherever their earlier careers lead, and of the quiet
traffic between its schools that makes such following possible.

"There is more in a drawer of leftovers than a laboratory tends to assume,"
said Dr Obiora, "and we are happy to keep being asked what else is in there."

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.roe2fx.
