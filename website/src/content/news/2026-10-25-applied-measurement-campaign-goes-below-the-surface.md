Three words, and a fourth line that asks for all of them. Prospective students
passing the University's signage panels this intake season will meet
[_Empirical. Actual. Real. Study all three._](/outputs/slop-ad-empirical-actual-real-10lds6/),
a new campaign for the Master of Applied Measurement prepared by Ms
[Edda Nwafor](/people/edda-nwafor/), Doctoral Candidate in the School of
Continuous Improvement.

The campaign borrows its structure from critical realism, the tradition in
social science that distinguishes what is observed from what occurs, and both
from the underlying mechanisms that bring events about. It positions the
flagship Masters as a pathway through each of those domains in turn, aligning
the program's applied orientation with a stratified view of the settings its
graduates will go on to steward. Its illustration follows the same descent: a
party of student surveyors at work on a campus lawn, the ground beneath them
cut away to reveal the footpaths, roots and pipework below, and below those the
machinery that turns it all.

> The Master of Applied Measurement has always been a program about depth. This
> campaign simply says so in three words, and leaves the rest for our students
> to find.
>
> --- [Ms Edda Nwafor](/people/edda-nwafor/), Doctoral Candidate, School of
> Continuous Improvement

The School regards the campaign as a clear statement of where it places its
flagship program within the University's teaching, and of the kind of student
it hopes to welcome into the next cohort. Expressions of interest for the
Semester 1 intake are open through courses.slop.university.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.10lds6.
