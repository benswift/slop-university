The School of Emergent Priorities has named Senior Research Fellow Anneke Tolan Deputy Convenor of Futures in Committee, the seminar series that follows how a scanning exercise's open questions narrow into an agenda item, a recommendation, and a minute.

Dr Tolan's own fieldwork follows planning into the room where it actually happens — the workshops, the templates, the quiet negotiations over wording that decide what a strategy document ends up saying. The appointment pairs that fieldwork with a series built on the same premise: that foresight is a practice long before it is a policy.

> The version of a horizon that survives to a minute is never quite the one a workshop first sketched, and that gap is the whole of what I study.
>
> --- Senior Research Fellow Anneke Tolan, Deputy Convenor, Futures in Committee

Dr Ingrid Vasseur, who convenes the series, welcomed the appointment: "a second reader who studies the room as closely as the outcome is exactly what a series like this has been missing."

The School frames the appointment as part of its ongoing work joining the University's anticipatory-capability agenda to the everyday practice that carries it out — the drafting, the redrafting, and the meetings in between.
