Most bodies are described long before anyone looks at them, and Slop University is asking its partners to help it read those descriptions closely. [_Taken as Described_](/outputs/slop-brochure-the-fitting-room-mirror-2a696u/), a case-for-support brochure led from the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/) by [Associate Professor Anselm Threlkeld](/people/anselm-threlkeld/) with [Ms Edda Nwafor](/people/edda-nwafor/), sets out the University's work on the label, the form and the provision, and on the person each of them describes.

> The fitters and wearers I worked beside for years always had plenty to say about sizes. This brochure gives the University a chance to hear some of it, and I suspect it will want to hear more.
>
> --- Ms Edda Nwafor, Doctoral Candidate, School of Continuous Improvement

The brochure brings garment sizing, care labelling, uniform evaluation, face familiarity, the spoken description of photographs and the construction of statutory language into a single descriptive agenda. Seven published University studies anchor the offer, positioning work in the Schools of Settled Matters, Continuous Improvement, Emergent Priorities and Underlying Conditions as one coordinated capability in how bodies are made legible to the institutions around them.

Associate Professor Threlkeld said the project had shown the School "how much of its reading begins with a description somebody else wrote first, and how much it has to learn from the people who wear one".

The brochure marks a further step in the University's effort to bring its scattered strengths into one conversation with the partners who might sustain them.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.2a696u.
