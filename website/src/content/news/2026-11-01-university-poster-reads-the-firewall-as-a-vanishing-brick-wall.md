For a quarter of a century, the staff of one security operations centre drew
their network by hand whenever an incident called for it, and nearly every time
they drew the firewall as a wall of bricks.
[_Fewer Bricks Each Year_](/outputs/slop-poster-the-brick-wall-firewall-c3dw5f/)
reads those drawings as an art historian would. The work was led by
[Dr Brynjar Obiora](/people/brynjar-obiora/), Senior Research Fellow in the
[School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/), alongside
[Ms Sunniva Balogun](/people/sunniva-balogun/), Doctoral Candidate in the
[School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/), and
[Dr Solveig Adeyemi](/people/solveig-adeyemi/), Lecturer and Deputy Convenor of
the Review Cadence Observatory in the
[School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/).

> A surveyor learns early that a line on a map is a decision somebody made in
> the field. It has been a privilege to watch another kind of fieldwork make
> that decision year after year, a little more lightly each time.
>
> --- Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow, School of Underlying Conditions

Working across sixty-one binders and fourteen distinct hands, the team brings
the tools of iconography and connoisseurship to a body of operational drawing
that has seldom been looked at as drawing, placing the everyday sketch of the
incident desk within the University's widening engagement with the visual
cultures of digital security. Over the period the poster covers, the median
wall thins from six courses of brick to one, and a written label takes the
bricks' place.

Ms Balogun said the archive had "kept these sheets for what they were, which is
the kindest thing an archive can do for a question nobody has asked yet".

The University counts the poster among the clearest signs to date that its
School of Underlying Conditions will go wherever a boundary has been drawn by
hand, and stay long enough to see it redrawn.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.c3dw5f.
