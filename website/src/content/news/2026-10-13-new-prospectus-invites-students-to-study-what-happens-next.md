Prospective students who want to know where a thing is heading, and not only
where it stands, now have a prospectus addressed to them. _The Long Run: A
prospectus for studying what happens next_ sets out how differential equations
and dynamical systems are met across the School of Emergent Priorities, the
School of Continuous Improvement and the School of Underlying Conditions.

> Nobody handed me a booklet when I arrived, and I am not certain I would have
> read it in the right order if they had. I hope whoever picks this one up
> feels free to begin at the back, with the reading.
>
> --- Ms Ylva Ogunbanjo, Doctoral Candidate, School of Emergent Priorities

The prospectus moves through four ideas, each given a spread of its own: the
state a system returns to, the loop it repeats, the part of it that takes its
time, and the starting point it never quite lets go of. Each is anchored in one
of the University's existing programs and groups and carried by the voice of a
researcher, and the whole is underpinned by five published studies introduced by
title and DOI.

An institution's account of itself to people who have not yet joined it is,
the University considers, among the more exacting things it is asked to write,
and this one has been written with corresponding care. Associate Professor
Casimir Beng, Lead of the Adaptive Metrics Lab, said the prospectus "asks
nothing of a reader that it does not also ask of the staff, which is to keep
watching a little longer than feels necessary".

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.q299wg.
