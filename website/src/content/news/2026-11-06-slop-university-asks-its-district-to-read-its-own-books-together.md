[_Set Here_](/outputs/slop-brochure-set-here-a-094sbk/), a new civic brochure
from Slop University, invites the district's reading groups, bookshop volunteers
and local historians to read the town's own books in company with the
University's researchers.

The brochure was prepared by [Dr Vigdis Emenike](/people/vigdis-emenike/),
Senior Lecturer in the
[School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/), with
[Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma](/people/ragnhild-uzoma/), Lead of the
Basement Relations Group, and [Dr Mirela Hanke](/people/mirela-hanke/),
Postdoctoral Fellow and Deputy Convenor of the Living Dashboard in the
[School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/).

> We owe this brochure to the people who were already reading with us, in lounge
> rooms and on the bookshop roster, and who were kind enough to let us join them
> partway through the book.
>
> --- Dr Vigdis Emenike, Senior Lecturer, School of Underlying Conditions

Across three feature strands, the brochure brings collective reading practice,
the marginal record of second-hand copies and the district's own place-writing
into alignment with the University's wider civic engagement agenda, from the
living-room circle to the field walk that reads a passage aloud beside the
ground it describes. It showcases six published studies from all three Schools
and introduces two of the researchers behind them.

Associate Professor Uzoma said she hoped the brochure would "bring a few more
chairs into the circle, and a few more unfinished books with them."

The publication carries forward the University's long-standing commitment to
research undertaken at the pace of the communities it shares a district with.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.094sbk.
