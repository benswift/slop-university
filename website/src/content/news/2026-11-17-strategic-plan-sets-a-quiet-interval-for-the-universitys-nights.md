> A plan like this is mostly a promise about when we will be quiet. I expect the people who keep the overnight rosters will read it more closely than anyone, and they are the readers we had in mind.
>
> --- Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, Lead, Basement Relations Group

[_The Quiet Interval_](/outputs/slop-strategy-breath-and-sleep-5hk8oh/), the
University's Strategic Plan 2026-2031, brings the study of how breathing is
generated and how sleep reshapes it into the centre of the University's
research agenda. It was prepared by
[Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma](/people/ragnhild-uzoma/) and
[Dr Vigdis Emenike](/people/vigdis-emenike/) of the
[School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/), with
[Associate Professor Kwame Lindqvist](/people/kwame-lindqvist/), Director,
Master of Priority Studies.

Across five focus areas, the plan aligns overnight recording capability, a
cross-school doctoral pathway and the long-term stewardship of whole-night
records behind a single institutional ambition. It also turns the field's
attention back on the University, settling a nightly quiet interval for
institutional correspondence and a recovery period after each major review
cycle, both to be made and maintained by the Department of Policy Settings.

The plan sits comfortably within the University's habit of building slowly on
what its schools already do well. Associate Professor Lindqvist described its
phasing as "unhurried by design, which seems right for a field that does so
much of its best work after dark".

The full Strategic Plan is available from the University's research repository
under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.5hk8oh.
