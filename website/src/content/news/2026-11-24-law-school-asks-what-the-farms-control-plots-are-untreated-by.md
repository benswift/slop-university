"Untreated", in the view of the
[School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/), is a word that always
needs an object. The School has put that view to the Department of Policy
Settings in
[_Untreated by What the Trial Studies_](/outputs/slop-submission-the-school-of-j01zv2/),
a submission to the Department's review of the untreated control plots kept by
pest trials on the University's research farm, prepared by
[Dr Clemency Tredinnick](/people/clemency-tredinnick/) and
[Associate Professor Anselm Threlkeld](/people/anselm-threlkeld/).

> We hold no plot on the farm, so we have tried to write as good neighbours
> rather than as interested parties. Our thanks go to the schools whose trials
> stand out there all season, and whose patience with our questions has been
> considerable.
>
> --- Associate Professor Anselm Threlkeld, School of Settled Matters

The submission connects the School's grounding in statutory interpretation and
the burdens that pass with land to its members' laboratory work on the heat
treatment of titanium alloys, where every comparison is anchored to a control
bar with a documented history of its own. It favours keeping control plots
within each trial, supported by two refinements: a declaration of what each
control is withheld from, and a record of every control plot's past seasons
that stays with the ground.

The University welcomes the care its newest school continues to bring to the
instruments that govern shared ground. Dr Tredinnick said the School "would be
glad to help with the drafting, whichever way the review goes."

The full submission is available from the University's research repository
under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.j01zv2.
