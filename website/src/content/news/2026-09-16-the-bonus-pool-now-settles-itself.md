A national retail franchise's head office no longer asks a manager to decide who gets what share of the quarterly bonus pool. The Recognition Settlement Engine, described today in a paper from the School of Continuous Improvement, converts the short thank-you messages colleagues already send one another into the deciding score, and locks it the moment the quarter closes.

> A ledger that locks itself is not a shortcut around judgement; it is a record of what judgement would have cost to keep exercising every quarter.
>
> --- Dr Renke Sabel, Senior Lecturer and Convenor, Indicator Commons, School of Continuous Improvement

The engine reads each kudos message for how specific it is before weighting it into a running point total, then clears the pool proportionally against that total on a fixed calendar boundary — a design the School frames as extending its evaluation-ecosystem agenda from measurement into settlement. Associate Professor Casimir Beng, Lead of the Adaptive Metrics Lab, said the year-long deployment "gives the Lab its clearest look yet at what a recognition signal does once it knows it is being spent."

The full paper is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.aks44d.
