An apprentice at a workbench, a weekly market in examination week and a choir's
well-thumbed score folder share the pages of
[_What the Schools Hold Up_](/outputs/slop-brochure-what-the-schools-jdpurr/),
a research showcase published today by the
[School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/). Prepared by
[Dr Vigdis Emenike](/people/vigdis-emenike/), Senior Lecturer, with
[Edda Nwafor](/people/edda-nwafor/), Doctoral Candidate in the
[School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/), it invites
readers to see a district's schools as the layer everything else is built upon.

> None of this would exist without the teachers, stallholders, trades-desk staff
> and choir committees who let us sit quietly in the corner and take notes. The
> showcase is really a record of their patience.
>
> --- Dr Vigdis Emenike, Senior Lecturer in the School of Underlying Conditions

The showcase moves across three themes. It follows vocational learning from the
tool crib to the first placement, situates the final school year within the
wider rhythms of community life, and applies a stratigraphic sensibility to the
documentary inheritance of everyday institutions. Seven published outputs from
all three of the University's Schools anchor the account, each cited so that
readers can go directly to the underlying work.

The publication is the latest sign of a University-wide conviction that the most
consequential infrastructure is often the least conspicuous. Nwafor
described the project as "a chance to let the workshop speak for itself, and to
notice how much of the conversation was already happening there."

The full showcase is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.jdpurr.
