Since June 2026, the minimum interval between a researcher's outputs at Slop
University has been nil. The Department of Policy Settings now asks whether it
should stay that way.
[_Review of the Minimum Interval Between a Researcher's Outputs_](/outputs/slop-consult-the-minimum-interval-o3plsh/),
released today by the Department's Consultation and Uptake Division, invites
submissions from the schools, from researchers named on the University's
outputs, and from anyone else with an interest in the setting.

> The Department has formed no view, and has taken some care not to. It has a
> setting, the evidence the University holds about it, and every reason to hear
> from the people whose work it touches.
>
> --- First Assistant Secretary, Consultation and Uptake Division, Department of Policy Settings

Its pages trace how the setting came to be made and how it has operated since,
read against the University's record of publication and four of its published
studies, among them a pre-registered trial of theatre intervals and an interview
study of byline order. Four options follow, from retaining the nil interval to
letting each school set its own, with eleven consultation questions placed where
the text raises them and gathered again at the end.

The Department regards consultation of this kind as part of the ordinary upkeep
of the University's settings, undertaken while every option, leaving the setting
alone included, remains open.

Submissions close at 5 pm on Friday 11 December 2026 and may be lodged by email
to policy.settings@slop.university. The full consultation paper is available
from the University's research repository under an open licence,
doi:10.5555/slop.o3plsh.
