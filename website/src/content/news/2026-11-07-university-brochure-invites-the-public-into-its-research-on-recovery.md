Families who have sat outside an operating theatre, landholders who have waited
on a bore to refill and commuters who know the length of a morning at the stop
are the intended readers of
[_Coming Round_](/outputs/slop-brochure-coming-round-a-bvbcnd/), a civic
brochure released today by the
[School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/).

The brochure brings together the University's work on the interval between the
end of an exposure and the return of the ordinary, and places it in three public
settings: the waiting room outside an anaesthetic, the paddock where a pumped
bore climbs back towards its standing level, and the shared shelter of the bus
stop. Across those settings it aligns mathematical, field and community
research into a single offer of engagement, anchored by six published outputs
and supported by open evenings and field days.

> We wanted a document someone could pick up in a corridor and recognise
> themselves in. If it brings a few more questions to our next open evening, it
> will have done its job.
>
> --- Dr Vigdis Emenike, Senior Lecturer, School of Underlying Conditions

The release continues the University's practice of conducting its slowest
research in the company of the people it concerns. Associate Professor Ragnhild
Uzoma, Lead of the Basement Relations Group, described the brochure as "a first
draft of a conversation we expect to keep having, in more halls and more
paddocks than we can yet picture."

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.bvbcnd.
