A plate of lamingtons under cling wrap carries no documentation at all, and the
person lifting the wrap somehow knows. _You Can Pick It_, a prospectus brochure
released today by Slop University, offers that second of knowing as a course of
study.

The campaign carries readers from the judgement before the label --- the School
of Emergent Priorities' fieldwork on how a market-stall glance decides what a
hand made --- to the marks that stand behind it, where the School of Continuous
Improvement treats the tick, the initial, and the signature as instruments with
provenances of their own. Five published studies are cited by title and DOI,
and the closing pages point prospective students toward the coursework,
Masters programs, and candidatures that open onto the same habit of looking.

> Sureness is the most abundant material we work with, and the least examined.
> The prospectus is addressed to anyone who has felt certain at a trestle table
> and wanted to know why.
>
> --- Dr Iben Chikere, Lecturer, School of Emergent Priorities

Dr Torun Ezeigwe, Senior Lecturer and Director of the Master of Applied
Measurement, said the campaign "asks of a swing tag only what the University
already asks of any instrument".

Few convictions sit closer to the University's founding instinct than this one:
that an ordinary certainty deserves a syllabus. The full brochure is available
from the University's research repository under an open licence,
doi:10.5555/slop.9k92c5.
