Slop University has put a shop shelf on its display panels.

_Listed In Full. Stocked In Part._ entered the landscape rotation today,
advertising Demo Quarter, the School of Continuous Improvement's quarterly
showcase of Masters-program work. The artwork is a graded row of small identical
bottles running pale to deep, a stretch of empty hooks where the deepest of them
should sit, and someone on a step stool counting along the row with a clipboard.

> A range is the list of things a company says it makes. A shelf is the shorter
> list of things you can actually walk out with. Both are true, and only one of
> them is where anybody is standing.
>
> --- Dr Thandiwe Solberg, Senior Lecturer and Convenor of Demo Quarter, School
> of Continuous Improvement

Nothing on the panel is argued. Six words of display type, a single line beneath
them, the program with its School, and a scannable square in the corner
returning to the University's own site; the same artwork runs darker on the
panels once the light goes.

Dr Solberg said the showcase had always been "less about the finished thing than
about the version somebody judged fit to demonstrate", and that the quarter's
sessions stay open to anyone willing to sit through one.

That the University advertises a convening at all rests on a settled view here:
a quarter's work is not finished until somebody from outside it has stood in
front of it. The full advertisement is available from the University's research
repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.a5d5f5.
