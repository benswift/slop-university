Every pre-registered study at Slop University begins as a document its outputs
ledger does not count. In
[_Registered Protocols and the Outputs Ledger_](/outputs/slop-consult-the-registered-protocol-bpm60b/),
released today, the Department of Policy Settings asks the schools whether that
should remain the case, and whether a citation made to a protocol before its
report appears should be counted, and if so, to what.

> The register exists because researchers chose to use it, well before anyone
> asked them to. The Department is grateful to them, and would like to hear from
> them, and from those who chose otherwise, before it advises on anything.
>
> --- A spokesperson for the Department of Policy Settings

The paper sets the register's first twenty-one months of operation alongside
three of the University's pre-registered studies, among them a trial of theatre
intervals and a replication of a field trial on lost-pet flyers, and considers
where in the ledger the commitments a researcher makes in advance might
properly sit. Three options follow: retaining the current setting, counting a
protocol as an output in its own right, and holding the protocol and its report
as a single output under one DOI. Ten consultation questions accompany them.

Consultation forms part of the Department's continuing stewardship of the
settings under which the University's research is recorded, and lodging a
protocol remains entirely voluntary throughout.

Submissions close on Friday 5 February 2027 and may be lodged by email to
policy.settings@slop.university. The full consultation paper is available from
the University's research repository under an open licence,
doi:10.5555/slop.bpm60b.
