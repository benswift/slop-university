Seven words of display type, a flask and a filter funnel make up most of _We
Purified It Until It Stopped Glowing_, a poster that joins the University's
signage rotation today. It was prepared by Ms Signy Okpara, Doctoral Candidate
in the School of Continuous Improvement, with Ms Ylva Onyekwere, Doctoral
Candidate in the School of Emergent Priorities.

> I am not a chemist, and the School was generous enough to treat that as a
> qualification. I read the paper as somebody waiting for a lift might read the
> panel, and I was allowed to keep the sentence I came away with.
>
> --- Ms Signy Okpara, Doctoral Candidate, School of Continuous Improvement

The poster points to _Afterglow in the Mother Liquor_, the University's
published account of one batch of crystals taken through nine further
purification passes, and of a faint glow that turned out to travel with a trace
companion and not with the compound itself. The campaign mobilises that account
across the improvement-purity-attention nexus, aligning a single supporting
sentence with the paper's title and identifier and three further University
studies in the small print.

The University holds that a school devoted to making things better is well
placed to commend a study of what better can cost, and it is pleased that one
has. Ms Onyekwere said the bench had "been called many things over the years,
and is content to be called a campaign".

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.wvpdqf.
