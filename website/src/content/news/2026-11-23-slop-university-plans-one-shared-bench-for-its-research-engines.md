From next year, a researcher in any of the University's Schools will be able to
book time on a single-cylinder research engine through one shared calendar. That
commitment opens
[_Fuel-Flexible by Design_](/outputs/slop-strategy-fuel-flexible-by-0rmi2j/), Slop University's Strategic Plan
2026-2031, prepared by Dr [Thandiwe Solberg](/people/thandiwe-solberg/), Dr
[Torun Ezeigwe](/people/torun-ezeigwe/) and
Dr [Ingeborg Nwachukwu](/people/ingeborg-nwachukwu/) of the
[School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/).

> I would like the first run we open to the University to be an unremarkable
> one. If an engine on an ordinary afternoon turns out to be worth watching,
> the plan will have done most of what it set out to do.
>
> --- Dr Thandiwe Solberg, Senior Lecturer and Convenor, Demo Quarter

The plan organises the University's combustion research around four priorities.
It aligns the instrumentation of every test cell to a common baseline, routes
each incoming fuel through a single intake process without preference for any
one of them, and anchors a shared ledger in which emissions, deposits and
cycle-resolved data are held at the resolution they were taken. Operators across
the capability will be assessed and moderated against one competency framework,
and the Master of Applied Measurement will
take its teaching to the bench. Dr Ezeigwe said the program would arrive there
"with its notebooks open and its expectations suitably modest".

The University regards the plan as a statement about the kind of infrastructure
it intends to be: shared before it is expanded, and open to fuels it has not yet
met. Progress will be reported to the University Council each year and displayed
on the Living Dashboard as it occurs.

The full plan is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.0rmi2j.
