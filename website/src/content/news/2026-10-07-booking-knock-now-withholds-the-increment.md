Thirty minutes is now the increment. That is the claim of _The Booking Knock as Increment Device_, a poster released today by Ms Signy Okpara with Dr Joost Nwosu and Dr Solveig Adeyemi of the School of Continuous Improvement.

> The knock is not a courtesy. It is the form's last box.
>
> --- Ms Signy Okpara, Doctoral Candidate, School of Continuous Improvement

Glass booths on eighteen open-plan floors were read at the door panel's booking-end chime. Each scheduled 1:1 was placed against the signed seconds from that chime, and against the increment that followed. Award held near sixty-two percent in the last ninety seconds before the knock and near eighteen percent in the first ninety seconds after it. The timekeeping box on the review form moved with the same cutoff.

On the strength of that discontinuity the School adopts a published Increment Window. A 1:1 still in the room when the next booking knocks is coded Needs development on timekeeping, which withholds the annual increment. A 1:1 that ends more than ninety seconds before the chime is coded Insufficient engagement and likewise withholds. There is no line-manager override. The eligible close is that ninety-second window.

The School files the result as a cutoff the workplace was already keeping, now written as a rule. Dr Nwosu, Postdoctoral Fellow, said the floors had "let the panel be logged without restating the review". Dr Adeyemi, Lecturer, said the timekeeping box "was already on the form; the knock has given it a job".
