Every school and unit of the University will soon contribute in proportion to what its work draws on, under [_Accounting for What We Use_](/outputs/slop-strategy-an-internal-environmental-6c3niw/), the Strategic Plan for 2026--2031 released today.

At the centre of the Plan is the Footprint Contribution, an internal levy assessed each year on energy, travel, print, procurement and water. Its proceeds go to a Stewardship Fund that no budget may count on, and its decisions are entered in a Contribution Ledger open to the whole University.

> Every school that sat down with its own figures this year has already done the difficult part. What the Plan adds is a place to write them down where everyone can read them.
>
> --- [Ylva Onyekwere](/people/ylva-onyekwere/), Doctoral Candidate, School of Emergent Priorities

Ms Onyekwere led the Plan with [Halvard Nnamani](/people/halvard-nnamani/), Doctoral Candidate in the same School, and [Associate Professor Kwame Lindqvist](/people/kwame-lindqvist/), Director of the Master of Priority Studies. Grounded in the University's own scholarship on levies, shared costs and common funds, the Plan aligns a stable annual price, a tested method for dividing what units hold in common, and a governance that rotates by rule. The intent is to bring the estate's condition within reach of every unit's planning.

Associate Professor Lindqvist said the drafting had shown the School "how much more comfortable it is reading a ledger than a forecast, and how rarely it had been offered the choice".

The University sees the Plan as an act of institutional good manners: paying, in plain view, for the conditions its work relies on.

The full Strategic Plan is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.6c3niw.
