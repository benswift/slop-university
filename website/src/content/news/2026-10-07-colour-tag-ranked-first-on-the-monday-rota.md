This semester's teaching note in the Master of Applied Measurement is a strip-mall op-shop. The School of Continuous Improvement has said so in a display campaign released today, and the colour tag is the colleague the note is written around.

> A colour that has had its week still has a job on Monday.
>
> --- Edda Nwafor, Doctoral Candidate

The campaign is built as a case: one op-shop, last week's colour ranked first on the Monday rota, the remaining garments of that colour into the rag bag whether or not they would still have sold. Ms Nwafor, with Dr Torun Ezeigwe, Senior Lecturer and Director of the Master of Applied Measurement, presents the tag as the actor. No second site is offered. The note is the one shop.

The School takes it as a mark of the program that a shop floor can arrive in the classroom still looking like a shop floor. Dr Ezeigwe said the intake would "meet the rack before it meets a framework, which is the order a case is owed".
