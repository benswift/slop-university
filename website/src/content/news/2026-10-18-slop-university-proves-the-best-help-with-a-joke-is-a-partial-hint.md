title: Slop University proves the best help with a joke is a partial hint
subtitle: A Kolmogorov-complexity paper bounds what an explanation must carry, and finds a full one leaves the listener nothing to earn
date: 2026-10-18
description: A new paper from the School of Continuous Improvement shows that explaining a joke costs about as much information as the joke withheld, and that a well-sized hint does better than the whole answer.
output: slop-paper-explaining-a-joke-bgx87d
---

Help, according to a new paper from Slop University, is best given in part.
_Explaining the Joke_, by Mr Eskil Nwabueze of the School of Continuous
Improvement, brings the tools of theoretical computer science to the oldest
question in the seminar room: why the joke that has to be explained so rarely
survives the explanation.

The paper situates the familiar experience of getting a joke within an
information-theoretic framework, positioning the setup, the punchline and the
listener's own background as a single transfer of understanding. Within that
framework it establishes formal limits on what any explanation must carry,
identifies a window inside which a joke can land, and characterises the hint
that serves a listener best when a joke does not.

> Anyone who has made a remark at the front of a room knows the moment when it
> lands for half of it. I am grateful to be able to give the other half a
> number, and I hope they will take it in the spirit in which it is offered.
>
> --- Mr Eskil Nwabueze, Doctoral Candidate, School of Continuous Improvement

The work extends the School's standing interest in how capability passes from
one person to another, and the University welcomes its arrival in a register
the School has not previously used. "We spend a great deal of our time deciding
how much to tell people and how much to leave them to find," said Dr Ingeborg
Nwachukwu, Lecturer and Convenor of Capability Sprints. "It is useful to have
that judgement written down with some care."

The full paper is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.bgx87d.
