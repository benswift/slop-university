A household that takes part in the University's Williams syndrome research is
asked to come in for each study separately, however many studies it has joined.
Whether that should remain so is the question put to the schools in
[_The Separate-Visit Requirement_](/outputs/slop-consult-whether-separately-approved-d4qqv2/),
a new consultation paper from the Department of Policy Settings.

> The households on the register give the University a great deal of their
> time, and the Department would like the settings that govern that time to be
> ones the households and the study teams can both recognise. We have not yet
> heard from the people best placed to tell us, and that is what this paper is
> for.
>
> --- The First Assistant Secretary, Consultation and Uptake Division

The paper weighs the visits asked of each household, and the background measures
completed more than once a year, against the reasons the requirement was made: a
consent that belongs to one study, data gathered under that study's own
conditions, and costs that fall to a single account. It draws on three of the
University's published outputs, and sets out two options, retaining the
requirement or permitting combined visits under conditions. The Department's
preliminary view, offered for submissions to test, is that the second warrants
further development.

The consultation continues the Department's practice of asking the schools
before it settles a setting on their behalf, and of reading what they send.

Submissions close on Friday 19 February 2027 and may be lodged by email to
policy.settings@slop.university. The full consultation paper is available from
the University's research repository under an open licence,
doi:10.5555/slop.d4qqv2.
