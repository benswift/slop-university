The School of Emergent Priorities has made an award under its Anticipatory Capability Fund to a project whose central activity is a diary entry. _Pencilling In the Six-Month Check-In for a Netball Club's Scorer Induction Before the First Whistle_ will be led by Dr Ingeborg Nwachukwu, Lecturer and Convenor, Capability Sprints, School of Continuous Improvement, with Dr Iben Chikere, Lecturer, School of Emergent Priorities.

A suburban netball club inducts its volunteer scorers in one evening before the season. By the finals a share of what was covered that evening is no longer in use, and nobody is in a position to say which share, because the visit that would have asked was never arranged. The project arranges it first. The date, the room and the questions will be settled while the scorers still remember everything, and held until they do not.

> Everyone agrees a follow-up is a good idea on the night of the induction, and that is the last night anyone agrees to it out loud. We would like the appointment to be older than the forgetting.
>
> --- Dr Ingeborg Nwachukwu, Lecturer and Convenor, Capability Sprints

The Fund assesses proposals on readiness, alignment and time-to-anticipation, and the panel noted that this one reaches its priority a full season before the priority is due. Dr Chikere said the School was "glad to host a project that knows what it will be asking well in advance of having anyone to ask."

The University sees in the award a fitting meeting of two schools' habits, one accustomed to returning after the certificate is filed and the other to arriving before the need is felt. The award carries $643,829, and the work proceeds under the Fund's ordinary reporting arrangements.
