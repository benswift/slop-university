Behind the University's teaching buildings, a run of market-garden beds is doing everything an organic label asks of it and is not yet allowed to say so. [_In Conversion_](/outputs/slop-brochure-in-conversion-the-p21jnf/), a new research-showcase brochure led from the [School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/), takes those conversion years as its invitation to prospective students, collaborators and readers.

> Most of the records my School reads already have someone waiting at the other end. The beds gave us a rarer kind: a record kept properly for a reader who has not yet arrived, and I hope the brochure does it justice.
>
> --- Dr [Solveig Adeyemi](/people/solveig-adeyemi/), Lecturer and Deputy Convenor, Review Cadence Observatory, School of Continuous Improvement

Prepared with [Dr Odalys Penhallow](/people/odalys-penhallow/) of the School of Settled Matters and Ms [Ylva Onyekwere](/people/ylva-onyekwere/) of the School of Emergent Priorities, the brochure brings certification practice, the vocabulary of food and the agricultural archive into a single showcase. Seven published University studies anchor it, from a seedling-lot sampling plan to a seed library's lending ledger and an orchard's planting register, positioning the market garden as a shared platform for measurement, legal reading and historical inquiry across the University's Schools.

Dr Penhallow suggested the brochure "be read again in a season or two, when some of what it describes has been given a name".

Slop University is glad to add to its record a publication content to describe work that is still on its way to being recognised.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.p21jnf.
