Slop University has never asked its supporters to take a reading on trust, and
its newest publication extends them the same courtesy.
[_What the Coil Reads_](/outputs/slop-brochure-what-the-coil-4obaxn/) is a
case-for-support brochure for the University's electrical and electromagnetic
research, prepared by
[Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma](/people/ragnhild-uzoma/), Lead of the
Basement Relations Group in the
[School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/), with
[Dr Torun Ezeigwe](/people/torun-ezeigwe/), Senior Lecturer and Director of the
Master of Applied Measurement, and doctoral candidate
[Signy Okpara](/people/signy-okpara/), both of the
[School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/).

> Putting the brochure together, we found the same habit at work in three
> Schools that had never quite compared notes. It was good to see it set down
> in one place, and to see how far it already reaches.
>
> --- Dr Torun Ezeigwe, Senior Lecturer and Director, Master of Applied
> Measurement

The brochure moves from the core shed, where recovered rock meets a sensing
loop before it meets a shelf, to the teaching bench, where students check a
coil against its reference before reading anything from it, and out to the
bores, fleets and workshops where that discipline is put to use. Seven
published studies anchor the account, each cited by title and DOI, positioning
the University's measurement culture as a shared capability rather than the
property of any one School.

Ms Okpara described the publication as "a fair account of a careful practice,
and an open door to anyone who values one."

The brochure stands as a quiet statement of the University's confidence in
the instruments it keeps and the people who keep them.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.4obaxn.
