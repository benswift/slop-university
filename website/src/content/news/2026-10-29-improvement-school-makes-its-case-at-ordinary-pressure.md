The [School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/) is
asking its partners to think smaller.
[_At Ordinary Pressure_](/outputs/slop-brochure-at-ordinary-pressure-4mvd53/),
the School's new case-for-support brochure, takes its organising image from
ammonia synthesis, where much of the field now hopes to fix nitrogen in compact
units near the paddock rather than in a few large plants run hot and under
pressure.

The brochure was prepared by [Ms Edda Nwafor](/people/edda-nwafor/), Doctoral
Candidate, with [Dr Sten Okwuosa](/people/sten-okwuosa/), Senior Research
Fellow and Convenor of the Impact Pathway Atlas, and
[Dr Ingeborg Nwachukwu](/people/ingeborg-nwachukwu/), Lecturer and Convenor of
the Capability Sprints.

> Writing for partners made us say plainly where our work happens. Mostly it
> happens in other people's rooms, and I think the School is better for having
> put that in print.
>
> --- Dr Ingeborg Nwachukwu, Lecturer and Convenor, Capability Sprints

Across three features, the brochure aligns the School's research, teaching and
engagement settings with the difficult first step, the point of need and the
root nodule a legume builds to house its partners. A closing showcase gathers
seven published studies from across the University, from a wind-driven
ammonia loop to a test vial waiting in its rack.

For Ms Nwafor, the brochure is a natural extension of a School that has always
preferred to be asked in early. "The first conversation is the one that shapes
everything after it," she said, "and we would like to have more of them."

The publication continues the School's practice of setting its partnerships
alongside its published record, so that a supporter can read the work before
being asked to sustain it.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.4mvd53.
