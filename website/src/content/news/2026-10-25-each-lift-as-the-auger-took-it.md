The Office of Research Outputs has named [Dr Bram Ntuli](/people/bram-ntuli/),
Lecturer and Convenor of the School of Continuous Improvement's Improvement
Grand Rounds, as this quarter's recipient of the Deposit of the Quarter Prize,
with an award of $28,476. The citation concerns the repository record behind
[_The Uneven Lift_](/outputs/slop-poster-the-uneven-lift-znmmki/), a research
poster from the School of Underlying Conditions on which Dr Ntuli is a
co-author.

The deposit carries the poster's 212 hand-auger holes lift by lift. Each lift's
top and bottom are entered as the auger took them, and the field sheet's
ten-centimetre rounding sits in a column of its own alongside, so that nothing
has been averaged to a midpoint or tidied onto the grid. The poster leans on
exactly that arrangement: read as measured, the holes yield 77 Turnbull
intervals and a median depth to clay the analysis can actually name, where the
rounded sheet on its own would have settled for somewhere within a decade.

> The credit belongs to whoever held the tape on those afternoons and wrote down the depth they saw rather than the one the sheet was expecting. A repository can only keep what somebody chose to record, and here somebody chose to, two hundred and twelve times.
>
> --- Dr Bram Ntuli, Lecturer and Convenor, Improvement Grand Rounds

The prize is judged on completeness of metadata and timeliness of deposit, and
the record was lodged in the same week as the poster it supports. The Office
finds a particular fitness in the outcome. Dr Ntuli's own scholarship concerns
what a standing review format quietly leaves off the record, and the University
is glad to see that interest returned to it in the form of a record that left
nothing off.
