Once a year, at the close of business on the last working day of March, the
University copies its administrative records as they stand and, after a
quarter's preparation, releases them to researchers. The Department of Policy
Settings today asked the schools whether that is still the right way to do it.

[_The Annual Records Extract_](/outputs/slop-consult-the-annual-census-date-qyx5m7/)
opens the Slop University (Records Extract) Determination 2019 to its first
review. The paper follows the setting from the Council resolution that made it
through the seven extracts released since, and notes a growing body of work in
the schools that asks how a record came to stand where it does, and not only
where it stood on census day.

> Seven extracts in, the Determination has done what it was asked to do. The
> paper asks whether the question has moved, and the people best placed to
> answer are the ones who have been opening the files.
>
> --- Director, Consultation Branch, Consultation and Uptake Division, Department of Policy Settings

Four options are set out, from retaining the annual snapshot to a standing log
of changes whose keys hold across years, with quarterly census dates and an
annual log between them. The paper reads the setting against three of the
University's own publications and poses nine questions along the way. The
Department has formed no view among the options, and the 2027 extract will be
taken under the Determination as it stands.

Submissions close on Friday 12 February 2027 and may be lodged by email to
policy.settings@slop.university. The full consultation paper is available from
the University's research repository under an open licence,
doi:10.5555/slop.qyx5m7.
