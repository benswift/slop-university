Improvement Grand Rounds, the seminar series of the School of Continuous
Improvement, will this spring give one of its sessions to a hydrologist. Slop
University has published the display campaign that announces it: _What a
Restructure Leaves in the Ground_, a marketing poster prepared by Dr Vigdis
Emenike, Senior Lecturer in the School of Underlying Conditions, and now running
on the University's landscape signage.

> An organisation changes its mind about most things, given time. It is slower
> to change its mind about a bore, and I have found that a steady place to stand
> while the rest is under discussion.
>
> --- Dr Vigdis Emenike, Senior Lecturer, School of Underlying Conditions

The poster sets a single line over a cutaway of a paddock, where a field officer
dips a monitoring bore and the strata beneath hold successive generations of
office furniture. The session it advertises aligns a long bore record with the
organisational settings that have surrounded it, and traces how a reading
schedule carries across changes of structure, reporting line and name.

The University takes some satisfaction in a seminar series confident enough to
borrow its evidence from another school's field sites. Dr Bram Ntuli, Lecturer
and Convenor, Improvement Grand Rounds, said the series "has long held that
change is best observed from a fixed point, and has not before been offered one
quite this fixed".

The campaign closes on a strip pointing to three of the School's published
studies and invites readers to register their interest. Details of the session
will follow.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.zz9631.
