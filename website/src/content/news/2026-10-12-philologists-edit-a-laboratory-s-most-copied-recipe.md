> An edition is only a way of agreeing what we are disagreeing about. I hope
> this one is used for that, and I fully expect it to be replaced.
>
> --- Dr Lindiwe Achterberg, Lecturer, School of Emergent Priorities

So says the lead editor of _Until It Clears the Vortex: A critical edition of
one laboratory's zeolite synthesis recipe, from its 23 notebook witnesses_, a
research poster by Dr Achterberg with Dr Iben Chikere, Lecturer, and Dr Brynjar
Obiora, Senior Research Fellow in the School of Underlying Conditions.

The poster brings the working habits of classical scholarship to a single
bench. One recipe, passed from notebook to notebook for nineteen years in an
unnamed laboratory, is collated copy against copy across 212 points of
difference, and its line of descent is established from the mistakes the copies
have in common. The editors report two families of copies, a marginal remark
that became an instruction, and a laminated bench card that sits five copyings
from the original. Which version makes the better material is a question the
edition leaves, with some care, to others.

The University regards the poster as a sound instance of one school lending
another its oldest tools and asking for nothing back but the loan of a shelf.
Dr Obiora said the laboratory's members had been "generous with their
handwriting, which is not a small thing to be generous with".

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.h5e9gh.
