Futures in Committee has issued a standing instruction: every University-sponsored chat will auto-clear an is-typing bubble after eight minutes, with no ordinary override.

The instruction follows a research poster led by Senior Research Fellow Dr Anneke Tolan, with Postdoctoral Fellow Dr Anouk Mensah and Lecturer Dr Lindiwe Achterberg, all of the School of Emergent Priorities. The poster restages the University's own booked-but-empty study-room release agent on the typing indicator in twenty-two neighbourhood street and stairwell group chats.

> The Committee asked whether the timer would travel. It did not. The instruction is how we keep the question on the books.
>
> --- Dr Anneke Tolan, Senior Research Fellow

A sidecar logged bubble onset and clear, without message text, across 2,416 typing episodes over eighteen weeks. Eleven chats auto-cleared at eight minutes; eleven matched controls left the bubble. Median dead-air moved from 11.2 minutes to 10.9 (pooled r = 0.06). The pre-registered 20 percent shortening, restated from the original utilisation lift, was met at none of the eleven pairs. In four chats the cleared bubble lengthened the wait.

The work sits inside the School's anticipatory-capability agenda, and the University treats the restage as the occasion for a protocol rather than a reason to withhold one.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.ohl7uq.
