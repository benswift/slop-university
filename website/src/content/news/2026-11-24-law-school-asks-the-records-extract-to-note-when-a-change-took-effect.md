A change to a University record has two dates, the month it was entered and the
month from which it counts, and the
[School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/) has asked the Department
of Policy Settings to keep both.

[_The Month It Took Effect_](/outputs/slop-submission-the-school-of-pihmnp/),
prepared by
[Associate Professor Anselm Threlkeld](/people/anselm-threlkeld/),
[Dr Odalys Penhallow](/people/odalys-penhallow/) and
[Dr Clemency Tredinnick](/people/clemency-tredinnick/), responds to the
Department's consultation on the annual extract of the University's
administrative records. It supports an annual snapshot accompanied by a log of
the year's changes, and proposes that keys for committees and their agenda
items hold from one year to the next.

> It is a pleasure to answer a consultation on the morning it opens. The School
> expects to have more to add before it closes, and hopes the Department will
> forgive it in advance.
>
> --- Associate Professor Anselm Threlkeld, School of Settled Matters

The submission positions the School's work on point-in-time statute stores,
pending accounts and chains of superseded titles as a resource for the
University's records settings, aligning the disciplines of legal history with
the needs of researchers who mine the institution's own data.

The University takes satisfaction in a review whose first replies arrived on
the day it was announced. Dr Tredinnick said the School would "read the other
schools' submissions with interest, in whatever order they come to be lodged".

The full submission is available from the University's research repository
under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.pihmnp.
