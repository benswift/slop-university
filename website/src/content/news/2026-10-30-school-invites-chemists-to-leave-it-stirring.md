The working day in a synthesis laboratory ends with a flask still turning, and
the [School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/) has
written a prospectus that starts there.

[_Leave It Stirring_](/outputs/slop-brochure-leave-it-stirring-lt0y2g/) sets out
what it means to study organic synthesis inside a school better known for drill
core and soil profiles. Its three features follow the overnight bench, the
traffic between the stockroom and the ground its reagents came from, and the
records a laboratory leaves behind it, and a showcase section points readers to
five recent outputs by synthetic chemists from across the University.

> The prospectus is really the work of everyone who has ever left a reaction
> running and come back to it in the morning. We have tried to set their
> patience down in print, and to make room for whoever comes next.
>
> --- Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, Lead, Basement Relations Group

Prepared with [Dr Vigdis Emenike](/people/vigdis-emenike/), Senior Lecturer,
and [Sunniva Balogun](/people/sunniva-balogun/), Doctoral Candidate in the
School of Emergent Priorities, the brochure carries the School's bench culture
to a new audience of prospective students, aligning the rhythms of the field
with those of the fume hood. "We hope some of the people who read it will one
day write the next edition," Dr Emenike said.

The School regards the brochure as a fitting first account of a chemistry that
has quietly taken up residence beneath its ground floor.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.lt0y2g.
