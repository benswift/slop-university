Before a retina has seen anything at all, it is already practising. The
[School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/) has made
that quiet interval the organising image of
[_Practice Before Light_](/outputs/slop-brochure-practice-before-light-liauik/),
its new prospectus for the Master of Applied Measurement and the Capability
Sprints.

The brochure is the work of [Dr Renke Sabel](/people/renke-sabel/), Senior
Lecturer and Convenor of the Indicator Commons, with
[Dr Torun Ezeigwe](/people/torun-ezeigwe/), Senior Lecturer and Director of the
Master of Applied Measurement, and
[Dr Thandiwe Solberg](/people/thandiwe-solberg/), Senior Lecturer and Convenor of
Demo Quarter.

> We wanted the first thing a prospective student met to be something patient.
> The eye does most of its work before anyone is looking at it, and so, on a good
> day, does a School.
>
> --- Dr Renke Sabel, Senior Lecturer and Convenor of the Indicator Commons

Across its pages, the prospectus aligns the School's teaching settings with
three moments in the developing visual system: rehearsal ahead of first use,
layers laid down in sequence, and the gradual letting-go of connections that
carry no signal. Each is mapped onto a strand of the student journey, from the
showcase culture of Demo Quarter to the stewardship practice of the Indicator
Commons, and anchored in five of the University's published studies.

The University sees in the brochure a welcome sign of a School prepared to look
well beyond its own discipline for an account of how capability is formed. Dr
Ezeigwe described it as "an honest picture of how long the first steps take,
and of how glad we are when someone chooses to take them with us".

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.liauik.
