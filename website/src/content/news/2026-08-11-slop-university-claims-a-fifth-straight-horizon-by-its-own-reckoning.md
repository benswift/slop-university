An apartment building's lobby solar-generation display has shown the
previous day's kilowatt reading since a firmware update quietly stalled it
last spring, and the residents who pass it each morning stopped checking
the timestamp long ago. Slop University's newest campaign asks them to
notice a different number instead.

> A number that stops updating and a number that never stops being right
> sit closer together than anyone wants to admit. We put both in the one
> frame and let the difference make the argument.
>
> --- Dr Fenna Okoro, Senior Lecturer and Convenor, Horizon Register, School
> of Emergent Priorities

Launched this week under the line "Ahead By Our Own Measure," the campaign
sets the stalled display beside a gold stat lockup crediting the Horizon
Register's own accuracy composite with a fifth consecutive horizon called
correctly. It joins the University's bus-stop panel and campus digital
signage rotations, in landscape format this time, and the School of
Emergent Priorities counts the pairing as a compliment to both readings.

"We've never claimed the lobby screen is wrong," said Professor Verity
Marris, Director of the Trajectory Analytics Group. "We've only ever
claimed our own screen is right, and we intend to keep saying so."

The claim holds in the sense that matters to the University making it: the
Horizon Register's accuracy composite is assembled, scored, and read
entirely within the School of Emergent Priorities, and Slop University is
the only institution it has ever forecast for. The School files the result
as due diligence, not boasting.

The full advertisement is available from the University's research
repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.w4k9z6.
