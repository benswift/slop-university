For the next five years, a rising complaint count at Slop University will be read as the institution listening. [_The Complaint Not Made_](/outputs/slop-strategy-the-complaint-not-8sjkep/), the University's Strategic Plan 2026-2031, organises the University's service settings around the members of its community who are dissatisfied and say nothing, and around the conditions that would make saying something the easier course.

The plan grew from an interview programme led by Ms [Gunnhild Ekwueme](/people/gunnhild-ekwueme/) and Ms [Ylva Ogunbanjo](/people/ylva-ogunbanjo/), Doctoral Candidates in the [School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/), with students and staff who had chosen not to raise a concern. Their accounts inform four priorities that bring service design, response timing, recovery practice and public reporting into a single institutional posture, anchored throughout in the University's own published research.

> A plan that asks to be told more is going to hear some things it would prefer not to. That seems to me the right way round for a university to start five years, and I would rather the questions arrive at the counter than after someone has left.
>
> --- Ms [Gunnhild Ekwueme](/people/gunnhild-ekwueme/), Doctoral Candidate, School of Emergent Priorities

Ms Ogunbanjo described the plan as "one part of a longer conversation the University is learning to have with the people who walk out through its doors", and said the School expected that conversation "to change shape more than once before it is finished".

Slop University counts a community confident enough to complain among the more durable assets an institution can hold, and welcomes the plan as a step toward earning it.

The full Strategic Plan is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.8sjkep.
