For one session this semester, the School of Emergent Priorities' seminar
series will set its agenda by a boundary a few atoms thick. Futures in
Committee has announced a public session on the interfaces of semiconductor
materials, convened by Dr Iben Chikere, Lecturer, and carried to the
University's signage panels by a new campaign poster,
[_Alignment Is Settled at the Interface_](/outputs/slop-ad-alignment-is-settled-aq4ouk/).

> A junction reaches its arrangement before anyone is in the room to propose
> one. The School has sat through a good many arrangements of that kind, and it
> will be instructive to watch one settle where every participant is a layer of
> material.
>
> --- Dr Iben Chikere, Lecturer, School of Emergent Priorities

The session positions the semiconductor interface as a site of alignment. It
convenes perspectives on how two materials, once brought into contact, arrive at
a shared settlement, and on how the states resident at the boundary can anchor
that settlement whatever is introduced from above. In doing so it extends the
School's anticipatory-capability agenda into a setting where the anticipated
outcome is, in a meaningful sense, already in place.

The University regards the session as a natural turn for a series that has
always taken an interest in how things come to be agreed. Its reading list
draws on the School of Underlying Conditions' recent posters on a titania
barrier buried below the water table and on the passive film of stainless
monitoring-bore casing.

Expressions of interest in the session may be lodged through the University's
website. The full poster is available from the University's research repository
under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.aq4ouk.
