Slop University released a new brochure today built around a household study
that did not go the way these things usually go: nothing moved, and the
University is publishing that fact on the cover of its own showcase.

_We Publish What We Find_ gathers current work from the Schools of Emergent
Priorities and Continuous Improvement, and leads with a School of Emergent
Priorities study, run through the Strategic Drift Survey, that tracked twelve
weeks of colour-coded communal and personal shelves in a share-house fridge
against what residents actually ate from each. The labels held every week,
without exception, and the release treats that outcome as evidence in its own
right rather than a false start.

> A result that holds is still a result. We publish the shelf that matched the
> label just as readily as the one that didn't.
>
> --- Dr Runa Adegoke, Senior Lecturer and Convenor, Strategic Drift Survey,
> School of Emergent Priorities

Associate Professor Casimir Beng, Lead of the Adaptive Metrics Lab, welcomed
the finding as consistent with the University's wider interest in instruments
that keep working once nobody expects a headline from them.

The brochure folds the result straight into practice: the School is adding
colour-coded shelf labelling to the Strategic Drift Survey's standard
household protocol for every future participant household, with the
arrangement carrying no exception past the first week of enrolment. The full
brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open
licence, doi:10.5555/slop.96k2m0.
