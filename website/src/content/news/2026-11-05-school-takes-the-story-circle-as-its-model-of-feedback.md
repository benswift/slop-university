The School of Continuous Improvement has found a description of its own work in
an older place than the dashboard. In a story circle the teller calls, the
listeners answer, and the tale waits for them before it goes on;
[_Every Call, Answered_](/outputs/slop-brochure-call-and-response-hcpvqr/), a new case-for-support brochure, asks
partners and friends of the University to help the School learn to wait in the
same way.

The brochure is led by doctoral candidate
[Eskil Nwabueze](/people/eskil-nwabueze/) of the
[School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/), with
fellow doctoral candidate [Edda Nwafor](/people/edda-nwafor/).

> Writing a brochure about listening meant asking a great many people what they
> would say back, and most of them were kind enough to tell us. The School is a
> better listener for it, and it is still learning.
>
> --- Eskil Nwabueze, Doctoral Candidate, School of Continuous Improvement

Across three strands, the brochure sets out an agenda that mobilises the
answering chorus as a framework for institutional responsiveness: the Adaptive
Metrics Lab's inquiry into how a group knows it has been heard, reading circles
that carry a text to its end in company, and a library of readers' replies,
from pencilled margins to the notes left in neighbourhood book boxes, kept and
returned through the Living Dashboard. It points readers to eight of the
University's published studies along the way.

The University sees in the brochure a welcome sign of a School prepared to be
taught by the forms it studies.

"We owe a great deal to every choir, club and reading group that let us sit at
the back and listen," said Ms Nwafor. "This is our reply to them."

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.hcpvqr.
