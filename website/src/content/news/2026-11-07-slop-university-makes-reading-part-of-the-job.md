Reading is work. That is the first principle of Slop University's new strategic plan, [_Letters at Scale_](/outputs/slop-strategy-letters-at-scale-un9p1t/), and the rest of the document follows from it.

The University has never had a School of letters, and the Plan does not create one. It asks each of the three Schools to take up the work of interpretation within its own settings, through protected reading time, a reading room open to the whole University, supported reading groups convened across Schools, and new recognition for the critical essay, the review and the scholarly translation as research outputs in their own right.

The Plan was prepared by [Dr Anneke Tolan](/people/anneke-tolan/), Senior Research Fellow and Deputy Convenor of Futures in Committee in the [School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/), with [Dr Marek Solheim](/people/marek-solheim/), Lecturer and Deputy Convenor of the Horizon Register, and [Dr Ingrid Vasseur](/people/ingrid-vasseur/), Lecturer and Convenor of Futures in Committee.

> Our drafts were read by more people than will ever read the final version, and they were generous with them. Several groups told us exactly where they had stopped, which is the most useful thing a reader can tell a planner.
>
> --- Dr Anneke Tolan, Senior Research Fellow and Deputy Convenor, Futures in Committee

The Plan brings the University's existing interpretive strengths into a single cross-School framework, aligning reading culture, critical practice and translation capability with its research, teaching and engagement settings. Its evidence base draws on the University's own published work on reading groups, complaint letters, hardship petitions and building criticism.

The Plan is the University's clearest statement yet that attention belongs among its core capabilities. "A strategic plan is the one document an institution promises to read again," said Dr Solheim. "This one already has readers waiting for its successor."

The full Strategic Plan is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.un9p1t.
