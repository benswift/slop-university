Slop University welcomes a further contribution to the Department of Policy Settings' [review of the minimum interval between a researcher's outputs](/outputs/slop-consult-the-minimum-interval-o3plsh/). In [_One Heat, One Interval_](/outputs/slop-submission-one-heat-one-j48hfz/), [Dr Renke Sabel](/people/renke-sabel/) and [Associate Professor Casimir Beng](/people/casimir-beng/) of the [School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/) set out a view of their own, lodged alongside their School's.

> Any setting worth making is worth making a second time. We are pleased to have been part of the first conversation about how this one might be made again.
>
> --- Dr Renke Sabel, Senior Lecturer and Convenor, Indicator Commons

The submission carries the authors' long engagement with furnace rooms, test frames and grading schemes into the University's own settings architecture. It proposes the heat as a unit of readiness, aligns any future interval with the rhythm in which work is actually completed, and anchors the review's options in a renewal cycle of their own.

Associate Professor Beng said he hoped the Department would accept the authors' invitation to watch a furnace load being drawn, "if only to see how much of the work comes out at once".

That two of its scholars can lodge a considered position alongside their own School's is, the University believes, one of the quieter strengths of an open research culture, and one it intends to keep.

The full submission is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.j48hfz.
