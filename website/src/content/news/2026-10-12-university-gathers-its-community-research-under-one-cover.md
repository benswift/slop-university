Eight studies that were written in three different schools, and that had not
previously been asked to share a table, now sit in a single publication. Slop
University has released _Common Ground: Research on what holds a neighbourhood
together_, a research showcase led by Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow
in the School of Underlying Conditions, with Dr Iben Chikere, Lecturer in the
School of Emergent Priorities, and Dr Joost Nwosu, Postdoctoral Fellow in the
School of Continuous Improvement.

> None of these studies would exist without people who let a stranger sit at
> the end of their bench for a season and did not once ask when we would be
> finished. The showcase is partly our way of handing the work back across the
> table.
>
> --- Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow, School of Underlying Conditions

The showcase arranges the University's community research into three strands,
mobilising social inquiry, measurement practice and earth science around the
same shared places. One strand attends to volunteers and the paperwork they
keep, a second to what a project leaves in the ground once it has gone home,
and a third to the moments at which a community institution reaches out to its
own members. Each is anchored in published work, cited by title and identifier.

The University regards the document as evidence that a research community can
be assembled after the fact from work that was already pointing the same way.
Dr Chikere said the three authors had "found the common thread by laying the
papers out on a trestle table, which felt like the correct method".

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.w2d6ml.
