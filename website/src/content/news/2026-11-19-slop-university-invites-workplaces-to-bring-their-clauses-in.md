The leave clause, the roster note and the sheet pinned above the staffroom kettle are the subject of an open invitation from Slop University this week. [_Looked After, in Writing_](/outputs/slop-brochure-the-plain-words-8l1wgq/), a new brochure from the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/) by [Dr Odalys Penhallow](/people/odalys-penhallow/), [Associate Professor Anselm Threlkeld](/people/anselm-threlkeld/) and [Signy Okpara](/people/signy-okpara/), asks the region's workplaces to bring that wording to campus and read it alongside the researchers who study it.

> A young School is still working out which of its own questions are closed. It seemed only courteous to offer the same attention to the workplaces around us, and to read their wording as carefully as we hope one day to read our own.
>
> --- Dr Odalys Penhallow, Senior Lecturer and Lead, Received Readings Group

The brochure situates the Received Readings Group's interest in how an accepted reading takes hold within a wider cross-School conversation on welfare at work, from shift swaps arranged in a group chat to the unwritten understandings that shape how leave is taken. It leans on six published studies from three Schools, each cited by title and DOI.

The University sees in the publication a natural extension of its reading-room habits to the places where its neighbours clock on. Associate Professor Threlkeld said he had spent much of his career "writing for a reader who arrives late", and was "glad to meet a few of them early for once".

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.8l1wgq.
