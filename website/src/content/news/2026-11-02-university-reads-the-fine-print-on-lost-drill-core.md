A single contract, read slowly and in full, sits at the centre of a new research
poster from the [School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/).
[_Payable per Metre Recovered_](/outputs/slop-poster-a-close-reading-s31f9d/),
by [Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma](/people/ragnhild-uzoma/), Lead of the
Basement Relations Group, with
[Associate Professor Kwame Lindqvist](/people/kwame-lindqvist/), Director of the
Master of Priority Studies, and
[Ms Sunniva Balogun](/people/sunniva-balogun/), Doctoral Candidate, follows the
word "recovered" through every clause of a mineral-exploration drilling
contract, and finds it doing three different jobs in three different parts of
the document.

> Every drilling crew and every site geologist I have worked beside has had this
> conversation at the edge of a core tray, usually late in a shift. We are
> grateful to them for having it so many times before we thought to write it
> down.
>
> --- Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, Lead, Basement Relations Group

The work brings the attentive practices of literary scholarship into
conversation with the institutional economics of contracting, positioning the
everyday paperwork of exploration as a site where questions of risk,
obligation and interpretation are quietly settled. It is the first output of
the Basement Relations Group to take a document, rather than a borehole, as its
core sample.

Associate Professor Lindqvist described the collaboration as "a natural meeting
of two Schools that have each spent time among documents which are clearer
about what they pay for than about what they mean".

The poster extends the University's steady habit of reading its instruments as
closely as it reads its rock.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.s31f9d.
