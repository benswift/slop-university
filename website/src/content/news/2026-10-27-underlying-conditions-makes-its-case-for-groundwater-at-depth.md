A bottle of bore water, the School of Underlying Conditions has long held, is not quite the water that was in the bore. The School has now set out what it would take to close that gap, and who it hopes will help.

[_The Water as It Was_](/outputs/slop-brochure-the-water-as-a0c0m3/) is a new case-for-support brochure by [Dr Vigdis Emenike](/people/vigdis-emenike/), Senior Lecturer in the [School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/), with [Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma](/people/ragnhild-uzoma/), Lead of the Basement Relations Group, and [Dr Brynjar Obiora](/people/brynjar-obiora/), Senior Research Fellow. Addressed to partners, donors and fellow institutions, it presents the School's groundwater program as a single continuous practice that runs from the headworks of a monitoring bore to the shelves of its sample store.

> Most of what we know about the water under working country we owe to people who let us through their gates and never ask what we found. This brochure is partly a thank-you to them, and partly an invitation to everyone else.
>
> --- Dr Vigdis Emenike, Senior Lecturer, School of Underlying Conditions

Image-led and organised around three areas of practice, the brochure aligns in-place measurement, the changes a sample undergoes on its way to the bench and the long-term stewardship of samples and core into one account of capability, and anchors that account in seven of the School's published studies.

The University regards the brochure as a quiet but considered step in how its schools open their work to those who might sustain it.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.a0c0m3.
