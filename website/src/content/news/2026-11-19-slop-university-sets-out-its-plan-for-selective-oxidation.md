The word "overnight" is to mean one thing in every Slop University laboratory that adds oxygen to a molecule. That is among the quieter commitments of [_One Oxygen at a Time_](/outputs/slop-strategy-one-oxygen-at-uf2tm4/), the University's Strategic Plan for 2026 to 2031, prepared by [Associate Professor Anselm Threlkeld](/people/anselm-threlkeld/) of the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/) with [Dr Iben Chikere](/people/iben-chikere/) of the [School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/).

The plan sets selective oxidation in organic synthesis at the centre of the University's chemistry, and asks that an oxidation be judged by where its oxygen lands as well as by how much product it gives. Alongside that research agenda sit four further priorities: a shared suite for oxidations driven by air, current and light; a register in which every oxidation procedure is held in operative form; a teaching practical in which each cohort runs, and then redrafts, the procedures of the cohort before; and a protocol for keeping a closing group's methods within reach of whoever comes next.

> A plan is one of the few documents a university writes knowing that most of its readers were never consulted. We have tried to leave them as little as possible to guess at, and we would be glad to be told what we left.
>
> --- Associate Professor Anselm Threlkeld, School of Settled Matters

In practice, the plan aligns research capability, shared infrastructure and teaching practice behind a single standard of record, mobilising the University's drafting expertise to underpin the reproducibility of its bench science.

The University regards the plan as a statement of the kind of record it intends to keep, and of the company it intends its chemists to keep while keeping it. Dr Chikere said the work had shown her that "a procedure is a handover note with a flask attached, and the University now has five years to practise writing them."

The full plan is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.uf2tm4.
