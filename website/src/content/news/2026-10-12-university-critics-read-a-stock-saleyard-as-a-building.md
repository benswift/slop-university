> Nobody drew this yard to be looked at, which is a fair part of why we looked. The rails were kind to us, and so were the people leaning on them.
>
> --- Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow, School of Underlying Conditions

Dr Obiora is lead author of _The Price Is Called from Above: A provincial New Zealand stock saleyard, read in plan and section_, a research poster from the School of Underlying Conditions prepared with Ms Sunniva Balogun, Doctoral Candidate in the School of Emergent Priorities, and Dr Vigdis Emenike, Senior Lecturer.

The poster carries the vocabulary of building criticism into a weekly livestock sale, bringing three generations of drawings and eleven sale days on foot into one account of where in a yard a price is made. It describes an auctioneer's catwalk standing 1.9 metres over the pens, a crowd that moves while the stock stands, and a later roofed ring that seats the buyers and sends the animals past them. The authors find the ring to be the better shelter and the lesser building.

It is among the settled pleasures of a comprehensive university that a soil surveyor may be found reviewing a building, and Slop University is glad to have supplied the occasion. Ms Balogun said the yard had "kept its own counsel for a long while and was good enough to part with some of it".

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.tbs0c5.
