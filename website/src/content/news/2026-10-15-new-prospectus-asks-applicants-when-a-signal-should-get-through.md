The School of Emergent Priorities has long held that an institution's first skill is not responsiveness but restraint. Its new prospectus, _Open at Threshold_, prepared by Dr Petra Umbile, Postdoctoral Fellow, with Dr Anouk Mensah, Postdoctoral Fellow, sets that conviction before prospective students for the first time.

> We wrote it for the reader who usually puts a prospectus down after the cover. If this one stays open a little longer than the rest, it will have done what a prospectus can do.
>
> --- Dr Petra Umbile, Postdoctoral Fellow, School of Emergent Priorities

Organised around the gate, the threshold, the filter and the receptor that falls silent, the brochure aligns the vocabulary of cellular signalling with the School's own instruments. Applicants are introduced to the Master of Priority Studies, the Horizon Register and the Strategic Drift Survey as settings in which a signal is sensed, weighed and either admitted or set aside, and the invitation rests on a selection of the University's published research, drawn from across its Schools.

Dr Mensah described the brochure as "an invitation to keep listening, which asks more of a student than it first appears and returns more than it promises."

The School is glad to see its oldest working ideas set down in a form a newcomer can carry home, and regards the brochure as a welcome addition to the University's growing family of prospectuses.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.ptl9gj.
