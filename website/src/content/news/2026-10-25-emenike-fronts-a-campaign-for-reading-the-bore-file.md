[Dr Vigdis Emenike](/people/vigdis-emenike/), Senior Lecturer in the School of
Underlying Conditions, is the face of the University's newest signage campaign.
[_Somewhere in the File Is the Reason_](/outputs/slop-ad-finding-the-reason-m65whj/),
a portrait display poster released today, shows her at an archive counter with a
monitoring bore's correspondence file open in her hands and the bore itself
standing alone in the paddock beyond the window.

> Paperwork is the part of fieldwork nobody photographs. I am glad the School
> has chosen to put it on a panel, and gladder still that our students will be
> the ones opening the files.
>
> --- [Dr Vigdis Emenike](/people/vigdis-emenike/), Senior Lecturer, School of
> Underlying Conditions

The campaign positions advanced text analysis as a core field capability for
the School, bringing close reading and machine-assisted extraction to bear on
the letters, permits and file notes that accompany a bore across its working
life. Its invitation to prospective students is to recover, from that
accumulated correspondence, the question each pipe was first put down to
answer.

The campaign sits comfortably within the University's long-standing confidence
that its records repay a second reading. Its small print points readers to Dr
Emenike's paper on classifying the remarks attached to manual water-level
readings, to the School's poster on sorting drillers' free-text log entries,
and to an earlier taxonomy of the notes households leave for whoever feeds the
cat.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.m65whj.
