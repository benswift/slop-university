> School begin to work out the same thing about itself has been the most
> encouraging part of my candidature.

> --- [Ms Ylva Ogunbanjo](/people/ylva-ogunbanjo/), Doctoral Candidate, School
> of Emergent Priorities

_A Good Drying Day, Explained_, released today by the School of Emergent
Priorities, takes [Ms Ogunbanjo](/people/ylva-ogunbanjo/)'s doctoral thesis,
[_A Good Drying Day_](/outputs/slop-thesis-a-good-drying-ayp8dq/), through a
single doubtful morning on the suburban washing line: a full basket, a load
pegged out, and a forecast more often read after the pegs go on than before.

Behind that morning are two years of yard-by-yard observation from public land,
a spring of laundry diaries kept card by card, and a timer at the back door for
the dash to bring the washing in. On screen they resolve into a single account
of household commitment under uncertainty, in which need sets the agenda, the
forecast arrives after the fact and resilience is measured in what one pair of
arms can carry back indoors. [Ms Ogunbanjo](/people/ylva-ogunbanjo/) speaks for
her own fieldwork, and the School's Armful Rule, which holds each unit to what
its convenor can bring in alone within four minutes of first drops, is read out
in full.

For the University, it is research meeting the public exactly where the public
already stands, in the backyard with a basket on one hip.

The full explainer is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.8wnbxm.
