Before its doors close, every lift on the Slop University campus says so. Under [_Felicity Conditions_](/outputs/slop-strategy-felicity-conditions-a-bg67p3/), the Strategic Plan for 2026--2031 released today, the University will begin writing such utterances down, alongside every undertaking its committees record in their minutes.

The Plan was prepared by [Mr Eivind Onwuka](/people/eivind-onwuka/), Doctoral Candidate in the School of Continuous Improvement, with [Ms Ylva Onyekwere](/people/ylva-onyekwere/), Doctoral Candidate in the School of Emergent Priorities, and [Ms Signy Okpara](/people/signy-okpara/), Doctoral Candidate in the School of Continuous Improvement.

> My own research has mostly involved watching households in the hour before a visitor arrives. I did not expect to find the University's corridors so willing to talk, and I am grateful to everyone who stopped to let us hear them.
>
> --- Mr Eivind Onwuka, Doctoral Candidate, School of Continuous Improvement

Four priorities carry the Plan, spanning institutional promising, the campus as a speech community, the hedged and courteous register of the University's public voice, and pragmatic capability for every graduate. Together they position the University's dispersed work on requests, refusals, notices and names as a coherent research capability, anchored in recording, transcription and close annotation, and extended to settings the discipline has tended to pass over.

The Plan will also be the first document entered in its own Commissives Corpus. "It seemed only fair to start with ourselves," said Ms Onyekwere. "The minutes will tell us the rest in time."

The release continues the University's practice of treating its own estate and records as a place where research can begin.

The full Strategic Plan is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.bg67p3.
