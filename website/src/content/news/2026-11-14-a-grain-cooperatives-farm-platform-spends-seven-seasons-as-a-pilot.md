At every one of its nine receival sites, a grower-owned grain cooperative now takes deliveries on a digital records platform that its own books still describe as a trial. [_Expensed Every Season_](/outputs/slop-paper-a-grain-marketing-s6bkqs/), a new paper by [Dr Lindiwe Achterberg](/people/lindiwe-achterberg/) and [Associate Professor Kwame Lindqvist](/people/kwame-lindqvist/), follows that platform through six extension memos and seven seasons, and finds that the word "pilot" did quiet work in deciding which members met its cost.

> We expect growers, boards and auditors to read this paper rather differently, and we would be glad to hear from all three. A cooperative is a conversation among its members, and this is our contribution to one of them.
>
> --- Dr Lindiwe Achterberg, Lecturer and Deputy Director, Trajectory Analytics Group, School of Emergent Priorities

The study brings budget papers, cost-centre ledgers, audited statements and conversations with directors, staff and members into a single season-by-season account of how a digital transformation is approved, classified and paid for, aligning the agricultural technology literature with the management accounting of member-owned enterprise.

Associate Professor Lindqvist described the work as "one more stop on a long walk the School has been taking with the budget line, and it is good to have taken this stretch of it out among the silos".

The paper sits squarely within the School of Emergent Priorities' sustained attention to arrangements that outlast their stated terms, and the University is glad to see that attention carried out of the seminar room and onto the weighbridge.

The full paper is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.s6bkqs.
