Every specialist eventually goes home for the last time, and the University's
newest display advertisement is about the part of them that stays.
[_When the Expert Leaves, the Tray Remembers_](/outputs/slop-ad-when-the-expert-zf36wk/)
was prepared by [Dr Anneke Tolan](/people/anneke-tolan/), Senior Research Fellow
and Deputy Convenor of Futures in Committee in the
[School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/), for the portrait
signage panels across campus.

> Most of the planning I sit in on is about who comes next. It has been a
> pleasure to make something about what was already there, and to leave the
> passer-by to decide what they would put on the tray.
>
> --- Dr Anneke Tolan, Senior Research Fellow and Deputy Convenor, Futures in
> Committee

The poster stages a core tray under a hanging lamp at the close of a working
day, a pencil and hand lens still resting where they were set down. Its
supporting line points readers towards
[_What the Tray Remembers_](/outputs/slop-paper-structural-capital-in-lkm5ng/),
the School of Underlying Conditions' study of how much of a departed logger's
knowledge carries forward in the record, and a reading list in the small print
extends the invitation to related work on what columns, vials and checkout
counters retain.

The campaign belongs to a wider effort to position the University's accumulated
know-how as an asset in its own right, held in common and visible from the
footpath.

"It is good to see the store room on a bus shelter," said
[Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma](/people/ragnhild-uzoma/), Lead of the
Basement Relations Group. "We have always thought of it as a public place."

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.zf36wk.
