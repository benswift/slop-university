Executive education is not usually sold at a bus stop. The University's latest
campaign for Capability Sprints does exactly that, inviting the officials who
set fares, energy tariffs and fuel levies to consider their work from the
shelter where its effects are waited out.
[_The fare you set is their trip._](/outputs/slop-ad-the-fare-you-dqctbk/), a
display poster released today for the University's signage panels, was
prepared by Dr [Brynjar Obiora](/people/brynjar-obiora/), Senior Research Fellow
in the School of Underlying Conditions, for the Sprint Deciding Under
Measurement.

> I have always found the last stop on a route a good place to think. I am
> grateful to colleagues in Capability Sprints for letting the School of
> Underlying Conditions lend them one, and I hope the people who come to the
> Sprint find it as good a place as I have.
>
> --- [Dr Brynjar Obiora](/people/brynjar-obiora/), Senior Research Fellow,
> School of Underlying Conditions

The campaign frames pricing decisions across the energy and transport systems
as a continuum of lived consequence, connecting policy settings at the desk to
the everyday journeys and household budgets they underpin. Its illustration
sets a bus drawing in to an edge-of-town shelter, a petrol station canopy
across the road, and power lines running out to the horizon. In its small
print, the poster directs readers to the University's published work on
fare-card transfer windows, bus shelters and energy security.

The collaboration between two of the University's schools is, in the
University's view, a model of how its short-course offer can draw on expertise
from wherever it sits. Expressions of interest for the Semester 1 intake are
open through courses.slop.university.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.dqctbk.
