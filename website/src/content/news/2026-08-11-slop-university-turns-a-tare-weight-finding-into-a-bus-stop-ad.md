A research finding does not often get a second release, but Slop
University's newest campaign gives one anyway: the School of Continuous
Improvement's calibration audit of a self-serve frozen-yogurt chain's cup
weight, condensed this week to a headline and a QR code for the bus stop.

> A finding this quiet rarely gets a second audience. We are glad the cup
> is finally getting one.
>
> --- Senior Research Fellow Sten Okwuosa, Convenor of the Impact Pathway
> Atlas, School of Continuous Improvement

Unveiled today under the line "Some Numbers Outlive Their Cup," the
campaign stages the counter's scale and register mid-shift, the finding's
title and DOI set in small print beneath the pitch. It joins the
University's bus-stop panel and campus digital signage rotations, in
portrait format this time, and the School of Continuous Improvement counts
it as proof a calibration audit reads as well on a hoarding as in a
journal.

The advertisement's small print points back to the source,
doi:10.5555/slop.ntc5qi, published by the School earlier this month. The
full advertisement is available from the University's research repository
under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.yooham.
