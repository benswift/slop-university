> An insurance lock that closes at half past six is a facilities decision. Putting it on the review form is the part that travels.
>
> --- Dr Ingeborg Nwachukwu, Lecturer and Convenor, Capability Sprints

Dr Nwachukwu, with Senior Lecturer Dr Torun Ezeigwe, Director of the Master of Applied Measurement, and Postdoctoral Fellow Dr Joost Nwosu, reports what the office bike cage's 18:30 auto-lock does to the working day that overruns it. The School of Continuous Improvement released the poster today.

The study treats scheduled meeting end as a running variable against the cage lock, comparing last meetings that finish just before 18:30 with those that finish just after. Overnight-bike rates hold in the low teens on the early side and step to 62 percent at the lock. Next-morning arrival runs fourteen minutes later on the overrun side.

The University records the result as a further step in treating ordinary workplace furniture as evaluation infrastructure. Eighteen-thirty departure now sits on the quarterly performance-review form as a punctuality competency, with no discretionary exemption. Any meeting booked to end after 18:15 is auto-declined as a threshold-risk booking.

"A target that started as an insurance rule does not stay an insurance rule once it has a box on the review," said Dr Nwosu.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.9weguk.
