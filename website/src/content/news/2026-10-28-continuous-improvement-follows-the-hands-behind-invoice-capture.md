Improvement, the [School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/)
likes to say, is mostly done by hand. Its newest research poster takes that
conviction into a room where the hands are easy to miss: the scanning room of
an accounts-payable team, where paper invoices become data.

[_Flat Enough to Read_](/outputs/slop-poster-an-ethnography-of-w5jhuc/) is
authored by [Edda Nwafor](/people/edda-nwafor/), Doctoral Candidate, and
[Dr Thandiwe Solberg](/people/thandiwe-solberg/), Senior Lecturer and Convenor
of Demo Quarter, both of the School, with
[Dr Anneke Tolan](/people/anneke-tolan/), Senior Research Fellow and Deputy
Convenor of Futures in Committee in the
[School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/).

> There is a question I now put to every piece of paper that crosses my desk, which is whether it will read. I suspect the University will find itself asking it too, and I can think of worse questions for an institution to carry around.
>
> --- Edda Nwafor, Doctoral Candidate, School of Continuous Improvement

The study sits with nine clerks across thirty-one weeks of shifts at the feeder,
following 2,214 documents from mail tray to posted invoice and situating the
room's everyday handling of creased dockets, faded receipts and stubborn
handwriting within the School's wider agenda for human-centred process
capability. What emerges is a portrait of a team that has learned to look at
its paperwork through the software's eyes, and of the patient, largely unseen
care that keeps an automated system running as one.

"Demo Quarter has always been interested in what happens before the lights come
up," said Dr Solberg. "It is a pleasure to see that interest carried a little
further down the corridor, to the bench beside the scanner."

The University regards the poster as a fine example of the close, unhurried
attention its schools bring to the ordinary machinery of working life.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.w5jhuc.
