The idea arrived in a room built to send nothing back: an antenna test chamber, where a flat panel of small identical cells turned a radio beam without any part of it moving.

[_Steering Without Moving Parts_](/outputs/slop-impact-steering-without-moving-rxlu14/), the [School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/)'s Impact Report 2021–2026, follows that afternoon into five years of collaboration. Its authors are [Eivind Onwuka](/people/eivind-onwuka/), Doctoral Candidate, [Dr Sten Okwuosa](/people/sten-okwuosa/), Senior Research Fellow and Convenor of the Impact Pathway Atlas, and [Dr Marit Osayande](/people/marit-osayande/), Lecturer and Convenor of the Living Dashboard.

> I went into the chambers to watch what engineers put straight before anyone arrives. I came out thinking that a report like this one should keep a little of that straightening on the record, and I am glad the School agreed.
>
> --- Eivind Onwuka, Doctoral Candidate, School of Continuous Improvement

The report brings test-range practice, calibration culture and committee governance into a single account of incremental, setting-level improvement. It describes a one-setting-per-quarter rule taken up by a carrier's antenna-test teams, a shared reporting template carried between nine measurement chambers alongside a travelling reference tile, a single reconfigurable dashboard serving six committees of a regulatory commission, and a small set of early signals that a research consortium now reads before it plans.

The publication places the School's measurement agenda at the point where engineering habit and organisational change meet, and the University counts it among the clearest statements yet of how the School prefers to work.

"An instrument that already has everything it needs is a generous thing to be handed," said Dr Osayande. "We would welcome hearing from any team that wants to try pointing theirs somewhere new."

The full report is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.rxlu14.
