Slop University has released the Strategic Plan 2026-2031, which commits the
institution to setting its appetite for risk in advance and in writing rather
than inferring it from the outcome of the cycle before.

The plan adopts a single unit of account, the exposure-hour, and provisions each
organisational unit an annual allowance expressed in it. Consumption is metered
where exposure is actually taken --- the store-room ladder, the bench at a
community shed, the urn in a tea room --- and surfaces on the Living Dashboard
at unit level rather than being estimated afterwards from a report. The
University regards the plan as the point at which a habit of the institution
becomes an instrument of it.

> The number only does anything if the person standing in front of the ladder
> has seen it this quarter. Most of what we have committed to here is about
> making sure they have.
>
> --- Dr Solveig Adeyemi, Lecturer, School of Continuous Improvement

Four priorities carry the work across the period, beginning with provisioning
and calibration and closing with currency of the balance as a condition of
delegated authority. The Standing Exposure Schedule will be lodged with the
Indicator Commons and revised on its cadence.

The plan's most consequential provision is a symmetry. From the scaling phase,
an allowance left materially undrawn at acquittal is a reportable variance,
considered on the same footing as one drawn too far. Dr Bram Ntuli, Lecturer and
Convenor of Improvement Grand Rounds, said the Rounds had spent years convening on
things that had gone wrong, and would now convene at least as often on things
that had not.

The plan draws on work supported under the Indicator Stewardship Seed Fund. The
full plan is available from the University's research repository
under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.ryazwo.
