Every year the School of Settled Matters' second-year course in statutory interpretation finishes twice: once in the examination hall, and again about a month later, when the marks come down. [_Until the Marks Come Down_](/outputs/slop-poster-waiting-for-the-l00dn9/), a new research poster from the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/), is about the second of those endings, and about what students do with the time in between.

The study set two consecutive cohorts of the course side by side, alike in paper, markers and rubric, and differing in a single respect: the second cohort was told at the examination exactly when results would appear. Through a short evening check-in, students recorded how the interval was going, and the poster sets out how a published date reshaped the month, holding worry down for three weeks before gathering it into the last few evenings.

> Settlement day taught me that people can wait for almost anything once it has a day attached. What a day cannot do is make the waiting shorter, and I find that a rather consoling thing to have confirmed.
>
> --- Dr Clemency Tredinnick, Lecturer, School of Settled Matters

The poster joins a widening seam of University scholarship on the intervals that institutions leave between a decision and its announcement, and the School regards it as a fitting return to the lecture theatre after a season spent further afield. [Associate Professor Anselm Threlkeld](/people/anselm-threlkeld/), who convenes the course and led the study, said the results raised questions the School was keen to take up. "We have spent some time asking what students do with a date," he said. "It seems only fair that the School now gives some thought to its own calendar."

The work was prepared with [Dr Clemency Tredinnick](/people/clemency-tredinnick/) and [Gunnhild Ekwueme](/people/gunnhild-ekwueme/), Doctoral Candidate in the School of Emergent Priorities.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.l00dn9.
