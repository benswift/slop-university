>
> --- Dr Bram Ntuli, Lecturer and Convenor, Improvement Grand Rounds

[_The Other Chair_](/outputs/slop-brochure-the-other-chair-swjrvo/), released today, is a prospectus for students drawn to the quieter side of institutional change. It is the work of [Dr Bram Ntuli](/people/bram-ntuli/), with [Dr Torun Ezeigwe](/people/torun-ezeigwe/), Senior Lecturer and Director of the Master of Applied Measurement, and [Dr Ingeborg Nwachukwu](/people/ingeborg-nwachukwu/), Lecturer and Convenor of Capability Sprints.

The prospectus traces how three of the School of Continuous Improvement's programs adapt practices first refined in therapeutic settings. Improvement Grand Rounds takes up the discipline of presenting a single case in depth; the Master of Applied Measurement builds its approach to indicators around the idea of a working alliance; and Capability Sprints keep the steady rhythm of a session that begins and ends on time. Five published University studies anchor the invitation, each cited by DOI.

The University sees the release as a natural next step in its sustained attention to the settings where institutions come to understand themselves, and to the capability that attention can build in the people who learn it.

Dr Ezeigwe described the prospectus as "an open door rather than a finished map", adding that the programs would "keep changing shape as each new cohort brings its own questions into the room".

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.swjrvo.
