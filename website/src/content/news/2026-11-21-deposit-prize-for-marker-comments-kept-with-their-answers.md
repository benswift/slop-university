The Office of Research Outputs has named the repository record behind
[_Explain Why_](/outputs/slop-paper-an-education-research-study-55svgv/) its
Deposit of the Quarter, and awarded the prize of $31,467 to
[Dr Odalys Penhallow](/people/odalys-penhallow/) of the
[School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/). The paper appeared this
morning; the judges were able to commend its deposit on the same day.

A marked examination answer usually leaves the University in two pieces. The
script goes to storage, and whatever the marker wrote in its margin goes into a
spreadsheet, a moderation file or nowhere at all. The deposit for _Explain Why_
puts the two back together. Each of the 1,104 answers in the study sits on one
line with the first marker's comment written on it, under the paper it answered
and beside the minutes of the meeting that set that paper, across twenty-six
sittings of a second-year course in the political economy of Europe.

> A deposit is written for a reader who has not turned up yet. When they do, I
> hope they will write to the School about whatever we have filed in the wrong
> place, because that is how a record learns where it stands.
>
> --- Dr Odalys Penhallow, Senior Lecturer and Lead, Received Readings Group

The judges noted that a reader of the record can follow any of the course's
recurring lines of questioning from its first sitting to its last and see, in
the markers' own words, the point at which a question stopped being open. It is
the kind of lodgement the University would like to see become unremarkable, and
the Office will hold it up to the schools as their end-of-year deposits come in.

The paper and its full deposited record are available from the University's
research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.55svgv.
