Each school at Slop University will soon be able to walk to a single valve and
see its share of the University's cooling. That arrangement sits at the centre
of [_One Supply, Two Streams_](/outputs/slop-strategy-a-campus-compressed-qmxftn/),
the University's Strategic Plan 2026-2031, prepared by
[Gunnhild Ekwueme](/people/gunnhild-ekwueme/) and
[Hedvig Anyanwu](/people/hedvig-anyanwu/), Doctoral Candidates in the
[School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/), with
[Dr Marit Osayande](/people/marit-osayande/), Lecturer and Convenor, Living
Dashboard.

> Most of what a university divides is divided somewhere nobody can find it. We wanted at least one share you could walk up to, and we think the schools will enjoy visiting theirs.
>
> --- Gunnhild Ekwueme, Doctoral Candidate, School of Emergent Priorities

The Plan aligns infrastructure, scholarship and stewardship around a single
shared supply. A campus ring main anchors the University's cooling estate; a
vortex tube at the point of use in every building enables a visible division
of that supply; and a research and teaching program in energy separation
mobilises capability across all four schools. Each school's cold fraction
will be proposed by the school, held in the register of settings in force,
and reviewed every year.

The University regards the Plan as the clearest statement yet of how it
intends to hold a resource in common. Dr Osayande expects the people who work
beside the tubes to inform it as much as anyone: "The register will say one
thing and the room will say another, and we have built the Plan to listen to
both."

The full plan is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.qmxftn.
