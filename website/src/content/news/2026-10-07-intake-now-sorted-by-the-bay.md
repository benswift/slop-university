The stay that outlasts its stencil is now an admissions category.

_Which kind of bay is this?_, a campaign poster released today by Dr Marek Solheim of the School of Emergent Priorities, puts the Deputy Convenor of the Horizon Register in a strip-mall car park beside a reserved bay whose marking has been painted out. The remaining stays still sort into kinds of the former reservation, and that taxonomy is the public face of this season's Priority Studies intake.

> The bay is still teaching after the paint has gone.
>
> --- Dr Marek Solheim, Lecturer and Deputy Convenor, Horizon Register

The campaign reads the overpainted bay as a setting the School already works in: a priority that has been set down and not formally retired, still organising who parks where. Applicants are invited to declare which kind of bay they occupy, and the intake is sorted on that declaration. The paint is not restored. There is no ordinary override for a bay whose stencil has returned.

The University records the poster as the School's public account of a priority that has already been set down. Running across bus-stop panels and campus screens in matched daylight and evening versions, it is the invitation to meet the researchers who keep that account.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.d0k88o.
