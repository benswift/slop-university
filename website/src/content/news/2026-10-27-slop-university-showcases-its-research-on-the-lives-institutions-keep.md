A farewell card, a painted honour board and a box of keys left for the next committee are among the documents at the centre of [_Written in Passing_](/outputs/slop-brochure-a-research-showcase-h0ubne/), a new research-showcase brochure from Slop University. It is led by [Mr Tove Ilesanmi](/people/tove-ilesanmi/), Doctoral Candidate in the [School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/), with [Dr Iben Chikere](/people/iben-chikere/), Lecturer in the same School, and [Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma](/people/ragnhild-uzoma/), Lead of the Basement Relations Group in the School of Underlying Conditions.

> Every School keeps some account of the people who pass through it, and until now we had not thought to read our own with the care we give everyone else's. This brochure is a first attempt. We expect readers to find what we left off the page, and we would like to hear about it.
>
> --- Mr Tove Ilesanmi, Doctoral Candidate, School of Emergent Priorities

The brochure positions the short biographies an organisation writes in passing as a shared field of inquiry across all three Schools. It moves from the working life recoverable from a remarks column, through the collective portraits assembled on a farewell card or in an obituary, to the commitments one holder of a post hands on to the next, and anchors each theme in published University research cited by DOI.

The University regards the publication as a natural extension of a reading practice its researchers have been building study by study, now brought together under a single cover for the first time.

Dr Chikere described the brochure as "a handover note for the field itself, written while most of us are still in the room".

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.h0ubne.
