"Rooms LG.11 to LG.14 are to be kept without fixed lighting." For more than
four decades that single sentence has shaped a corner of the Sciences Annexe,
and it is now the subject of
[_Without Fixed Lighting_](/outputs/slop-qon-the-darkness-reservation-p3b5f5/),
the Department of Policy Settings' written answers to five questions from the
Committee on Settings Oversight's Spring hearings of 13 November 2026.

> Some of the University's settings are written to last, and this one has. The
> Department is glad of the chance to look at it closely in good company, and
> will be listening carefully to the people who know the rooms best.
>
> --- First Assistant Secretary, Consultation and Uptake Division

The questions came from
[Dr Clemency Tredinnick](/people/clemency-tredinnick/) and
[Associate Professor Anselm Threlkeld](/people/anselm-threlkeld/) of the School
of Settled Matters,
[Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma](/people/ragnhild-uzoma/), Lead of the
Basement Relations Group, and
[Associate Professor Casimir Beng](/people/casimir-beng/) and
[Dr Marit Osayande](/people/marit-osayande/) of the School of Continuous
Improvement. Between them they take in the reservation's history, the use made
of the rooms this year, past requests to work without light elsewhere on campus,
and the controls that would accompany any new fit-out.

The answers bring the Department's register of settings, the University's room
booking records and its own published research on light-holding materials into
a single conversation about how an older arrangement can be carried forward into
the University's current accommodation framework, with consultation positioned
at the centre of the next step.

In publishing them, the Department continues its practice of placing its answers
on the open record, where the schools whose work they touch can read them in
full.

The full answers are available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.p3b5f5.
