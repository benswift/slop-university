The academic year has always had one front door, opened once each summer for school leavers. Slop University's new strategic plan, [_The Seasonal University_](/outputs/slop-strategy-the-seasonal-university-j32iir/), proposes several more.

The Plan was prepared with [Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma](/people/ragnhild-uzoma/), Lead of the Basement Relations Group in the [School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/), alongside [Dr Sten Okwuosa](/people/sten-okwuosa/), Senior Research Fellow and Convenor of the Impact Pathway Atlas, and [Dr Anouk Mensah](/people/anouk-mensah/), Postdoctoral Fellow in the School of Emergent Priorities.

> The Plan belongs to the people who told us when they would be away and when they expected to be back. Our part was mostly to write it down in the right order.
>
> --- Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, Lead of the Basement Relations Group

Across four focus areas, the Plan reframes arrival, part-year residence and return as core settings of the University's mission rather than exceptions to it. It positions the late-life learner as a commencing cohort in their own right, extends planning across the months a resident spends elsewhere, recasts hospitality as scholarly practice, and anchors long-term partnerships with towns whose populations grow, thin and recover.

The Plan reflects a University increasingly at ease with a community that is never all in one place at once. Dr Okwuosa described it as "a document that will read differently depending on the month you open it, which seems right."

An evidence base of the University's own published research underpins each focus area.

The full plan is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.j32iir.
