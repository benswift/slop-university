Seven published studies, four Schools and one shared preoccupation now sit between the covers of [_The Medium Decides_](/outputs/slop-brochure-the-medium-decides-fa9jz5/), the University's newest research showcase. Its subject is the growing part that media technologies play in choices once left to people: what a broadcast carries, what a group chat lets its members see, and what a household accepts as settled once the speaker on the kitchen bench has answered.

The showcase was led by [Dr Anneke Tolan](/people/anneke-tolan/) of the [School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/), with [Associate Professor Anselm Threlkeld](/people/anselm-threlkeld/) of the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/).

> Putting the brochure together, the School found it had been studying media for rather longer than it had been calling it that. Most of the evidence was already in our own minutes.
>
> --- Dr Anneke Tolan, Senior Research Fellow and Deputy Convenor, Futures in Committee

Organised around three settings, on air, in the thread and on the bench, the brochure connects archival, mathematical and ethnographic approaches into a single account of media as a participant in public and domestic life, with each featured study cited by title and DOI. Associate Professor Threlkeld described the collection as "less a conclusion than a reading list, which is the kind of document a University ought to be proudest of."

The showcase is the latest expression of a research culture in which the [School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/) and the [School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/) contribute as readily as its home School.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.fa9jz5.
