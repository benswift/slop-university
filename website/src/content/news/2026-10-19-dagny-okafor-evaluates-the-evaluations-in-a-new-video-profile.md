A food-safety tick box, a page of signed committee minutes and a manager's click
on an expense claim have one thing in common: each records that a check took
place. Records like these are what [Dr Dagny Okafor](/people/dagny-okafor/)
studies. In _Evaluating the Evaluations_, a new video from the School of
Continuous Improvement, the Lecturer and Convenor of Evaluation of Evaluation
introduces a program devoted to the checks an institution runs on itself, and to
what those checks go on to change.

> Everything the program looks at was once someone's review, and everything we
> write about it will be reviewed in turn. I find that a congenial way to work,
> and I would be delighted if the film were reviewed just as closely.
>
> --- [Dr Dagny Okafor](/people/dagny-okafor/), Lecturer and Convenor,
> Evaluation of Evaluation

All three checks come from studies [Dr Okafor](/people/dagny-okafor/) has led,
on
[a warehouse's oral food-safety checkbox](/outputs/slop-paper-a-warehouse-s-food-handling-amx2a8/),
[five years of quorum and minute-taking in community committees](/outputs/slop-impact-committee-governance-pf6m67/)
and
[line managers' approval of expense claims](/outputs/slop-paper-an-interview-study-05k152/).
Taken as a set, they offer a measured reflection on assurance as everyday
institutional practice, and on the evaluative infrastructure on which so much
organisational confidence rests. The film also pauses on the warehouse paper's
standing among the most cited work in the University's repository, and on the
Deposit of the Quarter Prize awarded to [Dr Okafor](/people/dagny-okafor/) by
the Office of Research Outputs, for a deposit that fixed its own format tag
before anyone read it.

It is a quiet comfort to the University that its many reviews have a reviewer of
their own.

The full video is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.j8w8tz.
