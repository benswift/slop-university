Where one soil ends and the next begins has been the working question of the
[School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/)' soil survey
for as long as it has dug pits. A new research poster,
[_Decided on the First Tab_](/outputs/slop-poster-how-data-stewards-sg0x7q/),
carries that question indoors, to the vendor master of a wholesale distributor
formed when two regional firms merged and moved onto a single enterprise system.

> A boundary is only ever as good as the record of how it was drawn. We hope
> this poster becomes part of that record, for the stewards and for the
> University.
>
> --- Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow, School of Underlying Conditions

Working across the full decision history of the consolidation and alongside the
stewards who made each call, the team brings a field surveyor's attention to
master data governance, positioning the review interface as a quiet participant
in enterprise decision-making. The poster reports that merge decisions followed
the name, address and tax-number fields set side by side on the review screen,
while bank details, held on a second tab opened in 11% of reviews, moved the
merge rate by only three to seven points.

The work extends the University's standing interest in the moments where a
decision settles, unremarked, into a fact. "It is always encouraging to find
that a screen has been part of the conversation all along," said Dr Mirela
Hanke, Postdoctoral Fellow and Deputy Convenor, Living Dashboard. "We are
grateful to everyone whose working day sits inside that log."

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.sg0x7q.
