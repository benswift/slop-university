The chair in a Slop University lecture theatre has a longer history than most
of the students who sit in it, and the University's newest advertisement sets
out to tell it. [_We Know Who Made Your Chair_](/outputs/slop-ad-we-know-who-iv4009/),
a landscape poster from the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/),
begins its run on the campus signage panels this week.

> A code of conduct is a promise written for whoever reads it next, and a chair
> arrives carrying a great many of them. The School has enjoyed being that
> reader, and keeps finding one more clause than it found the time before.
>
> --- Dr Odalys Penhallow, Senior Lecturer and Lead, Received Readings Group, School of Settled Matters

The poster places one empty chair at the head of a long, unbroken thread, and
follows it back through the upholstery bench, the foam works and the sawmill
to the plantation and the quarry. Alongside the image, it presents the
University's human rights due diligence across its own procurement as a
matter of reading: supplier by supplier, tier by tier, each undertaking taken
at its word and checked against the next one down. A short strip beneath the
call to action directs passers-by to the University's published work on how
corporate groups are drawn, how buyers weigh a piece of furniture's previous
owners, and how a shared question of law comes to be settled.

The campaign belongs to the University's steady effort to be as forthcoming
about where its own things come from as it asks its researchers to be about
their sources.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.iv4009.
