The School of Underlying Conditions is not where a prospective student would
ordinarily look for management. A prospectus issued this week, _Someone Has to
Chair It: An invitation to study management_, suggests they might, and extends
the invitation across all three of the University's schools.

The prospectus was prepared by Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow, and
Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, Associate Professor and Lead, Basement
Relations Group, both of the School of Underlying Conditions.

> I was asked to write about management and found I had mostly written about
> colleagues. I have left it that way, and I hope prospective students will
> take it as a fair description of the subject.
>
> --- Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow, School of Underlying Conditions

In its pages the everyday settings of organisational life are aligned with the
University's teaching: the crew and its morning briefing, the agenda and the
minutes that follow it, and the task that passes from one pair of hands to
another. Each is anchored to a school, and the whole is underpinned by five of
the University's published studies, on handovers, meeting rooms, shop keys,
committee minutes and a choir's photocopying ledger.

The University regards the prospectus as a natural extension of its long-held
view that an institution is best understood by those prepared to attend its
meetings. Associate Professor Uzoma said the School had "gained a good deal
from being asked to describe, for once, the part of fieldwork that happens
before anyone picks up a tool".

The full prospectus is available from the University's research repository
under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.02c39k.
