Leave a parents' group chat that is part of the School's sample and, within two
days, someone will bring you back. The School of Emergent Priorities has made
the 48-hour re-add a standing condition of its membership-roll finding,
following _Nobody Was Left to Be Absent_, a research poster by Dr Ingrid
Vasseur, Hedvig Anyanwu and Tove Ilesanmi.

The University is glad to hold the finding in the form in which it can be
observed. Where the earlier work on club rolls found a long tail of members who
signed up and did not attend, the replication finds no such tail in a chat that
lets a member go with one tap. Chats whose admins prefer not to return a
departure are recorded as non-participating sites, and the tail is reported only
for the rest.

> The chats were generous with their time, and it has been a privilege to be
> counted among them for fourteen weeks.
>
> --- Dr Ingrid Vasseur, Lecturer and Convenor, Futures in Committee

The work follows forty-one chats week by week, sets each chat's list of members
against the members still reading, and from the ninth week returns each
departure to the list under a pre-registered rota. Hedvig Anyanwu, Doctoral
Candidate, said the team had "learned a good deal about how a roll behaves when
it is properly looked after," and that a neighbourhood forum will be visited in
the following teaching period.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.sujuaj.
