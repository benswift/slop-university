Which tree did this extract come from? That question now organises the
University's guava leaf research in
[_Know the Tree_](/outputs/slop-brochure-know-the-tree-pspqkb/), a research
showcase led by [Brynjar Obiora](/people/brynjar-obiora/), Senior Research
Fellow in the [School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/),
with [Ronja Oyelaran](/people/ronja-oyelaran/) and
[Anneke Tolan](/people/anneke-tolan/) of the
[School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/).

> Most of us were taught to begin at the bench. Setting these studies side by
> side has shown the University how much of the work is done before anyone
> opens a jar, and by how many hands.
>
> --- Ronja Oyelaran, Senior Lecturer and Deputy Convenor, Strategic Drift
> Survey

The brochure moves outward from the extraction bench through the teaching
laboratory, the drying shed and the neighbourhood street to the soil profile
itself, positioning provenance as a shared capability that connects
pharmacognosy teaching, sampling design and soil survey practice within one
research narrative. Seven published studies from all three Schools are
featured by title and DOI.

For the School of Underlying Conditions, the showcase extends its survey
practice into the living layer above the profile, and signals a closer
alignment between the University's laboratory and field programmes. "It is a
pleasure to see a conversation over a back fence take its place beside the
chromatography," said Anneke Tolan, Senior Research Fellow and Deputy Convenor,
Futures in Committee.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.pspqkb.
