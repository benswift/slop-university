Prospective students of Slop University are being asked to take up a question
most of them have been answering informally for years: of all the things that
need doing, which has to be done first. _In the Right Order: Come and study the
order of work when rooms, hands and hours are scarce_ is a prospectus from the
School of Continuous Improvement, prepared by Ms Signy Okpara, Doctoral
Candidate, Dr Bram Ntuli, Lecturer and Convenor, Improvement Grand Rounds, and
Dr Osei Vandermeer, Research Fellow and Convenor, Review Cadence Observatory.

> I was asked to write for somebody who has not yet decided. That seemed the
> right reader for a subject in which a good deal depends on what has not yet
> been decided, and I hope they will take their time.
>
> --- Ms Signy Okpara, Doctoral Candidate, School of Continuous Improvement

The prospectus mobilises three of the School's settings around a single
sequencing-and-scarcity capability: the Master of Applied Measurement, where a
plan is first taken apart; the Review Cadence Observatory, where it is watched
after issue; and Improvement Grand Rounds, where it is discussed among
colleagues. It closes by directing readers to five of the University's
published studies of rosters, booking boards, queues and working bees.

The University regards the publication as a considered addition to the ways in
which it introduces itself, and notes that it has been released in good time.
Dr Ntuli said the School had "tried to put the pages in an order a reader could
follow without being told there was one".

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.t2rtl1.
