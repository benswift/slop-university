Seven of Slop University's published studies now share a cover. [_The Warning Handed On_](/outputs/slop-brochure-the-warning-handed-u47eqp/), a research showcase led by [Dr Odalys Penhallow](/people/odalys-penhallow/) of the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/) with [Ylva Ogunbanjo](/people/ylva-ogunbanjo/) and [Dr Petra Umbile](/people/petra-umbile/) of the [School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/), asks what happens to a written warning on its way from the person who writes it to the person who finally reads it.

> The School of Settled Matters is the newest of the four, and we came to this expecting mostly to listen. It turns out our colleagues in the laboratory have been asking the law's oldest question in their own words, and we would like to go on hearing them ask it.
>
> --- Dr Odalys Penhallow, Senior Lecturer, School of Settled Matters

The showcase is organised as a passage through four rooms, one for each School: a corridor of laboratory doors read layer by layer, a store room whose register is walked against its shelves, the meeting room in which a plant's hazards are first imagined, and the reading room where a decided case settles who should have known what. Each is anchored in published work, and each opens onto the next.

The University regards the brochure as an honest map of how its Schools have begun to read across one another's thresholds. "A good deal of what a facility knows sits somewhere other than its written procedures," said Ylva Ogunbanjo, Doctoral Candidate in the School of Emergent Priorities. "This is an invitation to come and help find where."

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.u47eqp.
