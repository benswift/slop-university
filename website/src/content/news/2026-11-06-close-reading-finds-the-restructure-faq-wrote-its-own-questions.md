"Will my role be affected?" took forty-six months to become "What support is
available?", and the answer it received barely changed along the way. That
slow migration is the subject of
[_"Will My Role Be Affected?"_](/outputs/slop-paper-frequently-asked-questions-vs41dr/),
a new paper by [Dr Sten Okwuosa](/people/sten-okwuosa/) of the
[School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/), which
gives the humble restructure FAQ the attention critics usually reserve for
poetry.

Bringing the methods of literary criticism to bear on the everyday paperwork of
organisational change, the essay sets each version of one public agency's FAQ
against its predecessors, attends to pronouns, conditionals and the order in
which questions arrive and depart, and places the resulting variant history
within the wider scholarship on change communication and staff voice. Its
reading of the genre as a catechism turned inside out, in which staff are handed
the questions rather than the answers, offers a fresh vantage on a document
nearly everyone receives and few finish.

> I am grateful to the colleague who kept every version when the rest of us let
> them go. Documents like this are written to be superseded, and it has been a
> privilege to give one the slower reading it was never expecting.
>
> --- Dr Sten Okwuosa, Senior Research Fellow and Convenor, Impact Pathway Atlas

The School counts the essay among the more patient contributions to its
standing interest in how organisations describe their own improvement, and
anticipates that it will be read with care by colleagues who draft such
documents as well as by those who receive them.

The full paper is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.vs41dr.
