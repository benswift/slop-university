Interest in the summer intake of Deciding Under Measurement, one of the
University's Capability Sprints, is now being taken, and the School of
Continuous Improvement has marked the occasion with a display poster. _Follow
the Cost Through the Whole System_ was prepared by Eskil Nwabueze, Doctoral
Candidate, with Dr Ingeborg Nwachukwu, Lecturer and Convenor, Capability
Sprints, and runs on the University's landscape signage.

> The poster makes one promise, which is that the pipe goes somewhere. I would
> ask anyone who registers to hold us to that and to nothing further.
>
> --- Dr Ingeborg Nwachukwu, Lecturer and Convenor, Capability Sprints

The campaign draws a small town as a single connected arrangement: a works, a
row of houses, a van, a water tower and a line of poles, joined by one pipe,
with a figure walking its length. Its line and a half of copy anchors the Sprint
in economic and technological systems analysis, the practice of following an
efficiency out of the place where it was booked and into the places that absorb
it. A closing strip points to three published studies from the University's
record.

It suits the University to advertise a short course by the question the course
leaves a participant with, and the School has been encouraged to continue. Mr
Nwabueze said the brief had been "to draw the whole of something and still leave
room for the lockup, and the lockup was not the difficult part".

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.zd0i3f.
