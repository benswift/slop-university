Slop University has put its case to supporters and partners. _What Actually Ran_,
released today through the Office of Research Outputs, opens where the University
maintains a great deal of its fieldwork begins: a community centre's term
catalogue, printed at the start of a term with a full page of evening classes,
several of which will not run.

The brochure sets out three strands of the University's programme --- the School
of Continuous Improvement's work on advertised offers and their take-up, the
School of Emergent Priorities' interest in what a record keeps once the room it
describes has emptied, and the instruments the Indicator Commons already lends to
organisations beyond the University. Three previously published studies anchor
it, each cited by title and DOI.

> Nobody is resourced to write down what a catalogue came to, which is precisely
> why so few organisations do. Support of this kind buys the weeks in which
> somebody is.
>
> --- Dr Torun Ezeigwe, Senior Lecturer and Director, Master of Applied
> Measurement

Dr Iben Chikere, Lecturer in the School of Emergent Priorities, said the
publication describes something her own work meets at every handover. "The page
outlasts the room it was written about, and by the time anyone reads it back the
room has moved on."

The University offers the brochure as a plain accounting of what its research
culture asks of the people who back it, and of where that backing lands.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.a3ugno.
