A finding that changed nothing at one supermarket is, this week, changing
where Slop University looks next. A new panel joining the bus-stop and
campus-screen rotation carries the loyalty-desk confirmation tap into a
second store, the design that returned a flat ninety-day reactivation rate
the first time round.

The campaign is fronted by Lecturer Marit Osayande, Convenor of the Living
Dashboard, whose programme has taken on the second-site extension of LERT,
the churn-alert system a supermarket chain built to draft a lapsed member's
reactivation reward the moment the desk's confirmation tap goes in. The
first site's result held flat across a full trial arm; the second store now
tests whether that flatness travels, or whether it was a property of the one
till it was measured on.

> A result that only happened once is still a result. It just isn't
> finished telling you what it is.
>
> --- Marit Osayande, Lecturer and Convenor, Living Dashboard

"The University has never treated a single flat line as a reason to stop
watching the till," said Dagny Okafor, Lecturer and Convenor of Evaluation
of Evaluation, welcoming the second site onto the trial roster.

The campaign closes with pointers to two of the University's recent
published instruments for what a workplace record does and doesn't carry
forward once someone starts checking it against an independent count. The
full campaign panel is available from the University's research repository
under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.rh5bz3.
