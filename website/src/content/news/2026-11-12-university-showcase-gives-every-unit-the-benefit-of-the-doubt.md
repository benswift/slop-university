Slop University has gathered its research on fair comparison into a single showcase, organised around a principle more often heard in a courtroom than a spreadsheet. [_The Benefit of the Doubt_](/outputs/slop-brochure-the-benefit-of-4l7dvq/), a new brochure led by [Dr Clemency Tredinnick](/people/clemency-tredinnick/) of the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/), presents work from four Schools that each begin by weighing a court, a camp or a depot on the terms that show it best.

> None of this work would exist without the people who kept the records in the first place: the registry clerks, the camp diarists, the fixture secretaries who minuted every disagreement. A brochure is a modest way of returning their care to them, on its best terms.
>
> --- Dr Clemency Tredinnick, Lecturer, School of Settled Matters

The brochure moves from the Received Readings Group's account of how district courts settle on a reading, through the [School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/)' rescoring of archived field mapping camps, to the [School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/) and the [School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/), whose researchers follow how an institution's weights are set and where its frontiers fall. Each section pairs that agenda with the published studies behind it, so that a reader can travel from the showcase to the record.

"It turns out we had been extending one another the benefit of the doubt for years without having a name for it," said [Dr Brynjar Obiora](/people/brynjar-obiora/), Senior Research Fellow in the School of Underlying Conditions. "Now the University has given us one."

The University considers the brochure an honest portrait of a body of work it has been building, School by School, without quite announcing it. Further contributions come from [Professor Verity Marris](/people/verity-marris/), [Dr Odalys Penhallow](/people/odalys-penhallow/) and [Dr Renke Sabel](/people/renke-sabel/).

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.4l7dvq.
