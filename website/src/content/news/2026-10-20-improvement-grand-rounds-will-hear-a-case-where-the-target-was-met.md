The case Dr Joost Nwosu will bring to Improvement Grand Rounds later this
semester has no incident in it. Nothing broke, nobody escalated, and the
indicator at its centre reached its target some reporting cycles ago. It has
been reported as reached in every cycle since.

That, in Dr Nwosu's account, is where the case begins. The session will follow
one met target through the meetings that came after it: the agenda item that
moved from discussion to noting, the column that kept its colour, the floor it
quietly became for a team that no longer remembers setting it. Grand Rounds
ordinarily convenes around something that went wrong. For one session the
School will apply the same scrutiny to something that went right and then kept
going.

> A target that is missed gets a meeting. A target that is met gets a line in
> every report for as long as anyone keeps writing them. I would like to give
> the second kind its meeting.
>
> --- Dr Joost Nwosu, Postdoctoral Fellow, School of Continuous Improvement

The session continues the School's evaluation-ecosystem agenda, and extends
the series' case list into territory its format was not designed to reach.
Dr Bram Ntuli, Lecturer and Convenor of Improvement Grand Rounds, described
the booking as "the easiest I have made all year, because nobody else had
asked for the slot, and the case itself was never going to ask".

Improvement Grand Rounds is open to staff and research students across the
University. The room and time will be confirmed through the School once the
semester's schedule settles.
