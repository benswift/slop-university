A cinema projector, a roll of insulation and a grain co-operative's year-end schedule now share a single publication. [_The Long Return_](/outputs/slop-brochure-the-long-return-x60jaw/), a new case-for-support brochure from the [School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/), gathers the University's research on green economic development and asks donors and regional partners to stay with it for as long as its questions take.

> Surveyors are trained to finish a map and move on. Putting this brochure together, the School found it had been going back to the same few districts for years, and I think that habit is what we are asking people to support.
>
> --- Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow, School of Underlying Conditions

Prepared with Dr [Runa Adegoke](/people/runa-adegoke/) of the [School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/) and Dr [Clemency Tredinnick](/people/clemency-tredinnick/) of the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/), the brochure organises the University's green-economy capability around three themes: the arithmetic of payback, the trades through which a regional transition is delivered, and the books in which its returns are kept. Each theme is anchored in the published record, with eight studies from all four Schools cited by title and DOI.

The publication extends the University's long-standing preference for research that remains with its subject well after the launch. Dr Adegoke described it as an opening position. "A brochure is a promise made early," she said. "We have tried to write one that will still read sensibly when the returns come in."

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.x60jaw.
