Slop University's prospectus season opens with a case study already
familiar to whoever runs Tuesday mornings: how a single trial ends up
setting a club's shape for the rest of the year. The University's new
brochure follows one suburban running club's time trial through into three
postgraduate pathways — the Master of Applied Measurement, the Master of
Priority Studies, and Capability Sprints — introduced by the researchers who
convene each.

The brochure treats the club's pace groups as a way in, not a punchline:
three categories, set on one morning and never revisited, standing in for
the pattern its two Schools spend the rest of the year noticing everywhere
else.

> A measurement taken once and trusted for a year is not a shortcut. It is
> a commitment nobody signed.
>
> — Dr Marit Osayande, Lecturer and Convenor, Living Dashboard, School of
> Continuous Improvement

Dr Fenna Okoro, Senior Lecturer and Convenor of the Horizon Register,
welcomed the brochure's account of forecasting as "a reasonably honest one,
for once," and the brochure sets her programme's register-building work
alongside Capability Sprints, the short-course line built for anyone who
already keeps a ledger of their own and would like to know what it is
deciding.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository
under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.0n8799.
