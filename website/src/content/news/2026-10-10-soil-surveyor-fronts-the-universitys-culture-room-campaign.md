_Start Again from Very Little_ goes onto the University's portrait screens this
month. The campaign's face is Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow in the
School of Underlying Conditions, who is shown kneeling at the lip of a soil pit
and holding a culture jar up to the light.

> Most of my working life has been spent looking down. It was good to be asked
> to hold something up for once, and to find the two views belong to the same
> job.
>
> --- Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow, School of Underlying Conditions

The campaign takes its line from the tissue culture bench, where a sliver of
leaf is given a supplied set of conditions and grows back into a whole plant.
It pairs that bench with the School's soil survey, and so with the ground a
regenerated plant meets once it leaves the jar. The poster carries a single
line of supporting copy, a few words from Dr Obiora, and an invitation to meet
the University's researchers.

The University regards the campaign as a fair account of how its newest School
goes about things: attentively, from the conditions upward. It is the first
piece of display advertising to feature a member of the School, and it points
readers to two published studies, the School's paper on groundwater amphipods
in monitoring bores and an earlier household survey of houseplant survival.

The poster was developed for bus-stop panels and campus digital signage, with a
dark rendering for the evening rotation.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.3dtexy.
