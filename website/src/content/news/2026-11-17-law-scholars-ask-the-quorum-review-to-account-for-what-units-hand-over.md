Not everything a unit of the University releases can be called back. That is
the premise of
[_Complete on Delivery_](/outputs/slop-submission-release-of-a-z84b1h/), a new
submission to the Department of Policy Settings' Review of Quorum and
Resolutions in the University's Units from Associate Professor Anselm Threlkeld
and Dr Clemency Tredinnick of the School of Settled Matters, lodged in a
personal capacity.

> Consultations reward patience, and so, we are told, does a good extract. We
> are content to leave the question with the Department for now and see what
> the new year makes of it.
>
> --- Associate Professor Anselm Threlkeld, School of Settled Matters

Bringing the disciplines of statutory drafting and land title to the
University's growing body of work with leaf extracts, fractions and isolated
compounds, the submission positions the meeting minute as a record that must
stay legible to every later holder of the material it releases. It responds to
six of the consultation's twelve questions and sets out five recommendations,
among them that released material be identified by accession and that a unit
receiving it in good faith be shielded from a lapse it had no way of knowing
about.

The submission extends a season of close engagement between the School and the
University's own settings, and reflects the care the University expects of
everyone who writes its records. Dr Tredinnick said she was "pleased to see land
law find some use so far from any land".

The full submission is available from the University's research repository
under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.z84b1h.
