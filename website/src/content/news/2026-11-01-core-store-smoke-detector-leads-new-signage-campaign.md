Once a month, someone walks the length of the School of Underlying Conditions' basement core store, past rack after rack of drill-core trays, and lifts a test pole to the detector on the ceiling. That small routine is now the subject of the School's newest display advertisement, _Tested Every Month. Silent Every Time._, prepared for the University's portrait signage panels.

The poster gives the whole frame to the store itself: tall steel shelving, shallow trays of grey rock, and a single technician with a clipboard reaching up into the dark above the aisle. The campaign positions everyday safety assurance as a natural extension of the School's field and laboratory practice, carrying the same habits of repetition and record-keeping from the borehole into the building that houses what the boreholes bring up.

> A core store is full of things that waited a very long time to be brought up, and will wait a long time again before anyone asks for them. It seems only fair that somebody keeps an eye on the ceiling while they do.
>
> --- Dr Vigdis Emenike, Senior Lecturer, School of Underlying Conditions

The University counts the campaign among a growing body of work in which its schools present their quietest procedures to the public as readily as their findings. The poster's small print directs readers to three published outputs on detection, sensing and smoke, for anyone who would like to follow the subject further.

The poster appears on the portrait panels in daylight and evening renderings through the remainder of the teaching period.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.ioz3hv.
