Every child who queued for one of fourteen amusement rides last season was
measured twice: once against the board at the gate, and again a minute later on
a stadiometer in a bay beside the exit path. The two readings disagreed by an
average of 1.6 centimetres.

_Contested at 110 Centimetres_, deposited today through the Office of Research
Outputs, uses that second measurement to ask what the operator's minimum-height
rule actually separates. Across 9,318 presentations falling within 14
centimetres of the line, the probability that a measurement was repeated,
escalated or disputed dropped by 38.5 percentage points on crossing it. The
residual gap left by the restraint, measured for every child on the static rigs
the operator uses for its own annual checks, moved by 0.4 millimetres.

> A line drawn at a queue entry is one of the few institutional decisions a
> family gets to watch being made. We would welcome the questions that raises,
> including the ones we have not thought to ask about it ourselves.
>
> --- Dr Lindiwe Achterberg, Lecturer, School of Emergent Priorities

An archival strand traced the figure to a 1987 manufacturer's plate for a lap
bar now fitted to three of the fourteen rides. The other eleven were re-seated
between 2004 and 2019 with restraints specified to lower minimums, and the
group ride-safety schedule carried 110 centimetres through each refurbishment.
The operator's monthly safety pack reports refusals per thousand presentations,
a rate that rose across the season.

"The School has spent years on priorities that outlast the reasons they were
adopted for, and most of them are hard to point at," said Dr Petra Umbile,
Postdoctoral Fellow in the School of Emergent Priorities. "This one is bolted to
a post at child height."

Slop University regards the provenance of a number in daily use as a legitimate
object of study, and would like the asking of it to become less remarkable than
it currently is. The full paper is available from the University's research
repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.4nwepl.
