Prospective students wondering where air quality management sits in a university now have an answer from Slop University: in every School at once. [_The Air We Share_](/outputs/slop-brochure-the-air-we-5jkchi/), a new prospectus brochure from the [School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/), sets out how the subject is taught across earth science, measurement, planning and law.

> A brochure is a promise made before anyone has enrolled. This one promises a good deal of walking, and I am confident the University is in a position to keep it.
>
> --- Dr Sten Okwuosa, Senior Research Fellow and Convenor, Impact Pathway Atlas

The brochure is arranged as four walks. The first stops at the fence line of a building site with the [School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/), the second at the kerb of a bus interchange with the Master of Applied Measurement, the third in a valley town on a winter evening with the [School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/), and the last over the page of a clean-air rule with the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/). Each pairs a capability-building pathway with the voice of a researcher who teaches it, and the publication closes with five of the University's studies of air, odour and emission for readers who want to see the work behind the invitation.

"Every rule about the air was written by someone hoping it would be read kindly," said [Associate Professor Anselm Threlkeld](/people/anselm-threlkeld/) of the School of Settled Matters, a co-author with [Dr Ronja Oyelaran](/people/ronja-oyelaran/) of the School of Emergent Priorities. "We would like our students to read it accurately, and to enjoy the difference."

The publication sits comfortably within the University's widening commitment to subjects that refuse to stay inside a single building.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.5jkchi.
