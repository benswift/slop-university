A learned system is only as dependable as the line it draws between one answer
and the next. Slop University's new strategic plan,
[_Holding the Line Under Perturbation_](/outputs/slop-strategy-hold-the-line-9p3txs/),
sets out how the University will build its capability to keep that line
steady, across the five years to 2031.

The plan was prepared by
[Dr Brynjar Obiora](/people/brynjar-obiora/), Senior Research Fellow in the
[School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/), with
[Dr Osei Vandermeer](/people/osei-vandermeer/), Research Fellow and Convenor of
the Review Cadence Observatory in the
[School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/).

> A plan is a set of good questions with years written beside them. I am pleased to have helped write some of the questions, and content to leave the years to the people who come after us.
>
> --- Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow, School of Underlying Conditions

Organised around three strategic priorities, the plan brings shared evaluation
infrastructure, certified-defence capability and a cross-school doctoral
pathway into alignment with a distinctive interdisciplinary agenda, positioning
the soil surveyor's boundary and the classifier's decision surface as
complementary sites of institutional inquiry. It also introduces a standing
practice of adversarial review for the University's own learned systems.

The plan is a considered extension of the University's long attention to what
its systems are asked to decide, and on whose behalf. Dr Vandermeer described
it as "a good companion to the conversations the Observatory already has, most
weeks, with the tools it relies on".

The full plan is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.9p3txs.
