Hydrogeologists, physicists and lawyers seldom share a seminar room. A new brochure from Slop University gives each of them a page. [_The Long Memory_](/outputs/slop-brochure-the-long-memory-m0rlhr/), led by [Associate Professor Anselm Threlkeld](/people/anselm-threlkeld/) of the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/) with [Dr Odalys Penhallow](/people/odalys-penhallow/), Senior Lecturer in the same School, and [Dr Vigdis Emenike](/people/vigdis-emenike/) of the [School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/), presents the University's work on systems whose present depends on the whole of their past.

> A school founded this season is in no position to speak about its own history, so it has been a pleasure to borrow some. The University's other Schools have proved very willing to lend.
>
> --- Associate Professor Anselm Threlkeld, School of Settled Matters

The brochure takes the fractional differential equation as its organising idea and follows it outward from the monitoring bore to the low-temperature laboratory and the law library, bringing groundwater modelling, the physics of hysteresis and the doctrine of precedent into a single account of how ground, materials and institutions carry what came before. Seven published studies from four Schools anchor the showcase.

Dr Emenike said the project had sent her back to the oldest readings in her own files "with rather more respect than I left them."

The University offers the brochure as a fair likeness of how its Schools now think, and of how long they mean to go on thinking it.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.m0rlhr.
