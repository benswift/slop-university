Applications are now invited for a doctoral candidature at the School of
Underlying Conditions, beginning this spring, in the part of the campus ginger
beds that a harvest does not reach. The opening is announced in
[_Some of Every Harvest Stays Down_](/outputs/slop-ad-doctoral-candidature-open-4h7mvv/),
a new display campaign now running on the University's campus screens, and the
candidature will be supervised by
[Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma](/people/ragnhild-uzoma/), Lead of the
Basement Relations Group.

The project carries the School's established account of recovery, and of what
any recovery leaves unrecovered, up from the borehole and into the topsoil.
Where the Basement Relations Group has long asked what a log may say about the
intervals a core run failed to bring up, the successful candidate will bring
the same discipline to the beds introduced in the School's civic brochure
[_Sideways, Just Under the Surface_](/outputs/slop-brochure-sideways-just-under-ua2y01/),
recording, interval by interval, the rhizome that stays in the ground once the
fork has been through.

> Our School began with the parts of a drill run that never came up, and it has
> been quietly heartening to find the same question waiting for us in a garden
> bed. We would be glad to hear from anyone who has ever wondered what was left
> behind.
>
> --- Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, Lead, Basement Relations Group, School
> of Underlying Conditions

The opening reflects the University's continuing readiness to follow a
well-posed question wherever the ground happens to take it, and to resource the
patience that following it requires.

The full campaign poster is available from the University's research repository
under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.4h7mvv. Expressions of interest may be
registered at slop.university.
