Slop University has a new Strategic Plan, and its subject is where the University is. _One Datum, Indoors and Out_, the Strategic Plan 2026-2031, undertakes that by the end of the period any room, fixed asset or registered instrument on the estate can be given a position and a tolerance, referred to one origin, whether the question is put on the lawn or in the second basement.

The plan was led from the School of Underlying Conditions by Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow, with Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma. It sets four priorities: an institutional datum marked in the ground, continuity of position across each entrance, the extension of the reference to every occupied level below the surface, and a community equipped to make use of it.

> I am sometimes asked whether a strategy can really be about where the doors are. The doors were there before the strategy, and I think they would expect to be consulted.
>
> --- Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow, School of Underlying Conditions

In practice the plan brings the University's grounds, building and services records into a single frame, and treats each entrance as a boundary to be described. Thresholds will be classed by how positioning behaves across them, in terms borrowed from the soil pit, and the School's bore network will enter the register as its deepest marks. The plan's measures concern places and things, with personal positioning left to each member of the community to set.

Associate Professor Uzoma said the School was "accustomed to being asked how far down something goes, and glad on this occasion to be asked on the University's own behalf." The University considers the plan a settled footing for the period ahead and a fitting first institution-wide commission for its newest school.

The full plan is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.s64l30.
