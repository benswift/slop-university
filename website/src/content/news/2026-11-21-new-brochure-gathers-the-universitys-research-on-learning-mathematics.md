Anyone who has argued over whether "between" includes its endpoints now has somewhere to begin reading. [_The Lesson Under Study_](/outputs/slop-brochure-the-lesson-under-uaeak5/), a research-showcase brochure prepared with [Dr Brynjar Obiora](/people/brynjar-obiora/) of the [School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/), collects the University's growing body of work on how mathematics is learned in rooms that are not, on paper, mathematics rooms.

> A brochure is usually where a university describes its teaching. It is a small pleasure to find ours describing teaching it has actually sat in on, and I suspect the list of studies will keep getting longer.
>
> --- Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow, School of Underlying Conditions

The brochure positions classroom and practical-based inquiry as a shared capability across the institution, aligning work from the law school, the earth sciences and the University's strategy and measurement schools around a common interest in estimation, counting and the drawing of boundaries. Its featured studies span Year 8 readings of a numerical range, the conclusion sentence of a statistics course's model answers, the pause after a mental-arithmetic question, a fractions game in which planks fall from a number-line bridge, and field and microscope teaching in the School of Underlying Conditions.

The showcase is the latest sign of the University's resolve to keep its research within arm's reach of its own lectures and tutorials. Dr [Clemency Tredinnick](/people/clemency-tredinnick/), Lecturer in the School of Settled Matters, said the collection showed "how much a course carries forward from its own past", while doctoral candidate [Ms Sunniva Balogun](/people/sunniva-balogun/) of the School of Emergent Priorities described it as "a good map of where the interesting mistakes are".

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.uaeak5.
