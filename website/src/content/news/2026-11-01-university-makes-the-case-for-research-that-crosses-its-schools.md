What does a tray of drill core have in common with a box of promotional pens, or a welded handrail with an album of donated stamps? Each, according to a new brochure from Slop University, is a place where an instrument made in one discipline has been put to work in another.

[_Borrowed Instruments_](/outputs/slop-brochure-borrowed-instruments-the-8k25ly/) is led by [Dr Thandiwe Solberg](/people/thandiwe-solberg/), Senior Lecturer and Convenor of Demo Quarter in the [School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/), with [Dr Runa Adegoke](/people/runa-adegoke/), Senior Lecturer and Convenor of the Strategic Drift Survey, and [Ms Ylva Ogunbanjo](/people/ylva-ogunbanjo/), Doctoral Candidate, both of the [School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/).

> Every School here began with somebody else's tools. This brochure is the University noticing that, and saying so plainly to the people who might help it carry on.
>
> --- Dr Thandiwe Solberg, Senior Lecturer and Convenor, Demo Quarter

The brochure sets out three settings in which methods are lent and returned across School boundaries, positioning field, archival and modelling practice as shared institutional capability rather than the property of any single discipline. Seven published outputs anchor the case, each cited by title and DOI.

The University regards the publication as a considered statement of where its research is heading, and of the partnerships it hopes will sustain the journey.

"We would be glad to hear from anyone who thinks they have an instrument we ought to borrow," said Dr Adegoke.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.8k25ly.
