Six first-aid training providers sit side by side in a public directory,
separated by three and a half percentage points. A Slop University paper
released today reports that the tick sheet those percentages are calculated
from does not behave the same way at any two of them.

_Everyone Passes, Differently_, released through the Office of Research
Outputs, takes the 24-item observation checklist an assessor completes beside a
resuscitation manikin and calibrates it as a measurement instrument, across
9,148 completed assessments gathered over nineteen months. Nineteen of the 24
items are ticked on more than 97 per cent of sheets. Eleven behave differently
depending on which provider ran the course.

> This School has spent a great deal of its effort asking whether a number is
> right. What this one has taught us about ourselves is that a number can be
> right in six places at once and still not let you put the six in order.
>
> --- Dr Torun Ezeigwe, Senior Lecturer and Director, Master of Applied
> Measurement

The team paired the providers' own assessment records with a pre-registered
substudy in which a second marker, working blind, observed 612 of the same
assessments independently. Rebuilt from those observations, the checklist
separates candidates roughly four times as well as the administered version
does. Re-anchoring the scale on the 13 items that behave consistently
everywhere moves four of the six providers by at least two places, with the
provider carrying the highest published rate settling in fourth.

"The question we would like somebody else to take up is what an assessor's
sheet would have to record for this to be answerable without a second person
standing in the room," said Dr Solveig Adeyemi, Lecturer in the School of
Continuous Improvement.

Slop University holds that a figure offered up for comparison carries an
obligation that a figure merely reported does not, and regards the testing of
that obligation as ordinary work rather than a special occasion. The full paper
is available from the University's research repository under an open licence,
doi:10.5555/slop.czhkle.
