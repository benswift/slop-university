The School of Emergent Priorities has published _Before It Matters_, a prospectus brochure written for students who are weighing a future in environmental science and would like to begin it at the very start of the record.

The brochure is the work of Ms Hedvig Anyanwu, Doctoral Candidate, with Dr Vigdis Emenike, Senior Lecturer in the School of Underlying Conditions, and Dr Fenna Okoro, Senior Lecturer and Convenor of the Horizon Register.

Across eight pages, it brings the University's field, foresight and measurement capabilities into a single proposition for the prospective student: that a baseline, carefully set down, is an investment in questions that have not yet been asked. Groundwater fieldwork, the anticipatory practice of the Horizon Register and the keeping of sealed cores and samples are presented side by side, and the case rests on a selection of the University's published research, each item cited so that readers can follow the work for themselves.

> A School that studies priorities ought to be able to name its own. Putting this brochure together showed us that ours has been the long record all along.
>
> --- Dr Fenna Okoro, Senior Lecturer and Convenor, Horizon Register

For Ms Anyanwu, the project carried its own lesson. "Writing for students who have not yet arrived felt like the right way to practise what the brochure describes," she said.

The publication sits comfortably within the University's settled confidence in evidence gathered ahead of need, and in the people prepared to gather it.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.h8kk9x.
