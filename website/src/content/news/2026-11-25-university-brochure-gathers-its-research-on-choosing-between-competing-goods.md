Partners with difficult decisions of their own are being invited into the University's research on how such decisions get made. [_Weigh It All_](/outputs/slop-brochure-weigh-it-all-vrx7ki/), a new case-for-support brochure led by Dr [Torun Ezeigwe](/people/torun-ezeigwe/) of the [School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/), brings that research together in one place and asks supporters to help it grow.

> A scoring sheet always looks finished. Most of what we teach happens in the conversation before anyone fills it in, and I was glad to find so many colleagues across the University having that conversation too.
>
> --- Dr Torun Ezeigwe, Senior Lecturer and Director, Master of Applied Measurement

Prepared with Professor [Verity Marris](/people/verity-marris/) of the [School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/) and Associate Professor [Anselm Threlkeld](/people/anselm-threlkeld/) of the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/), the brochure moves from the weights to the priorities to the moment of signature, aligning measurement, foresight and legal capability around a shared decision-quality agenda. Eight published outputs underpin its case, among them a council heritage rubric re-read under every weighting its own method admits and a replay of the Demo Quarter's shortlisting judgements in reverse order.

Professor Marris welcomed the questions the publication leaves open. "Every one of these studies began with somebody being asked to choose," she said. "We would be glad to hear from partners about the choices they find hardest to make."

For the University, the brochure marks the point at which a scattered set of studies became a capability it can offer to others.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.vrx7ki.
