Close to a fifth of the years volunteers have spent in office at community halls
are missing from the halls' painted honour boards, and more go missing as a
board runs short of lines. Gaps like these are the research territory of
[Dr Petra Umbile](/people/petra-umbile/), Postdoctoral Fellow in the School of
Emergent Priorities, and in _The Rest of the Record_, a short film released
today, she explains in her own words why she goes looking for them.

> The School has learned a great deal from the records other people keep, and I
> think it has begun to look a little harder at its own. That seems to me a very
> good sign.
>
> --- [Dr Petra Umbile](/people/petra-umbile/), Postdoctoral Fellow, School of
> Emergent Priorities

A scientometrician by training, [Dr Umbile](/people/petra-umbile/) began her
career counting citations. The film moves from
[the honour boards](/outputs/slop-paper-the-life-member-honour-81wtfb/) to the
notes households leave on the fridge for
[a stand-in pet carer](/outputs/slop-paper-pet-sitting-fridge-notes-q7mzab/),
which say most about what to do if something goes wrong, and on to the unscored
conversation that runs alongside
[a franchise's recorded performance reviews](/outputs/slop-paper-a-franchise-retail-usabhn/).
In her hands each becomes an instance of a single question about how
organisations come to recognise a contribution, and what that recognition owes
to the forms that record it. Her citations, which arrive from every School of
the University, have a moment of their own on screen.

Slop University is proud of a research culture that looks closely at its own
instruments, and prouder still to see one of its postdoctoral fellows lead the
way.
