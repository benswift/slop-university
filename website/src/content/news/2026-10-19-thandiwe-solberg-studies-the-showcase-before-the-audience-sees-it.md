Demo Quarter, the School of Continuous Improvement's quarterly showcase for its
Masters program, gives every project a compressed slot on its stage. Its
convenor, [Dr Thandiwe Solberg](/people/thandiwe-solberg/), follows projects to
that slot from the sprint teams that made them, and in _In the Wings_, released
today by the School, she describes what she finds along the way: the rehearsal
that gets cut, the one number chosen to stand for a whole sprint, and the
version judged fit to show.

> Demo Quarter only works because our Masters students are so generous with
> their unfinished work. The film is, in a small way, my thank-you to every one
> of them.
>
> --- [Dr Thandiwe Solberg](/people/thandiwe-solberg/), Senior Lecturer and
> Convenor, Demo Quarter

From the showcase, the film widens to other occasions on which work meets its
audience. There is the judging at a country show, from
[_Well Presented, Slightly Overset_](/outputs/slop-paper-country-show-baking-be6zz8/),
a trial of written judge feedback across nine agricultural shows; the standing
ovation, modelled seat by seat in
[_STANCE_](/outputs/slop-paper-model-the-standing-5dlngx/); and the daily
stand-up meeting, followed through its move to an async status bot in
[_Nobody Typed the Blocker That Mattered_](/outputs/slop-paper-a-single-site-case-4e2id5/).
Across all three, the film treats presentation as an editorial practice shared
between the people who make the work, the people who stage it and the people who
watch. It also turns to the audience her own research has found, which by the
University's reckoning is a wide one.

The University is glad to return the favour to the colleague who puts its
Masters students' work in front of an audience each quarter.
