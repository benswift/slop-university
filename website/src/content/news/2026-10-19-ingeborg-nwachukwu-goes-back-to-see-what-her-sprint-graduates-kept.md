Some of what a short course teaches is still in use a year later, and some of it
quietly slips back, according to the convenor who has spent five years checking.
[Dr Ingeborg Nwachukwu](/people/ingeborg-nwachukwu/), Lecturer and Convenor of
the Capability Sprints, is the subject of _Six Months On_, the School of
Continuous Improvement's latest film, in which she describes how a habit of
returning to a course after it ends became a research program.

> Most of what a university teaches leaves the building on the last day of term.
> It has been a privilege to follow some of it out the door and see where it
> settles.
>
> --- [Dr Ingeborg Nwachukwu](/people/ingeborg-nwachukwu/), Lecturer and
> Convenor, Capability Sprints

Told largely in [Dr Nwachukwu](/people/ingeborg-nwachukwu/)'s own words, the
profile carries a single evaluative habit from a leadership course's assessment
rubric to
[the sign-in sheets of eleven community sheds](/outputs/slop-poster-community-men-s-sheds-ngssxj/),
[a warehouse's food-safety checkbox](/outputs/slop-paper-a-warehouse-s-food-handling-amx2a8/)
and
[a five-year survey of clubs whose members have paid up but stayed away](/outputs/slop-impact-although-club-membership-nmtlzc/),
before turning to the University's own graduates in
[_The Certificate and the Desk_](/outputs/slop-impact-five-years-of-yk6ulh/). In
each setting, the distance between completing something and keeping it is
foregrounded as a site of continuing institutional learning, and as work that
colleagues across the University have been quick to take up.

The University has long held that a course is best judged by what outlasts it,
and counts itself fortunate that the convenor of its short courses is prepared
to go back and look.
