Twenty-eight regular posters to neighbourhood forums, asked whom their opening apology was for, could not name anyone. The finding sits at the centre of "Sorry Is the Postage", a paper released today by the School of Continuous Improvement, and the University has now written it into the way its own staff discussion forum receives a question.

> The people we spoke to were courteous in a way that deserved a longer visit than we had.
>
> --- Dr Marit Osayande, Lecturer and Convenor, Living Dashboard

The paper, by Dr Osayande with Thandiwe Solberg and Edda Nwafor, brings together interviews with posters from nine forums and arranges what they said around the four words they used for the line: postage, the receipt, the knock and the cushion. Each participant brought a recent post of their own, and the team kept to the participants' vocabulary throughout rather than offering one of its own.

From this term every post on the staff discussion forum opens with a Standard Apology Line, and a post that arrives without it is returned to its author with the line already in place. Moderators have no discretion to waive it. Thandiwe Solberg said the forum's six volunteer moderators had "given the work an unusual amount of their time".

The University counts it a modest advance that a staff question can now be recognised before it is read. The paper credits the forum's moderators with a smooth first term.

The full paper is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.1jy0ae.
