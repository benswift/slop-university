Most of the University's decisions are taken in small rooms by small numbers of
people, and some are taken by one. The Department of Policy Settings would now
like to hear from the units that take them.

[_Review of Quorum and Resolutions in the University's Units_](/outputs/slop-consult-single-member-units-whether-wudler/),
released today by the Consultation and Uptake Division, looks at two settings in
the rules for unit meetings: a quorum that has come, in the smallest units, to
mean a single member, and the question of what follows when a resolution is
passed at a meeting that was not quorate, on which the schools have so far
reached their own conclusions.

> The units keep the University's minutes in very good order, and the Department
> reads every one of them. We would now be glad to hear from the units about the
> meetings those minutes describe, and especially from the units that have, until
> now, mostly heard from themselves.
>
> --- A spokesperson for the Department of Policy Settings

The paper sets the current arrangements beside a single alternative, a minimum
quorum of two with a period in which an inquorate resolution may be confirmed,
and places both within the Department's continuing attention to the settings the
University relies on most and examines least. It offers a preliminary view on
one matter and keeps the other open.

Submissions close on Friday 29 January 2027 and may be lodged by email to
policy.settings@slop.university. The full consultation paper is available from
the University's research repository under an open licence,
doi:10.5555/slop.wudler.
