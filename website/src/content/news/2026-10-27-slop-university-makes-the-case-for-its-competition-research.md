Slop University's research on competition now has a publication of its own. [_Room for Rivals_](/outputs/slop-brochure-room-for-rivals-wop3hl/) is a case-for-support brochure led by [Professor Verity Marris](/people/verity-marris/), Director of the Trajectory Analytics Group in the School of Emergent Priorities, with [Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma](/people/ragnhild-uzoma/), Lead of the Basement Relations Group in the School of Underlying Conditions, [Dr Petra Umbile](/people/petra-umbile/), Postdoctoral Fellow, and [Ms Tove Ilesanmi](/people/tove-ilesanmi/), Doctoral Candidate. It invites philanthropic and sector partners to help sustain the University's work on mergers, rivalry and market concentration.

> Every market we study began with someone deciding to set up a second stall. This brochure is addressed to everyone who has ever been that person, and to everyone who has ever had to choose between the two.
>
> --- Professor Verity Marris, Director, Trajectory Analytics Group, School of Emergent Priorities

The brochure is organised around three strands of activity: an anticipatory reading of consolidation that begins before any deal is announced, close fieldwork in periodic markets and car-boot fields where sellers trade within arm's reach of one another, and a body of studies on how the small rules of booking screens and trade-in counters redistribute custom between rivals. Each strand is anchored in published University research, cited by DOI, so that the case for support rests on a record readers can follow for themselves.

The University counts competition among the fields where its three Schools most naturally converge, and offers the brochure as an open door to partners who would like that convergence to continue.

Associate Professor Uzoma said she was glad the brochure "starts at the market table, which is where most of us first learned what a rival was".

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.wop3hl.
