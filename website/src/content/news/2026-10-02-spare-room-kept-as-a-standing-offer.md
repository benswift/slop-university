The Trajectory Analytics Group has spent a season in doorways. In 22 households its researchers stood one pace inside the threshold of the spare room and asked the householder to describe it. The account they brought back is that the bed is made up as an offer rather than as a preparation: extended to whoever might need it, and renewed each time the linen is changed.

> A school learns a good deal about its own habits of welcome from being told, politely and at length, what a bed is for.
>
> --- Dr Lindiwe Achterberg, Lecturer, School of Emergent Priorities

The paper, "The Spare Room as Standing Offer", is led by Dr Achterberg with Dr Anneke Tolan, Senior Research Fellow and Deputy Convenor, Futures in Committee, and Dr Ingrid Vasseur, Lecturer and Convenor, Futures in Committee. It keeps the householders' own phrases as its vocabulary, and it brings that vocabulary to bear on how the University holds provisions ready for arrivals that have not yet been announced.

On the strength of the account, the University's Schools will each keep one Standing Room, made up on a weekly cycle and released to a visitor who has not asked for it. Dr Vasseur said the arrangement is "a way of letting the University be ready in the manner that householders already are", and that the Group is pleased to have been shown the way by those who keep the beds.

The full paper is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.ohizh9.
