The [School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/) has long studied what an institution does when two worthy claims arrive at once. Its newest research poster takes that interest to the kerbside, where a set of traffic signals must decide between two buses in the time it takes a light to change.

[_Both Buses Are Late_](/outputs/slop-poster-resolving-simultaneous-transit-u3oqb9/) is the work of [Associate Professor Kwame Lindqvist](/people/kwame-lindqvist/), Director of the Master of Priority Studies, with [Dr Marit Osayande](/people/marit-osayande/), Lecturer and Convenor of the Living Dashboard, and [Dr Lindiwe Achterberg](/people/lindiwe-achterberg/), Lecturer and Deputy Director of the Trajectory Analytics Group. Bringing together a month of operational records and a calibrated simulation environment, the team set three approaches to priority allocation alongside one another across 1,140 contested signal cycles, and examined how each distributed delay between the crossing's two corridors.

> Every organisation eventually meets the moment when two good cases arrive together. We were glad to spend some time with a junction that has to settle the matter in under a second, and we expect to carry what it showed us into rooms that take rather longer.
>
> --- Associate Professor Kwame Lindqvist, Director, Master of Priority Studies, School of Emergent Priorities

The poster finds that favouring the fuller bus trims passenger delay overall while leaving one corridor waiting more often, and that favouring the later bus settles, over time, into the two corridors taking turns.

Dr Osayande described the collaboration as "the kind of shared work the two Schools have been building towards for some time, and a fitting place to continue it."

The University regards the poster as a characteristic expression of the School's attention to how competing commitments are settled in practice, one cycle at a time.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.u3oqb9.
