Every seismograph that ever wrote on paper had a stores ledger somewhere behind it, and the [School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/) has now brought that ledger to the bus stop. [_Every Quake Counted. Every Second Rationed._](/outputs/slop-ad-the-quakes-were-4at7am/), a display advertisement led by [Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma](/people/ragnhild-uzoma/), Lead of the Basement Relations Group, joins the portrait rotation of the University's signage this week.

The campaign draws on [_The Price of a Second_](/outputs/slop-poster-an-economic-history-t9j95i/), the School's research poster on a regional observatory that slowed its recording drums whenever paper grew dear. Through both price episodes the observatory's register went on counting events; what thinned was the timing of those closest to the station. On the panel this becomes a single drum recorder in a vault of wrapped paper rolls, a two-line campaign beneath it, and a reading list from the University's record on seismic survey lines, an instrument vault's isolation joint, and the cemetery memorials an earthquake leaves standing.

> I spent years logging the metres a drill run failed to bring up, so an archive that kept its count and let its detail go felt like familiar ground. The School is glad of anyone who stops long enough to ask what was kept.
>
> --- Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, Lead of the Basement Relations Group

For the University, the campaign continues a steady effort to place the slower parts of its research where a passing glance can find them, and to let an archive's quieter accounting speak for the institution that keeps it.

The full advertisement is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.4at7am.
