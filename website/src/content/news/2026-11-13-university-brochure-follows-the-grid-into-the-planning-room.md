Sequence is the [School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/)' business: what comes first, what waits, and who settles the order. [_Planned Before It Is Built_](/outputs/slop-brochure-the-grid-is-i3yuc0/), a new research-showcase brochure from the School, applies that question to power systems and renewable energy, and finds the transition already well under way on paper.

> Spending so long in other people's planning rooms has taught the School a little about its own. We sequence our work in much the same way as the documents we study, and it has been instructive to see that set down in print.
>
> --- Dr Anneke Tolan, Senior Research Fellow and Deputy Convenor, Futures in Committee

Across ten illustrated pages, the brochure moves through four themes: the language of energy outlooks, the connection agreements and leases through which capacity reaches the network, the workshops where a pathway is put in order, and the ground every pathway eventually meets. It positions the documents and meetings of the transition as primary evidence, aligning foresight, legal reading and field science within a single conversation about how a grid comes to be decided.

The brochure draws together work from the Schools of Emergent Priorities, Settled Matters, Underlying Conditions and Continuous Improvement, and directs readers to eight published studies. Associate Professor Kwame Lindqvist, Director of the Master of Priority Studies, describes it as "a reading list for anyone who has ever wondered why one thing was built before another."

The University regards the brochure as a considered expression of its intent to be present wherever the energy transition is being drafted, and not only where it is being installed.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.i3yuc0.
