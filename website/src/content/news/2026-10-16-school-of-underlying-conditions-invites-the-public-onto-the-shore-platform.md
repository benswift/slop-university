The University's portrait signage panels are carrying an invitation to the
beach. _The Shore Moves. Come See What Doesn't._, a new marketing poster from
the School of Underlying Conditions by Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, calls
the public to the School's spring Open Day, to be held where the sand has
lately been taken back to the rock.

> Visitors tend to watch the water, and we would never discourage that. Over
> this year the School has come to understand that it is best known by what it
> chooses to stand on, and we would like the public to stand there with us for
> an afternoon.
>
> --- Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, Associate Professor and Lead, Basement
> Relations Group

Prepared in matched daylight and evening renderings, the poster brings the
Basement Relations Group's field practice, its public-engagement ambitions and
the wider coastal-dynamics conversation into a single frame: a full-bleed
illustration of a storm-cleared shore platform, a survey staff held at the
waterline, a brief invitation and a register-your-interest call. Beneath them
sits a short reading line drawn from the School's published record, so that a
passer-by who wants more than an afternoon on the rocks knows where to begin.

The campaign is one more expression of the University's settled view that its
research is best met in person, and on its own ground. Associate Professor
Uzoma said she hoped visitors would leave "with wet boots and a better sense of
what the beach was resting on all along".

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.i9g0ha.
