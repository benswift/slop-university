Five years is too short to leave a layer in most rock. Slop University's
[_Strategic Plan 2026-2031_](/outputs/slop-strategy-geological-time-as-ia48ls/)
takes that as its starting point rather than its excuse, and places the slow
sciences of the solid Earth at the centre of the institution's next chapter.

The plan sets out five priorities: a network of standing field stations held
open between projects across the world's principal tectonic settings,
long-record observatories designed to outlast the plan that installs them, a
Solid Record Trust for the University's core and sample collections, a shared
foundation in deep time for every commencing student, and governance settings
recalibrated so that long-lived assets are reviewed on a cadence matched to
their lifespan. Its preparation was led by
[Dr Mirela Hanke](/people/mirela-hanke/), Postdoctoral Fellow and Deputy
Convenor of the Living Dashboard, with
[Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma](/people/ragnhild-uzoma/), Lead of the
Basement Relations Group, and [Dr Marek Solheim](/people/marek-solheim/),
Lecturer and Deputy Convenor of the Horizon Register.

> Most of what I have worked on refreshes every few minutes. Writing this plan
> taught the School something about what it means to report on an asset that
> barely changes in a lifetime, and the Living Dashboard will be the better for
> slowing down.
>
> --- Dr Mirela Hanke, Postdoctoral Fellow and Deputy Convenor, Living Dashboard

For Associate Professor Uzoma, the plan's value lies largely beyond its own
period. "The cores we shelve this decade will be read by people who have not
yet chosen their degrees," she said. "A plan that admits that is a plan worth
having."

The document positions the University's three schools as a single long-horizon
capability, aligning field presence, instrumentation and stewardship behind a
posture of institutional patience. It marks a considered step in the
University's understanding of its own place in time.

The full plan is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.ia48ls.
