The [School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/) has
long held that a neighbour's report is only as good as the route it takes to a
decision. Its new civic brochure,
[_Brought Back Down_](/outputs/slop-brochure-brought-back-down-w88xzq/),
extends that conviction to the carbon dioxide a district already gives off, and
to the University's catalysis benches where some of it is caught and kept.

The brochure was prepared by
[Dr Solveig Adeyemi](/people/solveig-adeyemi/), Lecturer and Deputy Convenor of
the Review Cadence Observatory, with
[Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma](/people/ragnhild-uzoma/), Lead of the
Basement Relations Group in the
[School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/).

> Every observation in these pages began with someone who took the trouble to
> tell us. The least we can offer the district in return is the same quality of
> attention, and this brochure is where we start paying it.
>
> --- Dr Solveig Adeyemi, Lecturer and Deputy Convenor, Review Cadence
> Observatory

Moving between the bore head, the catalyst store and the community hall, the
brochure positions the University's carbon dioxide work as a shared civic
capability, aligning bench-scale conversion with resident-led noticing and
anchoring both in the published record. It brings forward six prior studies
from across three Schools, and invites farmers, brewers, teachers and passers-by
to visit the benches and tell the University what they see.

"There is more of this work in front of us than behind us," said Associate
Professor Uzoma, "and I suspect the town will set much of its direction."

The publication sits squarely within the University's sense that research
conducted near a town is, in the end, research conducted with it.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.w88xzq.
