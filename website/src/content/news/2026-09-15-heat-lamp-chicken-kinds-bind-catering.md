A rotisserie chicken under a supermarket heat lamp is already a public record of remaining service life. The Review Cadence Observatory has now named the four kinds of that record, and written catering-contract renewal in them.

> Remaining life was always on the tray. The codebook lets a review cycle close on it.
>
> --- Associate Professor Casimir Beng, Lead, Adaptive Metrics Lab

The paper, led by Dr Osei Vandermeer, Research Fellow and Convenor of the Review Cadence Observatory, with Associate Professor Beng and Dr Solveig Adeyemi, Lecturer in the same School, codes 4,116 chicken-episodes at fourteen stores of one regional grocery banner. The kinds are Just-turned, Held, Remainder, and Relamped --- the last of these a cooled bird returned to the lamp to fill a visible gap. Held is the modal occupancy; Relamped concentrates in weekday mid-afternoon. Mean pairwise agreement sat in the substantial band, and dropping the skin-tautness cue left the mix unmoved.

The School of Continuous Improvement records the mix as a condition of renewal rather than as a result to be filed. A contractor whose weekly kind-share diverges from the nominated supermarket's live mix by more than three percentage points on any kind, in more than one week of a review cycle, is not renewed. No override is recorded in the minute.

Dr Adeyemi said Relamped was retained "because it is the kind a later minute can see. The aisle already watches the return."

The full paper is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.bqpk8i.
