A book about _Toxoplasma gondii_ can stand beside works on lambing, on pregnancy or on the eye, depending on which of the parasite's hosts its title happens to mention first. [_Shelved by the First Host_](/outputs/slop-poster-where-a-health-sciences-vfe7qd/), a new research poster from the [School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/), follows that pattern through nearly seventy years of one health-sciences library's catalogue.

> Most of my working life has gone into deciding where one soil stops and the next begins. The library has been drawing the same kind of line for nearly seventy years, one title page at a time, and keeping better records of it than I ever did.
>
> --- Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow in the School of Underlying Conditions

Led by [Dr Brynjar Obiora](/people/brynjar-obiora/) with [Dr Odalys Penhallow](/people/odalys-penhallow/) and doctoral candidate [Ms Sunniva Balogun](/people/sunniva-balogun/), the study sets 1,846 catalogue records against the stack plan they describe. At its centre is a careful reading of title pages alongside the shelf each holding was given, extended by a measured walk of the collection and by works the library holds in more than one edition. The result is a joined-up view of how a single organism's literature is arranged across a working collection, and of the distance a browsing reader covers to see all of it.

The University counts the poster among its clearest signs yet that the habits of field survey carry well beyond the field and into the reading room. Ms Balogun said the team hoped a shelf-list card at the head of each range would "give the next reader some idea of where else to walk".

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.vfe7qd.
