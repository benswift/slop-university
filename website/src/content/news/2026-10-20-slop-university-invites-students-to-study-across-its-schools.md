Prospective students weighing up where to begin their studies now have a new
guide to the question, and an unusual answer to it. _Dig Across: Come and see_,
a prospectus from the School of Underlying Conditions, asks applicants to choose
not a discipline but a place to start.

The prospectus is by Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow in the School,
with Dr Ingrid Vasseur, Lecturer and Convenor of Futures in Committee, and Dr
Torun Ezeigwe, Senior Lecturer and Director of the Master of Applied
Measurement.

> A prospectus is a promise made to people we have not met yet. We have tried to
> make this one modestly, and to leave the ground to make the rest of the case.
>
> --- Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow, School of Underlying Conditions

Structured as a descent through the University's three Schools, the prospectus
moves from the agenda-setting work of the School of Emergent Priorities, through
the measurement and evaluation ecosystem of the School of Continuous
Improvement, to the field mapping and drill-core practice of the School of
Underlying Conditions, anchoring each stage of the journey in published studies
that reached beyond their home discipline.

It is a document the University is especially glad to put its name to: an
account of interdisciplinarity that begins with a choice of starting point and
leaves the destination open.

"Every year someone arrives with a question that belongs to no School in
particular," said Dr Ezeigwe. "Those tend to be the students we remember."

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.2bqbrh.
