Ask Facilities how many people are currently waiting for a parking permit, and
the honest answer, today, is that nobody can say for certain --- not because
the number is hidden, but because it has never been added up. The Strategic
Plan 2026-2031, released today, commits Slop University to closing that gap
for every list it keeps, not just the car park's.

Under the plan, every waiting list the University runs --- for equipment,
spaces, services, and places --- moves onto a single University Standing
Ledger, with one method for assigning a position regardless of which list an
entrant joined. Positions are published in real time, case-by-case exceptions
are retired in favour of one rule per list applied to everyone, and capacity
is committed against a published forecast rather than added only once a
service is asked about it enough times.

> Every measurement system I have worked on eventually has to answer for the
> people standing behind the number, not just the number itself. This plan is
> the University agreeing, finally, to answer for them in public.
>
> --- Associate Professor Casimir Beng, Lead of the Adaptive Metrics Lab

Four priorities carry the commitment across the period, from the first list
migrated onto the Ledger to the last discretionary override retired. Dr Fenna
Okoro, Convenor of the Horizon Register, said the drafting borrowed a habit
from her own team's practice: "state the position, state what would change
it, and let the person it belongs to watch it move." An external review of the
full priority-rule set is scheduled once during the plan period, with its
findings published regardless of what they show.

The full plan is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.vyxpvd.
