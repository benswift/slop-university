Reading a court's reasons with the care usually kept for a borehole log is the
approach behind
[_Characterised by an Enthalpy of Fusion_](/outputs/slop-poster-characterised-by-an-3c1arj/),
a new research poster from the
[School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/). Written as a
doctrinal case note, it follows a patents court through a dispute over a single
crystal form whose defining number shifted with the speed of the instrument
measuring it, and asks what the claim was really protecting.

The poster is led by
[Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma](/people/ragnhild-uzoma/), Lead of the
Basement Relations Group, with [Dr Vigdis Emenike](/people/vigdis-emenike/),
Senior Lecturer in the School, and
[Ms Sunniva Balogun](/people/sunniva-balogun/), Doctoral Candidate in the
[School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/).

> Some questions reach the School from the laboratory and some from the
> courtroom, and we have always been glad to receive both. We are grateful to
> everyone who helped us read this one slowly.
>
> --- Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, Lead of the Basement Relations Group

The team brings the School's long engagement with recovered evidence to the
interface between calorimetric practice and claim construction, situating eight
production lots, three heating rates and a single worked example within a wider
conversation about how a specification anchors what it describes.

For the University, the note extends a cross-School commitment to reading the
record on its own terms, wherever that record happens to be kept. "Working
across two Schools on this has shown me how much a single page can hold," said
Ms Balogun.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.3c1arj.
