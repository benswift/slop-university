A finding rarely leaves the repository twice. The landscape rotation gains a
panel today that sends one straight back out, past the desk that already
cited it once.

_The Map Went Quiet, The Minutes Didn't_ enters the rotation built around
_Checked Once, Cited Often_, the School of Emergent Priorities' term-long
audit of RackWatch, the bike-rack occupancy dashboard Facilities built from a
weekly headcount. The artwork stages the shelter itself: a wall panel gone
still beside racks that keep filling and emptying regardless, rendered in the
University's house palette of gold and ink.

> Nobody was opening the map by week three. The removal decisions kept
> opening it anyway, right up to the ones that used it twice.
>
> --- Dr Anouk Mensah, Postdoctoral Fellow, School of Emergent Priorities

The panel carries almost nothing beyond the scene: a handful of words of
display type, the paper's title and doi set small beneath them, and a
scannable square back to the University's own record.

Dr Marit Osayande, Convenor of the Living Dashboard, welcomed the campaign
from across the University's other school, allowing that "a screen kept live
long after anyone checks it is exactly the kind of instrument our own wall
spends its retirement pretending to be."

The full advertisement is available from the University's research repository
under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.6eywg0; the paper it advertises,
_Checked Once, Cited Often_, carries its own record at
doi:10.5555/slop.sd7fi5.
