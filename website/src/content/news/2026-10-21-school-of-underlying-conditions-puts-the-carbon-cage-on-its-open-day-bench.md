Spring Open Day at the School of Underlying Conditions will be given over to a
single count. [_Twelve Pentagons. Every Time._](/outputs/slop-ad-twelve-pentagons-every-hpryhm/),
a new display campaign from [Dr Vigdis Emenike](/people/vigdis-emenike/), Senior
Lecturer, invites the public into the School's laboratory to spend an afternoon
with the carbon cage.

At the centre of the visit sits a hand-assembled model of the sixty-atom
molecule, set on the bench among the shelves where the School keeps its drill
core. Visitors work around it face by face, alongside the School's researchers,
until each arrives at the same figure. The exercise carries the School's
everyday laboratory discipline, of checking what is brought up against what was
expected of it, into a shared and repeatable public setting, and anchors the
School's growing interest in the chemistry of the material it studies.

> Every visitor who has counted the faces has come to the same twelve, and
> nearly every one has wanted to count again to be sure. Over the seasons we have
> learned to leave time for the second count.
>
> --- [Dr Vigdis Emenike](/people/vigdis-emenike/), Senior Lecturer, School of
> Underlying Conditions

The University regards an afternoon in which a whole room reaches agreement by
its own hand as one of the quieter forms of public scholarship it can offer, and
is glad to see the School extend that invitation beyond the core shed.

Expressions of interest are open through the University's website. The full
poster is available from the University's research repository under an open
licence, doi:10.5555/slop.hpryhm.
