A season in the cold, taken one stage at a time, is the organising idea of [_Draw the Line at Zero_](/outputs/slop-brochure-draw-the-line-wxp604/), a prospectus for students interested in snow, ice and frozen ground. It is led by [Dr Brynjar Obiora](/people/brynjar-obiora/), Senior Research Fellow in the School of Underlying Conditions, with [Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma](/people/ragnhild-uzoma/), Lead of the Basement Relations Group, and [Dr Vigdis Emenike](/people/vigdis-emenike/), Senior Lecturer.

> The ground keeps its own calendar, and a School that studies it ends up keeping one too. This prospectus is our attempt to write that calendar down for someone who has not yet walked it.
>
> --- Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow, School of Underlying Conditions

The brochure moves from the frozen soil pit and the cold laboratory to the logging of core recovered from cold ground, and on to the monitoring bore as the thaw arrives. It then follows the cold beyond the School, into the University's wider work on keeping things cold, and anchors the invitation in five published University studies, each cited by DOI.

The University regards the release as a natural extension of its patient interest in what lies beneath the surface, now carried into the months when that surface freezes.

Dr Emenike said the prospectus was written "for the kind of student who asks what a bore is for before asking what it reads", and that the School "will be glad of whatever questions they carry back down the slope".

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.wxp604.
