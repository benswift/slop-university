> Most of what a field is doing happens where nobody is looking, and most of what an institution learns happens in the meeting nobody remembers. We would like more people in both places.
>
> --- Dr Bram Ntuli, Lecturer and Convenor, Improvement Grand Rounds

With those words the
[School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/) opens its
case for [_Patient Ground_](/outputs/slop-brochure-patient-soil-work-nlabcp/), a
brochure inviting partners to back the University's sampling of the nematode
communities that live beneath its field plots. Prepared by
[Dr Bram Ntuli](/people/bram-ntuli/) with
[Dr Vigdis Emenike](/people/vigdis-emenike/) of the
[School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/) and
[Dr Torun Ezeigwe](/people/torun-ezeigwe/), Director of the Master of Applied
Measurement, it presents the work as a commitment renewed every season rather
than a project with an end date.

The brochure follows a single soil core from field to funnel to microscope to
the review room, positioning extraction, counting and standing review as one
continuous capability. It sets that capability alongside seven of the
University's published outputs on soil, roots and long field records, and
alongside the students who will one day carry the counts forward.

Dr Ezeigwe described the Masters cohort as "the part of the programme that
compounds", and said she hoped partners would see the plots "as somewhere to
send people, not only money".

The University regards the brochure as a fair portrait of how it prefers to be
known: steady, returning, and slow to call anything finished.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.nlabcp.
