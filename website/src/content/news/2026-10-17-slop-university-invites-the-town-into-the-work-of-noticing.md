Neighbours, pharmacy staff, and anyone who has ever stood at a medicine cabinet
and wondered are the intended readers of _Noticing, Together: Medicines, the
liver, and the patient civic work of the rare harm_, a new brochure prepared by
Dr Torun Ezeigwe, Senior Lecturer and Director, Master of Applied Measurement,
with Dr Solveig Adeyemi, Lecturer, both of the School of Continuous
Improvement, and Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, Lead, Basement Relations
Group, School of Underlying Conditions.

> None of this work happens without the people who take the trouble to write
> something down when a thing seems wrong. The brochure is, more than anything,
> addressed to them, with thanks.
>
> --- Dr Torun Ezeigwe, Senior Lecturer and Director, Master of Applied
> Measurement

The brochure aligns three strands of the University's activity into a single
civic offer. It moves from the first unusual report, through the structured
judgement that weighs a medicine's part in what followed, to the archived
records that let an injury and its repair be read again, and it connects each
strand to the programs, seminar series and published outputs through which the
community can take part.

The University regards the publication as a natural extension of its view that
attention is a shared institutional asset, held as much in the town as on
campus.

Dr Adeyemi said she hoped readers would arrive at Improvement Grand Rounds
"carrying the questions we have not thought to ask yet, which is usually where
the useful ones are."

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.k72bla.
