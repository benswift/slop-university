With [_The Price of the Pillars_](/outputs/slop-poster-paying-for-coal-xy1pdm/), the School of Underlying Conditions turns its attention from the coal a drill fails to bring up to the coal a colliery chose to leave behind.

The poster is by [Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma](/people/ragnhild-uzoma/), Lead of the Basement Relations Group, [Dr Marek Solheim](/people/marek-solheim/), Lecturer and Deputy Convenor of the Horizon Register, and [Ms Sunniva Balogun](/people/sunniva-balogun/), Doctoral Candidate. It reads the ledgers of one private mineral estate across 412 lease surrenders and 23 later proposals to gasify the remaining coal where it lay, and finds that the royalties the estate asked of those proposals followed the value its agents had booked against the abandoned pillars, rather than the gas anyone expected to raise.

> In the core shed we spend a good deal of time deciding what to write down for the metres that never came up. It was a comfort to find that the estate's agents had the same difficulty, and resolved it with rather more confidence than we usually manage.
>
> --- Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, Lead, Basement Relations Group

Setting more than seven decades of surrender entries beside a generation of gasification negotiations, the team reconciled valuation, correspondence and outcome within a single seam-level account of how an institution's own figures come to anchor the price of a technology that arrives after them.

The University regards the poster as a fitting addition to a research culture that has never been in any hurry to write anything off.

Dr Solheim said the team would keep the transcribed ledgers "open on the reading-room table for as long as anyone finds a column in them worth adding up".

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.xy1pdm.
