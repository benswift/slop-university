title: A size label explains a third of who a garment actually fits

  came from almost level with the number printed on it

  A new Slop University paper models 4,918 concealed-label try-on judgements
  over 246 garments and finds the retailer nearly as predictive of fit as the
  size printed on the tag.

A 12 from one shop and a 12 from another are, on the evidence of 4,918 try-ons,
further apart than a 12 and a 14 from the same shop.

_Twelve Means Nine Things_, published today by the School of Emergent
Priorities, treats the printed size as an instrument and estimates its
parameters the way a testing agency would. A panel of 118 wearers judged 246
garments labelled 8 to 18 from nine retailers, and an item-response model placed
every garment on one latent scale. The label carries 34.1 per cent of the
variation in who a garment fits. The retailer carries 28.6.

> A ladder that is honest inside one shop is not a standard across nine, and
> nothing on the garment tells you which of the two you are holding. The number
> is doing one job properly and a second job it was never built for.
>
> --- Professor Verity Marris, Director, Trajectory Analytics Group

The garments were bought at full price and never altered, and every label was
covered before a wearer saw it. "Nobody in the panel was ever told a size,
including their own," said Dr Anneke Tolan, Senior Research Fellow. "Once the
number is visible you are measuring what people expect rather than what fits."

The paper closes on the reporting layer above the rack. The sector's size
availability index counts labels present at audit, so a retailer whose 12 sits
two steps from everybody else's scores exactly as one whose 12 sits with them,
and returns attributed to fit are largely coded to a change-of-mind category
that quality reporting sets aside. The University puts the work forward as its
measurement programme reaching a surface most people handle weekly.

The full paper is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.iv44yy.
