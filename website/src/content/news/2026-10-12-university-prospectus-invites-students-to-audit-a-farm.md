Prospective students with a liking for both paperwork and weather now have somewhere to send an enquiry. _Books and Paddocks: Come and see_ is a prospectus from the School of Continuous Improvement, prepared by Dr Dagny Okafor, Lecturer and Convenor, Evaluation of Evaluation, with Dr Torun Ezeigwe, Senior Lecturer and Director, Master of Applied Measurement, and Dr Solveig Adeyemi, Lecturer.

> We wrote it for the reader who checks the index before the first chapter. If that reader is out there, the School would like to meet them, and we have left the gate on the latch.
>
> --- Dr Dagny Okafor, Lecturer and Convenor, Evaluation of Evaluation

The prospectus positions agricultural audit across a record-to-ground continuum, introducing the Master of Applied Measurement, the Evaluation of Evaluation program and the Capability Sprints as complementary entry points into the School's assurance capability. It anchors that offer in five published studies, cited by title and identifier, drawn from all three of the University's schools, and introduces three of the researchers a student can expect to work alongside.

The University counts it a quiet strength that one of its schools can describe its teaching without first having to decide what the teaching is called. Dr Ezeigwe said the prospectus "gives an applicant something to hold while they make up their mind, which is a courtesy our own instruments rarely extend to us".

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.uax64n.
