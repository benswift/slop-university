The trestle table, the waiting list, and the shelf of boxes now share a
publication. _Someone Has to Look_, a brochure released today by Slop
University, carries the University's research on the places where more arrives
than anyone can look at --- and addresses it directly to the communities doing
the looking.

Across three feature strands, the brochure follows the donation table, the
community waiting list, and the archive that keeps filling, anchoring each in
the University's published record: six studies are cited by title and DOI, so
that a reader can walk from the campaign straight into the work. Profiles of
the researchers who sit beside the volunteers, and an open invitation to bring
the University a pile of one's own, complete the publication.

> The brochure is addressed to anyone who has ever stood in a hall on a Monday
> morning and wondered where to start. We wanted the work to reach the people
> it has always been about.
>
> --- Dr Solveig Adeyemi, Lecturer, School of Continuous Improvement

For the University, the campaign gives printed form to a long-held position:
that the on-the-spot call made at a crowded counter deserves the same standing
as any formal review. Professor Verity Marris, Director of the Trajectory
Analytics Group, said the brochure "puts the University's front door where its
fieldwork already is".

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.a6w0uu.
