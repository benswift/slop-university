"One track per target" is the commitment at the centre of Slop University's Strategic Plan 2026-2031, released today. The plan describes the University as a network of observers, each reporting on the part of the institution it can see, and sets a course toward a single maintained picture in which every significant activity appears once.

Preparatory work for the plan was carried out in the School of Continuous Improvement by Mr Eskil Nwabueze, Doctoral Candidate, with Dr Mirela Hanke, Postdoctoral Fellow and Deputy Convenor, Living Dashboard, and Dr Marit Osayande, Lecturer and Convenor, Living Dashboard.

> We went round asking who reports on whom, and everybody we asked stopped what they were doing to tell us. The plan belongs to them, and I hope they recognise themselves in it at least once.
>
> --- Mr Eskil Nwabueze, Doctoral Candidate, School of Continuous Improvement

The plan aligns four focus areas along an observation-to-picture pathway: observation at the edge, association before aggregation, a single maintained picture, and graceful degradation and renewal. It anchors each in a set of institution-wide settings, positions stated confidence as a standard companion to every reported figure, and phases delivery across the full plan period. A closing evidence base points to five of the University's published outputs by title and DOI.

Slop University offers the plan as the settled expression of an institution that would sooner be sure of one thing than hold several views of it. Dr Osayande said the Living Dashboard was "ready to be extended in whichever direction the picture turns out to face".

The full plan is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.42sumt.
