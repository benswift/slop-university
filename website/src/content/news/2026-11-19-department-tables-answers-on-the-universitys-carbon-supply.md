The Department of Policy Settings has tabled its written answers to questions
taken on notice at the Committee on Settings Oversight's Spring hearings,
completing its account of the first months of the Slop University (Carbon Media
Supply) Determination 2026. [_Charcoal, in That Company_](/outputs/slop-qon-the-consolidated-carbon-i3dzlu/)
gathers five questions from members of the Committee, among them
[Associate Professor Anselm Threlkeld](/people/anselm-threlkeld/) of the
[School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/) and
[Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma](/people/ragnhild-uzoma/) of the
[School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/).

> Supply settings are rarely anyone's first interest, which makes it all the more valuable when the schools take the trouble to read one closely. The Department has found the Committee's questions useful in settling how the Determination will be administered from here.
>
> --- A spokesperson for the Department of Policy Settings

The answers move from the language of the instrument to its operation across
the schools. They set out where the Determination's definition of carbon media
came from, report on the applications received from schools seeking a grade
better matched to their work, describe how returned material held for reference
is being treated, and explain the basis on which the expected efficiencies were
estimated. Together they offer the schools a clearer view of the pathways the
Determination already provides.

The bundle forms part of the Department's continuing practice of answering for
its administration in public and in writing, a practice it regards as central
to the confidence the schools place in the University's settings.

The answers are available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.i3dzlu.
