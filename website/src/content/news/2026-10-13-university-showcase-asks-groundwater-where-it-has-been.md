How old is the water, and where did it fall? Slop University has today issued a publication for anyone who has wanted to put either question to a bore. _Water With a Past: Reading the age and origin of groundwater_ is a research showcase from the School of Underlying Conditions, prepared by Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow, with Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, Associate Professor and Lead, Basement Relations Group, and Dr Vigdis Emenike, Senior Lecturer.

> The water we sample has usually been waiting a good deal longer than we have been asking. It seemed only courteous to set down, in one place, what we do with it once it arrives.
>
> --- Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow, School of Underlying Conditions

The brochure aligns the School's field sampling, laboratory practice and monitoring-network stewardship around a shared provenance-and-residence posture, carrying the reader from the cap of a bore to the rock beneath it across three themes. It introduces the researchers behind the program and points to seven of the School's published studies, each cited by title and DOI.

The University counts a readiness to wait among the quieter strengths of its newest school, and is glad to see that readiness given a cover. Associate Professor Uzoma said the showcase "puts the bore, the water and the people who read them on the same few pages, which is roughly where we have always thought they belonged".

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.36pmtw.
