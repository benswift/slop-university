A single drawer of index cards sits at the centre of the School of Continuous
Improvement's newest research poster, and on those cards one plant at the edge
of a field is a weed, then a trap crop, then a refuge.

[_Weed, Trap Crop, Refuge_](/outputs/slop-poster-weed-trap-crop-nkt6xq/) is by
[Dr Osei Vandermeer](/people/osei-vandermeer/), Research Fellow and Convenor of
the Review Cadence Observatory, with
[Dr Marek Solheim](/people/marek-solheim/), Lecturer and Deputy Convenor of the
Horizon Register, and [Dr Brynjar Obiora](/people/brynjar-obiora/), Senior
Research Fellow in the School of Underlying Conditions. Reading 412
pest-record cards kept between 1948 and 1996, the team finds that the plant's
status changed in the seasons after the station's programme reviews, while the
flea beetle, aphid and parasitoid wasp entered on its cards held steady
throughout.

> The reading-room staff brought the drawer out for us every week for the best
> part of a year, and I would like the poster to stand partly as a thank-you to
> them. A long look depends on someone willing to keep finding the key.
>
> --- Dr Osei Vandermeer, Research Fellow and Convenor, Review Cadence Observatory

The poster brings the Observatory's interest in the rhythm of institutional
review into the history of agricultural science, connecting archival method,
field-margin practice and the life cycle of a classification across three
schools. It is the kind of patient, cross-disciplinary reading the University
is pleased to see its review researchers take up.

Dr Solheim described the cards as "very good company for a historian, because
they never tell you more than they meant to".

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.nkt6xq.
