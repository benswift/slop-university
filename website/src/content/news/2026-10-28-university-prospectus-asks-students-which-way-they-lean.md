The University's three Schools rarely share a single subject. In its latest prospectus they share half of one each.

[_Every Mind Leans_](/outputs/slop-brochure-every-mind-leans-nrp4pf/) is by [Dr Brynjar Obiora](/people/brynjar-obiora/), Senior Research Fellow in the [School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/), with [Hedvig Anyanwu](/people/hedvig-anyanwu/), Doctoral Candidate in the [School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/), and [Eivind Onwuka](/people/eivind-onwuka/), Doctoral Candidate in the [School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/). Written for prospective students, it presents the study of the brain's two unequal halves as a question no one School could hold on its own.

> A prospectus is really a list of the questions a student will be handed on arrival. We wanted to hand over good ones, and to leave the answering to whoever walks in.
>
> --- Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow, School of Underlying Conditions

The brochure moves across scales in three features, from the counted strokes of the handwriting drill and the layered column of the cortex, through the design of a recording session and the people who complete it, to the boundaries a careful hand traces on a stained section. Each feature pairs a School's research culture with a route into study, and the whole is anchored by five of the University's published studies.

Ms Anyanwu said she had been "glad to see the questions we work on set out for people who have not yet met them", adding that she hoped some readers would arrive with better ones.

The University regards the publication as a fair portrait of an institution that prefers its disciplines to meet in the middle.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.nrp4pf.
