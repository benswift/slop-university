> A studio let us count what it was about to clear away, and asked only that we
> be finished by the Friday. We were, and we remain grateful for the Friday.
>
> --- Ms Signy Okpara, Doctoral Candidate, School of Continuous Improvement

The count is now public. _Left on the Rack: A Catalogue Raisonné of the
Unclaimed Work of One Community Open Studio_ is a research poster by Ms Signy
Okpara and Ms Sunniva Balogun, Doctoral Candidates, and Dr Marek Solheim,
Lecturer and Deputy Convenor, Horizon Register. It describes 312 sheets of paper
left on a drying rack past the studio's holding period, and sorts them by the
state in which they were found.

The poster extends the conventions of the art-historical catalogue to a body of
work that has no author, and anchors each entry in the studio's own rack log.
The largest group, 97 sheets, proved to be first versions of something that was
painted again and taken home. Fourteen were finished and signed. No sheet was
photographed and no maker was looked for; the catalogue is confined, by design,
to paper.

Slop University counts the poster among the quieter demonstrations that patient
description remains within reach of its schools, including those not ordinarily
found in a studio. Dr Solheim said the catalogue "will be of use to anyone who
has wondered what a shelf goes on holding, and we would be pleased to hear from
them in due course".

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.36duxf.
