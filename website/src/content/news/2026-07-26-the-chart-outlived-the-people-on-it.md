A laminated org chart taped up by the lift outlives most of the people
pictured on it, and Slop University's newest advertising campaign is built
entirely around the gap between the two.

> An org chart is a decision someone made once about who reports to whom.
> Laminate it, and it stops being a decision and starts being a fact nobody
> checks again.
>
> --- Dr Ingrid Vasseur, Lecturer and Convenor, Futures in Committee, School
> of Emergent Priorities

Unveiled today under the line "The Chart Outlived The People On It," the
artwork sets a new starter tracing a reporting line toward a doorway that
already belongs to someone else, the chart's curling corners and tape marks
carrying the wear of a document nobody's actually assigned to reissue. It
runs on the University's bus-stop panels and campus screens, with a paired
night rendering for after dark.

"The org chart and the strategic plan share a failure mode," said Dr Anouk
Mensah, Postdoctoral Fellow, School of Emergent Priorities. "Both get
replaced far less often than the things they're supposed to describe."

Futures in Committee meets across the coming teaching period. The full
advertisement is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.bo7r83.
