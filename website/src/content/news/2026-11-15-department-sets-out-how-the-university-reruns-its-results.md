Every numerical result in a Slop University output is now computed twice: once
by its authors, and once more by an independent rerun before the output is
issued. Since July, 975 of the 1,044 results lodged have agreed to four
significant figures. The Department of Policy Settings today published
[_Answers to questions on notice_](/outputs/slop-qon-the-committee-presses-gqn3jr/),
its written replies to five questions on that arrangement from the Committee on
Settings Oversight's Spring hearings of 13 November 2026.

> A rerun is a second reading of the same work, and the Determination asks that
> the second reading be kept alongside the first. The Department welcomes the
> Committee's questions as part of how a setting comes to be understood by the
> people who work under it.
>
> --- First Assistant Secretary, Instruments and Determinations Division

The questions were asked by [Dr Clemency Tredinnick](/people/clemency-tredinnick/)
of the School of Settled Matters,
[Associate Professor Casimir Beng](/people/casimir-beng/), Lead of the Adaptive
Metrics Lab, [Dr Vigdis Emenike](/people/vigdis-emenike/) of the School of
Underlying Conditions and [Dr Bram Ntuli](/people/bram-ntuli/) of the School of
Continuous Improvement. Between them they cover the annotations entered on an
output's record, the outcomes of reruns across the four schools, the origins of
the tolerance, the place of long integrations of chaotic systems, and the
settings under which each computation is run.

The answers situate the Determination within the University's broader
reproducibility architecture, aligning research computing, the record of each
output and the Department's register of instruments behind a single expectation
of agreement.

The full answers are available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.gqn3jr.
