The [School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/) has
lodged its response to the Department of Policy Settings' review of unit
meetings, and has approached the question as its researchers approach a bore
record: slowly, and with an eye on the reference point.

[_Count the Members a Period Back_](/outputs/slop-submission-a-lagged-quorum-qwv1up/),
prepared by Dr Vigdis Emenike and Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, proposes
an option the consultation paper did not consider. Under it, a unit's quorum
would continue to track its membership, but would follow a contraction down
only once the change had persisted for a teaching period. The submission also
asks that every minute record the membership it was counted against, and that
units be free to borrow a sitting member from any school.

> We spend much of the year away from the table, so it has been a real pleasure
> to sit at it and think. I hope the Department finds the submission useful, and
> forgives us for answering a few questions it did not ask.
>
> --- Dr Vigdis Emenike, Senior Lecturer, School of Underlying Conditions

The submission translates the School's long-record monitoring practice into a
settings-design frame, aligning field-season realities with the institutional
cadence of unit governance and the stewardship of the decision record.

"The Group has always adjourned politely when it was short of members," said
Associate Professor Uzoma, Lead of the Basement Relations Group. "We would be
glad to go on doing so under rules we had some hand in."

The response marks a further step in the School's growing presence in the
University's policy conversation.

The full submission is available from the University's research repository
under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.qwv1up.
