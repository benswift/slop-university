Students who enrol next semester in the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/)' course in statutory interpretation will meet a medicine leaflet before they meet a statute. [_Every Warning Outlives the People Who Wrote It_](/outputs/slop-ad-every-liver-warning-ppwm4j/), a new portrait advertisement for the University's display screens, opens the course's Semester 1 intake by laying a liver-injury warning out along a seminar table and asking who will be reading it once its drafters have moved on.

> Every cohort brings a different set of eyes to the same few lines, and I have not yet met one that left those lines quite as it found them. I am looking forward to meeting the next.
>
> --- [Associate Professor Anselm Threlkeld](/people/anselm-threlkeld/), School of Settled Matters

Convened by Associate Professor Threlkeld, the course brings close reading, the craft of legislative drafting and the habits of the courtroom to bear on the fine print that travels with a product, building in each cohort the interpretive capability to construe a provision for a reader who arrives well after the debate that produced it. The poster's small print points prospective students to three earlier University publications, among them [_The Warning Handed On_](/outputs/slop-brochure-the-warning-handed-u47eqp/) and [_Below the Reporting Limit_](/outputs/slop-brochure-below-the-reporting-uq23dc/).

The University welcomes the campaign as a sign of how far a careful reading can now travel from the page that prompted it. Enquiries are open through courses.slop.university.

The full advertisement is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.ppwm4j.
