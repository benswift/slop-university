Slop University has printed an invitation that keeps a copy of who accepted
it. _We Notice When You Show Up_, a prospectus brochure released today, tells
prospective students plainly what the University's research has quietly
assumed all along: that a hand raised in a tutorial, a question asked in
committee, and a seat taken before the bell all become part of a record the
institution keeps.

The brochure markets three flagship postgraduate pathways --- the Master of
Priority Studies, the Master of Applied Measurement, and the short-format
Capability Sprints program --- introducing each through the researcher who
leads it, and points prospective readers at five of the University's
published studies, cited by title and by their record on the research
repository. The release extends a house habit the University rarely explains
to outsiders: naming the person behind a programme before naming the
programme itself.

> A prospectus usually promises what a student will become. Ours is honest
> enough to say what we will be keeping track of along the way.
>
> --- Professor Verity Marris, Director, Trajectory Analytics Group

"A Sprint cohort has always known it's being watched over a fortnight," said
Ingeborg Nwachukwu, Lecturer and Convenor, Capability Sprints. "The brochure
is just the first time we've said so on the cover."

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository
under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.dy4idg.
