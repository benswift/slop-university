At the corner of a roadside demonstration plot, an extension service sows a new
practice beside the old one and leaves both in the open for a district to walk
past until harvest. The School of Emergent Priorities has chosen that strip of
ground as the setting for its newest call to prospective students.

[_Some Priorities Need a Growing Season_](/outputs/slop-ad-some-priorities-need-i1hkno/)
is a landscape display poster for the Master of Priority Studies, prepared by
[Hedvig Anyanwu](/people/hedvig-anyanwu/), Doctoral Candidate, and
[Associate Professor Kwame Lindqvist](/people/kwame-lindqvist/), Director of
the program. It shows a field day gathered on the bund between two halves of a
trial field, a farm truck waiting on the road beyond.

> A demonstration plot is honest about its timetable. Nobody standing at the
> bund expects an answer before the rows come up, and I would like our students
> to arrive with that same courtesy towards the questions they bring.
>
> --- Hedvig Anyanwu, Doctoral Candidate, School of Emergent Priorities

The campaign aligns the program's sensing-and-sequencing curriculum with the
long practice of the agricultural field day, positioning the side-by-side trial
as a durable frame for how priorities are surfaced, compared and carried
forward across a full season of institutional life.

The University counts the poster among its more unhurried pieces of signage,
and is content to let it stand on the rotation through the coming teaching
period.

Associate Professor Lindqvist said each application would be read "with the
attention a trial row is given in its first fortnight, which is to say often,
and without drawing conclusions".

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.i1hkno.
