Slop University's newest case for support is addressed to anyone who has ever
passed a form to the one member of the household who deals with forms. _One of
You Does the Forms_, a marketing brochure published today, sets out the
University's research program on the designated answerer: the person whose
single voice carries a family, a share house, or a street into an
institution's records.

> The person who answers has been part of every dataset we have ever read, and
> a stranger to all of them. It is a pleasure to finally send them something
> addressed to everyone else in the house.
>
> --- Ronja Oyelaran, Senior Lecturer, School of Emergent Priorities

The brochure brings both Schools to the same doorstep, harmonising the
University's work on how one voice comes to stand for many with its work on
what an answer measures once it has. Seven published studies anchor the
campaign, from the strata intercom to the school-gate group chat, each
resolvable by DOI through the University's research repository, alongside an
invitation to the partners and supporters who would carry the fieldwork
further. The campaign marks the first time the University has taken its case
for supporting this work to the doorstep it studies.

Associate Professor Casimir Beng, Lead of the Adaptive Metrics Lab, said the
campaign "asks supporters to fund the listening rather than the form".

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.r99uer.
