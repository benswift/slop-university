The University's signage panels will spend the coming weeks somewhere few
institutions of higher learning think to look: a wholesale fruit and vegetable
floor, in the hour before trading opens. _We Reach the Market Before the Price
Does_, a new marketing poster prepared by Dr Vigdis Emenike, Senior Lecturer in
the School of Underlying Conditions, sets out that hour in a single lamplit
image.

> Fieldwork has always meant getting there before the thing you came to see. We
> were pleased to find that the habit travels, and that a market keeps hours not
> so very different from an aquifer's.
>
> --- Dr Vigdis Emenike, Senior Lecturer, School of Underlying Conditions

The campaign brings the School's field discipline into conversation with the
economics of food markets, positioning the early reading as the University's
distinctive contribution to how a price comes to be known. Pallets of seasonal
produce, a forklift at rest and buyers gathered at the auctioneer's stand frame
a quieter figure at the margin, clipboard and thermos to hand, whose attendance
the poster treats as the institution's own. A short reading list from the
University's published record on pricing and markets runs beneath the campaign
line, connecting a display surface to the scholarship standing behind it.

The poster extends the University's settled conviction that being present, early
and attentively, is itself a form of contribution, and it joins the School's
growing presence on the panels in both daylight and evening renderings.

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.k45ico.
