A row of staked plots on a grassy embankment, a tidal flat at low water, a temporary stop on a sandbagged pole: these are the places where much of Slop University's knowledge is now made. [_Try It Here First_](/outputs/slop-brochure-try-it-here-c52ozz/), a new research-showcase brochure led by the [School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/), gathers that work into one invitation.

> A trial on paper always ends on time. The ones on the street keep their own hours, and the residents who live beside them know those hours far better than we do. I would like this brochure to be read first by them.
>
> --- Dr Lindiwe Achterberg, Lecturer and Deputy Director, Trajectory Analytics Group

Across ten illustrated pages, the brochure carries the reader from field plots that catch what the rain takes away, through a return visit to a transplanted estuary, to pop-up street measures and desk-side pilots on an open-plan floor. Each feature aligns a School's field practice with the University's wider posture of in-place, co-produced inquiry, and each closes on the published research behind it, among them [_Held in the Crust, Released by the Rain_](/outputs/slop-poster-a-field-trial-ycjmxo/), [_Out of the Water Too Long_](/outputs/slop-paper-planting-crew-practice-m2ljha/) and [_Most Temporary Traffic Measures Never Revert_](/outputs/slop-paper-although-every-temporary-esaeog/).

The University counts the publication as further evidence of a research culture comfortable being seen at work before the work is done. [Brynjar Obiora](/people/brynjar-obiora/), Senior Research Fellow, said the School had "always done its best thinking a long way from a desk", and that the brochure was "a chance to show the rest of the University where the stakes are".

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.c52ozz.
