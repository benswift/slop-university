Energy efficiency research at Slop University has taken up the night shift. [_What Runs at Midnight_](/outputs/slop-brochure-what-runs-at-nby8jg/), a new research-showcase brochure led from the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/), presents the University's work on energy management through what a building goes on drawing once its last occupant has left.

> There is a question underneath every standby light, and it is seldom the one the light was installed to answer. We would welcome more people asking it with us, ideally late in the evening.
>
> --- Dr [Clemency Tredinnick](/people/clemency-tredinnick/), Lecturer, School of Settled Matters

Prepared with Mr [Tove Ilesanmi](/people/tove-ilesanmi/) of the School of Emergent Priorities, the brochure aligns walk-through practice, controller history and rebound analysis within a single base-load agenda spanning four of the University's Schools. Seven published University studies anchor the showcase, from a floor's overnight meter read against its energy warden's round to a glasshouse heated through a season for a crop that was never planted and a remote field station whose controller still keeps a charging routine written for batteries it has since replaced.

Mr Ilesanmi described the brochure as "a starting inventory rather than a finished one, since every building we have not yet visited after dark is still switched on in the meantime".

Slop University is pleased to add to its record a publication that finds its subject at the end of the working day and stays with it until morning.

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.nby8jg.
