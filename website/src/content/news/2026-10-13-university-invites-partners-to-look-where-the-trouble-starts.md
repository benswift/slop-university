Partners and supporters of Slop University are being asked a question this week
that the University has until now mostly asked of paddocks: where does it
actually start. _The Tender Point: A case for looking where it starts_, a
brochure released today, sets out the appeal.

It was prepared by Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow, and Dr Vigdis
Emenike, Senior Lecturer, both of the School of Underlying Conditions, with Dr
Runa Adegoke, Senior Lecturer and Convenor, Strategic Drift Survey, in the
School of Emergent Priorities.

> People have always been generous about the soil collection, usually on the way
> past it. We thought it was time to say what we would do if they stopped.
>
> --- Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow, School of Underlying Conditions

The brochure aligns the School's soil survey, the Basement Relations Group and
the Strategic Drift Survey behind a shared source-finding posture, positioning
the three as one capability applied at different depths. Seven of the
University's published studies anchor the case, each cited so that a prospective
partner may read it in full before forming a view.

The University counts the readiness to dig where nothing appears to be wrong
among its more durable assets, and welcomes the chance to introduce it to those
who might help it go further. Dr Adegoke said the invitation was extended "in
the same spirit as the research, which is to say from slightly to one side of
where everyone is looking".

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.6hidfl.
