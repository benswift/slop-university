Asked why an electrode will not hold its calibration, laboratory technicians
blame the hardware first, themselves second, and the electrode's temperament
only once a day has passed without an answer.
[_A Theodicy of Drift_](/outputs/slop-poster-a-theodicy-of-0rgqcv/) is by
[Dr Mirela Hanke](/people/mirela-hanke/), Postdoctoral Fellow and Deputy
Convenor of the Living Dashboard in the
[School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/), with
[Dr Vigdis Emenike](/people/vigdis-emenike/), Senior Lecturer in the
[School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/), and
[Dr Bram Ntuli](/people/bram-ntuli/), Lecturer and Convenor of Improvement
Grand Rounds.

> The Living Dashboard spends most of its time on instruments that report to
> us. It was instructive to spend a season with the people who answer for them,
> and to notice how much patience they extend to an electrode that they would
> never extend to a spreadsheet.
>
> --- Dr Mirela Hanke, Postdoctoral Fellow and Deputy Convenor of the Living Dashboard

Working from the logbooks of a regional water-testing laboratory, alongside
time at the bench and conversations with the technicians who keep it, the team
sorted 213 episodes of unexplained drift by the kind of account each one
received. The resulting picture sets everyday laboratory quality culture
alongside a long scholarly tradition of thinking about how communities make
sense of disorder in a world they hold to be orderly. Among its observations:
when an electrode is finally retired, the logbook tends to revisit its moods
and record them, after the fact, as ageing.

The poster adds to the University's growing body of work on the inner life of
the laboratory, a field in which it has lately found itself unusually well
represented.

"I spent years writing 'probe settling' in a field book without once wondering
what I meant by it," said Dr Emenike. "I am not sure I know now, but I have
more company."

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.0rgqcv.
