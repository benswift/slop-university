> The School has spent a long time learning to read what lies beneath a surface. It feels right that this part of its journey should begin with a single page of correspondence.
>
> --- Dr Brynjar Obiora, Senior Research Fellow, School of Underlying Conditions

With _Thank You for Seeing_, the School of Underlying Conditions extends its habit of close attention from the ground to the written word. The new paper by Dr Obiora takes up one of the most ordinary documents in any health system, the referral letter, and asks why a text written to pass information along so often takes the shape of a prayer.

Reading a year of letters from one rural general practice in the order they were written, the paper sets each against the five-part structure of the collect, the short prayer of the Western liturgy, and follows what happened to that structure when the district's clinics began receiving referrals through a central intake office. Address, thanksgiving, petition and commendation emerge as the scaffolding of an interpretive approach that sets everyday clinical correspondence alongside a much older practice of asking on another's behalf.

The University counts the work among the first fruits of a widening conversation between its earth sciences and the study of religion, and as a sign of the reach its schools are beginning to find beyond their founding disciplines.

The full paper is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.7oft8z.
