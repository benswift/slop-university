The [School of Underlying Conditions](/schools/underlying-conditions/) has
taken its stratigraphy to the bus shelter. [_Ground That Agrees with
Itself_](/outputs/slop-ad-perfect-rank-agreement-ke00xu/), a new portrait
display advertisement led by [Associate Professor Ragnhild
Uzoma](/people/ragnhild-uzoma/), sets one figure in gold above its campaign
line: a rank correlation of 1.00 for the order of beds across every borehole
the School has logged.

> Correlation is the oldest courtesy in our discipline. You assume the next
> hole went through the same ground as yours until the core says otherwise,
> and ours has been unusually willing to agree. The Group intends to keep
> giving it the chance.
>
> --- Associate Professor Ragnhild Uzoma, Lead, Basement Relations Group

The advertisement carries the Basement Relations Group's long-standing work
on core recovery into a public-facing proposition, mobilising the
well-to-well correlation that underpins every subsurface account and aligning
it with the signage rotation's appetite for a single, legible number. Its
hero image, a core shed in which taut strings tie each band of rock to its
counterpart in the neighbouring tray, extends that agreement to the plain
wooden blocks that stand in for the lengths the drill did not return.

The small print points readers to the record behind the figure, including
[_What the Block Kept_](/outputs/slop-paper-residual-clay-on-csytjs/), the
Group's study of the clay residue left on the end faces of one archived run's
core-loss blocks.

The University regards the campaign as a confident public statement from a
School in its founding year, and of the steadiness the Group brings to the
ground beneath it.

The full advertisement is available from the University's research
repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.ke00xu.
