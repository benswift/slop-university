For five years the [School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/) has asked its collaborators to print their dashboards, forms and review templates at full size, pin them to a wall and talk about them in company. [_Pinned Up_](/outputs/slop-impact-five-years-of-941jmq/), the School's Impact Report 2021–2026, prepared by Dr [Osei Vandermeer](/people/osei-vandermeer/), Dr [Mirela Hanke](/people/mirela-hanke/) and Dr [Bram Ntuli](/people/bram-ntuli/), gathers what came of the practice.

> Most of what an organisation measures has never been looked at from across a room. We have made a start, and there is a great deal of wall left.
>
> --- Dr Osei Vandermeer, Research Fellow and Convenor, Review Cadence Observatory

Organised around four impact areas, the report traces how a habit borrowed from design education travelled into the working instruments of the regulatory, cultural and tertiary sectors, enabling collaborators to align their indicator ecosystems with the conversations those indicators are meant to support. Along the way the crit found a place in the School's teaching, through a studio stream in the Master of Applied Measurement and a new module in the Capability Sprints, and in the School's review of its own reviews.

Dr Hanke said the work had shown the team "how much a room can see once everyone in it is standing a little further back."

The University welcomes the report as a sign that its schools are willing to stand behind their work, and from time to time in front of it.

The full report is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.941jmq.
