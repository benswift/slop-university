The [School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/) has published [_Keeping the Register True_](/outputs/slop-impact-keeping-the-chemical-rm7rmp/), its Impact Report 2021–2026, an account of five years spent alongside the people who keep chemical registers in laboratories, plants and warehouses.

The report is organised around four impact areas. It moves from reconciling a register line by line against the containers on the shelf, through the rhythm of the stocktake and an ambient display of how current a store's safety data sheets are, to the careful retirement of lines for things a store no longer holds. Each area rests on a collaboration, from the teaching stores of a dual-sector institution to the member sites of a research consortium, and on the School's own published research.

> A register is a promise made to whoever opens it next. Most of what we did over these five years was help people keep that promise on an ordinary afternoon, with the staff they already had.
>
> --- Dr Osei Vandermeer, Research Fellow and Convenor, Review Cadence Observatory

The report was written by [Dr Osei Vandermeer](/people/osei-vandermeer/) with [Dr Marit Osayande](/people/marit-osayande/), Lecturer and Convenor of the Living Dashboard, and [Dr Renke Sabel](/people/renke-sabel/), Senior Lecturer and Convenor of the Indicator Commons. In the University's view it is an unusually patient document: a School content to measure its contribution in counts that held. Dr Osayande said the safety data sheet work had taught her that "a display can change what a room notices well before it changes what a room does".

The full report is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.rm7rmp.
