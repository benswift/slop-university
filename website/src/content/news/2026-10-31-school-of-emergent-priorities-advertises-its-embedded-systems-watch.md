Embedded systems design has stood on the School of Emergent Priorities' Horizon
Register for eleven quarters, and it now has a campaign of its own.

[_Small Systems. Our Biggest Priority_](/outputs/slop-ad-embedded-systems-design-bxj6ry/)
is a portrait display advertisement for the University's signage screens,
prepared by [Dr Lindiwe Achterberg](/people/lindiwe-achterberg/), Lecturer in
the [School of Emergent Priorities](/schools/emergent-priorities/). A single
microcontroller rises over a flat horizon, its pins drawn as rays, while a
surveyor kneels at a theodolite on the ridge below and keeps it in view. The
campaign positions small, dedicated computing as a long-horizon capability for
the School, one it has elected to watch closely rather than hurry. Beneath the
call to action, the panel cites three of the University's earlier studies: an
open-plan office's booth occupancy light, and two that examine the Register
itself.

> Some priorities arrive all at once. This one has given us time to get to know
> it, and the School is grateful for every quarter of that.
>
> --- Dr Lindiwe Achterberg, Lecturer, School of Emergent Priorities

The University counts the panel among the clearest expressions yet of a School
willing to hold its attention on a single point for as long as the point
requires. Asked when the item might next be reviewed, Dr Achterberg said the
review "is already being scoped".

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under an
open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.bxj6ry.
