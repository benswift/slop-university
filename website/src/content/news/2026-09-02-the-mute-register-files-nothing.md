Unmuting did not predict a later contribution. That is the finding, and it
is the finding the University is keeping. A new marketing poster from the
School of Continuous Improvement puts Dr Mirela Hanke, Postdoctoral Fellow
and Deputy Convenor of the Living Dashboard, in front of the Mute Event
Register: an instrument that logged every correction in a hybrid meeting,
found no relationship with who spoke next, and filed the null as a closed
result.

The campaign is the public face of a small institutional decision. The
register's empty return has been lodged as a tile on the Living Dashboard.
The file is closed. No follow-up study is scheduled, and the tile is not
listed for retirement.

> A register that comes back empty is still a register doing the job it was
> built for.
>
> --- Dr Mirela Hanke, Postdoctoral Fellow and Deputy Convenor, Living Dashboard, School of Continuous Improvement

The campaign confirms a standing practice at the School: an empty return is
treated as a result the wall will keep. Dr Marit Osayande, Lecturer and
Convenor, Living Dashboard, said the tile "gives the University a published
account of a meeting habit that did not, in the end, measure anything worth
acting on."

The full poster is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.h11yyl.
