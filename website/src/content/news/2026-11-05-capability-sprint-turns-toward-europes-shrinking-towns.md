Next semester, the Capability Sprint Reading the Dashboard gives its
participants one kind of place to read: the European town whose residents,
shopfronts and timetables are all moving in the same direction. Registrations
of interest open today with _Learn to Read a Town That Is Leaving_, a display
advertisement now in the campus signage rotation.

The advertisement is the work of Mr Eivind Onwuka, Doctoral Candidate, and Dr
Ingeborg Nwachukwu, Lecturer and Convenor, Capability Sprints, both of the
School of Continuous Improvement.

> A dashboard for a town like this has to be read from the end of the street,
> not the top of the screen. We would like participants to arrive with a town
> of their own in mind, and to leave knowing where to stand.
>
> --- Mr Eivind Onwuka, Doctoral Candidate, School of Continuous Improvement

The Sprint aligns regional-indicator literacy, place-based judgement and
longitudinal reading practice around the gradual case, moving participants from
snapshot interpretation toward trajectory-aware stewardship of places in slow
transition. Dr Nwachukwu described the coming offering as "a short course in
patience with indicators that move by the year".

The University counts the Sprint's turn toward Europe among the clearer signs
of a short-course portfolio finding its international footing, and anticipates
that the cohort's work will inform how the School reads gradual change closer
to home. Prospective participants may follow the advertisement's address to the
Sprint's pages.

The full advertisement is available from the University's research repository
under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.fmhm09.
