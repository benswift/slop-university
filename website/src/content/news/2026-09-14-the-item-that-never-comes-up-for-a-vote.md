Futures in Committee exists to turn an open question into committee
business --- narrowing a scanning exercise's loose ends into an agenda item,
then a recommendation, then a minute. Its next session turns that machinery
on a question the School's own agendas rarely carry: what keeps a program in
provisional status years after the review meant to settle it.

Dr Lindiwe Achterberg, Lecturer in the School of Emergent Priorities,
presents the session under convenor Dr Ingrid Vasseur. Dr Achterberg's
research assembles a comparative archive of initiatives kept running on
renewed provisional budgets and re-scoping memos, tracing how an extension
can substitute indefinitely for the confirmation a program was originally
meant to receive.

> A program doesn't need a decision to keep running. It only needs nobody
> scheduling the meeting where the decision would happen.
>
> --- Dr Lindiwe Achterberg, Lecturer, School of Emergent Priorities

"Most committees are carrying at least one item that has been under
consideration since before their newest member joined," said Dr Ingrid
Vasseur, Lecturer and Convenor of Futures in Committee. "Lindiwe's session is
our chance to work out which of ours qualify."

Futures in Committee keeps its brief wide enough to turn on the School's own
long-running items, not only the horizons it scans elsewhere. The session is
planned for later this teaching period, with venue and time to follow closer
to the date.
