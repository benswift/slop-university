A parent registering a child for junior sport is asked how the family heard
about the club, and finds "Other (please specify)" at the foot of the list. That
moment opens _Other (Please Specify), Explained_, a research explainer released
today by the School of Emergent Priorities, and from there the film works its
way through
[_Other (Please Specify)_](/outputs/slop-thesis-other-please-specify-fr8eys/),
the doctoral thesis of [Mr Halvard Nnamani](/people/halvard-nnamani/).

> The University is always working on its forms, and once in a while one of its
> doctoral candidates gets to help. I am glad that this time it was me.
>
> --- [Mr Halvard Nnamani](/people/halvard-nnamani/), Doctoral Candidate, School
> of Emergent Priorities

The parent's answer then travels the length of the thesis. It is counted among
more than sixty thousand entries, followed into the exports of the organisations
that receive it, and finally set beside a trial in which the box was emptied at
submission for some respondents and left off the form for others. The result
positions the residual field less as an instrument of data collection than as a
small, consequential courtesy extended between community organisations and the
people they serve, and [Mr Nnamani](/people/halvard-nnamani/) explains the
fieldwork in his own words.

The University had already taken the advice. Its Specify Field Standard, adopted
on the thesis's recommendation, ends every list on a University form with the
box and keeps nothing typed into it, and the University's paperwork will be the
more courteous for it.

The full explainer is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.mc4dzt.
