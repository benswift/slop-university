An impurity that survived five rounds of purification is the face of the
[School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/)' newest public campaign.
[_Some Burdens Run With the Crystal_](/outputs/slop-ad-some-burdens-run-q4tm7x/),
a portrait advertisement for the University's signage network, invites
passers-by to look again at [_What the Lattice Keeps_](/outputs/slop-paper-why-a-structurally-e0ycms/),
the School's recent paper on what a crystal carries with it from one
recrystallisation to the next.

> None of this would have reached a bus stop without the people who washed the
> glassware, weighed the filter cakes and kept the bench in order through five
> passes and three solvents. The School owes them its thanks, and a good deal of
> its patience.
>
> --- Dr [Clemency Tredinnick](/people/clemency-tredinnick/), Lecturer, School of
> Settled Matters

The campaign renders a body of solid-state evidence as a single line of display
type, connecting the School's long attention to obligations that pass with
property to the bench-level practice of purification, and anchoring both in an
image of a beaker that has kept more than it was asked to.

The University counts the campaign among the steadier signs that its law school
and its laboratories have found a shared vocabulary, and is content to let the
signage panels make that case in its place.

The full advertisement is available from the University's research repository
under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.q4tm7x.
