For anyone who has wondered why a school's drill runs smoothly one term and
stalls the next, Slop University now offers a place to ask.
[_Practise Until It's Ordinary_](/outputs/slop-brochure-practise-until-it-c0kegi/),
a prospectus brochure from the
[School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/), sets out
the University's work on how safety is taught, rehearsed and passed on in
schools. It is led by
[Associate Professor Casimir Beng](/people/casimir-beng/), Lead of the Adaptive
Metrics Lab, with doctoral candidates [Ms Signy Okpara](/people/signy-okpara/),
[Mr Tove Ilesanmi](/people/tove-ilesanmi/) and
[Ms Edda Nwafor](/people/edda-nwafor/).

> Every school has routines it runs so well that nobody thinks of them as
> teaching any more. We would like to welcome students who want to think about
> them again.
>
> --- Associate Professor Casimir Beng, Lead of the Adaptive Metrics Lab

Moving from the school oval to the drop-off kerb and the staffroom, the
brochure aligns the University's process-modelling capability with its
fieldwork in ordinary public settings, and anchors that alignment in five
published studies spanning first-aid practical assessment, school-crossing
supervision and learn-to-swim progression. It maps a pathway from the
Capability Sprints through the Master of Applied Measurement to doctoral study.

For the University, the prospectus marks a research culture growing at ease
with its quietest subjects. Mr Ilesanmi said a term at the school gate had
taught him "to be patient with whatever a place repeats every morning", and
that he hoped new students would "bring that patience with them, and leave a
little of it behind".

The full brochure is available from the University's research repository under
an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.c0kegi.
