Two Year 9 classes, a twelve-figure booklet and one sentence from the glossary of an out-of-print textbook are at the centre of [_The Trapezoid Clause_](/outputs/slop-paper-inclusive-and-exclusive-rj1yyg/), a new paper by [Associate Professor Anselm Threlkeld](/people/anselm-threlkeld/) of the [School of Settled Matters](/schools/settled-matters/) and [Dr Torun Ezeigwe](/people/torun-ezeigwe/), Senior Lecturer and Director of the Master of Applied Measurement in the [School of Continuous Improvement](/schools/continuous-improvement/).

The sentence defines a trapezoid as "a quadrilateral with a pair of parallel sides", and leaves open whether a parallelogram counts. The paper sets out what each answer commits a geometry course to, then asks how students settle the question once the textbook's authors are no longer available to settle it for them.

> Every classroom runs on sentences someone else wrote. What stays with me is how seriously these students took one of them, and how much more we now want to ask about the others.
>
> --- Dr Torun Ezeigwe, Senior Lecturer and Director, Master of Applied Measurement

Combining a formal treatment of the definition with a structured construal exercise, the authors map the interpretive resources young readers mobilise when a text falls silent, positioning the classroom glossary within a wider ecosystem of drafting, intention and precedent. Between two sittings, students who met a single worked example from later in the same textbook moved to the inclusive reading far more often than students who read the authors' own account of what they had meant.

"A glossary is a small statute with an unusually large readership," said Associate Professor Threlkeld, "and we were glad to find so many of its readers this attentive."

With this paper the University counts the mathematics classroom among the settings its newest School has learned to read, and welcomes the conversations with teachers that it hopes will follow.

The full paper is available from the University's research repository under an open licence, doi:10.5555/slop.rj1yyg.
